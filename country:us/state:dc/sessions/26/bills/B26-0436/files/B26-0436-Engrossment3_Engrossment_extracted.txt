10 To amend the District of Columbia Election Code of 1955 to require the Board of Elections to
11 reject initiatives which address more than one subject matter; to extend the amount of
12 time for the Office of the Attorney General and the General Counsel of the Council to
13 provide an advisory opinion on whether a proposed measure is proper subject for an
14 initiative from 15 days to 30 days after receipt from the Board of Elections; to require the
15 Office of the Chief Financial Officer to provide a fiscal impact statement for a proposed
16 initiative measure within 15 days after receipt from the Board of Elections and to require
17 the Board to post the fiscal impact statement on its website for a period of one year; to
18 require the Board to post on its website the full legislative text and the proposer’s verified
19 statement of contributions filed with the Director of Campaign Finance; to require the
20 Board to accept and post on its website public comments on the measure; to require the
21 Board of Elections to hold a public meeting to issue a determination accepting or refusing
22 a proposed initiative measure; to require the Board to suspend action on an initiative
23 pending review in the Superior Court; to require the Board prepare a fiscal impact
24 summary of an accepted initiative measure; to require the petition sheet to include the
25 include the text of the official summary, short title, and fiscal impact summary in at least
26 11-point font; to require the petition sheet to include the name of the proposer and
27 indicate where the verified statement of contributions can be found; to require the fiscal
28 impact summary be included on the ballot; and for other purposes.
29
30 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
31 act may be cited as the “Fairness and Accountability in Initiative Reform Amendment Act of
32 2026”.
