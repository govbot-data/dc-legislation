3 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
4
5 _________________________
6
7 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
8 _________________________
9
10
11 To celebrate the 40th anniversary of Bagels Etc. and the impact the establishment has had on
12 Washingtonians seeking a comforting start to their day.
13
14 WHEREAS, for 40 years, Bagels Etc. has continued its dedication to traditional bagel-
15 making techniques;
16 WHEREAS, Bagels Etc. has prided itself in creating a welcoming atmosphere with cozy
17 nooks and local artwork displayed;
18 WHEREAS, the staff’s friendly demeanor fosters a genuine sense of community to
19 newcomers and regular customers;
1

20 WHEREAS, Bagels Etc. is located in the bustling P Street corridor steps from cultural
21 landmarks and Embassy Row;
22 WHEREAS, Bagels Etc. adds to the neighborhood charm of Foggy Bottom and is
23 frequented by students, commuters, and visitors.
24 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
25 resolution may be cited as the “Bagels Etc. 40th Anniversary Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
26 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and celebrates the 40th
27 Anniversary of Bagels Etc. and the community the establishment fosters in the District.
28 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
