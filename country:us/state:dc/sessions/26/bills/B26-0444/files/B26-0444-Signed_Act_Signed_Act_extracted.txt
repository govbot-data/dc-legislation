To amend the Food Policy Council and Director EstablishmentAct of2014 to change the
timelinefortheFood Policy Council annual report from annually to triennially and
expand the authorityof the Food Policy Council to evaluate food procurement practices
for Districtagencies and contractorsthat regularly purchase food and beverages.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OFTHE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited asthe “Food Policy Council Procurement Amendment Actof2026”.
Sec. 2. The Food Policy Council and Director Establishment Act of2014, effective
March 10, 2015 (D.C. Law20-191; D.C. Official Code § 48-311 efseq.), is amended as follows:
(a) Section 2 (D.C. Official Code § 48-311) is amended as follows:
(1) Paragraph (1) is redesignated as paragraph (1A).
(2) A new paragraph(1) is added to read as follows:
“(1) “Covered agency” means a District agency oragency contractorthat
regularly procures food or beverages. The term “covered agency” shall not include aDistrict
agency oragency contractor that procures food orbeverages solely forvendingmachinesorone-
offcatering services forevents.”.
(b) Section3(c) (D.C. Official Code § 48-312(c)) isamendedto read as follows:
“(c) Beginning September 30, 2027, and every 3 years thereafter, the Food Policy
Council shall providea report tothe Council onthe state ofthe local food system across the
District. The report shall assessDistrictfood access, including an identification oflow food
access areas, and the stateof the local food economy, and provide policy recommendations to
improve the local food economy and food access, including priorities forlocal investments.”.
(©) Section 5 (D.C. Official Code § 48-314) is amended as follows:
(1) Subsection (b) is amended as follows:
(A) Paragraph (5) is amended by striking thephrase “; and” and inserting
a semicolon in its place;
(B) Paragraph (6) is amended by striking theperiod and insertingthe
phrase “; and” in its place.
(©)A new paragraph(7) is added to read as follows:
