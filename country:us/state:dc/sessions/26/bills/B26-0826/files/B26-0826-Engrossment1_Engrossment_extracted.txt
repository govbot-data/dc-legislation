10 To modify, on a temporary basis, the requirements for District school breakfast service.
11
12 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
13 act may be cited as the “Alternative Breakfast Service Model Temporary Act of 2026”.
14 Sec. 2. The Healthy Schools Act of 2010, effective July 27, 2010 (D.C. Law 18-209;
15 D.C. Official Code §§ 38-821.01 et seq.) is amended as follows:
16 (a) Section 101(1B) (D.C. Official Code § 38–821.01) is amended to read as follows:
17 “(1B) “Breakfast in the classroom” means an alternative breakfast serving model where
18 students are served breakfast in the classroom.”
19 (b) Section 203(a) (D.C. Official Code § 38–822.03(a)) is amended as follows:
20 (1) Subparagraph (2)(A) is amended by striking the phrase “breakfast in the
21 classroom” and inserting the phrase “all students grab-and-go breakfast, breakfast in the
22 classroom, or a combination of both breakfast models” in its place.
23 (2) Subparagraph (2)(C) is amended by striking the phrase “, without breakfast-in-
24 the-classroom”.
