ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-200
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To recognize, honor, and celebrate the 100th Annual Holy Convocation of The United House of
Prayer for All People.
WHEREAS, The United House of Prayer for All People was founded in 1919 by Bishop
C.M. Grace in West Wareham, Massachusetts;
WHEREAS, Bishop Grace built the first edifice with his own hands for the cost of $39,
and the structure still stands today;
WHEREAS, Bishop Grace declared the First Annual Holy Convocation of The United
House of Prayer to improve the spiritual condition of “all people”;
WHEREAS, The United House of Prayer first purchased property in 1926 by acquiring a
mansion and its slave quarters, which now serve as the organization’s national headquarters;
WHEREAS, the public outreach of Christian fellowship, including the Convocation,
began under Bishop Grace and was exemplified by the Church’s willingness to feed the needy
during the Great Depression and many declared that “Daddy Grace’s Church had soup with meat
in it!”;
WHEREAS, Bishop W. McCollough grew the Church’s outreach by launching the
McCollough Voters League into a major electoral force in the City; establishing a homegrown
scholarship, the McCollough Scholarship College Fund, which for 55 years has been operated
and funded by the Church’s membership; and a massive housing program, which caused Bishop
McCollough to be called a “one-man religious HUD,” that has made it possible for many citizens
from the downtrodden to the rising, thriving middle class to have an opportunity to live, work,
and flourish in the District of Columbia;

WHEREAS, Bishop S.C. Madison, a longtime District resident and pastor, led his
congregations into 17 Convocations, increased the Church’s real estate holdings, and advanced
the scholarship initiative by beginning the First Lady’s Scholastic Achievement Program to
complement and feed into the organization’s scholarship program;
WHEREAS, Bishop C.M. Bailey worked with DC residents to encourage them to turn
their lives, hearts, and souls over to a higher power, leading members into 15 Convocations, even
spending his last moments on earth administering the 97th Annual Holy Convocation; and
WHEREAS, Bishop A.D. Cunningham, the fifth and current leader of the United House
of Prayer for All People, has protected the Church’s intellectual properties and does the
important work of building buildings and homes for people from all walks of life while also
working to improve the spiritual walk of “all people,” as the Organization’s name implies.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “The United House of Prayer for All People 100th Annual Holy
Convocation Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and celebrates the 100th
Annual Holy Convocation of The United House of Prayer for All People, its past leadership, and
its current leader Bishop A.D. Cunningham, who are commended for the efforts to improve the
District of Columbia, its residents, and the lives of “all people.”
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
