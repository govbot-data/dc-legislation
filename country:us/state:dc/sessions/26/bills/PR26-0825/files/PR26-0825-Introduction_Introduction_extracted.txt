A PROPOSED RESOLUTION
________
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
_____________
To disapprove proposed contract No. 2025-149 with Flats at South Capitol LLC to approve a
loan amount of $3,664,444.19.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Proposed Contract No. 2025-147 with Southern Avenue Owner
LLC Disapproval Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. Pursuant to section 451 of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved
December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 803; D.C. Official Code § 1-204.51), and section 202 of the
Procurement Practices Reform Act of 2010, effective April 8, 2010 (D.C. Law 18-371; D.C.
Official Code § 2-352.02), the Council disapproves Contract No. 2025-147 with Southern
Avenue Owner LLC in the amount of $3,664,444.19.
Sec. 3. Transmittal.
The Council shall transmit a copy of this resolution, upon its adoption, to the Mayor.
Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Budget Director as the fiscal impact
statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved
October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
Sec. 5. Effective date.
This resolution shall take effect immediately.

Secretary Howard,
I am introducing the “Proposed Contract No. 2025-149 with Southern Avenue Owner LLC Disapproval Resolution of
2026” alongside Councilmember Henderson and Silverman to preserve time for Council review of CA26-1027, a
proposed $3,664,444.19 Housing Production Trust Fund stabilization loan for Livingston Place at Southern Avenue in
Ward 8. The contract is scheduled for passive approval on October 15.
On October 7, the Committee on Housing requested information and supporting records necessary to evaluate the
proposed financing. DHCD committed to providing comprehensive, line-by-line responses by October 13. Although the
agency has provided decision memoranda and clarified the property’s ownership and management, substantive responses
and records remain outstanding.
These include the development team’s past-performance evaluation and property management improvement plan,
DHCD’s consideration of the OAG allegations involving a minority ownership participant and related entities, and the
financial analysis establishing how the proposed financing will stabilize operations and address continuing shortfalls.
Committee staff had a constructive conversation with Gilbane, the project’s financial guarantor, and we remain open to
moving the transaction forward with appropriate safeguards. Preserving affordable assisted living for the property’s senior
residents is a priority. We also need sufficient information to assess the proposed use of District funds.
Introducing this resolution will prevent the contract from receiving approval through the initial passive approval period
and allow additional time to obtain and review DHCD’s responses. It does not itself disapprove the contract or determine
the outcome of the Council’s review.
Sincerely,
