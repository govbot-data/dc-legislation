14 To declare the existence of an emergency, due to congressional review, with respect to the need
15 to amend the Department of For-Hire Vehicles Establishment Act of 1985 to clarify the
16 definition of a private vehicle-for-hire operator.
17
18 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
19 resolution may be cited as the “Private Vehicle-for-Hire Operator Clarification Congressional
20 Review Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
21 Sec. 2. The Council has passed emergency and temporary legislation to clarify that the
22 Department of For-Hire Vehicles (DFHV) has had and continues to have the authority to address
23 the range of threats to passenger and driver safety caused by illegal for-hire vehicle operators and
24 companies, empowering DFHV to continue enforcing registration, insurance, and trade dress
25 requirements. However, the Council anticipates a potential lapse in the law while the most recent
26 temporary legislation undergoes congressional review. Further emergency legislation is therefore
27 necessary.
28 Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
29 enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances, making it necessary that the Private
1

30 Vehicle-for-Hire Operator Clarification Congressional Review Emergency Amendment Act of
31 2026 be adopted after a single reading.
32 Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
