ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-464
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
July 14, 2026
To confirm the appointment of Michael Wakefield to the District of Columbia Board of Elections.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “District of Columbia Board of Elections Michael Wakefield
Confirmation Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia confirms the appointment of:
Michael Wakefield
Quincy Place, NE
Washington, DC 20002
(Ward 5)
as a member of the District of Columbia Board of Elections, established by section 3 of the
District of Columbia Election Code of 1955, approved August 12, 1955 (69 Stat. 699; D.C.
Official Code § 1-1001.03), for a term to end July 7, 2029.
Sec. 3. The Council shall transmit a copy of this resolution, upon its adoption, to the
nominee and to the Mayor.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
