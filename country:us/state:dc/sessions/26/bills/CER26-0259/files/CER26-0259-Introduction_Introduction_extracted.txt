12 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
13 __________
14
15 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
16 __________
17
18
19 To express appreciation to the Office of Labor Relations and Collective Bargaining
20 (OLRCB) for providing outstanding representation by the District government on all aspects of
21 labor relations, overseeing the administration of collective bargaining agreements, and working
22 with unions to develop policies in support of District government employees.
23
24 WHEREAS, the District of Columbia recognizes that a dedicated, professional, and
25 effective workforce is essential to deliver quality services to residents;
26
27 WHEREAS, the Office of Labor Relations and Collective Bargaining advances the public
28 interest by supporting the District government’s labor-management relations and promoting
29 constructive engagement between District agencies and employee representatives;
30
31 WHEREAS, through its work in collective bargaining, labor negotiations, and contract
32 administration, the OLRCB promotes a fair, productive, and cooperative working environment
33 for District government employees;
34
35 WHEREAS, effective labor relations require professionalism, open communication,
36 mutual respect, and a commitment to resolving differences while balancing the needs of
37 employees with the responsibilities of efficient government;
38
39 WHEREAS, Director E. Lindsey Maxwell and the very capable team of Asha Ault,
40 Kevin Stokes, Michael Kentoff, Nina Jones, Michelle Dunn, Vincent Harris, DeYan Harris,
41 Caleb Condor, Neah Evering, Kieshorne Dennie, Brittany Smith-Harding, Alexis Martinez, Kyle
42 Bradley, Kyle Simmons, Mary Redfearn, Nina Glamp, Keith Butler, Raven Tittle, and Pedro
43 Agosto are committed public servants, whose dedication and diligence have created a lasting
44 professional impact within the District;
45

46 WHEREAS, OLRCB's Negotiations and Contract Administration unit is responsible for
47 negotiating collective bargaining agreements, training management representatives, and
48 conducting impact and effects bargaining over subjects that are statutorily deemed management
49 rights;
50
51 WHEREAS, OLRCB’s Litigation unit is focused on initiating, prosecuting, defending
52 and monitoring a wide range of litigation activity, for and on behalf of agencies under the
53 personnel authority of the Mayor;
54
55 WHEREAS, OLRCB's Administrative unit is responsible for providing general agency
56 support and conducting research and analysis necessary to support management’s position during
57 negotiations and litigation;
58
59 WHEREAS, OLRCB has delivered and working on agreements benefitting the
60 Washington Teachers Union, the Firefighters, the Fraternal Order of Police, Compensation
61 Units 1 and 2, Compensation Unit 13, Compensation Unit 3758, Compensation Unit LIUNA,
62 Compensation Unit 13(B), Compensation Unit 29, Compensation Unit 19(B), Compensation
63 Units 6 and 17, Compensation Units 8, Compensation Units 5 and 16, Compensation Units 9,
64 Compensation Unit 4, Compensation Unit 3, Compensation Unit 35, Compensation Unit 33,
65 Compensation Unit 8, Compensation Unit 34 and other organized union members that constitute
66 the vast majority of DC government employees through their service;
67
68 WHEREAS, the Office has worked with District agencies, labor organizations, and
69 stakeholders, supporting continuity of government operations, and strengthening the
70 relationships that contribute to effective public service; and
71
72 WHEREAS, Councilmember Anita Bonds wishes to express her sincere appreciation to
73 the Office of Labor Relations and Collective Bargaining for its service to DC and for its
74 contributions to strengthening the accountability and effectiveness of District government
75 operations.
76
77 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
78 resolution may be cited as the “Office of Labor Relations and Collective Bargaining Recognition
79 Resolution of 2026”.
80
81 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the Office of Labor Relations
82 and Collective Bargaining for its role in representing the District on all aspects of labor relations
83 and extends its appreciation to the Office for assuring that District government employees serve
84 with distinction and professionalism.
85
86 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
87 the District of Columbia Register.
