12 To amend the Advisory Neighborhood Councils Act of 1975 to provide an opportunity for the
13 Office of Advisory Neighborhood Commissions to evaluate any proposal to establish
14 single-member districts to ensure the neighborhood cohesiveness and viability of such
15 districts, and for the OANC to submit a report to the Council on its findings and
16 recommendations, to require the Council to consider the report of the OANC when
17 making adjustments in the boundaries of the Advisory Neighborhood Commission
18 single-member districts and ANC areas, to provide for the sufficiency of a certification
19 by the Department of Corrections that a Commissioner who is a resident of the Central
20 Detention Facility or the Correctional Treatment Facility has been transferred or is
21 otherwise no longer a resident of the facility in order to declare the seat vacant, to
22 provide that the Board of Elections may accept a funeral home death notice or obituary,
23 or an obituary published in a newspaper of general circulation or any other proof of
24 death that the Board deems sufficient in order to declare the seat vacant, to clarify that if
25 a single-member district contains the CDF and CTF, the Board of Elections shall make
26 in-person voting available to qualified registered electors who are not in the care and
27 custody of DOC, that the Department of Corrections shall make voting available to
28 registered qualified electors in its care and custody in consultation with the Board of
29 Elections, that the Board of Elections shall transmit the results of the voting to the
30 OANC and the affected ANC, and that ballot counting for the voting shall be facilitated
31 by at least two representatives of the Board of Elections, to correct an erroneous citation
32 in Section 13 regarding reference to the ability of an ANC to object to a license for the
33 sale or on-premises consumption of alcohol, to repeal a provision regarding the
34 discretionary requirement for a Commission to final a fiscal year annual report with the
35 Council and the Mayor, to add a provision that quarterly allotments will be withheld
36 from any Commission without an approved annual fiscal year spending plan by
37 November 30th of each year until such time as an annual budget is approved and filed
38 with the OANC, to clarify that no Commission may solicit donations unless specifically
39 authorized, to provide that a copy of each Commission’s bylaws and all amendments
40 thereto shall not be effective until transmittal to the Council and the OANC and posting
41 on the OANC website, to provide that the Commission vice-chairperson shall schedule a
42 special meeting to remove an officer after a proper request for removal by the
43 Commission is made if the Chairperson does not schedule a meeting within 30 days of
44 the request, to clarify that the OANC shall provide advice regarding Commission
45 expenditures and responses to inquiries from Commissions on the legality of proposed
46 actual expenditures, to require that any release of ANC allotment funding which will
1

47 result in a Commission’s bank account or bank accounts exceeding a combined balance
48 of $50,000 will be transferred to the Advisory Neighborhood Commissions Technical
49 Support and Assistance Fund to be used in support of ANCs, to provide that a
50 Commission can expend funds received through the annual allocation on independent
51 contractors, contracts for services, memoranda of understanding with District
52 government agencies and telecommunications service requests through the Request
53 Telecommunications Services, to require that the contact number of a telephone must be
54 publicly available when Commission funds are used to pay for the cost or use of the
55 telephone, and to provide that a Commission may through an interoffice transfer
56 managed by the OANC transfer a portion of their annual allotment to a District agency
57 that provides food, shelter, or healthcare; and to amend the Redistricting Procedure Act
58 of 1981 to provide that the Office of Advisory Neighborhood Commissions shall appoint
59 one or more staff members to serve as a technical advisor to each ward task force on
60 ANCs.

63 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
64 act may be cited as the “Advisory Neighborhood Commissions Functions Modernization
65 Amendment Act of 2026”.
