ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-501
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve Modification Nos.
M04, M05 and M06 to Contract No. CW106884 with See Forever Foundation, and to
authorize payment for the goods and services received and to be received under the
contract.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Modification Nos. M04, M05 and M06 to Contract No.
CW106884 with See Forever Foundation Approval and Payment Authorization Emergency
Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve Modification Nos. M04, M05 and
M06 to Contract No. CW106884 with See Forever Foundation to provide an education program
to youth who are in the custody of the DYRS at the Youth Services Center, and to authorize
payment for the goods and services received and to be received under the contract.
(b) By Modification No. M04, issued on July 7, 2026, the Office of Contracting and
Procurement (“OCP”), on behalf of the Department of Youth Rehabilitation Services (“DYRS”),
exercised a partial option of option year 3 in the not-to-exceed amount of $1 million for the
period July 7, 2026, through September 30, 2026.
(c) By Modification No. M05, issued on July 28, 2026, OCP exercised another partial
option extending the period of performance through October 7, 2026, at no additional cost.
(d) Proposed Modification No. M06 will exercise the remainder of option year 3 and
increase the not-to-exceed amount by $2,968,957.85, to a total not-to-exceed amount of
$3,968,957.85.
(e) Council approval is necessary because the modifications will increase the value of the
contract to more than $1 million during a 12-month period.
(f) Council approval is necessary to allow the continuation of these vital services.
Without Council approval, See Forever Foundation cannot be paid for goods and services
provided in excess of $1 million for the period from July 7, 2026, through July 6, 2027.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
