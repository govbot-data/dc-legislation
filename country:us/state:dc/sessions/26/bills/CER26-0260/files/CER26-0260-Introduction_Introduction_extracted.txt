12 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
13 __________
14
15 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
16 __________
17
18
19 To honor and recognize the Office of Employee Appeals for rendering impartial, legally
20 sufficient, and timely decisions on appeals filed by District of Columbia government employees.
21
22 WHEREAS, the Office of Employee Appeals (OEA) serves an important role in the
23 District of Columbia government as an independent agency governed by a five-member Board of
24 Directors by providing impartial administrative review of personnel actions through mediation,
25 adjudication, and petitions for review;
26
27 WHEREAS, OEA helps ensure that covered employees have access to a fair and
28 independent process for solving employment disputes, while supporting accountability and
29 adherence to applicable personnel laws and regulations;
30
31 WHEREAS, through its administrative hearings, consideration of appeals, and issuance
32 of decisions, the Office of Employee Appeals contributes to the fair and consistent
33 administration of the District’s personnel system;
34
35 WHEREAS, Pia Winston, Jeanne Moorehead, and LaShon Adams serve as OEA’s Board
36 of Directors, leading the agency in its fervor and objectivity in serving District government
37 employees and District agencies;
38
39 WHEREAS, Executive Director Sheila Barfield and her team of Lasheka Brown,
40 Sommer Murphy, Hemchand Hemraj, Joseph Lim, Eric Robinson, Monica Dohnji, Natiya
41 Curtis, ChoNayse Sellers, and Wynter Clarke, have demonstrated outstanding commitment to
42 public service, creating a lasting impact within the District’s workforce;
43
44 WHEREAS, OEA exceeded their Fiscal Year 2025 targets by issuing 95 Initial Decisions
45 and 22 Opinions and Orders on Petitions for Review;
46

47 WHEREAS, OEA continues to issue decisions that successfully withstand judicial
48 scrutiny, and are often relied upon and cited by various entities as courts continue to uphold a
49 majority of OEA’s decisions brought forth on appeal to either the DC Superior Court or to the
50 DC Court of Appeals;
51
52 WHEREAS, the professionalism, dedication, and commitment of the Office of Employee
53 Appeals staff support the effective operation of District government and reinforce the principles
54 of fairness and due process in public service;
55
56 WHEREAS, the Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the importance of the
57 Office of Employee Appeals and its contributions to maintaining public confidence in the
58 District's administrative processes; and
59
60 WHEREAS, Councilmember Anita Bonds wishes to express her sincere appreciation to
61 the Office of Employee Appeals for its service to DC and for its contributions to strengthening
62 the accountability and effectiveness of District government operations.
63
64 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
65 resolution may be cited as the “Office of Employee Appeals Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
66
67 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia extends its gratitude to the leadership and
68 staff of the Office of Employee Appeals for their continued commitment to fairness,
69 professionalism, and public service.
70
71 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
72 the District of Columbia Register.
