15 To recognize and celebrate Old City Market and Oven, on the occasion of its 10th anniversary, for
16 its exceptional offerings, welcoming ambiance, and commitment to neighbors and the
17 Capitol Hill community.
18
19 WHEREAS, Old City Market and Oven opened its doors in 2016 at 522 K Street, NE, to
20 provide delicious wood-fired, brick oven pizza, fine wines, art, and music to the Near Northeast
21 neighborhood on Capitol Hill;
22
23 WHEREAS, Old City Market and Oven is owned by Curt Hansen, who grew up in New
24 York City and moved to the District of Columbia in 1977;
25
26 WHEREAS, after arriving in the District, Hansen first took a job on Capitol Hill as a
27 dishwasher at the Tune Inn Restaurant & Bar, and later, after years of working in law and
28 diplomacy abroad, found the perfect two-story, corner, Victorian-style building on K Street, NE;
29
30 WHEREAS, together with his wife, Irene Koek, Hansen began to envision plans for a
31 neighborhood convenience store which would also serve take-out pizza;
32
33 WHEREAS, this concept became Old City Market and Oven, which serves a variety of
34 foods and beverages, including those with Mediterranean, Lebanese, Turkish, Moroccan, and
35 Italian influences;
36
37 WHEREAS, customer favorites include fire-roasted pizzas, fresh salads, cold and hot
38 sandwiches, quesadillas, calzones, chicken wings, and delicious sides;
39
40 WHEREAS, Old City Market and Oven maintains a rich and varied selection of fine wines
41 from across the globe, including organic, natural, and bio-dynamic wines;
42
43 WHEREAS, Old City Market and Oven also curates a diverse collection of records and
44 books, which customers can listen to and read while eating in the cozy and atmospheric downstairs
45 seating area;
46
1

47 WHEREAS, Old City Market has been a generous, longstanding supporter of the
48 neighborhood, including numerous partnerships with J.O. Wilson Elementary School;
49
50 WHEREAS, Hansen has been instrumental in supporting the arts in the local community,
51 from displaying paintings made by students in art class at J.O. Wilson Elementary School,
52 supporting veterans' art shows, and hanging local artists' work;
53
54 WHEREAS, Hansen has built a dedicated team, many of whom have grown their careers
55 and families while at Old City; and
56
57 WHEREAS, in 2023, Old City Market and Oven won a Ward 6 “Brickie” Business Award
58 for its significance to the Ward 6 community.
59
60 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
61 resolution may be cited as the “Old City Market and Oven 10th Anniversary Recognition
62 Resolution of 2026”.
63
64 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and celebrates Old City Market
65 and Oven, on the occasion of its 10th anniversary, for its exceptional offerings, welcoming
66 ambiance, and commitment to neighbors and the Capitol Hill community.
67
68 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
