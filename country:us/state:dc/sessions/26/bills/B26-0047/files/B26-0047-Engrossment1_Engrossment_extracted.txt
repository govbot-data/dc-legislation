11 To amend the District of Columbia Funeral Services Regulatory Act of 1984 to modify and add
12 definitions related to the practice of funeral directing and embalming, to change the
13 requirements for a funeral director’s license and to create an embalmer’s license, to
14 modify requirements for a funeral directing or embalming apprenticeship, to revise
15 supervision requirements for embalming and funeral director apprentices, and to
16 incorporate the practice of embalming into the provisions governing fraud and grounds
17 for denial, suspension, or revocation of a license; and to amend section 47-2853.06 of the
18 District of Columbia Official Code to update the composition of the Board of Funeral
19 Directors.
20
21 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
22 act may be cited as the “Funeral Services Modernization Amendment Act of 2026”.
