25 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26 __________
27
28 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
29 __________
30
31
32 To honor Vida Senior Services (VIDA) for more than five decades of extraordinary
33 service to older adults, acting as a home-away-from-home for older adults that offers free meals,
34 health screenings, nutritional guidance, and mental health support, all in a multilingual setting.
35
36 WHEREAS, Vida Senior Services, founded in 1969, is the oldest Hispanic nonprofit
37 organization in the District of Columbia and has served the community for more than 55 years;
38
39 WHEREAS, VIDA is dedicated to improving the lives of older adults, particularly
40 through culturally and linguistically appropriate programs and services that recognize the
41 dignity, traditions, and unique needs of the communities it serves;
42
43 WHEREAS, VIDA provides essential services that promote health and wellness, social
44 connection, independence, dignity, and quality of life for older adults and their families;
45

46 WHEREAS, Vida Senior Services has been a trusted community partner, helping older
47 Washingtonians remain engaged, connected, healthy, and independent while strengthening the
48 families and neighborhoods in which they live;
49
50 WHEREAS, VIDA offers nutritious food, including a daily congregate meal site and a
51 food distribution program that since the Covid-19 pandemic has delivered 9,500 boxes of fresh
52 produce and 2,970 Food Bank boxes;
53
54 WHEREAS, VIDA provides programming that addresses the mental health needs of
55 older adults, including individual and group counseling sessions, tai-chi classes, and positive
56 thinking workshops;
57
58 WHEREAS, VIDA recognizes the importance of lifelong learning and engages seniors
59 with classes on embroidery, knitting, music, computer and technology, painting, and more;
60
61 WHEREAS, VIDA promotes the health and wellness of seniors by offering medical
62 referrals, support with doctors’ appointments, programming on chronic diseases and diabetes,
63 and exercise classes in dance, Zumba, tai-chi, and yoga;
64
65 WHEREAS, VIDA’s mission focuses on the unique needs of Spanish-speaking seniors,
66 offering Spanish literacy and English language classes to seniors who might otherwise
67 themselves isolated culturally and linguistically;
68
69 WHEREAS, VIDA’s enduring commitment reflects the District of Columbia’s
70 recognition that every older adult deserves to age with dignity, respect, opportunity, and access
71 to the resources necessary to live a healthy and fulfilling life; and
72
73 WHEREAS, the District recognizes and celebrates the invaluable contributions of VIDA
74 Senior Services and its staff, volunteers, supporters, and community partners who have devoted
75 themselves to serving older adults and strengthening the District’s diverse communities.
76
77 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
78 resolution may be cited as the “Vida Senior Services Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
79
80 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors Vida Senior
81 Services for more than five decades of commitment to older adults, caregivers, and families in
82 the District of Columbia, extending its sincere appreciation for its continued dedication for
83 improving the health, independence, and quality of life for the District’s senior population.
84
85 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
86 the District of Columbia Register.
