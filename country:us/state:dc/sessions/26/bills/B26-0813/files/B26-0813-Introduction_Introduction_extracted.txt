SUBJECT: Fiscal Impact Statement – Commercial Bingo Amendment Act of 2026
REFERENCE: Draft bill as provided to the Office of Revenue Analysis on March 19,

Conclusion
Funds are sufficient in the revised fiscal year 2026 and proposed fiscal year 2027 through fiscal year
2030 budget and financial plan to implement the bill. The Alcoholic Beverage and Cannabis
Administration (ABCA), Office of Lottery and Gaming (OLG), and Office of Tax and Revenue (OTR)
can implement the bill within current resources.
Background
District law1 established the Lottery and Gaming Control Board as a subordinate division known as
OLG, within the Office of the Chief Financial Officer (OCFO), to regulate lotteries, daily numbers
games, bingo, and raffles for charitable purposes, in the District. The bill amends2 the definition of
“commercial bingo” to mean a bingo game where players pay to participate and the numbers are
drawn and verbally announced in person, with all participants physically present and competing in
the same game. However, the term does not include:
• Bingo organized by certain entities3, including nonprofits, senior groups, and citizen-service
programs;

• Bingo played using a federally defined4 gambling device;
• Bingo played online or over the internet;
• Bingo played on machines that resemble or qualify as class III gaming devices under federal
law5; and
• Bingo played on slot machines.
The bill repeals the prohibition on bingo or raffle devices that allow a person to play bingo against a
machine or computer instead of other in-person players, or that allow a person to join a commercial
bingo game from a location outside the licensed establishment where a live caller is announcing the
numbers. These changes clarify that Class II electronic bingo machines are legal, but still prohibit
commercial bingo that is played over the internet or in an online environment, or on any slot
machine.
Title 47 of District Code governs taxation in the District, including definitions of key terms related to
the imposition of taxes. The bill adds a definition for “net gaming revenue”6 and sets the sales7 and
use8 tax rates for commercial bingo at 25% of net gaming revenue, with proceeds credited9 to the
Lottery, Gambling, and Gaming Fund10 and the General Fund. Taxing bingo operators on their net
proceeds will allow bingo operators to deduct the amount of money, winnings, or prizes paid out to
players.
Financial Plan Impact
Funds are sufficient in the revised fiscal year 2026 and proposed fiscal year 2027 through fiscal year
2030 budget and financial plan to implement the bill. ABCA, OLG, and OTR can implement the bill
within current resources. ABCA requires no additional resources to approve commercial bingo
endorsements.11 Positions necessary for OLG to regulate and enforce laws and rules pertaining to
commercial bingo operations were included in OLG’s fiscal year 2026 budget.12 OTR requires no
additional resources to develop materials related to the implementation of the bill. There is no
additional revenue estimated as a result of this bill since it is unknown how many licensees will
operate, nor if commercial bingo activity will displace other existing taxable sales.
