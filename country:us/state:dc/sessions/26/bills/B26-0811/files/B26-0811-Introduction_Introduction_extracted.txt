Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Enclosed for consideration and approval by the Council of the District of Columbia is the
“Opening of Streets, Designation of Opened Streets, and Closing of a Portion of a Street in
Square 1112-E Act of 2026”.
The bill will approve the opening of streets in Square 1112-E and designate the opened streets as
20th Street, S.E., 21st Street, S.E., Burke Street, S.E., 20th Place, S.E., 21st Place, S.E., C Place,
S.E., and C Street, S.E. It will also close a portion of 20th Street, S.E. in Ward 7. These openings
and closing will facilitate the construction of Phase 2 of the Hill East Master Plan.
As always, I am available to discuss any questions you may have regarding this bill. To facilitate
a response to any questions, please contact District Department of Transportation Director
Sharon Kershbaum at (202) 671-2317.
I urge the Council to take prompt and favorable action on the enclosed legislation.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowser

Conclusion
Funds are sufficient in the fiscal year 2026 budget and proposed fiscal year 2027 through fiscal year
2030 budget and financial plan to implement the bill.
Background
The bill authorizes the Mayor to establish and designate seven new street segments in the Hill East
neighborhood of the District. The bill extends eastward Burke Street, S.E.1 for approximately three
blocks and C Street, S.E.2 for approximately two blocks from their current terminus just west of the
planned 20th Street, S.E. The bill extends 20th Street, S.E.3 approximately one- and one-half blocks
northward from its current terminus just north of Massachusetts Avenue, S.E. The bill establishes
three new north to south roadways. The bill establishes 20th Place, S.E.4 and 21st Place, S.E.5 from the
newly extended C Street, S.E. to the newly extended Burke Street, S.E. The bill also establishes 21st
Street, S.E.6 from approximately Massachusetts Avenue, S.E. to Independence Avenue, S.E. Finally, the

bill establishes C Place, S.E.7 from 20th Street, S.E. just north of Massachusetts Avenue, S.E. eastward
to the starting point of the newly established 21st Street, S.E.
The bill also proposes to close an approximately 1,800 square foot segment of 20th Street, S.E. along
the east side of the existing roadway north of newly established C Place, S.E.
The build-out and designation of these roadways will support the development of properties
disposed for private development in 2022.8
Financial Plan Impact
Funds are sufficient in the fiscal year 2026 budget and proposed fiscal year 2027 through fiscal year
2030 budget and financial plan to implement the bill. The Department of General Services will build
out the new street segments through funding provided by the Deputy Mayor for Planning and
Economic Development (DMPED). DMPED’s Hill East project includes over $62 million over the
proposed fiscal year 2026 to fiscal year 2032 capital improvements plan to support infrastructure
development in the Hill East neighborhood. DGS expects to complete the street segments by the end
of 2028. Once the streets are complete, the District Department of Transportation (DDOT) will
assume control and be responsible for ongoing maintenance. DDOT anticipates that the full roadways
will require maintenance every five years, with the first scheduled maintenance expected around
2034.9
