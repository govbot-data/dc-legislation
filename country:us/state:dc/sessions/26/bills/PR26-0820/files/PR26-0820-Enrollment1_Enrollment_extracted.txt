ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-521
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency, due to congressional review, with respect to the need
to amend the Language Access Act of 2004 to authorize the Mayor to issue rules to
implement the provisions of that act.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Language Access Rulemaking Authority Congressional Review
Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council has passed emergency and temporary legislation to ensure that the
Mayor, through the Office of Human Rights, can promulgate and amend rules to allow for the
fair and effective enforcement of the Language Access Act of 2004, effective June 19, 2004
(D.C. Law 15-167; D.C. Official Code § 2-1931 et seq.). However, the Council anticipates a
potential lapse in the law while the most recent temporary legislation undergoes congressional
review. Further emergency legislation is therefore necessary.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Language Access Rulemaking Authority Congressional Review Emergency Amendment Act of
2026 be adopted after a single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
