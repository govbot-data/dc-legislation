ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-457
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
July 14, 2026
To confirm the appointment of Ahnna K. Smith to the Public Charter School Board.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Public Charter School Board Ahnna K. Smith Confirmation
Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia confirms the appointment of:
Ahnna K. Smith
I Street, NE
Washington, DC 20002
(Ward 6)
as a member of the Public Charter School Board, established by section 2214 of the District of
Columbia School Reform Act of 1995, approved April 26, 1996 (110 Stat. 1321-132; D.C.
Official Code § 38-1802.14), for a term to end February 24, 2030.
Sec. 3. The Council shall transmit a copy of this resolution, upon its adoption, to the
nominee and to the Mayor.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
