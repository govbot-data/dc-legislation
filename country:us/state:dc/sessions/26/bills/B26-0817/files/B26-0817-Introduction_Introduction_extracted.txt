Dear Secretary Howard:
Today, I am introducing the “Electric Way Designation Act of 2026”. Please find enclosed a signed
copy of the legislation.
At the beginning of the 20th century, the primary mode of public transit in the District was the electric
streetcar. While the electric streetcar may be a thing of the past, vestiges of its existence can still be
found in the District through tracks, repurposed car barns, and rights-of-way. One such example in
Ward 6 is the now-residential block containing Kingsman Field and Dog Park in Capitol Hill, which,
according to historical maps, contained a former streetcar barn for the Washington Railway and
Electric Company. These historical maps also show that the public alley system located within the
block was called “Electric Alley”.
This legislation would symbolically designate the public alley system in Square 1030 abutting Lots
812 and 814 as “Electric Way”. Symbolically designating the alley would be historically accurate and
recognize an important part of Capitol Hill’s history. I would note that Advisory Neighborhood
Commission 6A voted unanimously in support of this symbolic designation on May 31, 2026.
Please feel free to reach out to me or my Legislative Director, Antonio Nunes, with any questions or
for additional information.
Sincerely,
Councilmember Charles Allen, Ward 6
Chair, Committee on Transportation & the Environment
