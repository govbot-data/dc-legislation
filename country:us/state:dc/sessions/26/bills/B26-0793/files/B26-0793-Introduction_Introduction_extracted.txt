Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Enclosed for consideration and enactment by the Council of the District of Columbia is an
emergency bill, the “Modification Nos. M0008, M0009 and M0010 to Contract No. CW110323
with Tag-B Parking, LLC Approval and Payment Authorization Emergency Act of 2026,” and
the accompanying emergency declaration resolution, not-to-exceed $18,710,208.

CW110323 with Tag-B Parking, LLC, to provide private student transportation and attendant
services to eligible students, to exercise the remainder of option year three of the contract. In
addition, the legislation will approve payment for the goods and services received and to be
received under the modifications.
My administration is available to discuss any questions you may have regarding the proposed
contract modifications. In order to facilitate a response to any questions you may have, please
have your staff contact Marc Scott, Chief Operating Officer, Office of Contracting and
Procurement, at (202) 724-8759.
I urge the Council to take prompt and favorable action on the enclosed legislation.

(D) The selection process, including the number of offerors, the evaluation criteria, and the
evaluation results, including price, technical or quality, and past performance components:
The Office of Contracting and Procurement (OCP) issued an Invitation for Bid, (solicitation number
Doc661483), on July 21, 2023, seeking multiple contractors to provide student transportation
services. OCP’s intent was to provide an award to all responsible and responsive bidders with
reasonable prices. Of seventeen vendors who were invited, eight were District-certified business
enterprises (CBEs). Of fourteen bids received, eight were from CBEs. Six were nonresponsive (did
not meet the insurance requirements) and two were rejected for unreasonable pricing (over 220% of
the average price).
There were no evaluation criteria, but the contractors were required to provide past performance
documentation demonstrating prior experience in providing door-to-door transportation services to
students in accordance with the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act.
Five contracts were ultimately awarded, four to CBEs and one to the open market.
