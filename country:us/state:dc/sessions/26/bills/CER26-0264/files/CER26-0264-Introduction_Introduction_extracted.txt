8 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
9
10 _________________________
11
12 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
13 _________________________
14
15
16 To recognize and celebrate Craig Cobine for his dedication to the Logan Circle neighborhood.
17
18 WHEREAS, Craig Cobine has been a long-time member of the Logan Circle Community
19 Association, beginning his service to the neighborhood on the house tour committee, which was a
20 successful fundraiser for local school campaigns, neighborhood non-profits, and civic initiatives;
21 WHEREAS, Mr. Cobine was a leading force for Logan Circle neighborhood
22 beautification––leading, launching and supporting initiatives such as the annual garden awards,
23 monthly clean-up efforts, and public art initiatives like “The Dancer” and “Let’s Paint the Streets”;
24 WHEREAS, Mr. Cobine helped preserve Logan Circle’s LGBTQ history through the
25 “LGBTQ Oral Histories” project;
26 WHEREAS, Mr. Cobine helped start an annual canned food drive for neighbors in need in
27 the wake of inflation and rising grocery costs;
28 WHEREAS, Mr. Cobine has helped many residents and families find their forever homes
29 in the District;
30 WHEREAS, Mr. Cobine has served on the Logan Circle Community Association board
31 for over ten years and now serves as the Vice President; and
1

32 WHEREAS, Mr. Cobine continues to create a community where neighbors know, love,
33 and support each other.
34 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
35 resolution may be cited as the “Craig Cobine Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
36 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and celebrates Craig Cobine on
37 his impactful role in shaping and supporting the Logan Circle community and the District.
38 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
