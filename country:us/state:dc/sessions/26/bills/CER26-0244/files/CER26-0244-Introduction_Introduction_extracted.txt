16 To recognize Maurice Cates II for his outstanding service as Legislative Assistant to
17 Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr., and for his leadership on building conditions,
18 housing, government operations, and public service.
19 WHEREAS, Maurice Cates II serves as Legislative Assistant in the office of
20 Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr., contributing legislative research, policy development,
21 oversight preparation, and coordination across significant areas of District government;
22 WHEREAS, Maurice Cates II has led the office’s work addressing building conditions,
23 elevating concerns about the safety, habitability, and maintenance of the places where District
24 residents live;
25 WHEREAS, through his work on building conditions, Maurice Cates II has helped
26 connect residents’ experiences with the responsibilities of housing providers and the agencies
27 charged with inspection and enforcement;
28 WHEREAS, Maurice Cates II has led housing policy assignments that require close
29 attention to resident protections, the operation of District programs, and the practical
30 implementation of Council decisions;
31 WHEREAS, he has handled matters involving the Committee on Executive
32 Administration and Labor, helping the office examine government administration and labor-
33 related policies;

34 WHEREAS, Maurice Cates II has also led work involving public works and operations,
35 bringing attention to the quality, reliability, and accountability of essential government services;
36 WHEREAS, his broad portfolio has required him to engage with agencies, review
37 complex information, and prepare the Councilmember for decisions that affect the everyday
38 experiences of District residents;
39 WHEREAS, Maurice Cates II has supported public safety work, contributing research
40 and coordination to the office’s efforts to promote safer communities and responsive agencies;
41 WHEREAS, his willingness to move among housing, building conditions, labor, public
42 works, and public safety reflects both a versatile approach to legislative service and a
43 commitment to teamwork; and
44 WHEREAS, Maurice Cates II has demonstrated that effective oversight begins by
45 listening closely to residents and following through on the government responsibilities that shape
46 their daily lives.
47 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
48 resolution may be cited as the “Maurice Cates II Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
49 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Maurice Cates II for his
50 leadership on building conditions, housing, executive administration and labor, and public works
51 and operations, and for his valued support of the office’s public safety work.
52 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
53 the District of Columbia Register.
