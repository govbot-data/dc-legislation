ENROLLED ORIGINAL
AN ACT
_________________________
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
_________________________
To amend the Captive Insurance Company Act of 2004 to update requirements for formation,
capital, certificate of authority applications, transacting business, taxes on premiums, and
annual reports, and to establish requirements for inactive captive insurance companies; to
amend the Risk Retention Act of 1993 to provide rulemaking authority; and to amend
section 204 of the Freedom of Information Act of 1976 to make a conforming change.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited as the “Captive Insurance Company Amendment Act of 2026”.

(B) New subsections (d-1) and (d-2) are added to read as follows:
“(d-1)(1) The Commissioner may issue a certificate of authority for one or more
protected cells of a protected cell captive insurer on an expedited basis. To be eligible for an
expedited certificate of authority, the captive manager shall file a complete application for a
protected cell. Upon issuance of the certificate of authority to the protected cell, the captive
manager may, for each subsequent application by a protected cell for a certificate of authority,
file:
“(A) A captive insurance company application;
“(B) A business plan;
“(C) A 5-year financial proformas, including support for premiums and
estimated losses;
“(D) Draft corporate documents, if the application is for an incorporated
protected cell; and
“(E) Any additional information and documents that differ from the
information and documents in the application for the initial protected cell.
“(2) Upon a protected cell’s successful submission of the documents outlined in
paragraph (1) of this subsection, the Commissioner shall issue a certificate of authority to the
captive manager for that protective cell within 3 business days.
“(d-2) Notwithstanding subsection (d-1) of this section, the Commissioner may establish
through rulemaking an alternative process to issue an expedited certificate of authority to a
protected cell of a protected cell captive insurer; provided, that the process is substantially
similar to subsection (d-1) of this section.”.

“(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1) of this section, a pure captive insurer or a
protected cell captive insurer, including its protected cells, except for protected cells of a risk

(6) A new section 12a is added to read as follows:
“Sec. 12a. Inactive captive insurance companies.
“(a) The Commissioner may place a captive insurer, except a risk retention group, on
inactive status, if:
“(1) The captive insurer has ceased transacting the business of insurance;
“(2) There are no remaining liabilities associated with policies written or assumed
by the captive insurer; and
“(3) The captive insurer is compliant with all applicable District laws and
regulations.
“(b) An inactive captive insurer shall maintain unimpaired capital and surplus in a
minimum amount of $5,000.
“(c) Notwithstanding any other provision of this act, an inactive captive insurer shall not
be subject to, or be liable for, the payment of any premium taxes or fees for any full year the
captive insurance company is inactive.
“(d) The Commissioner may, by rulemaking, exempt an inactive captive insurer from any
filing or reporting requirements required by this act.
“(e) An inactive captive insurer shall obtain written approval from the Commissioner
prior to resuming the business of insurance to issue any insurance policies.”.

Sec. 3. The Risk Retention Act of 1993, effective October 21, 1993 (D.C. Law 10-46;
D.C. Official Code § 31-4101 et seq.), is amended by adding a new section 14 to read as follows:
“Sec. 14. Rulemaking authority.
“The Mayor, pursuant to Title I of the District of Columbia Administrative Procedure Act,
approved October 21, 1968 (82 Stat. 1204; D.C. Official Code § 2-501 et seq.), may issue rules
relating to captive insurers licensed as risk retention groups as necessary to implement the
provisions of this act.”.
