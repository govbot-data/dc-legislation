Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Enclosed for consideration and adoption by the Council of the District of Columbia are the “Cedar
Hill Regional Medical Center Hospital Operations Agreement Second and Third Amendments
Approval Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026” and the “Cedar Hill Regional Medical
Center Hospital Operations Agreement Second and Third Amendments Approval Emergency Act
of 2026.”
The legislation would approve two amendments to the Hospital Operations Agreement
(“Agreement”) between the Government of the District of Columbia and UHS East End Sub, LLC,
the operating entity for Cedar Hill Regional Medical Center. The amendments revise the Hospital
Fund provisions of the Agreement to establish the process for making a subsidy payment based on
the hospital’s operating-period deficit during the first year of operations and provide a support
payment for maternal and fetal care provided at the hospital to sustain labor and delivery, maternal,
fetal, and newborn care capacity.
Cedar Hill’s maternal and fetal service lines have experienced significant startup losses as the
hospital works to establish and stabilize these services. In 2025, approximately $8.6 million in
losses were reported, and in 2026 they are projected to reach $11.5 million. The losses reflect low
utilization and the substantial fixed costs associated with maintenance of the 24-hour unit. Cedar
Hill also experienced significant financial losses during its initial operating period following the
hospital’s opening in 2025; however, the Hospital Fund subsidy payment for the partial 2025
period was not previously clearly accounted for under the original agreement. The proposed
amendments authorize up to $5 million from the existing Hospital Fund, subject to the deficit-
review and fund-availability requirements of the Agreement, and a $10 million calendar year 2026
maternal and fetal care support payment.
The proposed support payments are intended to preserve critical access requirements and general
operations while Cedar Hill builds greater financial stability. I urge the Council to take prompt

and favorable action on the enclosed legislation.
If you have any questions regarding this legislation, please contact Wayne Turnage, Deputy Mayor
for Health and Human Services.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowser

22 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this

26 Sec. 2. (a) Cedar Hill Regional Medical Center (the "Hospital") opened in April 2025 as
27 the District's first new full-service hospital in more than 25 years. It provides inpatient,
28 emergency, specialty, and maternal and fetal health services to District residents, including
29 residents of Wards 7 and 8.
30 (b) As part of the development of the Hospital, the District and Universal Health
31 Services, Inc., through a subsidiary, UHS East End Sub, LLC ("UHS"), entered into an
32 agreement (the "Hospital Operations Agreement" or "Agreement") whereby UHS is responsible
33 for operating the Hospital and providing certain services at the Hospital and the District agreed
34 to provide certain financial support to the Hospital in certain circumstances.
1

35 (c) The Hospital Operations Agreement requires the District to maintain a hospital fund
36 (“Hospital Fund”) funded through annual deposits of $5 million during the first five fiscal years
37 specified in the Agreement; under the Agreement, the District agreed to make support payments
38 to UHS if the Hospital operated at a deficit, in order to help ensure the ongoing operation of
39 comprehensive, high-quality services at the Hospital. The Hospital Operations Agreement,
40 however, does not include a clear mechanism for providing the support payment in the
41 Hospital’s first fiscal year of operation, if that first fiscal year of operation is only a portion of a
42 year. To address this issue, the District and UHS agreed to a second amendment (“Second
43 Amendment”) to the Hospital Operations Agreement to establish a process for the District to
44 make a support payment based on the results of the Hospital’s initial operating period ending
45 December 31, 2025, subject to the amount available in the Hospital Fund and the District’s
46 review of supporting financial information. This subsidy payment will help ensure the ongoing
47 operation of the hospital with a comprehensive, high-quality level of services.
48 (d) The Second Amendment also amends the Hospital Operations Agreement to provide
49 up to $10 million in support of Cedar Hill’s maternal and fetal service lines and established a
50 conditional support framework for later years. A third amendment (“Third Amendment”) to the
51 Agreement limits the maternal and fetal services lines support arrangement to calendar year
52 2026. The Second Amendment required payment of the support payment by September 30,
53 2026, and funds have been accrued to make this payment, subject to the Council’s approval of
54 the Second Amendment.
55 (e) Maternal and fetal services require continuous staffing and specialized clinical
56 capacity. UHS has reported maternal and fetal service line incurred an approximately $8.6
57 million loss during its partial 2025 operating year and is projected to incur an approximately
2

58 $11.5 million loss in calendar year 2026. The payment will support the ongoing operation of the
59 hospital with comprehensive maternal and fetal lines of services.
60 (e) The Second and Third Amendments are conditioned on receiving the Council’s
61 approval of the amendments by December 31, 2026.
62 (f) Prompt Council action is necessary to approve both the Second and Third
63 Amendments and to satisfy the Second and Third Amendments’ December 31, 2026, approval
64 deadline so that the revised support payments framework may timely take effect.
65 (g) Emergency legislation is necessary because the ordinary legislative process would not
66 provide sufficient time to approve the Second and Third Amendments before December 31,
67 2026. Without timely approval and payment within the time periods contemplated by the
68 amendments, delay could interfere with the ability to maintain continuity of critical services at
69 the Hospital.
70 Sec. 3. The Council determines that the circumstances enumerated in section 2 constitute
71 emergency circumstances making it necessary that the Cedar Hill Regional Medical Center
72 Hospital Operations Agreement Second and Third Amendments Emergency Approval Act of
73 2026 be adopted after a single reading.
74 Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
