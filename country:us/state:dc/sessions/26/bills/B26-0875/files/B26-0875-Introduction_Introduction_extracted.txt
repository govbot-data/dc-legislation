3 A BILL
4 _____
5 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
6 _____
7 To amend the District of Columbia Traffic Adjudication Act of 1978 to remove the penalty equal
8 to the amount of a civil fine for failure to answer a notice of infraction within 30 days when
9 the infraction has been detected by an automated traffic enforcement system or involves a
10 parking, standing, stopping or pedestrian offense.
11
12 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
13 act may be cited as the “Parking and Traffic Ticket Penalty Reduction Amendment Act of 2026”.
14 Sec. 2. The District of Columbia Traffic Adjudication Act of 1978, effective September
15 12, 1978 (D.C. Law 2-104; D.C. Official Code § 50-2301.01 et seq.), is amended as follows:
16 (a) Section 105(a)(2)(A) (D.C. Official Code § 50-2301.05(a)(2)(A)) is amended by
17 striking the phrase “and section 305(d)(1)” and inserting the phrase “and section 305(d)” in its
18 place.
19 (b) Section 205(d) (D.C. Official Code § 50-2302.05(d)) is amended to read as follows:
20 “(d) Except for notices of infraction that were detected by an automated traffic
21 enforcement system pursuant to section 901 of the Fiscal Year 1997 Budget Support Act of
22 1996, effective April 9, 1997 (D.C. Law 11-198; D.C. Official Code § 50-2209.01), if a person
23 fails to answer a notice of infraction within 30 calendar days of the date the notice was issued, or
1

24 within a greater period of time as prescribed by the Director by regulation, a penalty equal to the
25 amount of the fine shall be added pursuant to section 105(a).”.
26 (c) Section 304(c) (D.C. Official Code § 50-2303.04(c)) is amended by striking the
27 phrase “The lessor’s failure to answer the notice of infraction within 30 days after mailing shall
28 result in the imposition of monetary penalties established by section 205, in addition to the
29 potential civil fine for the infraction.”.
30 (d) Section 305(d) (D.C. Official Code § 50-2303.05(d)) is amended to read as follows:
31 “(d) A person to whom a notice of infraction has been issued shall answer within 60
32 calendar days of the date the notice was issued. If a person fails to answer within 60 days, or
33 within a greater period of time as prescribed by the Director by regulation, the commission of the
34 infraction shall be deemed admitted and all penalties, fines, and any vehicle conveyance fees
35 shall be assessed. Not more than 50 days after the notice is issued, the Director shall send, by
36 regular mail, to the address in the Department of Motor Vehicles’ records, if such address was
37 supplied to the Department of Motor Vehicles, notice of the outstanding notice of infraction and
38 of the impending deemed admission. This subsection shall not apply to any participant in the
39 fleet adjudication program.”
40 Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.
41 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal
42 impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
43 approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
44 Sec. 4. Effective date.

47 as provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December
