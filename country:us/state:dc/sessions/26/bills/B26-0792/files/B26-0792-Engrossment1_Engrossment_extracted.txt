10 To require, on a temporary basis, funding extension to all Fiscal Year 2026 grantees awarded to
11 implement the Substance Use and Behavioral Health Services Targeted Outreach Grant
12 Act of 2025 pilot program in order to continue services without disruption; to authorize
13 DBH to expend funds from the Opioid Abatement Fund for the existing Targeted
14 Outreach pilot program.

16 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
17 act may be cited as the “Extension of Substance Use and Targeted Outreach Grants Temporary
18 Amendment Act of 2026”.

27 the grantees operated in Fiscal Year 2026.

51 Sec. 3. Section 5012 of the Opioid Abatement Fund Establishment Act of 2022 (D.C.
52 Law 24-315; D.C. Official Code § 7–3221) is amended by inserting a new sub-section (b-7) to
53 read as follows:
54 “(b-7) Before Fiscal Year 2026 grant agreements expire, the Department of Behavioral
55 Health (DBH) shall fully extend all Fiscal Year 2026 grantee organizations’ awards to continue
56 implementation of the Substance Use and Behavioral Health Services Targeted Outreach Grant
57 Act of 2025 pilot program, in order to avoid disruption of services. This requirement applies
58 regardless of whether new Fiscal Year 2027 grant awards for additional locations are made by
59 the agency under D.C. Law 26-0142, the Place-Based Substance Use Disorder Outreach
60 Amendment Act of 2026, A26-0320, effective from Jul 18, 2026).
