D.C. ACT 26-478
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICTOF COLUMBIA
NOVEMBER 9, 2026
To amend, on an emergency basis,the Healthy Schools Actof2010 to modify the requirements
for District school breakfast service.
BEIT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICTOF COLUMBIA, Thatthis
act may be cited as the “Alternative Breakfast Service Model Emergency AmendmentAct of
2026”.
Sec. 2. The Healthy SchoolsActof2010, effective July 27, 2010 (D.C. Law 18-209;
D.C. Official Code § 38-821.01 etseg.), is amended as follows:
(a) Section 101(1B) (D.C. Official Code § 38-821.01(1B)) is amended to read as follows:
(1B) “Breakfast in the classroom”meansan alternative breakfast servingmodel
where students are served breakfast in the classroom.”.
(b) Section 203(a) (D.C. Official Code § 38-822.03(a)) is amended as follows:
(1) Paragraph (2) is amended as follows:
(A) Subparagraph (A) is amended by striking the phrase “breakfast in the
classroom” and insertingthe phrase “all students grab-and-go breakfast, breakfastin the
classroom, ora combination ofbothbreakfastmodels” in its place.
(B) Subparagraph (C) is amended by striking thephrase “rate, without
breakfast-in-the-classroom,” and insertingthe word “rate” in its place.
(2) Paragraph (3) is amended as follows:
(A) Subparagraph (A) is amended by striking the phrase “breakfast inthe
classroom” and insertingthe phrase “grab-and-go breakfast, breakfast inthe classroom, ora
combination ofboth breakfast models” in its place.
(B) Subparagraph (B) is amended by striking the phrase “breakfast in the
classroom” and insertingthe phrase “grab-and-go breakfast, breakfast in the classroom, ora
combination ofboth breakfast models” in its place.
Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.

ENROLLED ORIGINAL

Sec. 4. Effective date.
This act shall takeeffect following approval by the Mayor (or in the eventofveto by the
Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), and shall remain in effect forno longer than
90 days, as provided for emergency acts ofthe Couneil ofthe DistrictofColumbia in section
412(a)ofthe District ofColumbia Home RuleAct, approved December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 788;
D.C. Official Code § 1-204.12(a)).
