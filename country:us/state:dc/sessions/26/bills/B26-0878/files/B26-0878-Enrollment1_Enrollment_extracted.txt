ENROLLED ORIGINAL
AN ACT
______
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
____________
To approve, on an emergency basis, amendments to the Hospital Operations Agreement between
the District of Columbia and UHS East End Sub, LLC, governing Cedar Hill Regional
Medical Center.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited as the “Cedar Hill Regional Medical Center Hospital Operations Agreement
Second and Third Amendments Emergency Approval Act of 2026”.
Sec. 2. Pursuant to section 451 of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved
December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 803; D.C. Official Code § 1-204.51), and notwithstanding the
requirements of the Procurement Practices Reform Act of 2010, effective April 8, 2011 (D.C.
Law 18-371; D.C. Official Code § 2-351.01 et seq.), the Public-Private Partnership Act of 2014,
effective March 11, 2015 (D.C. Law 20-228; D.C. Official Code § 2-271.01 et seq.), or any other
law, the Council approves the Second and Third Amendments to Cedar Hill Regional Medical
Center Hospital Operations Agreement between the District of Columbia and UHS East End
Sub, LLC, transmitted by the Mayor to the Council on November 4, 2026, as a contract
involving expenditures in excess of $1 million during a 12-month period, in the not-to-exceed
amount of $10 million.
Sec. 3. For calendar years after 2026, the Mayor shall not make subsidy or deficit
payments to UHS East End Sub, LLC for maternal and fetal service lines at Cedar Hill Regional
Medical Center (“East End Hospital”), unless:
(1) UHS East End Sub, LLC includes the percentage of physicians who are
practicing as locum tenens in maternal and fetal services in the East End Hospital’s Maternal and
Fetal Care Service Line Report required by subsection (d)(1) of the amended Hospital
Operations Agreement, as approved by section 2; and
(2) The Noncompliance Reduction Ratio defined in subsection (e)(3) of the
amended Hospital Operations Agreement, as approved by section 2, includes a metric that would
decrease the subsidy payment to UHS East End Sub, LLC if the East End Hospital is not meeting
established goals to decrease the percentage of physicians who are practicing as locum tenens in
maternal and fetal services.

Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Chief Financial Officer as the fiscal
impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
Sec. 5. Effective date.
This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the Mayor,
action by the Council to override the veto), and shall remain in effect for no longer than 90 days,
as provided for emergency acts of the Council of the District of Columbia in section 412(a) of
the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 788; D.C.
Official Code § 1-204.12(a)).
