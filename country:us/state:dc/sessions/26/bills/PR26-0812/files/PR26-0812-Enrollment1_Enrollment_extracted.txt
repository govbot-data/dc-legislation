ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-525
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to amend the Healthy Schools
Act of 2010 to modify the requirements for District school breakfast service.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Alternative Breakfast Service Model Emergency Declaration
Resolution of 2026.”
Sec. 2. (a) The Healthy Students Amendment Act of 2018, effective March 13, 2019
(D.C. Law 22-240; 66 DCR 912), was passed to, among other things, increase participation in
school breakfast. The law requires elementary schools where 40% or more of the students at a
school qualify for free or reduced-price meals (“Title I schools”) to take specific actions if
participation in school breakfast is below 75%. In the first year where participation lags, the
school can receive a waiver from the Office of the State Superintendent of Education to
implement alternative breakfast services models to improve participation; however, if a school’s
participation rate does not demonstrate incremental progress, the law requires breakfast to be
served in the classroom.
(b) After that legislation took effect, the District of Columbia Public Schools (“DCPS”)
and the Washington Teachers’ Union negotiated a new collective bargaining agreement (“CBA”)
that covers the period from October 1, 2023, to September 30, 2028. Section 23.8 of the CBA
reserves the 30-minute block between 8:10 AM and 8:40 AM for teacher-initiated or principal-
led planning. Because classroom teachers cannot begin supervising students until 8:40 AM, an
elementary school that is required by law to serve breakfast in the classroom cannot do so until
8:40 AM.
(c) As a result of the classroom breakfast requirement and the requirements of the CBA,
some District elementary schools now face logistical challenges that are cutting into instructional
time. One school community reports that the morning routine has been profoundly disrupted.
Last year, all on-time students proceeded to the school cafeteria for breakfast and late-arriving
students would receive grab-and-go breakfast near the front door. This year, all students from
kindergarten through fifth grade must gather in the auditorium until 8:40 AM and then grab
breakfast to go on their way to their classrooms where they spend the first minutes of their day
eating rather than beginning instruction.

(d) Seventy percent of DCPS’s 61 Title I elementary schools and education campuses are
impacted. For the 2026-2027 school year, 15 DCPS elementary schools and education campuses
have been required to serve breakfast in the classroom for all grades. A further 28 DCPS
elementary schools and education campuses have received a waiver this year but may be subject
to the requirement next year.
(e) The underlying emergency legislation amends current law to provide Title I
elementary schools with breakfast participation rates under 75% with more flexible options to
increase participation. The new enhanced breakfast service options will include breakfast in the
classroom, grab and go breakfast, or a combination of both breakfast models. By providing
impacted schools with this flexibility, the District can continue improving participation in school
breakfast without creating logistical challenges that reduce instructional time at Title I public
elementary schools and education campuses.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Alternative Breakfast Service Model Emergency Amendment Act of 2026 be adopted after a
single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
