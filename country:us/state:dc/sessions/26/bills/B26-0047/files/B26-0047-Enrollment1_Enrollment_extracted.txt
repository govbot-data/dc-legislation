ENROLLED ORIGINAL
AN ACT
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
To amend the District of Columbia Funeral Services Regulatory Act of 1984 to modify and add
definitions related to the practice of funeral directing and embalming, to change the
requirements for a funeral director’s license, to create an embalmer’s license, to modify
the requirements for a funeral directing or embalming apprenticeship, to revise
supervision requirements for embalming and funeral director apprentices, and to
incorporate the practice of embalming into the provisions governing fraud and grounds
for denial, suspension, or revocation of a license; and to amend section 47-2853.06 of the
District of Columbia Official Code to update the composition of the Board of Funeral
Directors.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited as the “Funeral Services Modernization Amendment Act of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The District of Columbia Funeral Services Regulatory Act of 1984, effective
May 22, 1984 (D.C. Law 5-84; D.C. Official Code § 3-401 et seq.), is amended as follows:
(a) Section 2 (D.C. Official Code § 3-401) is amended by striking the phrase “apprentice
funeral directors,” and inserting the phrase “embalmers, apprenticeships,” in its place.
(b) Section 3 (D.C. Official Code § 3-402) is amended as follows:
(1) Paragraph (2) is repealed.
(2) Paragraph (3) is amended by striking the phrase “for the District of Columbia”
and inserting the phrase “established by D.C. Official Code § 47-2853.06(f)” in its place.
(3) A new paragraph (7A) is added to read as follows:
“(7A) “Embalmer” means a person licensed in the District to perform the practice
of embalming.”.
(4) Paragraph (9) is amended to read as follows:
“(9) “Funeral director” means a person licensed by the District to perform the
practice of funeral directing.”.
(5) Paragraph (14) is amended to read as follows:

(6) A new paragraph (17A) is added to read as follows:
“(17A) “Practice of embalming” means engaging in the disposal of human
remains by chemically treating the human remains by arterial injection and cavity treatment or,
when necessary, hypodermic tissue injection to reduce the presence and growth of
microorganisms to temporarily delay organic decomposition.”.
(7) Paragraph (18) is amended to read as follows:
“(18) “Practice of funeral directing” means the for-profit profession of directing
or supervising funerals, preparing human dead for burial by means other than through the
practice of embalming, including by burial, cremation, and alkaline hydrolysis, or making
arrangements for funeral services or the financing of funeral services.”.

(f) Section 8 (D.C. Official Code § 3-407) is amended to read as follows:

“Sec. 8. Apprenticeships.
“(a)(1) A person seeking to qualify:
“(A) As a funeral director, shall serve as an apprentice under the direct
supervision of a funeral director licensed in the District; or
“(B) As an embalmer, shall serve as an apprentice under the direct
supervision of an embalmer licensed in the District.
“(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1) of this subsection, an apprentice who is
required to serve a 2-year minimum apprenticeship may serve up to one year of the
apprenticeship outside of the District, if the period of the apprenticeship served outside of the
District is served under the supervision of an individual who has passed the National Board
Exam administered by the International Conference of Funeral Service Examining Boards.
“(b) To qualify for an apprenticeship, the applicant shall furnish proof that he or she:
“(1) Is at least 18 years of age; and
“(2) Holds a high school diploma or its equivalent, or higher.
“(c) An apprenticeship for funeral directing or embalming shall consist of at least 2,000
hours of training to be completed in 2 years; except, that an apprenticeship may be extended, for
good cause, to no more than 4 years .
“(d) A funeral directing apprentice shall receive training in all aspects of the practice of
funeral directing, including assisting with at least 25 funerals and 25 arrangement conferences,
visitations, and the financing of funeral services.
“(e) An embalming apprentice shall receive training in all aspects of the practice of
embalming, including assisting in at least 25 embalmings, treatment, restorative art, safety and
sanitation, and organ, tissue, or anatomical donations.
“(f)(1) The apprentice and the licensee supervising the apprentice shall submit a written
report to the Board at the end of every 1,000 hours of training that specifies the period of time in
which the 1,000 hours has been completed and verifies that the apprentice has actually served in
the required capacity during the preceding period. The report shall be submitted no more than 30
days following the completion of the 1,000 hours.
“(2) An apprentice shall not receive credit for any new training hours until the
previous 1,000-hour report has been submitted and approved.
“(g) An apprentice whose employment under the supervision of a funeral director or
embalmer is terminated shall, within 30 days of being employed to work under the supervision of
another funeral director or embalmer, notify the Mayor of the change of employment and the
name, address, and license number of the funeral director or embalmer under whose supervision
the apprentice is continuing his or her apprenticeship.
“(h) A funeral director or embalmer shall, upon employing an apprentice or terminating
the employment of an apprentice, notify the Mayor in writing. The notification shall contain the
name and address of the apprentice, and the date on which the apprentice was employed or
terminated.

“Sec. 22b. Services requiring immediate supervision.
“(a) A funeral directing apprentice shall not perform the following services unless he or
she is under the immediate supervision of a licensed funeral director:
“(1) The handling or preparing human remains for identification, viewing,
transportation, funeral services, or disposition;
“(2) Conducting or directing a funeral; or
“(3) Advising consumers making arrangements for the care and disposition of
human remains, including arrangements made prior to the death of a person.
“(b) An embalming apprentice shall not perform the following services unless he or she is
under the immediate supervision of a licensed embalmer:
“(1) Embalming human remains, including the preservation, disinfection,
sanitization, or treatment of human remains for the purpose of delaying decomposition;
“(2) Performing any procedure involving arterial injection, cavity treatment,
tissue preservation, restorative treatment, or other preparation of human remains that constitutes
embalming; or
“(3) The removal or transport of human remains when such removal or transport
is performed for the purpose of embalming or in connection with embalming services.”.

one shall be a consumer member and inserting the phrase “7 members of whom 4 shall be
funeral directors licensed in the District, 2 shall be consumer members, and one shall be a
member with expertise in cremation or alkaline hydrolysis” in its place.
