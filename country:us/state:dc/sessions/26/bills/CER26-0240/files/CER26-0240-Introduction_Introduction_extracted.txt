12 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA

15
16 To recognize Whitney Sweetwine for her outstanding service as Communications Director to
17 Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr., and for her commitment to informing, engaging,
18 and uplifting residents of the District of Columbia.
19 WHEREAS, Whitney Sweetwine serves as Communications Director to Councilmember
20 Robert C. White, Jr., helping the office communicate its legislative priorities, oversight work,
21 and public-service commitments to residents and the broader community;
22 WHEREAS, Whitney Sweetwine has helped make complex questions of housing, public
23 safety, and District governance understandable and accessible, ensuring that policy discussions
24 remain connected to the people whose lives those decisions affect;
25 WHEREAS, through public statements, press engagement, community communications,
26 and the coordination of public events, Whitney Sweetwine has strengthened the office’s ability to
27 keep residents informed and make their concerns visible;
28 WHEREAS, she recognizes that effective government communication is more than
29 distributing information: it requires listening carefully, communicating with accuracy, and telling
30 the stories of residents with respect and integrity;
31 WHEREAS, Whitney Sweetwine has helped the office respond to fast-moving
32 developments with clarity and discipline while maintaining a consistent focus on transparency
33 and public accountability;

34 WHEREAS, Whitney Sweetwine has worked to present the Councilmember’s positions
35 accurately amid difficult conversations and fast-moving public developments, helping
36 constituents understand both the challenges facing the District and proposed paths forward;
37 WHEREAS, she has played a vital coordinating role when the office has engaged with
38 journalists and residents on questions of government accountability, housing policy, and the
39 treatment of vulnerable communities;
40 WHEREAS, Whitney Sweetwine’s work reflects an understanding that good
41 communication makes room for both the technical details of legislation and the lived experiences
42 that give those details their urgency;
43 WHEREAS, her creativity, sound judgment, and collaborative approach have supported
44 colleagues across legislative, committee, and constituent-services teams as they work to reach
45 the communities they serve; and
46 WHEREAS, Whitney Sweetwine’s dedication demonstrates the importance of a
47 thoughtful public voice in building trust between District residents and the institutions that
48 represent them.
49 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
50 resolution may be cited as the “Whitney Sweetwine Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
51 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Whitney Sweetwine for her
52 dedicated service, communications leadership, and contributions to transparent, accessible, and
53 effective District government.
54 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
55 the District of Columbia Register.
