15 To amend the Advisory Neighborhood Commissions Act of 1975 to authorize Advisory
16 Neighborhood Commissions to expend funds through certain third-party digital payment
17 platforms.
18
19 BE IT ENACTED by the Council of the District of Columbia, that this act may be cited as the
20 “Advisory Neighborhood Commissions Payment Modernization Act of 2026.”
21 Sec. 2. Section 16 of the Advisory Neighborhood Commissions Act of 1975, effective
22 March 26, 1976 (D.C. Law 1-58; D.C. Official Code § 1-309.13), is amended as follows:
23 Section 16 of the Advisory Neighborhood Commissions Act of 1975, effective March 26,
24 1976 (D.C. Law 1-58; D.C. Official Code § 1-309.13), is amended as follows:
25 (a) Subsection (b-2) is amended as follows:
26 (1) Paragraph (1) is amended by striking the phrase "payments." and adding the
27 phrase "payments, and by transfers made through a third-party digital payment platform."
28 (2) Paragraph (2) is amended to read as follows
29 “(2) Each Commission expending funds by EFT, ACH payments, or a third-party
30 digital payment platform shall do so pursuant to a procedure determined by the OANC that limits
31 monthly EFT, ACH payments, or a third-party digital payment platform expenditures relative to
32 the Commission's quarterly allotment.”

33 (3) Paragraph (3) is amended by striking the phrase "EFT or ACH payments" and
34 adding the phrase "EFT or ACH payments, or transaction identifiers assigned by a third-party
35 digital payment platform”
36 (b) Subsection (f) is amended as follows:
37 (1) Paragraph (2A)(A) is amended by striking the phrase "debit card or ACH" and
38 adding the phrase "debit card, ACH, or a third-party digital payment platform".
39 (c) A new subsection (t-1) is added to read as follows:
40 "(t-1)(1) For the purposes of this section, the term "third-party digital payment
41 platform" means a payment service, accessible by website or mobile application, that:
42 (A) Allows electronic transfers of Commission funds from a depository
43 account or debit card to a vendor, grant recipient, or Commissioner;
44 (B) Provides the Commission with itemized transaction records that
45 include, at a minimum, the date, amount, payee, and an associated transaction identifier for each
46 transfer; and
47 (C) Allows the Commission to associate each transfer with a description
48 or memo field stating the purpose of the expenditure.
49 Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.
50 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal
51 impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
52 approved October 16, 2006 (D.C. Law 16-191; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
53 Sec. 4. Effective date.
54 This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
55 Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), a 30-day period of Congressional review as

56 provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December
57 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)), and publication in the District of
58 Columbia Register.
