15 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
16
17 _________________________
18
19 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
20
21 _________________________
22
23
24 To recognize, honor, and celebrate the life of Antonio “Tony” Campanaro, the owner of Tony’s
25 Barber Shop.
26
27 WHEREAS, Antonio “Tony” Campanaro was a beloved local barber for 55 years in the
28 Tenleytown and Van Ness neighborhoods of the District of Columbia;
29 WHEREAS, Mr. Campanaro was born on January 20th, 1946, in Castelluccio
30 Valmaggiore, Foggia, Italy and eventually settled in the United States in 1967;
31 WHEREAS, Mr. Campanaro was trained as a barber, and began working at Camillo
32 Barber Shop in Tenleytown upon arriving in the District;
33 WHEREAS, Mr. Campanaro opened Tony’s Barber Shop in Van Ness in 1988;
34 WHEREAS, Mr. Campanaro served generations of families, becoming a pillar of the
35 community and was known for his lengthy and spirited discussions with clients and profound joy
36 in his work;
1

37 WHEREAS, Mr. Campanaro also enjoyed woodworking, winemaking, and gardening;
38 and
39 WHEREAS, Teresa Campanaro, Tony’s wife of 53 years and business partner, worked
40 alongside him as a seamstress in the adjoining room where she continues to work.
41 WHEREAS, Tony was first involved with Holy Rosary Church when he moved to
42 Washington DC’s historic Italian American neighborhood before becoming long-term
43 parishioners of Blessed Sacrament Parish;
44 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
45 resolution may be cited as the “Tony Campanaro Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
46 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes, remembers, and celebrates
47 the life of Tony Campanaro and his importance to the Tenleytown and Van Ness communities.
48 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
49 the District of Columbia Register.
