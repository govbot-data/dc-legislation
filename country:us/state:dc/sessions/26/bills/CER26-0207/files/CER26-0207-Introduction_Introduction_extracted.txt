12 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
13 __________
14
15 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
16 __________
17
18 To honor and recognize Dr. Tiffany L. Lancaster for her many contributions to the
19 District of Columbia and celebrate her continued commitment to public service, culture, and the
20 advancement of others.
21
22 WHEREAS, Dr. Tiffany L. Lancaster is a proud and involved community resident and an
23 artist, poet, and advocate whose life reflects a deep commitment to service, creativity, and the
24 residents of our city;
25
26 WHEREAS, Dr. Tiffancy L. Lancaster has demonstrated her commitment to the District
27 through public service, including her service as an Advisory Neighborhood Commissioner in
28 Ward Eight and her appointment by Mayor Muriel Bowser to serve as a Commissioner with the
29 D.C. Mayor's Office on African Affairs, where she has contributed to efforts involving economic
30 development and the advancement of African and African Diaspora communities;
31
32 WHEREAS, Dr. Lancaster has consistently sought to build bridges among people and
33 communities, using her voice and her talents to encourage greater understanding, opportunity,
34 cultural appreciation, and unity;
35
36 WHEREAS, Dr. Lancaster is a poet and artist whose creative work has provided a
37 powerful means of expressing hope and love while inspiring others to see the potential in every
38 person;
39
40 WHEREAS, her humanitarian and community efforts reflect a willingness to stand with
41 others, advocate for those in need, and contribute her time and talents to making the District and
42 the wider community a better place;
43
44 WHEREAS, Dr. Tiffany L. Lancaster has won many awards and has been recognized by
45 many for her contributions including a Lifetime Achievement Award from President Joseph
46 Biden.

47
48 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, that the
49 Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Dr. Tiffany L. Lancaster for her contributions to
50 our community, her humanitarian service, and advocacy for the great diversity in the District.
51
52 BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF
53 COLUMBIA, that the Council extends its appreciation to Dr. Tiffany L. Lancaster for the many
54 ways she has given of herself to help others and wishes her continued success in all her
55 endeavors.
56
57 Sec. 2. The resolution may be cited as the “Dr. Tiffany L. Lancaster Recognition
58 Resolution of 2026.”
59
60 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
61 the District of Columbia Register.
