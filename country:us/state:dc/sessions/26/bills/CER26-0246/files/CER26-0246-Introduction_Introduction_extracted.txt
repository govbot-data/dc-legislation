15 To recognize Christian Wiskur for his outstanding service as Deputy Legislative and Committee
16 Director to Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr., and for his leadership in housing
17 finance, economic development, tax policy, and matters concerning the RFK campus.
18 WHEREAS, Christian Wiskur serves as Deputy Legislative and Committee Director to
19 Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr., helping shape legislative strategy and oversee policy work
20 on matters with substantial consequences for the District;
21 WHEREAS, Christian Wiskur has led the office’s work involving the District of
22 Columbia Housing Finance Agency, examining the financing tools and public responsibilities
23 that support affordable housing in the District;
24 WHEREAS, he has led matters involving the Department of Housing and Community
25 Development, bringing careful analysis to housing programs, agency accountability, and
26 investments in District neighborhoods;
27 WHEREAS, Christian Wiskur has directed the office’s economic development policy
28 work, evaluating proposals intended to support growth while considering their implications for
29 residents, businesses, and public resources;

30 WHEREAS, he has taken responsibility for tax policy matters, helping colleagues
31 understand the fiscal, legal, and economic tradeoffs that accompany revenue proposals and tax
32 incentives;
33 WHEREAS, Christian Wiskur has also led the office’s work on matters involving the
34 RFK campus, a consequential subject for the District’s land use, development, and long-term
35 public interests;
36 WHEREAS, the breadth of these portfolios has demanded an ability to assess complex
37 financing arrangements, negotiate difficult policy questions, and keep the public interest at the
38 center of the discussion;
39 WHEREAS, as Deputy Legislative and Committee Director, Christian Wiskur has
40 supported the coordination of legislative assignments and the preparation of the office for
41 hearings, budget decisions, and Council deliberations;
42 WHEREAS, his policy work has required sustained collaboration with colleagues,
43 stakeholders, and District agencies, and a willingness to engage seriously with competing
44 perspectives;
45 WHEREAS, Christian Wiskur cares deeply about his neighbors and community, bringing
46 to his work not only policy expertise but a genuine concern for the people and neighborhoods
47 affected by the decisions made at the Council;
48 WHEREAS, his basketball skills and his signature dad jokes have brought good-natured
49 fun, laughter, and welcome levity to the office, reminding colleagues that camaraderie can make
50 even the most demanding days a little brighter; and
51 WHEREAS, Christian Wiskur’s attention to financial detail, legislative process, and
52 effective governance has made him a valued member of the Councilmember’s team and a
53 dedicated advocate for the residents of the District.
54 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
55 resolution may be cited as the “Christian Wiskur Recognition Resolution of 2026”.

56 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Christian Wiskur for his
57 leadership in housing finance, community development, economic development, tax policy, and
58 RFK campus matters, as well as his dedicated service as Deputy Legislative Director.
59 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
60 the District of Columbia Register.
