ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-508
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To amend the Council Period 26 Appointment of Chairperson Pro Tempore, Committee
Chairpersons, and Committee Membership Resolution of 2025 to make membership
changes to Council committees.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Council Period 26 Committee Membership Amendment
Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. Section 3 of the Council Period 26 Appointment of Chairperson Pro Tempore,
Committee Chairpersons, and Committee Membership Resolution of 2025, effective January 2,
2025 (Res. 26-2; 72 DCR 376), is amended as follows:
(a) Paragraph (2) is amended by striking the phrase “Doni Crawford, Matthew Frumin,
and Janeese Lewis George” and inserting the phrase “Matthew Frumin, Janeese Lewis George,
and Elissa Silverman” in its place.
(b) Paragraph (7) is amended by striking the phrase “Charles Allen, Anita Bonds, Doni
Crawford, and Wendell Felder” and inserting the phrase “Charles Allen, Anita Bonds, Wendell
Felder, and Elissa Silverman” in its place.
(c) Paragraph (8) is amended by striking the phrase “Wendell Felder, Janeese Lewis
George, and Robert White” and inserting the phrase “Wendell Felder, Janeese Lewis George,
Elissa Silverman, and Robert White” in its place.
(d) Paragraph (9) is amended by striking the phrase “Doni Crawford, Christina
Henderson, Janeese Lewis George, and Zachary Parker” and inserting the phrase “Christina
Henderson, Janeese Lewis George, Zachary Parker, and Elissa Silverman” in its place.
(e) Paragraph (10) is amended by striking the phrase “Doni Crawford, Brooke Pinto, and
Robert White” and inserting the phrase “Brooke Pinto, Elissa Silverman, and Robert White” in
its place.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
