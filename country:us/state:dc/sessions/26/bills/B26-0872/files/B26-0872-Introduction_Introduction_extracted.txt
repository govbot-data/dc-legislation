Dear Secretary Smith,
I am introducing the “Closing of Public Streets and Dedication of Land for Street and Alley Purposes in
and abutting Squares 3953, 3954, 4024, 4025, and Parcel 143/45, S.O. 14-20357 Repeal Act of 2026”.
Please see enclosed signed copy of the legislation.
This legislation will repeal the Closing of Public Streets and Dedication of Land for Street and Alley
Purposes in and abutting Squares 3953, 3954, 4024, 4025, and Parcel 143/45, S.O. 14-20357, Act of 2016
(Act). This Act was originally passed in support of an approved mixed-use project in Ward 5, known as
the RIA Project, which included eight development blocks, including the current Brookland Manor
Apartment complex, with a variety of housing types and retail uses. The Act served to close a portion of
14th Street, N.E., and to accept the dedication of portions of land in Ward 5 for public street and alley
purposes. Due to planning and economic feasibility concerns, the RIA Project is not moving forward and
the current legislation will authorize the DC Surveyor’s Office to return the property to the lot
configurations that existed prior to the recordation of the street closing and street/alley dedication.
Please feel free to reach out to me or my Legislative Director, Neferteria Brown, with any questions or for
additional information. She can be reached at nbrown@dccouncil.gov or by phone at (771) 333-9734.
Sincerely,
Zachary Parker
Ward 5 Councilmember
Chair, Committee on Youth Affairs
