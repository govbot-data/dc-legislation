14 To require, on a temporary basis, that the Department of Small and Local Business Development
15 to award a $1 million dollar grant in Fiscal year 2027 to the Duke Business Improvement
16 District Corporation.
17
18 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
19 act may be cited as the “Duke District BID Grantmaking Temporary Amendment Act of 2026”.
20 Sec. 2. Amendatory paragraph (4)(A) of section 6302(c)(3) of the Fiscal Year 2027
21 Budget Support Act of 2026, enacted on August 21, 2026 (D.C. Act 26-418; 73 DCR 11735) is
22 amended to read as follows:
23 “(4)(A) Notwithstanding paragraph (3) of this subsection, within the first quarter
24 of Fiscal Year 2027, the Department of Small and Local Business Development shall award a
25 grant in the amount of $1 million from the Fund to support the Duke Business Improvement
26 District Corporation, d/b/a Duke District BID, to improve conditions for property owners,
27 businesses, residents, and visitors within the Greater Shaw/U Street area.”.

36 Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
37 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Budget Director as the fiscal impact
38 statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved
39 October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
40 Sec. 5. Effective date.
41 (a) This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by
42 the Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), a 30-day period of congressional review
43 as provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December
44 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)), and publication in the District of
45 Columbia Register.
46 (b) This act shall expire after 225 days of its having taken effect.
