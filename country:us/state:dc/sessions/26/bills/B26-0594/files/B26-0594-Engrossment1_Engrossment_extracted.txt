9 To amend the Procurement Practices Reform Act of 2010 to transfer the Office of the
10 Ombudsman of Contracting and Procurement to the Office of the City Administrator and
11 expand the related duties, to establish pay-for-performance contracting standards, to
12 authorize contracting officers to appoint technical evaluation panels and delineate the
13 manner in which they are utilized by contracting officers in evaluating proposals, to
14 update and clarify guidelines around cooperative purchasing, to reform the District
15 supply schedule for direct purchases, to establish a consolidated procurement portal
16 system, to require a forecast for public notice of anticipated solicitations, to require
17 clarity and comparability in solicitations, and for other purposes; and to amend the Small
18 and Certified Business Enterprise Development and Assistance Act of 2005 to permit
19 contracting officers to update subcontracting plans with vendors, and for other purposes.
