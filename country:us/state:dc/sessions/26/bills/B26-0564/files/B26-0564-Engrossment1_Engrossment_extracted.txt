10 To amend Chapter 10 of Title 47 of the District of Columbia Official Code to provide a property
11 tax exemption for the property situated on Lot 0005, Square 3766 at 219 Riggs Road,
12 N.E.
13
14 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
15 act may be cited as the “Food & Friends Property Tax Exemption Amendment Act of 2026”.
16 Sec. 2. Chapter 10 of Title 47 of the District of Columbia Official Code is amended as
17 follows:
18 (a) The table of contents is amended by adding a new section designation to read as
19 follows:
20 “47-1099.15 Food & Friends, Inc.; Lot 0005, Square 3766.”.
21 (b) A new section 47-1099.15 is added to read as follows:
22 “§ 47-1099.15. Food & Friends, Inc.; Lot 0005, Square 3766.
23 “(a) The real property, and any improvements on the property, located at 219 Riggs
24 Road, NE, known for tax and assessment purposes as Lot 0005, Square 3766 (“Property”), shall
25 be exempt from the tax imposed by Chapter 8 of this title; provided, that the Property is owned
26 by Food & Friends, Inc; and provided further, that Food & Friends, Inc., or any lessee of the
1

ENGROSSED ORIGINAL
27 Property, continues to use the Property to provide charitable food distribution or related services.
28 “(b) This exemption, based on the square footage of the site, shall apply to 100% of the
29 land.
30 “(c) The tax exemption provided pursuant to this section shall be in addition to and not in
31 lieu of any other tax relief or assistance from any other source applicable to Food & Friends,
32 Inc.”
33 Sec. 3. Applicability.
34 This act shall apply as of October 1, 2025.
35 Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
36 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal
37 impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
38 approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
39 Sec. 5. Effective date.
40 This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
41 Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), a 30-day period of congressional review as
42 provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December
43 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)), and publication in the District of
44 Columbia Register.
