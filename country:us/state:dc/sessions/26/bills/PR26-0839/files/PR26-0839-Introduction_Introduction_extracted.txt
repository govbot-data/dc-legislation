14 To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to amend the Deputy Mayor
15 for Planning and Economic Development Limited Grant-Making Authority Act of 2012
16 to expand permissible uses of certain grant funding; and to amend the District of
17 Columbia Taxicab Commission Establishment Act of 1985 to permit rulemaking to
18 implement a carrier-for-hire support surcharge.
19
20 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
21 resolution may be cited as the “Budget Support Act Implementation Facilitation Emergency
22 Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
23 Sec. 2. (a) The Fiscal Year 2027 Budget Support Act of 2026, effective October 2, 2026
24 (D.C. Law 26-189; D.C. Official Code passim) (“BSA”) contains numerous amendatory
25 provisions to conform the practices of the District government to the Council-approved budget
26 for Fiscal Year 2027.
27 (b) BSA subtitle II.F, the Rosemount Center Grant Amendment Act of 2026, instructed
28 the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic Development to issue a grant to the Rosemount
29 Center to support acquisition of the Center’s then-leased facility. Center representatives have
30 informed the Council that the purposes of the grant would be better served if the grant funding
31 could also be used for capital improvements.
32 (c) BSA subtitle VI.T, the Carrier-for-Hire and Food Access Support Amendment Act of
33 2026, established a surcharge on certain carrier-for-hire trips to raise revenue for food access

34 supports and other District government programs. The Department of For-Hire Vehicles, which
35 administers carrier-for-hire industry regulation, has noted that neither the BSA nor the
36 underlying law amended by this subtitle expressly authorizes the Mayor to issue regulations to
37 carry out the implementation of the surcharge.
38 (d) Immediate legislative action is necessary to address the emerging BSA
39 implementation challenges enumerated in subsections (b) and (c) of this section.
40 Sec. 3. The Council determines that the circumstances enumerated in section 2 constitute
41 emergency circumstances making it necessary that the Budget Support Act Implementation
42 Facilitation Emergency Amendment Act of 2026 be adopted after a single reading.
43 Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
