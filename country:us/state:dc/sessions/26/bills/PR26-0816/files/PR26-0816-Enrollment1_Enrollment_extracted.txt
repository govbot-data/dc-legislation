ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-527
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve a Reimbursable
Agreement with the Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority to develop a bus
rapid transit system on H Street and Benning Road between Union Station and RFK
Stadium named the “Gold Line”.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority Gold Line Bus
Rapid Transit Phase 1 Fiscal Year 2026, 2027, and 2028 Reimbursable Agreement Emergency
Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve a Reimbursable Agreement
(“Agreement”) with the Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority, for the period from
August 5, 2026, to September 30, 2028, to develop a bus rapid transit system, named the “Gold
Line,” of which Phase 1 will support the development of the H Street and Benning Road corridors,
Union Station, and the area surrounding the existing RFK Stadium.
(b) Council approval is necessary because this is a multiyear agreement in excess of $1
million.
(c) Furthermore, Council approval is necessary to ensure the frequent bus service
operating in dedicated, enforced center-running bus lanes along H Street and Benning Road, NE,
connecting the RFK stadium and redevelopment to Union Station is completed in time for the
opening of the new RFK stadium.
(d) The total amount of the Agreement is $5 million from capital funds appropriated in
Fiscal Year 2027.
(e) The District Department of Transportation Director has the authority to enter into this
Reimbursable Agreement under the general authority specified in sections 3 and 5(a)(1)(A) and
(P) of the Department of Transportation Establishment Act of 2002, effective May 21, 2002
(D.C. Law 14-137; D.C. Official Code §§ 50-921.02 and -921.04(a)(1)(A) and (P)), and section
1(j-1) of An Act To grant additional powers to the Commissioners of the District of Columbia,
and for other purposes, approved December 20, 1944 (58 Stat. 819; D.C. Official Code § 1-
301.01(j-1)).

Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority Gold Line Bus Rapid Transit Phase 1 Fiscal
Year 2026, 2027, and 2028 Reimbursable Agreement Emergency Act of 2026 be adopted after a
single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
