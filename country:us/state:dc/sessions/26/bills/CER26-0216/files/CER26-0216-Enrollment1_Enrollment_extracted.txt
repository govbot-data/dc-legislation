ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-216
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To honor and recognize Kevin Donahue for his outstanding leadership and dedicated
service to the residents of the District of Columbia as City Administrator.
WHEREAS, Kevin Donahue has devoted his professional career to improving
government agency performance, strengthening public administration, and ensuring the
advancement effective, accountable government for the people of the District of Columbia;
WHEREAS, in January 2021, Mayor Muriel Bowser appointed Mr. Donahue as City
Administrator, the District’s chief administrative officer responsible for overseeing the day-to-
day operations of the District government and coordinating the work of agencies across the
Executive Branch;
WHEREAS, as City Administrator, Mr. Donahue has provided critical and steady
leadership to ensure that the government of the District of Columbia operates efficiently,
transparently, and responsively on behalf of our residents by implementing policies adopted by
the Mayor and the Council of the District of Columbia;
WHEREAS, Mr. Donahue played a key role in leading our government’s response to the
COVID-19 pandemic by establishing a whole-of-government approach that strengthened
interagency collaboration, improved communications, and ensured the continued delivery of
essential services during an unprecedented public health crisis;
WHEREAS, throughout his tenure he has advocated for the use of data, performance
metrics, and evidence-based decision-making to improve government including through the
launch of The Lab @ DC, an innovative team of scientists and policy experts dedicated to
evaluating programs and improving outcomes for District residents;
WHEREAS, prior to serving as City Administrator, Mr. Donahue served as Deputy City
Administrator and Deputy Mayor for Public Safety and Justice, where he oversaw critical public
safety agencies and led initiatives to strengthen public safety coordination across the District
government;

WHEREAS, throughout his career he has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to
public service, professional excellence, and the well-being of the residents of the District of
Columbia;
WHEREAS, during his service, he helped launch the Office of Neighborhood Safety
and Engagement, a groundbreaking initiative focused on community-based violence prevention,
intervention, and youth engagement designed to build safer and stronger neighborhoods;
WHEREAS, Mr. Donahue also oversaw key operational improvement across
government, including modernization efforts in agencies such as the District’s Fire and
Emergency Medical Services Department and the expansion of data-driven accountability
systems that help ensure agencies meet performance goals;
WHEREAS, he has further contributed to the District’s long-term resilience by
convening multi-agency initiatives including efforts to strength the District’s climate
preparedness and flood resilience planning;
WHEREAS, Mr. Donahue also served in senior federal leadership roles including as
Executive Director of the Federal Government’s Performance Improvement Council and as a
senior advisor at the United States Department of the Treasury and the United States General
Services Administration, bringing national expertise in performance management and
operational reform to his several roles in D.C. government; and
WHEREAS, throughout his distinguished career, Mr. Donahue has demonstrated a
deep commitment to innovation in government, effective management, and the well-being of the
residents of the District.
RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Kevin Donahue Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Kevin Donahue for his
distinguished service and expresses our appreciation for his leadership, professionalism,
dedication, and commitment to excellence in his many decades of public service in our city.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
