31 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
32 __________
33
34 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
35 __________
36
37 To honor and recognize Kevin Donahue for his outstanding leadership and dedicated
38 service to the residents of the District of Columbia as City Administrator.
39
40 WHEREAS, Kevin Donahue has devoted his professional career to improving
41 government agency performance, strengthening public administration, and ensuring the
42 advancement effective, accountable government for the people of the District of Columbia;
43
44 WHEREAS, in January 2021, Mayor Muriel Bowser appointed Mr. Donahue as City

45 Administrator, the District’s chief administrative officer responsible for overseeing the day-to-
46 day operations of the District government and coordinating the work of agencies across the
47 Executive Branch;
48
49 WHEREAS, as City Administrator, Mr. Donahue has provided critical and steady
50 leadership to ensure that the government of the District of Columbia operates efficiently,
51 transparently, and responsively on behalf of our residents by implementing policies adopted by
52 the Mayor and the Council of the District of Columbia;
53
54 WHEREAS, Mr. Donahue played a key role in leading our government’s response to the
55 COVID-19 pandemic by establishing a whole-of-government approach that strengthened
56 interagency collaboration, improved communications, and ensured the continued delivery of
57 essential services during an unprecedented public health crisis;
58
59 WHEREAS, throughout his tenure he has advocated for the use of data, performance
60 metrics, and evidence-based decision-making to improve government including through the
61 launch of The Lab @ DC, an innovative team of scientists and policy experts dedicated to
62 evaluating programs and improving outcomes for District residents;
63
64 WHEREAS, prior to serving as City Administrator, Mr. Donahue served as Deputy City
65 Administrator and Deputy Mayor for Public Safety and Justice, where he oversaw critical public
66 safety agencies and led initiatives to strengthen public safety coordination across the District
67 government;
68
69 WHEREAS, throughout his career he has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to
70 public service, professional excellence, and the well-being of the residents of the District of
71 Columbia;
72
73 WHEREAS, during his service, he helped launch the Office of Neighborhood Safety
74 and Engagement, a groundbreaking initiative focused on community-based violence prevention,
75 intervention, and youth engagement designed to build safer and stronger neighborhoods;
76
77 WHEREAS, Mr. Donahue also oversaw key operational improvement across
78 government, including modernization efforts in agencies such as the District’s Fire and
79 Emergency Medical Services Department and the expansion of data-driven accountability
80 systems that help ensure agencies meet performance goals;
81
82 WHEREAS, he has further contributed to the District’s long-term resilience by
83 convening multi-agency initiatives including efforts to strength the District’s climate
84 preparedness and flood resilience planning;
85
86 WHEREAS, Mr. Donahue also served in senior federal leadership roles including as
87 Executive Director of the Federal Government’s Performance Improvement Council and as a
88 senior advisor at the United States Department of the Treasury and the United States General
89 Services Administration, bringing national expertise in performance management and
90 operational reform to his several roles in D.C. government; and

91
92 WHEREAS, throughout his distinguished career, Mr. Donahue has demonstrated a
93 deep commitment to innovation in government, effective management, and the well-being of the
94 residents of the District.
95
96 RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
97 resolution may be cited as the “Kevin Donahue Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
98
99 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Kevin Donahue for his
100 distinguished service and expresses our appreciation for his leadership, professionalism,
101 dedication, and commitment to excellence in his many decades of public service in our city.
102
103 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
104 the District of Columbia Register.
