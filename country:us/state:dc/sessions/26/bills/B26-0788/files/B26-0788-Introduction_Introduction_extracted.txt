14 To amend, on a temporary basis, the Community Schools Incentive Act of 2012 to revise the

16 residents.

18 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
19 act may be cited as the “Community Schools Grant Program Temporary Amendment Act of
20 2026”.
21 Sec. 2. Section 403(i)(2) of the Community Schools Incentive Act of 2012, effective June
22 19, 2012 (D.C. Law 19-142; D.C. Official Code § 38-754.03(i)(2), is amended by striking the
23 phrase “District residents” and inserting the word “individuals” in its place.
