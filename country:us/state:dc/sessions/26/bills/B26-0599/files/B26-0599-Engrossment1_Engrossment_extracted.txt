43 Sec. 3. The Office of Unified Communications Establishment Act of 2004, effective
44 December 7, 2004 (D.C. Law 15-205; D.C. Official Code § 1-327.51 et seq.), is amended by
45 adding a new section 3205e to read as follows:
46 “Sec. 3205e. Emergency Medical, Police, and Fire Dispatch certification.
47 “(a) The Office shall require that all Office call takers and dispatchers obtain Emergency
48 Fire Dispatch certification, Emergency Medical Dispatch certification, and Emergency Police
49 Dispatch certification within one year after being hired.
50 “(b) The Office shall require that all call takers and dispatchers already employed in the
51 Office obtain Emergency Fire Dispatch certification, Emergency Medical Dispatch certification,
52 and Emergency Police Dispatch within one year after the applicability date of the Emergency
53 Medical Services Clarification Amendment Act of 2026, approved by the Committee on the
54 Judiciary and Public Safety on DATE, 2026 (Committee print of Bill 26-XXX).
55 “(c) For the purposes of this section, the term:
56 “(1) “Emergency Fire Dispatch certification” means a specialized public safety

57 designation, which shall be approved by the Chief of the Fire and Emergency Medical Services
58 Department, that authorizes telecommunicators to perform analysis, prioritization, and
59 processing of calls while maintaining radio contact with responders to ensure safe, efficient, and
60 effective responses to requests for fire services, in accordance with local, state, or national

62 “(2) “Emergency Medical Dispatch certification” means a specialized public
63 safety designation, which shall be approved by the Medical Director of the Fire and Emergency
64 Medical Services Department, that authorizes telecommunicators to perform medical triage and
65 provide life-saving pre-arrival instructions under the authority of the Medical Director, ensuring
66 a standardized, scientifically-validated response to medical emergencies.
67 “(3) “Emergency Police Dispatch certification” means a specialized public safety
68 designation, which shall be approved by the Chief of the Metropolitan Police Department, that
69 authorizes telecommunicators to perform analysis, prioritization, and processing of calls while
70 maintaining radio contact with responders to ensure safe, efficient, and effective responses to
71 requests for law enforcement services, in accordance with local, state, or national standards.”.
