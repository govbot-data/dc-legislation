ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-472
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
July 14, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve Contract No.
CW133373 with JJ Prime Services, LLC, and to authorize payment for the goods and
services received under the contract.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Contract No. CW133373 with JJ Prime Services, LLC Approval
and Payment Authorization Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve Contract No. CW133373 with JJ
Prime Services, LLC to provide emergency snow removal equipment and operators, and to
authorize payment for the goods and services received under the contract.
(b) The Office of Contracting and Procurement, on behalf of the Department of Public
Works, executed emergency Contract No. CW133373 in the not-to-exceed amount of
$1,511,658.90 for the period from January 29, 2026, through February 13, 2026.
(c) Council approval is necessary because the contract is more than $1 million during a
12-month period.
(d) Council approval is necessary to pay for these vital services. Without Council
approval, JJ Prime Services, LLC cannot be paid for goods and services provided in excess of $1
million for the period from January 29, 2026, through February 13, 2026.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Contract No. CW133373 with JJ Prime Services LLC Approval and Payment Authorization
Emergency Act of 2026 be adopted after a single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
