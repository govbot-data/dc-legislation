12 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
13 __________
14
15 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
16 __________
17
18
19 To honor and recognize the Office of the Secretary for its efforts in preserving the history
20 and heritage of the District of Columbia.
21
22 WHEREAS, the Office of the Secretary of the District of Columbia serves an essential
23 role in the effective operation of our government by preserving official records, maintaining
24 public documents, and supporting the transparency, accountability, and continuity of
25 government;
26
27 WHEREAS, the Office of the Secretary of the District of Columbia, directed by
28 Secretary Kimberly Bassett, is entrusted with a broad range of responsibilities, including the
29 publication of official documents, led by Victor Reid; the administration of notarial and apostille
30 services led by Greer Hill and his staff; the Director of the Archives and the District's history and
31 other important records, led by Dr. Lopez Matthews, Jr.; the Ceremonial Services Unit led by
32 Terry Handy and Gina Henderson; and the Office of Protocol and International Affairs;
33
34 WHEREAS, through service to our government, residents, businesses, agencies and
35 international visitors, the Office helps ensure that these official government functions are
36 professional, accurate, and conducted with integrity, on behalf of our government and all our
37 residents;
38
39 WHEREAS, the Secretary of the District government, many times referred to as our
40 Secretary of State, is the face of our government and represents the DC government, promoting
41 over 700,000 residents, speaking on behalf of the District, serving as the host city for the
42 diplomatic corps, and highlighting the city’s hub of governmental, cultural and business activity;
43
44 WHEREAS, Councilmember Anita Bonds wishes to express her sincere appreciation to
45 the Office of the Secretary for its service to DC and recognizes that the work of this Office is

46 fundamental to preserving the public record, serving the community, and acting on behalf of our
47 government and all our residents;
48
49 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
50 resolution may be cited as the “Office of the Secretary Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
51
52 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the Office of the Secretary for
53 its efforts in preserving the history and heritage of the District of Columbia.
54
55 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
56 the District of Columbia Register.
