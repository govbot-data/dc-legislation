28 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
29
30 __________
31
32 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
33
34 ____________________
35
36 To recognize the Transgender Day of Remembrance, to celebrate the resilience of transgender
37 individuals and communities, to honor the lives of transgender people lost to violence, and
38 to declare Friday, November 20, 2026, as Transgender Day of Remembrance in the District
39 of Columbia.
40
41 WHEREAS, the government of the District of Columbia has affirmatively and officially
42 recognized the right of transgender people to live openly and freely as their authentic selves,
43 without fear of discrimination or violence;
44
45 WHEREAS, examples of the District’s official commitment to the legal rights of
46 transgender residents include the prohibition of discrimination on the basis of gender identity or
1

47 expression since 2005, and the refusal to facilitate cross-border interference in gender-affirming
48 health care under the Human Rights Sanctuary Amendment Act of 2022;
49
50 WHEREAS, the District of Columbia nevertheless has a particularly alarming history of
51 violence against transgender individuals including the murders of Daquane “Dream” Johnson,
52 A’Nee Roberson, Jasmine Parker, Zoe Spears, Ashanti Carmon, Deeniquia Dodds, Deoni Jones,
53 Lashai Mclean, Tyli’a Mack, Elexius Woodland, Bella Evangelista, Emonie Spaulding, Stephanie
54 Thomas, Ukea Davis, and too many others;
55
56 WHEREAS, bias, violence and systemic oppression against transgender people remain
57 unacceptably prevalent, including in the District of Columbia;
58
59 WHEREAS, incarceration, homelessness, poverty and addiction disproportionately harm
60 transgender people, especially transgender women of color, and continue to claim lives;
61
62 WHEREAS, transgender people have the same human rights as all people to be free from
63 violence and discrimination, and to live their lives with joy, dignity, and hopes for the future,
64 respected and loved as any other person;
65
66 WHEREAS, transgender people in the District of Columbia have long exhibited resilience
67 and bravery, including by organizing in response to the 1997 death of Tyra Hunter, a Black
68 transgender woman whom District employees refused to treat for life-threatening injuries after
69 determining that she was transgender;
70
71 WHEREAS, transgender people in the surrounding area have continued to be murdered,
72 including Dream Johnson, while misgendering by media and police may have obscured additional
73 instances of violence against transgender people in the region;
74
75 WHEREAS, the people and government of the District will continue to fight for
76 transgender people’s well-being, even in the face of escalating, cruel, politically motivated
77 attempts to discriminate against, stigmatize, and criminalize transgender people in legislatures and
78 political campaigns; and
79
80 WHEREAS, the transgender community and allies in the District of Columbia have
81 commemorated Transgender Day of Remembrance since 2001 and communities and organizations
82 here and around the world will observe the Transgender Day of Remembrance on November 20,
83 2026.
84
85 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
86 resolution may be cited as the “Transgender Day of Remembrance Recognition Resolution of
87 2026”.
88
89 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia declares November 20, 2026, as the
90 Transgender Day of Remembrance in the District of Columbia.
91
2

92 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
93 the District of Columbia Register.
