ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-229
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 20, 2026
To celebrate the 40th anniversary of Bagels Etc. and the impact the establishment has had on
Washingtonians seeking a comforting start to their day.
WHEREAS, for 40 years, Bagels Etc. has continued its dedication to traditional bagel-
making techniques;
WHEREAS, Bagels Etc. has prided itself in creating a welcoming atmosphere with cozy
nooks and local artwork displayed;
WHEREAS, the staff’s friendly demeanor fosters a genuine sense of community to
newcomers and regular customers;
WHEREAS, Bagels Etc. is located in the bustling P Street corridor steps from cultural
landmarks and Embassy Row;
WHEREAS, Bagels Etc. adds to the neighborhood charm of Foggy Bottom and is
frequented by students, commuters, and visitors.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Bagels Etc. 40th Anniversary Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and celebrates the 40th
Anniversary of Bagels Etc. and the community the establishment fosters in the District.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
