ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-213
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To honor and recognize Robert Vinson Brannum, a native Washingtonian, United States
Air Force veteran, community leader, civil rights advocate, tireless champion for the
people of the District of Columbia, and outstandingly active member of the Ward 5
community.
WHEREAS, Robert Vinson Brannum has devoted his life to the principle that being a
resident of the District carries with it a responsibility to serve the District;
WHEREAS, Mr. Brannum has demonstrated his commitment through decades of
military, civic, veterans, and community service;
WHEREAS, as a veteran of the United States Air Force, Mr. Brannum proudly served his
country and has continued his tradition of service by advocating for veterans and ensuring that
their voices are heard and their contributions recognized;
WHEREAS, Mr. Brannum has been a longtime leader within the NAACP Washington,
DC Branch, including serving as Chair of its Veterans Committee, where he has worked to
advance the interests of veterans while carrying forward NAACP's historic commitment to
equality, justice, and civil rights;
WHEREAS, Robert Vinson Brannum has been a steadfast advocate for full voting rights
and statehood for the District of Columbia, upholding with conviction the premise that residents
of the Nation's Capital deserve the same democratic voice and representation as residents of
every state in the United States;
WHEREAS, his commitment to our city is not simply political but deeply personal,
reflecting his identity as a native Washingtonian and his belief that everyone in the District
deserves a government that listens to and serves them;
WHEREAS, Mr. Brannum has also given his time to neighborhood and civic affairs,
including service as an Advisory Neighborhood Commissioner;

WHEREAS, his dedication to public service has extended well beyond the boundaries of
any one organization or cause but has earned him the reputation of consistently being willing to
speak up, advocate, and work on behalf of principles in which he believes;
WHEREAS, in 2017, Robert Vinson Brannum's commitment to Democratic principles
led him to seek the chairmanship of the Democratic National Committee; and
WHEREAS, Robert Vinson Brannum has remained true to the values of service, justice,
equality, democracy, and community.
RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Robert Vinson Brannum Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors Robert Vinson
Brannum as a proud Washingtonian and United States Air Force veteran who has engaged in a
lifetime of military and community service, advocacy on behalf of veterans and civil rights, and
unwavering commitment to the people and the future of the District of Columbia.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
