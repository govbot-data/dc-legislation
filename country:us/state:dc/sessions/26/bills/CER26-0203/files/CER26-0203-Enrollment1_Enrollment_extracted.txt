ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-203
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To honor and recognize Roy Leon Price as a leader, legend, role model, and exceptional
Washingtonian.
WHEREAS, Roy Leon Price was educated in the District of Columbia Public Schools
and graduated from Dunbar High School, one of the nation’s historic African American
educational institutions and a source of generations of distinguished Washingtonians;
WHEREAS, as a talented young athlete, Roy Leon Price earned recognition for his
accomplishments on the football field and was selected for the Washington Afro-American All-
High Football Team in 1951, an honor that placed him among the outstanding Black high school
athletes of Washington DC during the era of segregation;
WHEREAS, Mr. Price’s athletic accomplishments and place in Washington’s history are
documented in Earl Telfair’s The Black Athletes of the District of Columbia During the
Segregated Years, preserving the important story of the Black athletes who excelled despite the
barriers imposed on them by segregation;
WHEREAS, following his high school years, Roy Leon Price answered the call to serve
his country by joining the United States Air Force, where he served four years before receiving
an honorable discharge;
WHEREAS, Roy Leon Price has shared his life with his beloved wife, the late Eloise
Simpkins, and together they built a family and a legacy rooted in love, commitment, and
community;
WHEREAS, throughout his life, Roy Leon Price has demonstrated the qualities of
discipline, perseverance, leadership, and service that characterized both his athletic achievements
and his military service;
WHEREAS, Roy Leon Price’s life represents an important chapter in the history of
Washington, DC, from the segregated schools and athletic fields of his youth to military service
to his nation, to the many ways in which he has contributed to the life and legacy of the District;

ENROLLED ORIGINAL
WHEREAS, Roy Leon Price was employed as the first Black air traffic controller at
Washington National Airport (now Reagan National Airport);
WHEREAS, Roy Leon Price was promoted to the position as the first Black air traffic
controller supervisor at Friendship Airport (now Baltimore/Washington International Thurgood
Marshall Airport);
WHEREAS, Mr. Price has been a member of good standing of the Greater New Hope
Baptist Church since the age of three and has served on many ministries, including over 30 years
as an usher and church treasurer;
WHEREAS, in 1965, Mr. Price was instrumental in the creation and development of his
home on a new street in Ward 7, where he remains one of two original residents and where he
continues to be very active with the Penn Branch Community Association;
WHEREAS, Roy Leon Price is a 33rd degree mason, currently holds the title of sovereign
grand commander emeritus, and is highly respected nationally for his governing leadership and
community outreach;
WHEREAS, Roy Leon Price is an avid, longstanding, and loyal Washington
Redskins/Commanders fan and has been a season ticket holder since the first Black player,
Bobby Mitchell, joined the team in 1962;
WHEREAS, at his current age of 93, Mr. Price remains an enthusiastic fan, arriving at
home games two hours before kick-off and staying until the end of the last play regardless of the
score or weather conditions;
WHEREAS, Mr. Price has reserved his season tickets for the first game at the new
Commander’s stadium and was present for the first and last Commander games played at R.F.K.
Stadium;
WHEREAS, Mr. Price retired from the Federal Aviation Administration after serving for
39 years;
WHEREAS, today you can find Mr. Price at the Planet Fitness gym at 6:00 a.m., 6 days
of the week, inspiring and motivating all with his vigorous workout routine;
WHEREAS, his story is a reminder that the achievements of African American
Washingtonians who excelled during segregation deserve to be remembered, celebrated, and
passed on to future generations; and
WHEREAS, Roy Leon Price has earned the distinction of being recognized as a leader, a
legend, a role model, and an exceptional Washingtonian whose life and accomplishments honor
his family, his community, and the District of Columbia.

ENROLLED ORIGINAL
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this resolution
may be cited as the “Roy Leon Price Recognition Resolution of 2026.”
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors Mr. Roy Leon
Price for his extraordinary life, accomplishments, military service, athletic achievement, and
enduring contributions to the Washington, DC community. I extend my deepest appreciation to
Roy Leon Price for the example he has set and for the legacy he represents as a leader, legend,
role model, and exceptional Washingtonian.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
