Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Pursuant to section 451 of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act (D.C. Official Code § 1 -
204.51) and section 202 of the Procurement Practices Reform Act of 2010 (D.C. Official Code §
2-352.02), enclosed for consideration and approval by the Council of the District of Columbia is a
proposed resolution, the “Contract No. DCAM-21-NC-RFQuals-0001C PPA-26-01 with Flywheel
Development, LLC Emergency Approval Resolution of 2026”, and accompanying emergency
declaration resolution.
Under the proposed definitive contract, Flywheel Development, LLC will develop solar
photovoltaic systems at twenty-seven (27) District-owned sites at no cost to the District. Flywheel
Development, LLC’s sole compensation is the future transfer of Solar Renewable Energy Credits
and the District’s agreement to offtake 100% of the electricity generated once systems are
operational.
My administration is available to discuss any questions you may have regarding the proposed
contract. In order to facilitate a response to any questions you may have, please contact Delano
Hunter, Director, Department of General Services (“DGS”), or have your staff contact Dr. Jacque
McDonald, Associate Director and Chief Procurement Officer of Contracts and Procurement,
DGS, at (202) 727-2800.
I look forward to the Council’s prompt and favorable action on the enclosed resolutions.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowser
