9 To amend, on a temporary basis, the Homeless Services Reform Act of 2005 to clarify the
10 authorization to share certain data within the Department of Human Services and between
11 the Department of Human Services and other District agencies to facilitate the
12 determination of eligibility for federal and District health and human service programs;
13 and to amend the Data-Sharing and Information Coordination Amendment Act of 2010 to
14 clarify the definition of the term “agency” and the intent of the grant of data system
15 authority.
16
17 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
18 act may be cited as the “Health and Human Service Programs and Benefits Eligibility and Delivery
19 Data-Sharing Clarifications Temporary Amendment Act of 2026”.
20 Sec. 2. The Homeless Services Reform Act of 2005, effective October 22, 2005 (D.C. Law
21 16-35; D.C. Official Code § 4-751.01 et seq.), is amended as follows:
22 (a) Section 9(a)(7) (D.C. Official Code § 4-754.11(a)(7)) is amended by striking the phrase
23 “consistent with the confidentiality requirements of District and federal law;” and inserting the
24 phrase “consistent with the confidentiality requirements of District and federal law, subject to the
25 authorized uses and mandatory disclosures of such records and information in accordance with
26 Title I of the Data-Sharing and Information Coordination Amendment Act of 2010, effective
27 December 4, 2010 (D.C. Law 18-273; D.C. Official Code § 7-241 et seq.);” in its place.
