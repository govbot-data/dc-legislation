9 To amend Title 12-G of the District of Columbia Municipal Regulations to revise dates requiring
10 the provision of heat and of air conditioning by owners and operators of rental housing
11 who provide air conditioning as a service and to set minimum and maximum year-round
12 internal temperature requirements for rental housing where heating and air conditioning
13 are under the control of the tenant.
14
15 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
16 act may be cited as the “Climatizing Our Overheated Living Spaces Regulation Amendment Act
17 of 2026”.
18 Sec. 2. Chapter 6 of Subtitle G of Title 12 of the District of Columbia Municipal
19 Regulations is amended as follows:
20 (a) Subsection 602.3 (12G DCMR § 602.3) is amended to read as follows:
21 “602.3. Heat Supply. Every owner and operator of any building who rents, leases or lets
22 one or more dwelling units, or rooming units, dormitory or guestrooms on terms, either
23 expressed or implied, to furnish heat to the occupants thereof shall supply sufficient heat during a
24 period starting no later than October 15 and ending no earlier than April 15 to maintain a
25 temperature of not less than 68ºF (20ºC) in all habitable rooms, bathrooms and toilet rooms;
26 except that, when the outdoor temperature is below the winter outdoor design temperature for the

27 District, maintenance of the minimum room temperature shall not be required provided that the
28 heating system is operating at its full capacity. The winter outdoor design temperature for the
29 District of Columbia as set by the current District of Columbia Residential Code, Chapter 3
30 Design Criteria.”.
31 (b) Section 608 (12G DCMR § 608) is amended to read as follows:
32 “608 Residential Air Conditioning and Heating Supply
33 “608.1 Air Conditioning. Every owner or operator of a housing business or dormitory,
34 who provides air conditioning as a service either through individual air conditioning units or a
35 central air conditioning system, shall maintain each such air conditioning unit or system in a safe
36 and good working condition so that it is capable of providing, during a period starting no later
37 than May 1 and ending no earlier than October 1, an inside temperature, in the rooms it is
38 intended to serve, equal to the greater of: (a) 78 ºF (26 ºC); or (b) at least 15 ºF (9 ºC) less than
39 the outside temperature.
40 “608.2 Heat Supply. Every owner or operator of a building who rents, leases, or lets one
41 or more dwelling units, rooming units, dormitory rooms, or guestrooms, shall provide heating
42 services pursuant to Section 602 of this chapter. Any owner or operator who does not provide air
43 conditioning as a service shall furnish heating services through a period starting no later than
44 October 15 and ending no earlier than April 15.
45 “608.3 Tenant control of internal temperature. A owner or operator of building who
46 provides air conditioning as a service and heating to a tenant and where air conditioning and

47 heating is under the control of the tenant shall maintain the central heating systems and central
48 air conditioning system or individual air conditioning units in safe and good working condition
49 and capable of maintaining, throughout the year, a room temperature of no less than 68°F (20°C)
50 and, when air conditioning is operating, no more than the greater of (a) 78ºF (26ºC); or, (b) at
51 least 15 ºF (9ºC) less than the outside temperature.
52 “608.4. Two-pipe system turnover. In a building with a two-pipe system, air conditioning
53 and heat provision may be transitioned at the discretion of a building owner or manager,
54 provided that air conditioning is available to tenants no later than May 1, and heat is available to
55 no later than October 15.
56 “608.5. Inspection of air conditioning equipment by qualified persons. The owner of a
57 structure containing two or more units served by central or shared air conditioning equipment, or
58 wall-mounted air conditioning units (not including window air conditioning units), shall cause
59 the air conditioning equipment to be inspected by a qualified person annually. A copy of current
60 inspection and service reports shall be available for public inspection on site in the office of the
61 building operator.
62 Exception: If the building operator does not have an on-site office, the reports shall be
63 made available for inspection:
64 1. By posting the reports in an on-site location accessible to all building residents; or
65 2. By mailing or delivering a copy of the reports to each unit; or

69 Sec. 3. Applicability.
70 This act shall apply as of May 1, 2027.
