ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-478
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
July 14, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve Contract No.
CW128161 with OceanPro Industries, Ltd., and Modification Nos. M001, M002, M003,
M004, and M004a thereto, and to authorize payment for the goods and services received
and to be received under the contract.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Contract No. CW128161 with OceanPro Industries, Ltd. and
Modification Nos. M001, M002, M003, M004 and M004a Approval and Payment Authorization
Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve Contract No. CW128161 with
OceanPro Industries, Ltd. to provide groceries for the preparation of meals for youth, and M001,
M002, M003, M004 and M004a thereto, and to authorize payment for the goods and services
received and to be received under the contract.
(b) On August 4, 2025, the Office of Contracting and Procurement (“OCP”), on behalf of
the Department of Youth Rehabilitation Services (“DYRS”), executed Contract No. CW128161
in the amount of $181,031.59.
(c) By Modification No. 001, OCP increased the not-to-exceed amount of the contract by
$155,000, to a total not-to-exceed amount of $336,031.59.
(d) By Modification No. 002, OCP increased the not-to-exceed amount of the contract by
$617,426.42, to a total not-to-exceed amount of $953,458.01.
(e) By Modification No. 003, OCP increased the not-to-exceed amount of the contract by
$400,698.88, to a total not-to-exceed amount of $1,354,156.89.
(f) By Modification No. 004, OCP increased the amount of the contract by $383,200.92,
to $1,737,357.81.
(g) Modification No.004a revised the contract amount to a total not-to-exceed amount of
$1,737,357.81.
(h) Council approval is necessary because the modifications will increase the value of the
contract to more than $1 million during a 12-month period.
(i) Council approval is necessary to allow the continuation of these vital services.
Without Council approval, OceanPro Industries, Ltd. cannot be paid for goods and services
provided in excess of $1 million for the period from August 4, 2025, through August 3, 2026.

Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Contract No. CW128161 with OceanPro Industries, Ltd. Approval and Payment Authorization
Emergency Act of 2026 be adopted after a single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
