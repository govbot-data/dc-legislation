21 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
22 __________
23
24 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
25 __________
26
27
28 To honor and recognize Iona’s Washington Home Center, located in Ward 8, for its
29 commitment to older adults, caregivers, and families in the District of Columbia and
30 acknowledge its vital role as a place of support and comfort to District residents.
31
32 WHEREAS, the Washington Home has for generations has been an important part of
33 caring for older adults and supporting individuals caring for or living with serious illnesses in the
34 District of Columbia;
35
36 WHEREAS, the Washington Home, a charitable foundation, expanded its commitment to
37 District residents east of the Anacostia River through the establishment of the Washington Home
38 Center, located at Alabama Avenue and Stanton Road Southeast;
39
40 WHEREAS, the Washington Home Center opened in 2020, recognizing that with rising
41 rates of Alzheimer’s, Parkinson’s, and Type 2 diabetes, and high prevalence among African
42 Americans, there was an urgent need for specialized, supervised care in the area;
43
44 WHEREAS, the Washington Home donated one million dollars to Iona Senior Services
45 to fund the construction of a new adult day center to support older adults and their families in
46 Wards 7 and 8;

47
48 WHEREAS, the Washington Home’s legacy reflects the fundamental idea that every
49 older adult deserves to live with dignity, compassion, and the opportunity to remain connected to
50 their community; and
51
52 WHEREAS, the work carried forward in Southeast Washington, D.C. ensures that the
53 growing population of seniors throughout the District has access to services that provide them
54 with support and the opportunity for meaningful engagement;
55
56 WHEREAS, the center represents an important investment in bringing high-quality
57 senior services closer to the homes and communities of District residents east of the Anacostia
58 River;
59
60 WHEREAS, the Washington Home Center helps older adults remain connected to their
61 communities while providing families and caregivers with valuable support and resources;
62
63 WHEREAS, we recognize and appreciate the many caregivers, professionals, volunteers,
64 families, and community partners whose dedication has sustained this important work over many
65 years of care, and
66
67 WHEREAS the Washington Home’s long-standing legacy of service demonstrates the
68 importance of community-based service that responds directly to the needs of District seniors
69 and their families.
70
71 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
72 resolution may be cited as the “Washington Home Center Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
73
74 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors the Washington
75 Home Center for its commitment to older adults, caregivers, and families in the District of
76 Columbia, extending its sincere appreciation for its continued service to our community.
77
78 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
79 the District of Columbia Register.
