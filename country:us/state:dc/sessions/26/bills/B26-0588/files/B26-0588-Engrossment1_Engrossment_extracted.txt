11 To amend the Captive Insurance Company Act of 2004 to update protected cell captive insurers’
12 requirements for formation, capital, certificate of authority applications, transacting
13 business, taxes on premiums, and annual reports, and to establish requirements for
14 inactive captive insurance companies; and to amend the Risk Retention Act of 1993 to
15 provide rulemaking authority.
16
17 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
18 act may be cited as the “Captive Insurance Company Amendment Act of 2026”.

96 (6) A new section 12a is added to read as follows:
97 “Sec. 12a. Inactive captive insurance companies.
98 “(a) The Commissioner may place a captive insurer, except a risk retention group, in an
99 inactive status, if:
100 “(1) The captive insurer has ceased transacting the business of insurance;
101 “(2) There are no remaining liabilities associated with policies written or assumed
102 by the captive insurer; and
103 “(3) The captive insurer is compliant with all applicable District laws and
104 regulations.
105 “(b) An inactive captive insurer shall maintain unimpaired capital and surplus in a
106 minimum amount of $5,000.
