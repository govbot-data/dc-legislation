INTHECOUNCIL OF THEDISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
OCTOBER 29, 2026
To amend, on atemporarybasis, Chapter 39ofTitle 28 oftheDistrict ofColumbia Official Code
to deem certain’ federally regulated consumer price transparency practices in the
telecommunications industrycompliantwith Districtpricetransparencyrequirements.
BE ITENACTED BYTHE COUNCIL OFTHE DISTRICTOF COLUMBIA, Thatthis
actmaybe cited as the“BroadbandPriceTransparency RegulationTemporaryAmendmentAct
of2026”.
Sec. 2. Chapter 39 of Title 28ofthe District ofColumbiaOfficial Code is amended as
follows:
(a)The tableofcontents is amended by adding new section designations to read as
follows:
“28-3903a. Cease and desist orders; summary suspension; summary closure.
“28-3904a, Special price information standard forbroadband services complying with
federal rules.”.
(b) Anew section 28-3904ais added to read as follows:
“ § 28-3904a, Special price information standard forbroadband services complying with
federal rules.
“Amerchantprovidingbroadband intemet access who complies with thebroadband
consumer label requirements of47 C.ER. § 8.1 as those requirements existed onJanuary 1,
2026, shall be deemed compliant with therequirementsof § 28-3904(a)(12).”.
Sec. 3. Applicability.
Thisact shall apply as ofthe effective dateofthe Enhancing ConsumerProtection
Procedures AmendmentAct of2026, effective September 26, 2026(D.C. Law26-187; 73 DCR
9162).

(a) This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (orin the eventofveto by
