ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-212
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To honor and recognize Battle’s Transportation, Inc., for 50 years of excellence in
service.
WHEREAS, in 1976, Battle’s Transportation, Inc. was founded in the District of
Columbia and has proudly served the District and the surrounding community for 50 years;
WHEREAS, as a passenger transportation company dedicated to safety, reliability and
exceptional service, Battle’s Transportation, Inc. has built a distinguished reputation through 5
decades of commitment to its customers and our community;
WHEREAS, from its early years, Battle’s Transportation, Inc. established a legacy of
service by securing a major contract to provide non-emergency medical transportation to the
D.C. Veterans Medical Center, reflecting the company’s longstanding commitment to serving
our nation’s veterans;
WHEREAS, over the past 50 years, Battle’s Transportation, Inc. has grown into a
trusted provider of executive transportation and passenger services continuing to uphold the high
standards of professionalism, dependability, and customer service upon which the company was
founded;
WHEREAS, 50 years of successful service is a remarkable milestone, representing
generations of dedication, hard work, perseverance, and a steadfast commitment to excellence;
and;
WHEREAS, the Council of the District of Columbia is proud to recognize businesses that
have deep roots in our community and have contributed to the District’s economic vitality and
quality of life through decades of responsible and dedicated service;
WHEREAS, the Council of the District of Columbia hereby recognizes Battle’s
Transportation, Inc. for 50 years of service to the District and neighboring communities.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Battle’s Transportation, Inc. Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
1

ENROLLED ORIGINAL
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors Battle’s
Transportation, Inc. on the occasion of its 50th anniversary, celebrating 5 decades of excellence,
safety, reliability, and exceptional service, and extending its congratulations and best wishes for
continued success in the years ahead.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
