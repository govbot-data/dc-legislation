12 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
13 __________
14
15 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
16 __________
17
18
19 To honor and recognize all the offices that comprise the Executive Office of the Mayor
20 (EOM) for their service to the residents of the District of Columbia and for their collaboration
21 with the Council of the District of Columbia in advancing quality-of-life issues for residents in
22 all wards of the District.
23
24 WHEREAS, the work of the Executive Office of the Mayor requires ongoing
25 collaboration with the Council of the District of Columbia to address the needs of our residents,
26 develop effective public policy, and respond appropriately to challenges and opportunities;
27
28 WHEREAS, the staff of the Executive Office of the Mayor contributes their professional
29 knowledge, dedication and public service to the development and implementation of policies and
30 initiatives intended to improve the quality of life in DC;
31
32 WHEREAS, effective communication and cooperation between the Executive and
33 Legislative branches are essential to the responsible stewardship of public resources, the
34 effective operation of District government, and the continued advancement of District priorities;
35
36 WHEREAS, Councilmember Anita Bonds recognizes the importance of the Executive
37 Office of the Mayor's work and expresses deep appreciation for the cooperation, engagement,
38 and service of the leadership and staff of all EOM offices throughout her tenure of the Council;
39
40 WHEREAS, as Councilmember Bonds concludes her service on the Council of the
41 District of Columbia, she feels it only fitting to acknowledge and state her appreciation for the
42 contributions of the public servants who work in the Executive Office of the Mayor every day to
43 serve the residents of the nation's capital;
44

45 WHEREAS, the offices and the staff of the Executive Office of the Mayor play a vital
46 role in coordinating the executive functions of our government by supporting the Mayor in the
47 vital functions and responsibilities of her office; and
48
49 WHEREAS, the Mayor’s Office of Community Relations and Services serves as a vital
50 connection between District residents and their government by addressing neighborhood
51 concerns, coordinating constituent services, improving accessibility, and fostering meaningful
52 relationships between communities and government agencies;
53
54 WHEREAS, the Mayor’s Office of Volunteerism and Partnerships through Serve DC,
55 strengthens the spirit of civic responsibility and community service by expanding volunteer
56 opportunities, fostering public-private partnerships, and connecting residents and organizations
57 committed to serving the District;
58
59 WHEREAS, the Mayor’s Office of Policy and Legislative Affairs plays a critical role in
60 shaping and advancing the Mayor’s legislative and policy agenda by providing policy analysis,
61 coordinating legislative priorities, and facilitating collaboration between the Executive Office of
62 the Mayor and the Council of the District of Columbia;
63
64 WHEREAS, the Mayor’s Office of Community Partnerships strengthens the relationship
65 between District government and the communities it serves by cultivating partnerships with
66 residents, stakeholders, and community organizations, promoting inclusion, expanding access to
67 government resources, and ensuring that diverse perspectives inform the Mayor’s initiatives;
68
69 WHEREAS, the Mayor’s Office of Cultural Affairs enriches the District’s social and
70 cultural landscape by fostering collaboration among neighborhood organizations, community
71 stakeholders, and government agencies, while promoting inclusive services and addressing the
72 unique needs of the District’s diverse populations; and
73
74 WHEREAS, collectively, these offices reflect the Executive Office of the Mayor’s
75 commitment to accessible government, responsive public service, meaningful community
76 partnerships, and the recognition of the diverse communities that contribute to the District of
77 Columbia’s identity and continued progress.
78
79 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
80 resolution may be cited as the “Executive Office of the Mayor Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
81
82 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and commends the Executive
83 Office of the Mayor for its essential role in advancing the District’s priorities, strengthening
84 relationships across its diverse communities, fostering meaningful civic engagement, and
85 demonstrating an enduring commitment to responsive governance and the well-being of all
86 District residents.
87
88 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
89 the District of Columbia Register.
