ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-522
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve the expenditures
for Multiyear Contract No. PO-GF-2026-C-0019 by and between the University of the
District of Columbia and Ellucian Company LLC in the amount of $10,157,746.00 for
the strategic transition of the University’s Enterprise Resource Planning infrastructure to
a Software as a Service environment.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “University of the District of Columbia Multiyear Contract No.
PO-GF-2026-C-0019 with Ellucian Company LLC Contract Emergency Declaration Resolution
of 2026”.
Sec. 2.
Emergency approval of Multiyear Contract No. PO-GF-2026-C-0019 is necessary to allow the
University of the District of Columbia (“University”) to take advantage of certain financial
incentives and key support resources included in the agreement that would support the initial
transition to the Ellucian Software as a Service model. A delay affects the timing and availability
of those benefits and approval on an emergency basis allows the University to potentially take
advantage of those financial incentives and support resources included in the agreement.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
University of the District of Columbia Multiyear Contract No. PO-GF-2026-C-0019 Ellucian
Company LLC Contract Emergency Act of 2026 be adopted after a single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
