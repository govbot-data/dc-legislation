24 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
25
26 _________________________
27
28 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
29
30 _________________________
31
32
33
34 To recognize and celebrate the inaugural Capital Queens Women’s Sports Week, held
35 September 20th through September 26th, and jointly established by the Washington Spirit and
36 Washington Mystics, celebrating the value of girls’ and women’s participation in sports.
37
38 WHEREAS, the Washington Mystics and Washington Spirit came together to launch a
39 weeklong crossover called Capital Queens, pioneering the District of Columbia’s first women’s
40 sports week;
41
42 WHEREAS, Washington D.C. is the epicenter of women’s sports, where women’s teams
43 have brought national championships in professional sports, collegiate programs, and high
44 school programs;
45
1

46 WHEREAS, the collaboration between two of DC’s longest women’s professional sports
47 teams will bring together players, fans, local businesses, and the community at large to celebrate
48 women’s sports and increase awareness of the importance of girls’ participation in youth sports;
49
50 WHEREAS, girls’ participation in sports leads to more healthier communities, stronger
51 leaders, robust professional careers, and more equitable futures, empowering girls to build
52 confidence, and leadership, especially during critical developmental years;
53
54 WHEREAS, girls’ participation in sports supports stronger self-worth and emotional
55 resilience as they grow and face increased social pressures, body image concerns, fear of
56 judgment and feelings of not belonging;
57
58 WHEREAS, according to the Women’s Sports Foundation, girls still have fewer
59 opportunities to play sports than boys, considering barriers such as limited resources, access to
60 school sports, transportation, and community programs that make it more difficult for girls to
61 participate and stay engaged;
62
63 WHEREAS, more than 38% of U.S. girls do not participate in youth sports, and by the
64 age of 14 are twice as likely to drop out of sports completely compared to boys;
65
66 WHEREAS, 69% of women who earned more than $100,000 per year and are in
67 leadership roles played competitive sports and 85% of women who played sports report the skills
68 they developed playing sports were important to success in their professional careers; and
69
70 WHEREAS, the correlation between professional success and adolescent athletics has
71 demonstrated that 94% of women in C-suite positions played sports, more than half played at the
72 collegiate level, and approximately 80% of Fortune 500 women executives played sports in their
73 earlier years.
74
75 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
76 resolution may be cited as the “Capital Queens Women’s Sports Week Recognition Resolution
77 of 2026.”
78
79 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Capital Queens Women’s
80 Sports Week, encourages the participation of girls and women in sports, and celebrates the
81 athletes, fans, and local community in making the District a leader in women’s sports.
82
83 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
84 the District of Columbia Register.
