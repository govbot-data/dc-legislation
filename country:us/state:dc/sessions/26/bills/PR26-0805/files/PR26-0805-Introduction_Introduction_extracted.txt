Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Enclosed for consideration and approval by the Council of the District of Columbia is a proposed
resolution entitled “Transfer of Jurisdiction of Portions of Squares 1177, 1178, and 1179 and a
Portion of 35th Street, NW, S.O. 26-01542, S.O. 26-01542, Approval Resolution of 2026.”
The resolution will approve the transfer of jurisdiction of approximately 69,444 square feet of land
comprising a portion of Squares 1177-1179 and a portion of 35th Street, NW (to be closed) from
the Department of the Interior, National Park Service to the District of Columbia. This resolution
is necessary to advance the tri-party agreement for the transfer and disposition of parcels along the
Potomac River between Georgetown University, the National Park Service, and the District to
complete a decade-long effort to realize expanded non-motorized boating activities along the
Potomac River in Georgetown. Bill 26-740, the Georgetown Boathouse Surplus Declaration and
Disposition Approval Act of 2026, is a companion to this resolution.
I urge the Council to take prompt and favorable action on the enclosed resolution.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowser

SUBJECT: Fiscal Impact Statement – Transfer of Jurisdiction over property in
Squares 1177-1179 and a Portion of 35th Street NW (to be closed), S.O.
26-01542, Act of 2026
REFERENCE: Draft Bill as provided to the Office of Revenue Analysis on July 23, 2026
Conclusion
Funds are sufficient in the proposed revised fiscal year 2026 budget and proposed fiscal year 2027
through fiscal year 2030 budget and financial plan to implement the bill.
Background
The bill approves the transfer of jurisdiction over approximately 69,000 square feet of National Park
Service owned land along the Potomac River to the District of Columbia. The properties are located
south of and along Water Street, N.W. on either side of 35th Street, N.W. Georgetown University
intends to develop a non-motorized boathouse on the land west of 35th Street, N.W., inclusive of a
portion of 35th Street, N.W. upon closure of the roadway.1 The land east of 35th Street, N.W., including
the remainder of 35th Street, N.W. upon closure, will be retained by the District to expand the
Georgetown Waterfront Park.2
1 The transferred lands to the west of 35th Street, N.W. include parcels known for assessment and tax
purposes as Square 1179, Lots 800 (Lot 800 is also known as Lots 812 and 813 in some project documents)
and 805. A portion of the closed 35th Street, N.W. will revert to Lot 800.
2 The transferred lands to the east of 35th Street, N.W. include parcels known for assessment and tax purposes
as Square 1177, Lot 802 and Square 1178, Lots 800, 801, and 802. A portion of the closed 35th Street, N.W.
will revert to Lot 801.

Additional legislation is required to close the segment of 35th Street, N.W. between Water Street, N.W.
and the Potomac River. Upon approval of the roadway closure, a portion will revert to the abutting
property to the west and a portion to the property to the east. The District also requires additional
legislation to declare as surplus and dispose of the newly transferred properties to the west of 35th
Street, N.W. to Georgetown University for the development of the boathouse.
Financial Plan Impact
Funds are sufficient in the proposed revised fiscal year 2026 budget and proposed fiscal year 2027
through fiscal year 2030 budget and financial plan to implement the bill. There are no costs
associated with transferring jurisdiction over the properties from the National Park Service to the
District. The District will declare as surplus and dispose of a portion of 35th Street, N.W. and the
properties to the west to Georgetown University for the development of a boathouse. The District
will retain the remainder of the closed 35th Street, N.W. and the properties to the east to expand the
adjacent Georgetown Waterfront Park. The District does not have funding in its capital
improvements plan to expand the park, but there is also no requirement to do so following the
transfer of jurisdiction. The District will control the land if a future budget elects to fund a park
expansion or other use.
No additional legislation is required to transfer jurisdiction over the properties from the National
Park Service to the District, but additional legislation is required for the District to close 35th Street,
N.W. and to dispose of a portion of 35th Street, N.W. and the properties to the west to Georgetown
University.
