10 To amend the Department of Health Care Finance Establishment Act of 2007 to require the
11 Department of Health Care Finance to develop a single, centralized application process
12 for providers seeking to be credentialed or recredentialed with a managed care
13 organization, to set timeframes and notice requirements for the Department of Health
14 Care Finance to respond to applications and notify managed care organizations, to require
15 transferal of credentialing status if a provider changes employers, and to ensure a
16 complete application is consistent with the standards set forth in Title I of the Health
17 Insurers and Credentialing Intermediaries Uniform Credentialing Form Act of 2022.
18
19 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
20 act may be cited as the “Streamlining Medicaid Credentialing Amendment Act of 2026”.
21 Sec. 2. The Department of Health Care Finance Establishment Act of 2007, effective
22 February 27, 2008 (D.C. Law 17-109; D.C. Official Code § 7-771.01 et seq.), is amended as
23 follows:
24 (a) Section 2 (D.C. Official Code § 7-771.01) is amended by adding a new paragraph (9)
25 to read as follows:
26 “(9) “Provider” shall have the same meaning as provided in section 2(7) of the
27 Prompt Pay Act of 2002, effective July 23, 2002 (D.C. Law 14-176; D.C. Official Code § 31-
28 3131(7)).”.
29 (b) A new section 8e is added to read as follows:

30 “Sec. 8e. Medicaid provider consolidated credentialing.
31 “(a) By no later than January 1, 2028, the Mayor shall develop and establish a single,
32 consolidated, and uniform application process for a provider seeking to be credentialed or
33 recredentialed with a managed care organization (“MCO”) under the District’s Medicaid
34 program.
35 “(b) A provider whose credentials are approved by the Department shall be deemed
36 credentialed with all MCOs, and an MCO may not require a provider to
37 be separately credentialed by that MCO in order to receive reimbursement.
38 “(c) Notwithstanding any other provision of this section, the Department may not require
39 a provider to submit a recredential application solely because the provider’s:
40 “(1) Federal tax identification number changes;
41 “(2) Employer’s federal tax identification number changes; or
42 “(3) Employer changes; provided, that their new employer is a participating
43 provider on one of the District’s MCO provider panels.
44 “(d)(1) The Department shall issue a credentialing or recredentialing determination to the
45 provider within 30 days of receiving a complete application; except, that, if the Department
46 requires additional time beyond the 30 days to render its decision, the Department shall notify
47 the provider and provide an estimated date of determination.
48 “(2) If the Department receives an incomplete application, the Department shall
49 return the application to the provider at the address listed in the application within 10 days of

50 receiving the application and notify the provider on the information missing from their
51 application.
52 “(e) The Department shall provide the approved application to the MCOs concurrently
53 with the Department’s issuance of credentials or recredentials to the provider.
54 “(f) An MCO shall upload provider information on all applicable billing platforms,
55 including any subcontractors and vendors, within 7 business days of receiving a provider’s

60 Credentialing Form Act of 2022, effective April 13, 2022 (D.C. Law 14-96; D.C. Official Code §
61 31-3251 et seq.).
