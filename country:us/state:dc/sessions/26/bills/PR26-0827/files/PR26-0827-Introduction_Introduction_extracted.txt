15 To declare the existence of an emergency, due to congressional review, with respect to the need
16 to amend An Act To create a Department of Corrections in the District of Columbia and
17 the Corrections Oversight Improvement Omnibus Amendment Act of 2022 to designate
18 the Department of Corrections as the District agency to provide a free appropriate public
19 education under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act and District law to
20 certain individuals in its custody and detained in its secure facilities.

24 Department of Corrections Congressional Review Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.

26 in the Custody of the Department of Corrections Emergency Amendment Act of 2026, effective
27 July 30, 2025 (D.C. Act 26-387; 73 DCR 11165) (“emergency act”), which expires on October
28 28, 2026.

30 Custody of the Department of Corrections Temporary Amendment Act of 2026, enacted on _________,
31 2026 (D.C. Act 26-___; __ DCR ___) (“temporary act”), which does not yet have a projected law date.

32 (c) This congressional review emergency legislation is necessary to prevent a gap in the
33 law between the expiration of the emergency act and the effective date of the temporary act.
34 Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
35 enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the Special
36 Education for Young Adults in the Custody of the Department of Corrections Congressional
37 Review Emergency Amendment Act of 2026 be adopted after a single reading.
