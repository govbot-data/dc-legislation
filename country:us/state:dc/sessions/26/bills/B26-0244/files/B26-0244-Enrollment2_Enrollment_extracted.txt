ENROLLED ORIGINAL
AN ACT
_________________________
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
_________________________
To amend Title II of the District of Columbia Comprehensive Bicycle Transportation and Safety
Act of 1984 to require that electric bicycles, electric mobility devices, and traction
batteries manufactured, distributed, sold, or offered for lease or rent in the District meet
certain safety standards; to amend the District of Columbia Traffic Act, 1925 to make
conforming amendments; to amend the Department of Transportation Establishment Act
of 2002 to authorize the Director of the District Department of Transportation to develop
policies for the safe use of electric bicycles, motorized bicycles, and electric mobility
devices; and for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited as the “Micromobility Fire Safety Standards Amendment Act of 2026”.
Sec. 2. Title II of the District of Columbia Comprehensive Bicycle Transportation and
Safety Act of 1984, effective May 1, 2008 (D.C. Law 17-149; D.C. Official Code § 50-1611 et
seq.), is amended by adding a new section 203 to read as follows:
“Sec. 203. Requirements for manufacturer, distribution, sale, lease, rent of micromobility
and traction batteries.
“(a) For the purposes of this section, the term:
“(1) “Certification” means the attestation by a certification body, indicated by a
certification mark on the equipment, device, or product, that the equipment, device, or product
has been evaluated, tested, and found to conform to the standards specified in section 3.
“(2) “Certification body” means an independent third-party organization
providing certification for micromobility devices that:
“(A) Is recognized by the U.S. Department of Labor’s Occupational Safety
and Health Administration (“OSHA”) as a Nationally Recognized Testing Laboratory; or
“(B) Has received ISO/IEC 17065 accreditation from an independent
accreditation body that is a member of the Global Accreditation Cooperation Incorporated, or
any successor organization.
“(3) “Certification mark” means a mark of conformity owned by a certification
body and registered with the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office that is visible and affixed to a
product that has received certification.

“(4) “Electric bicycle” shall have the same meaning as provided in section 2(6A)
of the District of Columbia Traffic Act, 1925, approved March 3, 1925 (43 Stat. 1119; D.C.
Official Code § 50-2201.02(6A)).
“(5) “Electric mobility device” shall have the same meaning as provided in
section 2(6B) of the District of Columbia Traffic Act, 1925, approved March 3, 1925 (43 Stat.
1119; D.C. Official Code § 50-2201.02(6B)).
“(6) “Micromobility device” means an electric bicycle or electric mobility device.
“(7) “Nationally Recognized Testing Laboratory” means an organization that
meets the qualifications provided in 29 C.F.R. § 1910.7(b) and is recognized as a Nationally
Recognized Testing Laboratory by the Occupational Safety and Health Administration.
“(8) “SFD operating company” shall have the same meaning as provided in
section 2(14C) of the District of Columbia Traffic Act, 1925, approved March 3, 1925 (43 Stat.
1119; D.C. Official Code § 50-2201.02(14C)).
“(9) “Traction battery” means a rechargeable battery used to power the electric
motors of a micromobility device.
“(b) All micromobility devices, and traction batteries for such devices, that are
manufactured, distributed, sold, or offered for lease or rent in the District shall meet the
following safety standards:
“(1) Electric bicycles shall be evaluated, tested, and certified to UL 2849 or EN
15194 and bear the certification mark, except, that for 6 months following the effective date of
the Micromobility Fire Safety Standards Amendment Act of 2026, passed on 2nd reading on
September 22, 2026 (Enrolled version of Bill 26-244), an SFD operating company may satisfy
the requirements of this paragraph by evaluating, testing, and certifying any electric bicycles
offered for rental or lease to UL 2272;
“(2) Electric mobility devices shall be evaluated, tested, and certified to UL 2272
and bear the certification mark; and
“(3) Traction batteries for use in micromobility devices shall be evaluated, tested,
and certified to UL 2271 or EN 50604 and bear the certification mark.
“(c) Notwithstanding subsection (b) of this section, a micromobility device or traction
batteries may not display a certification mark if:
“(1)(A) The micromobility device or the traction battery is offered for rental or
lease by an SFD operating company or by the Capital Bikeshare program; and
“(B) The SFD operating company or the Capital Bikeshare program has
provided documentation to DDOT establishing that the electric bicycle, electric mobility device,
or traction battery meets the applicable safety standard as described in subsection (b) of this
section; or
“(2) The micromobility device is being offered for a secondhand sale.
“(d) Evaluation, testing, and certification of any standard listed in subsection (b) of this
section shall be performed by a certification body.”.

Sec. 3. Section 2 of the District of Columbia Traffic Act, 1925, approved March 3, 1925
(43 Stat. 1119; D.C. Official Code § 50-2201.02), is amended as follows:
(a) Paragraph (6A) is redesignated as paragraph (6B).
(b) A new paragraph (6A) is added to read as follows:
“(6A) Electric bicycle” means a vehicle with:
“(A) A post-mounted seat or saddle;
“(B) Two or 3 wheels that are in contact with the ground;
“(C) Fully operative pedals for human propulsion; and
“(D) One or more electric motors to either assist the rider when pedaling
or provide motive power to the wheels when the rider is not pedaling.”.
(c) Paragraph (6B)(A) is amended to read as follows:
“(6B)(A) “Electric mobility device” means a mobility device with a rechargeable
electric drive train that propels the rider, and which may be provided with a handle for grasping
while riding, regardless of whether the device is self-balancing or if the rider may or may not be
seated.”.
(d) Paragraph (11) is amended to read as follows:
“(11)(A) “Motor vehicle” means any vehicle propelled by internal combustion
engines, electricity, or steam.
“(B) The term “motor vehicle” shall not include motorized bicycles,
traction engines, road rollers, vehicles propelled only upon rails or tracks, electric mobility
devices, personal mobility devices, and a battery-operated wheelchair when operated by a person
with a disability.”.
Sec 4. Section 4(a)(1)(K) of the Department of Transportation Establishment Act of 2002,
effective May 21, 2002 (D.C. Law 14-137; D.C. Official Code § 50-921.04(a)(1)(k)), is amended
to read as follows:
“(K) Develop policies and programs to encourage and provide for the safe
use of bicycles, electric bicycles, motorized bicycles, and electric mobility devices, as those
terms are defined in section 2 of the District of Columbia Traffic Act of 1925, approved March 3,
1925 (43 Stat. 1119; D.C. Official Code § 50-2201.02), for recreation, commercial use, and
work-related travel, including planning, developing, operating, and regulating a Bicycle Sharing
program, and administering the Bicycle Sharing Fund established by section 9h to fund a Bicycle
Sharing program;”.
Sec. 5. Subsection 3399.1 of Title 24 of the District of Columbia Municipal Regulations
(24 DCMR § 3399.1) is amended by striking the phrase “Electric mobility device – A device
weighing less than seventy-five (75) pounds that has an electric motor, is solely powered by the
electric motor or human power, is designed to transport only one (1) person in a standing or
seated position, where the rider is not enclosed, and is no greater than twenty-four (24) inches
wide and fifty-five (55) inches long, but does not include a motorized bicycle, personal mobility

Sec. 8. Effective date.

24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)).
