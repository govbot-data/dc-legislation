19 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That
20 this resolution may be cited as the "New Communities Initiative Bond Issuance
21 Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026".
22 Sec. 2. (a) The New Communities Initiative ("NCI") is a District government
23 program designed to revitalize distressed subsidized housing and redevelop
24 neighborhoods into vibrant mixed-income communities.
25 (b) The New Communities Initiative includes four District neighborhoods: Barry
26 Farm in Ward 8; Lincoln Heights -Richardson Dwellings in Ward 7; Northwest One in
27 Ward 6; and Park Morton in Ward 1, which includes the Bruce Monroe redevelopment.
28 (c) The New Communities Initiative is funded through public bonds that allows
29 the District to leverage funding for development projects. The District's approved Fiscal
30 Years 2027-2032 Capital Improvement Plan includes approximately $318 million in bond
31 financing for Barry Farm, Northwest One, and the Bruce Monroe development. This

32 represents the estimated amount of additional funding needed to complete development
33 in all the NCI Communities, while meeting NCI’s core principles.
34 (d) Before the NCI bonds may be issued, however, separate bond approval
35 legislation is required to be adopted by the Council.
36 (e) Emergency action to approve the issuance of the bonds is necessary for the
37 District to have the authority to advance work on the New Communities Initiative and to
38 ensure that the District can issue bonds and notes in a timely manner and take advantage
39 of favorable market conditions to provide funding for or to reimburse the District for
40 funds already expended on projects approved and undertaken as part of the New
41 Communities Initiative.
42 Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
43 enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
44 New Communities Initiative Bond Issuance Emergency Approval Resolution of 2026 be
45 adopted on an emergency basis.
46 Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
