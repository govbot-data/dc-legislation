26 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
27 __________
28
29 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
30 __________
31
32 To honor and recognize the Bernice Fonteneau Senior Wellness Center of Ward 1 for
33 being a vital community resource since 2011, dedicated to enhancing the health, well-being,
34 independence, and quality of life of adults aged 60 and above throughout the District of
35 Columbia.
36
37 WHEREAS, September is Healthy Aging Month and this year’s theme celebrates the
38 value of being curious throughout our lives by staying connected with others, staying active, and
39 trying new things;
40
41 WHEREAS, we in the District of Columbia are fortunate to have in place Senior
42 Wellness Centers that actively involve any resident over the age of 60 who wishes to participate
43 or enjoy the services offered by the Centers;
44

45 WHEREAS, the Senior Wellness Centers of the District of Columbia have served as a
46 vital community resource since 1985 and are dedicated to enhancing the health, well-being,
47 independence, and quality of life of seniors throughout the District of Columbia;
48
49 WHEREAS, these centers provide opportunities for lifelong learning, physical fitness,
50 nutrition education, health promotion, disease prevention, social engagement, and cultural
51 enrichment;
52
53 WHEREAS, the Senior Wellness Centers foster meaningful connections among
54 residents, helping to reduce isolation, strengthen community bonds, and promote active and
55 healthy aging;
56
57 WHEREAS, the dedicated staff, volunteers, community partners, and supporters of the
58 Senior Wellness Centers work tirelessly to provide programs and services that empower older
59 adults to live with dignity, purpose and independence;
60
61 WHEREAS, the Senior Wellness Centers reflect the District of Columbia’s commitment
62 to honoring and supporting its senior residents, recognizing their contributions, wisdom, and
63 continued engagement in civic and community life;
64
65 WHEREAS, the success of the Senior Wellness Centers is a testament to the vision,
66 leadership, and dedication of those who have championed services for older adults in DC;
67
68 WHEREAS, it is with pride that we extend our congratulations to the Bernice Fonteneau
69 Senior Wellness Center for fifteen remarkable years of providing a place where seniors in Ward
70 1 can gather;
71
72 WHEREAS, the Bernice Fonteneau Center is full of warmth, friendship, love, and
73 activity; and
74
75 WHEREAS, the center continuously embodies the vision of their namesake, the
76 trailblazing Bernice Fonteneau whose advocacy for older adults helped shape senior services in
77 the District.
78
79 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
80 resolution may be cited as the “Bernice Fonteneau Senior Wellness Center Recognition
81 Resolution of 2026”.
82
83 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the Bernice Fonteneau Senior
84 Wellness Center for fifteen years of excellence as a community hub, a source of fellowship, and
85 a place where experience is honored and lives are enriched, and extends its best wishes that the
86 center’s mission of empowerment, inclusion, and opportunity for seniors continues to flourish for
87 many years to come.
88
89 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
90 the District of Columbia Register.
