To amend, on atemporarybasis,Title47ofthe DistrictofColumbia Official Codeto clarifythe

BE ITENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICTOF COLUMBIA, Thatthis
act may be cited as the “Peer-to-Peer Car Sharing Rental Vehicle Sales Tax Clarification
‘TemporaryAmendmentAct of2026”.
Sec, 2. Title47ofthe DistrictofColumbia Official Code is amended as follows:
(a) Section 47-2002(a)(4B) is amended to read as follows:
“(4B) The rateoftax on the gross receipts from the sale ofor charges forthe
rental orleasingofrental vehicles and utility trailers as defined in § 50-1505.01 shall be:
“(A) 9.25% beginningOctober 1, 2018, through September 30,2026; and
“(B) 11% beginning on October 1, 2026, and continuing thereafier.”.
(b) Section 47-2202(a)(3B) is amended to read as follows:
“(3B) The rateoftaxonthe gross receipts from the saleofor charges forthe
rental or leasing ofrental vehicles and utility trailers as defined in § 50-1505.01 shall be:
“(A) 9.25%beginningOctober 1, 2018,through September 30, 2026; and.
“(B) 11% beginning on October 1, 2026, and continuing thereafier.”.
Sec. 3. Applicability.
This actshall apply as ofOctober 1, 2026.
