ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-473
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
July 14, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve Contract No.
DCKA-2026-C-0900 with Fort Myer Construction Corporation, and to authorize payment
for the goods and services received under the contract.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Contract No. DCKA-2026-C-0900 with Fort Myer Construction
Corporation Approval and Payment Authorization Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve Contract No. DCKA-2026-C-
0900 with Fort Myer Construction Corporation to provide emergency snow removal equipment
and operators, and to authorize payment for the goods and services received under the contract.
(b) The Office of Contracting and Procurement, on behalf of the District Department of
Transportation, executed emergency Contract No. DCKA-2026-C-0900 in the not-to-exceed
amount of $8,842,541.25 for the period from January 27, 2026, through February 19, 2026.
(c) Council approval is necessary because the contract is more than $1 million during a
12-month period.
(d) Council approval is necessary to pay for these vital services. Without Council
approval, Fort Myer Construction Corporation cannot be paid for goods and services provided in
excess of $1 million for the period from January 27, 2026, through February 19, 2026.
(e) It is also necessary for the legislation to state that the Council approves the contract
notwithstanding the requirements of 27 D.C.M.R. § 2425.7 because the letter contract was
approved at 100% of the anticipated overall price ceiling.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Contract No. DCKA-2026-C-0900 with Fort Myer Construction Corporation Approval and
Payment Authorization Emergency Act of 2026 be adopted after a single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
