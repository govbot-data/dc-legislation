10 To enact the Uniform Unregulated Child Custody Transfer Act, to regulate
11 the transfer of the custody of a child by a parent, guardian, or custodian to someone
12 beyond family members or other specified categories of individuals; to prohibit the use of
13 advertising or solicitation to identify a prospective transferee of the child; to provide the
14 Child and Family Services Agency and Attorney General for the District of Columbia with
15 authority to investigate violations of the act; to provide criminal penalties for violating the
16 act, prosecutable by the Attorney General; and for other purposes.
17
18 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
19 act may be cited as the “Uniform Unregulated Child Custody Transfer Act of 2025”.

44 In this chapter:

52 “(5) “Intercountry adoption” means an adoption or placement for adoption of a child who

69 “Part 2. Prohibition of Unregulated Custody Transfer.
70 “§ 16-5702.01 Definitions.
71 “In this part:
72 “(1) “Custodian” means a custodian under other District law.
73 “(2) “Guardian” means a person recognized as a guardian under District Law.
74 “(3) “Intermediary” means a person that assists or facilitates a transfer of custody of a
75 child, whether or not for compensation.
76 “§ 16-5702.02. Applicability
77 “This part does not apply to a transfer of custody of a child by a parent, guardian, or
78 custodian of the child to:
79 “(1) A parent of the child”
80 “(2) A stepparent of the child;
81 “(3) An adult who is related to the child by blood, marriage, or adoption”
82 “(4) An adult who, at the time of the transfer, had a close relationship with the child or
83 the parent, guardian, or custodian of the child for a substantial period, and whom the parent,
84 custodian, or guardian reasonably believes, at the time of the transfer, to be a fit custodian of the
85 child;

88 “§16-5702.03. Prohibited custody transfer.
89 “(a) Except as provided in subsection b), a parent, guardian, or custodian of a child or an
90 individual with whom a child had been placed for adoption may not transfer custody of the child
91 to another person with the intent, at the time of the transfer, to abandon the rights and
92 responsibilities concerning the child.
93 “(b) A parent, guardian, or custodian of a child or an individual with whom a child has
94 been placed for adoption may transfer custody of the child to another person with the intent, at
95 the time of transfer, to abandon the rights and responsibilities concerning the child only through:
96 “(1) Adoption or guardianship;
97 “(2) Judicial award of custody;
98 “(3) Placement by or through a child-placing agency; or
99 “(4) the Newborn Safe Haven Amendment Act of 2010 (D.C. Law 18-158; D.C.
100 Official Code § 4-1451.01 et seq.)
101 “c) A person may not receive custody of a child, or act as an intermediary in a transfer of
102 custody of a child, if the person knows or reasonably should know the transfer violates
103 subsection (a). This prohibition does not apply if the person, as soon as practicable after the
104 transfer, notifies the Child and Family Services Agency, the Metropolitan Police Department, or
105 Attorney General of the District of Columbia of the transfer or takes appropriate action to

107 “(d) Violation of this section is a misdemeanor, punishable by imprisonment for up to six

109 “(e) Violation of subsection (a) of this section is not established solely because a parent,
110 guardian, or custodian that transfers custody of a child does not regain custody.
111 “§ 16-5703.04. Authority and responsibility of Child and Family Services Agency.
112 “(a) If the Child and Family Services Agency has a reasonable basis to believe that a

117 or placed through an intercountry adoption, the Agency shall:
118 “(1) Prepare a report on the welfare and plan for permanent placement of the child; and
119 “(2) Provide a copy to the United States Department of State,
120 “ (c) This chapter does not prevent the Child and Family Services Agency from taking
121 appropriate action under other District law.
