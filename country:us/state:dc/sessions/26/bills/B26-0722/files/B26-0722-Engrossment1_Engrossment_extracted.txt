10 To amend, on a temporary basis, the Language Access Act of 2004 to authorize the Mayor to
11 issue rules to implement the provisions of that act.
12
13 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
14 act may be cited as the “Language Access Rulemaking Authority Temporary Amendment Act of
15 2026”.
16
17 Sec. 2. Section 6 of the Language Access Act of 2004, effective June 19, 2004 (D.C. Law
18 15-167; D.C. Official Code § 2-1935), is amended by adding a new subsection (c) to read as
19 follows:
20 “(c) The Mayor, pursuant to Title I of the District of Columbia Administrative Procedure
21 Act, approved October 21, 1968 (82 Stat. 1204; D.C. Official Code § 2-501 et seq.), shall issue
22 rules to implement the provisions of this act.”.
23 Sec. 3 Applicability.
24 This act shall apply as of June 19, 2004.
25 Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
26 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Chief Financial Officer as the fiscal
27 impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
28 approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
