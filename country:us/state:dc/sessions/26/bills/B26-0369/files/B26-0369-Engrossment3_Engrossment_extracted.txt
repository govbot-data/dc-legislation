9 To amend Title 25 of the District of Columbia Official Code to add definitions for the terms
10 “abutting property”, “noise”, “sidewalk café”, and “summer garden”, to require a licensee
11 to designate the location of a summer garden on its summer garden endorsement
12 application, to amend the requirements for a group of persons and an association to have
13 standing to protest a license, to confer standing to successor Advisory Neighborhood
14 Commissions as a result of redistricting, to update the documents the Alcoholic Beverage
15 and Cannabis Administration is to make available to include a copy of a noise complaint,
16 to clarify that the Alcoholic Beverage and Cannabis Board may limit a licensee’s hours of
17 operation and permitted hours for entertainment, sidewalk café, and summer garden
18 endorsements, to add a new section regarding the requirements for the submission,
19 assessment, compilation, and reporting of noise complaints and to clarify which
20 complaints the Alcoholic Beverage and Cannabis Administration has jurisdiction to
21 enforce, to amend the scope of a noise violation and codify how the Alcoholic Beverage
22 and Cannabis Administration is to assess complaints for a noise violation, to require the
23 Mayor to establish a nightlife noise balance plan, to update and remove expired
24 provisions from the civil penalty rulemaking review process, to establish a separate fine
25 schedule for noise violations, to allow the Alcoholic Beverage and Cannabis Board to
26 suspend a liquor license and place restrictions on the hours of an endorsement for a 4th or
27 subsequent noise violation within a 2-year period, and to establish requirements for
28 licensed establishments that receive first noise violations; and to amend the Harmonious
29 Living Amendment Act of 2024 to expand the term “activity area”, to update the content
30 and timeline for the sound level reduction design guidelines and requirements
31 rulemaking, to update when the owner of a residential property or dwelling unit located
32 in an entertainment area or activity area shall make sound level disclosures, to authorize
33 the Mayor to establish the soundproofing upgrades grant program, and to update the
34 applicability of the act.
35
36 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
37 act may be cited as the “Nightlife Noise Balance Amendment Act of 2026”.
