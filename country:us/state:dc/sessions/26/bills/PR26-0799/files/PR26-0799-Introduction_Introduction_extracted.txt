Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Enclosed for consideration and enactment by the Council of the District of Columbia is an
emergency bill, the “Modification No. M003 to Contract No. DCRL-2026-C-0024 with
Crawford Consulting and Mental Health Service, Inc. Approval and Payment Authorization
Emergency Act of 2026” and the accompanying emergency declaration resolution in the not-to-
exceed amount of $1,774,850.84
The legislation will approve modification M003 to Contract No. DCRL-2026-C-0024 with
Crawford Consulting and Mental Health Service, Inc., for Community-Based Residential and
Social Services. In addition, the legislation will approve payment for the goods and services
received and to be received under the contract and the modification.
My administration is available to discuss any questions you may have regarding the proposed
contract modification. In order to facilitate a response to any questions you may have, please
have your staff contact Ebony Terrell, Agency Chief Contracting Officer, Child and Family
Services Agency, at (202) 724-7415.
I urge the Council to take prompt and favorable action on the enclosed legislation.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowser
