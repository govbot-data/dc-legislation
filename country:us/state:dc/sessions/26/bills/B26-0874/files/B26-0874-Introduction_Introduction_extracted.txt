Dear Secretary Howard:
Today, I am introducing the “Matthews Court Designation Act of 2026”. Please find enclosed a
signed copy of the legislation.
The Matthews family, residing in an alley accessed off the 1400 block of G Street, SE, has been a part
of the Capitol Hill community for generations, and members of the Matthews family helped build the
infrastructure that made the District what it is today. The family descends from American Freedmen,
who are the descendants of emancipated people in the United States. Members of the Matthews
family have served as a mason on the Pentagon construction project during World War II, cable
splicer for C&P Telephone, manager of one of the District’s largest 24-hour security contracts for
federal agencies, and firefighter responding to the September 11, 2001 attack on the Pentagon. The
Matthews family has contributed to building, protecting, and serving the District through
construction, telecommunications, public safety, security, civic engagement, and community service.
This legislation would officially designate a portion of the public alley system in Square 1063 abutting
Lots 85, 86, and 817 as “Matthews Court”. This designation would recognize a multigenerational
family tradition of service to the District, the region, and the country. Advisory Neighborhood
Commission 6B voted unanimously in support of this designation on June 9, 2026.
Please feel free to reach out to me or my Legislative Director, Antonio Nunes, at
anunes@dccouncil.gov, with any questions or for additional information.
Sincerely,
Councilmember Charles Allen, Ward 6
Chair, Committee on Transportation & the Environment
