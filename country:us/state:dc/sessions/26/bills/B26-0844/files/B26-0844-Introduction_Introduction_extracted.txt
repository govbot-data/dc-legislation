Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Enclosed for consideration and adoption by the Council of the District of Columbia is a bill
entitled “Nationals Park Financing Act of 2026”.
This bill authorizes the issuance of one or more series of bonds in an aggregate principal amount
not to exceed $410 million or such lesser amount as necessary in order to fund the financing,
refinancing, or reimbursing of development costs incurred for the renovation of Nationals Park.
These forward-looking improvements will transform the stadium into a vibrant, state-of-the-art
venue that competes with newer facilities. With the Nationals’ lease going through 2059, the
project reflects a long-term commitment to creating an exceptional, modern home for fans and
the community for decades to come.
I urge the Council to take prompt and favorable action on the enclosed measure.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowser

Conclusion
Funds are sufficient in the revised fiscal year 2026 budget and the fiscal year 2027 through fiscal year
2030 budget and financial plan to implement the bill. The bill authorizes the Mayor to issue up to
$410 million of bonds to support upgrades at Nationals Park. The District will begin debt service
payments in fiscal year 2028 and the expected debt service payments are captured in the District’s
debt capacity in the fiscal year 2027 through fiscal year 2030 budget and financial plan.
Background
The Washington Nationals major league baseball team plays in Nationals Park, which was built by
the District and is owned by Events DC. The Ballpark Preservation and Improvement Fund1 (Fund)
was established to provide funding to the Washington Convention and Sports Authority (Events DC)
to pay for the District’s obligations under the ballpark lease for the maintenance, repairs, and capital
improvements of the ballpark.
The bill authorizes the Mayor to issue one or more series of bonds that aggregate to no more than
$410 million to support upgrades at Nationals Park. The Mayor should not issue any more than is
needed to generate $380 million in net proceeds2 for the development costs of the project. The Mayor

support the upgrades at Nationals Park. The Chief Financial Officer does not expect to issue the bonds
until late fiscal year 2027, with the first debt payments due in fiscal year 2028.
