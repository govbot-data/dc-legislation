ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-474
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
July 14, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve Contract No.
DCKA-2026-C-0901 with Strittmatter Metro, LLC, and Modification Nos. M001 and
M002 thereto, and to authorize payment for the goods and services received under the
contract and modifications.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Contract No. DCKA-2026-C-0901 with Strittmatter Metro, LLC
Approval and Payment Authorization Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve Contract No. DCKA-2026-C-
0901 with Strittmatter Metro, LLC, and to authorize payment for the goods and services received
under the contract.
(b) On January 23, 2026, the Mayor issued Mayor’s Order 2026-005, declaring a public
emergency in response to the significant snow and ice event that was forecasted for the
Washington metropolitan area.
(c) On January 27, 2026, the Office of Contracting and Procurement (”OCP”), on behalf
of the District Department of Transportation, executed emergency letter contract Contract No.
DCKA-2026-C-0901 with Strittmatter Metro, LLC to provide emergency snow removal
equipment and operators in the not-to-exceed amount of $1.6 million.
(d) On February 12, 2026, by Modification No. M001, OCP increased the not-to-exceed
amount by $1.6 million to a total not-to-exceed amount of $3.2 million.
(e) On April 10, 2026, by Modification No. M002, OCP and Strittmatter Metro, LLC
acknowledged that Strittmatter Metro, LLC had been paid $3.2 million under Contract No.
DCKA-2026-C-0901.
(f) Contract No. DCKA-2026-C-0901 expired by its own terms after 90 days.
(g) Council approval is necessary because the contract is more than $1 million during a
12-month period.
(h) Council approval is necessary to pay for these vital services. Without Council
approval, Strittmatter Metro, LLC cannot be paid for goods and services provided in excess of $1
million for the 90-day period.

enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Contract No. DCKA-2026-C-0901 Strittmatter Metro, LLC Approval and Payment

Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
