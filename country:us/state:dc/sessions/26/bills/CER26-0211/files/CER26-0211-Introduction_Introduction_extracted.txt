35 To recognize the contributions of the Kappa Psi chapter of the Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc. has
36 made to the District of Columbia.
37
38 WHEREAS, the Kappa Psi chapter was founded on November 19, 1926, at Howard
39 University for students enrolled in the School of Law, College of Medicine and the College of
40 Dentistry;
41 WHEREAS, the Kappa Psi chapter was originally established for professional students at
42 Howard University focused on applying the fraternity’s four cardinal principles of manhood,

43 scholarship, perseverance and uplift through initiatives and projects in support of the District of
44 Columbia;
45 WHEREAS, the Kappa Psi chapter was founded by Dr. William Montague Cobb, the
46 first African American to earn a Ph.D. in physical anthropology, a long-time professor of
47 anatomy at the Howard University College of Medicine, and president of the NAACP;
48 WHEREAS, the Kappa Psi chapter has been a prestigious training ground for
49 accomplished leaders in academia, law, medicine, politics and federal service due to its historical
50 ties to Howard University’s professional schools;
51
52 WHEREAS, the Kappa Psi chapter has served as a home chapter for undergraduate and
53 professional brothers who attended Georgetown University, George Washington University,
54 American University and Catholic University;
55 WHEREAS, the Kappa Psi chapter has acted as an incubator for Omega Psi Phi’s growth
56 across the DC and Maryland region;
57
58 WHEREAS, it has led to the establishment of three undergraduate chapters: the Omicron
59 Gamma chapter at the University of the District of Columbia; the Epsilon Sigma chapter at
60 Bowie State University and the Delta Theta chapter established at Southeastern University,
61 which now covers Georgetown University and George Washington University;
62 WHEREAS, the Kappa Psi chapter is highly active in the District of Columbia
63 community, managing prominent social action initiatives including the DC Rhinos Mentoring
64 program to promote STEM education and life skills for male youth;
65 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this resolution
66 may be cited as the “Kappa Psi Chapter Centennial Ceremonial Recognition Resolution of
67 2026”.
68
69 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors the Kapps Psi
70 chapter of the Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc. for their century of leadership and service in support
71 of the District of Columbia community.
72
73 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in the
74 District of Columbia Register.
