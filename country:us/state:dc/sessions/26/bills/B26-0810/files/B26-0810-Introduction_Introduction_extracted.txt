Dear Secretary Howard:
Today, I am introducing the “Veola Jackson Way Designation Act of 2026”. Please find enclosed a
signed copy of the legislation.
Veola Jackson was a beloved educator and administrator in the District who later became the first
principal of the Capitol Hill Cluster School, now comprised of Peabody Elementary School, Watkins
Elementary School, and Stuart-Hobson Middle School. Principal Jackson first became principal of
Peabody Elementary School in 1973 and later became principal of then-Hobson Middle School before
becoming principal of all three schools. She worked tirelessly to integrate public education on Capitol
Hill by making its schools representative of the diversity of the community’s population and, in her
own words, “good for everybody”. It was through her vision that the Capitol Hill Cluster School was
formed. She was known as a dynamic educator and one who would seek out challenges so she could
conquer them. Her legacy is the unique, diverse, and excellent education that our Capitol Hill Cluster
School provides daily.
This legislation would symbolically designate C Street, NE, between 4th Street, NE, and 6th Street,
NE, as “Veola Jackson Way”. I would note that Advisory Neighborhood Commission 6C voted
unanimously in support of this symbolic designation on September 9, 2026.
Please feel free to reach out to me or my Legislative Director, Antonio Nunes, with any questions or
for additional information.
Sincerely,
Councilmember Charles Allen, Ward 6
Chairperson, Committee on Transportation & the Environment
