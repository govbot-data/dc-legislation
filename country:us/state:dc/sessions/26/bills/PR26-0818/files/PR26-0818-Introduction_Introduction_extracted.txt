16 To declare the existence of an emergency, due to congressional review, with respect to
17 the need to amend he Green Building Act of 2006 to exempt certain District owned and funded
18 residential and nonresidential projects from complying with net-zero-energy standards and to
19 modify net-zero-energy standard compliance requirements for nonresidential and residential
20 projects; to amend section 2 of the Clean Energy DC Building Code Amendment Act of 2022 to
21 modify the definition of the net-zero-energy standard and the types of projects to which the net-
22 zero-energy standard applies; and for other purposes.
23
24 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
25 resolution may be cited as the “Net Zero Continuity Congressional Review Emergency
26 Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
27 Sec. 2. (a) On July 14, 2026, the Council adopted the Net Zero Continuity Emergency
28 Amendment Act of 2026, effective July 30, 2026 (D.C. Act 26-0394; 73 DCR 011187), which
29 will expire on October 28, 2026.
30 (b) On September 22, 2026, the Council adopted the Net Zero Continuity Temporary
31 Amendment Act of 2026, passed on 2nd reading on September 22, 2026 (Enrolled version of Bill
32 26-768), (“temporary act”), which has not completed congressional review.
33 (c) Identical emergency legislation is necessary to prevent a gap in the law between the
34 expiration of the emergency act and the effective date of the temporary act.
1

35 Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
36 enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the Net
37 Zero Continuity Congressional Review Emergency Amendment Act of 2026 be adopted after a
38 single reading.
39 Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
