A PROPOSED RESOLUTION
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICTOF COLUMBIA.
To establishthe date by which the Mayor shall submit to the Council the proposed budget forthe
government ofthe District ofColumbia for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2028, to
identify information and documentation to be submitted to the Council with the proposed
budget for the governmentofthe District ofColumbia forthe fiscal year ending
September 30, 2028, and to require the Mayorto submit performance plans and
accountability reports pursuant to Title XIV-Aofthe District ofColumbiaGovernment
Comprehensive Merit Personnel Act of 1978.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, Thatthis
resolution may be cited as the “Fiscal Year 2028 Budget Submission Requirements Resolution of
2026”.
