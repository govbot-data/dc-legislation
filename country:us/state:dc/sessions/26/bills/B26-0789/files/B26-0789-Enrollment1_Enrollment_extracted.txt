ENROLLED ORIGINAL
AN ACT
_________________________
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
_________________________
To amend, on an emergency basis, section 2a of the Performance Parking Pilot Zone Act of 2008
to require the Department of Small and Local Business Development to award a $1
million dollar grant in Fiscal Year 2027 to the Duke Business Improvement District
Corporation; and to make a conforming amendment.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited as the “Duke District BID Grantmaking Emergency Amendment Act of 2026”.
Sec. 2. Section 2a(c)(4)(A) of the Performance Parking Pilot Zone Act of 2008, effective
September 14, 2011 (D.C. Law 19-21; D.C. Official Code § 50-2531.01(c)(4)(A)), is amended to
read as follows:
“(4)(A) Notwithstanding paragraph (3) of this subsection, within the first quarter
of Fiscal Year 2027, the Department of Small and Local Business Development shall award a
grant in the amount of $1 million from the Fund to support the Duke Business Improvement
District Corporation, d/b/a Duke District BID, to improve conditions for property owners,
businesses, residents, and visitors within the Greater Shaw/U Street area.”.
Sec. 3. Amendatory section 2a(c)(4)(A) in section 6302(c)(3) of the Performance Parking
Zone Fund Modification Amendment Act of 2026, enacted on August 14, 2026 (D.C. Act 26-
418; 73 DCR 11735), is amended to read as follows:
“(4)(A) Notwithstanding paragraph (3) of this subsection, within the first quarter
of Fiscal Year 2027, the Department of Small and Local Business Development shall award a
grant in the amount of $1 million from the Fund to support the Duke Business Improvement
District Corporation, d/b/a Duke District BID, to improve conditions for property owners,
businesses, residents, and visitors within the Greater Shaw/U Street area.”.
Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Budget Director as the fiscal impact
statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved
October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).

Sec. 5. Effective date.
This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), and shall remain in effect for no longer than
90 days, as provided for emergency acts of the Council of the District of Columbia in section
412(a) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 788;
D.C. Official Code § 1-204.12(a)).
