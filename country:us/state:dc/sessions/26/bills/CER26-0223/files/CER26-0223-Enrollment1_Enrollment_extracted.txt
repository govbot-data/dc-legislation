ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-223
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2023
To honor and recognize the Model Cities Senior Wellness Center of Ward 5 for serving as a vital
community resource dedicated to enhancing the health, well-being, independence, and
quality of life of seniors in the District of Columbia.
WHEREAS, September is Healthy Aging Month and this year’s theme celebrates the
value of being curious throughout our lives by staying connected with others, staying active, and
trying new things;
WHEREAS, we in the District of Columbia are fortunate to have in place Senior
Wellness Centers that actively involve any resident over the age of 60 who wishes to participate
or enjoy the services offered by the Centers;
WHEREAS, the Senior Wellness Centers of the District of Columbia have served as a
vital community resource since 1985 and are dedicated to enhancing the health, well-being,
independence, and quality of life of seniors throughout the District of Columbia;
WHEREAS, these Centers provide opportunities for lifelong learning, physical fitness,
nutrition education, health promotion, disease prevention, social engagement, and cultural
enrichment;
WHEREAS, the Senior Wellness Centers foster meaningful connections among
residents, helping to reduce isolation, strengthen community bonds, and promote active and
healthy aging;
WHEREAS, the dedicated staff, volunteers, community partners, and supporters of the
Senior Wellness Centers work tirelessly to provide programs and services that empower older
adults to live with dignity, purpose and independence;

WHEREAS, the Senior Wellness Centers reflect the District of Columbia’s commitment
to honoring and supporting its senior residents, recognizing their contributions, wisdom, and
continued engagement in civic and community life;
WHEREAS, the Model Cities Senior Wellness Center, located at 1901 Evarts Street NE,
has served as an important resource for older adults in the District of Columbia, providing
opportunities to engage in important areas such as recreation, education, social connection, and
health;
WHEREAS, Model Cities is part of the District’s network of senior wellness centers
dedicated to helping older Washingtonians remain involved and active in their surroundings and
connected to their communities;
WHEREAS, the Center provides D.C. residents ages 60 and above with a welcoming
place to participate in programs featuring sessions in areas such as health, arts and crafts,
technology, and education;
WHEREAS, Model Cities further offers seniors the opportunity to embark on field trips
and participate in community meals thus helping to reduce the feeling of social isolation;
WHEREAS, the center has played an especially important role in Ward 5 by providing
residents in that area with accessible programs and services that keep them engaged, active, and
feeling valued; and
WHEREAS, continued investment in Model Cities, including improvements and the
recently added multipurpose space, reflects the District’s commitment to providing welcoming
and effective places where older adults can feel relevant, be active, remain engaged, and stay
involved in the community.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Model Cities Senior Wellness Center Recognition Resolution of
2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Model Cities for its
continuing commitment to building a supportive community and longstanding service to District
seniors, and honors the dedicated staff, partners, volunteers, instructors, and participants who
contribute to the vitality of this important institution.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
