11 To establish a reimbursable detail program for parking enforcement by Department of Public
12 Works officers, and to clarify the authority of special police and campus police to
13 conduct parking enforcement.
14
15 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
16 act may be cited as the “Strategic Parking Enforcement Enhancement Act of 2026”.
17 Sec. 2. Definitions.
18 For the purposes of this act, the term:
19 (1) “Agreement” means a written contract, including provisions for the staffing
20 requirement of the reimbursable details in accordance with subsection (c) of this section, and
21 compensation of DPW by the eligible entity when reimbursable details are requested by eligible
22 entity.
23 (2) “Campus police officer” has the same meaning as provided in D.C. Official
24 Code § 47-2853.207(1).
25 (3) “DPW” means the Department of Public Works.
26 (4) “Eligible entity” means a business, group of businesses, a promoter or
27 organizer of a District-licensed special event, a Business Improvement District, a DC Main
28 Streets Program, and other entities designated by the Mayor.

29 (5) “Reimbursable detail” means an assignment of DPW parking enforcement
30 officers to patrol a designated area for the purpose of enforcing parking violations and managing
31 curbside access.
32 (6) “Special police officer” has the same meaning as provided in D.C. Official
33 Code § 47-2853.207(6).
34 Sec. 3. Reimbursable detail for parking enforcement.
35 (a) An eligible entity may enter into an agreement with DPW to provide for reimbursable
36 details.
37 (b) Subject to adequate staffing and an assessment of its staffing requirements, DPW may
38 staff reimbursable details as requested, provided, that:
39 (1) DPW shall only use parking enforcement officers for this purpose who are
40 overtime and would not otherwise be on duty at the time of the reimbursable detail; and
41 (2) Parking enforcement officers participating in reimbursable details shall
42 enforce parking restrictions impartially according to their own discretion, and shall remain
43 subject solely to the direction of their commanding officers unless expressly stated in the
44 relevant agreement.
45 (c) Pursuant to funding, the Mayor shall establish a program to subsidize eligible entities
46 for a portion of reimbursement costs borne by eligible entities for reimbursable detail for parking
47 enforcement pursuant to this act.
48 Sec. 4. Parking enforcement by special police officers and campus police officers.
49 (a) Except as provided in subsection (b), special police officers and campus police

50 officers may conduct parking enforcement activities, including the issuance of parking citations,
51 regarding vehicles:
52 (1) On property subject to their jurisdiction;
53 (2) In areas of the public right of way bordered on both sides by property subject
54 to their jurisdiction; and
55 (3) In parking spaces in the public right of way that are reserved for persons
56 affiliated with or visiting properties subject to their jurisdiction.
57 (b)(1) Nothing in this section shall be interpreted to supersede any other law that
58 expressly precludes a specific category or organization of special police officers or campus
59 police officers from enforcing parking violations
60 (2) The corporation or person at whose request a special police officer or campus
61 police officer was appointed may direct the special police officer or campus officer not to engage
62 in parking enforcement and curbside management activities, unless directed otherwise by the
63 Mayor.

66 approved October 21, 1968 (82 Stat. 1204; D.C. Official Code § 2-501 et seq.), shall issue rules
67 to implement the provisions of this act. Such rules may include provisions to facilitate campus
68 police and special police’s enforcement of the Fraudulent Vehicle Tag and Parking Enforcement
69 Modernization Amendment Act of 2024, effective May 2, 2025 (D.C. Law 25-325; D.C. Official
70 Code § 50-2431 et seq.), including provisions to address officer training, the issuance and
