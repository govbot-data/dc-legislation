16 To amend, on an emergency basis, due to congressional review, the Green Building Act of 2006 to
17 exempt certain District-owned and -funded residential and nonresidential projects from
18 complying with net-zero-energy standards and to modify net-zero-energy standard
19 compliance requirements for nonresidential and residential projects; to amend section 2 of
20 the Clean Energy DC Building Code Amendment Act of 2022 to modify the definition of
21 the net-zero-energy standard and the types of projects to which the net-zero-energy standard
22 applies; and for other purposes.
23
24 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this act
25 may be cited as the “Net Zero Continuity Congressional Review Emergency Amendment Act of
26 2026”.

111 Sec. 3. Section 2 of the Clean Energy DC Building Code Amendment Act of 2022, effective
112 September 21, 2022 (D.C. Law 24-177; D.C. Official Code § 6-1453.01), is amended as follows:
113 (a) Subsection (a) is amended as follows:
114 (1) A new paragraph (2A) is added to read as follows:
115 “(2A) “Level 3 alteration” shall have the same meaning ascribed to it in the
116 Building Codes.”.
117 (2) Paragraph (3) is amended as follows:
118 (A) Subparagraph (A) is amended by striking the phrase “; and” and inserting a
119 semicolon in its place.
120 (B) Subparagraph (B) is amended to read as follows:
121 “(B) A building obtains energy from renewable energy sources in the amount
122 that would be required by the most recent version of Appendix Z;”.
123 (C) New subparagraphs (C) and (D) are added to read as follows:
124 “(C) Renewable energy shall be generated at the building site wherever
125 feasible; and”.
5

126 “(D) On-site fuel combustion shall not be permitted for the provision of thermal
127 energy to the building except for backup power generation.”.
128 (b) Subsection (b) is amended as follows:
129 “(b)(1) By December 31, 2027, the Mayor, pursuant to pursuant to Title I of the
130 District of Columbia Administrative Procedure Act, approved October 21, 1968 (82 Stat. 1204; D.C.
131 Official Code § 2-501 et seq.), shall issue final regulations requiring all new construction or Level 3
132 alterations to covered buildings to be constructed to a net-zero-energy standard.
133 “(2) By December 31, 2026, the Mayor, pursuant to Title I of the District of Columbia
134 Administrative Procedure Act, approved October 21, 1968 (82 Stat. 1204; D.C. Official Code § 2-501
135 et seq.), shall issue final regulations prohibiting the use of on-site fuel combustion for the provision of
136 thermal energy for all new construction of covered buildings and additions greater than 10,000 square
137 feet to covered buildings.
138 “(3) No permit application for new construction of covered buildings or an addition
139 greater than 10,000 square feet to covered buildings submitted after December 31, 2026, shall be
140 approved if the building or addition design provides for the use of on-site fuel combustion for the
141 provision of thermal energy for space heating and water heating.
142 “(4) Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no building permit application for
143 new construction of covered buildings, an addition greater than 10,000 square feet to covered
144 buildings, or Level 3 alteration to covered buildings submitted after December 31, 2026, shall be
145 approved unless the building design is such that the building conserves an amount of energy
146 attributable to building operation that is equal to or greater than the amount that would be conserved if
147 such building complied with the 2024 International Energy Conservation Code.”.
