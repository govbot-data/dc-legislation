Dear Secretary Howard:
Today, I am introducing the “DC Government Employee Home Purchase Support Amendment
Act of 2026”, along with Councilmembers Zachary Parker, Janeese Lewis George, and Wendell
Felder. Please find enclosed a signed copy of the legislation.
The District’s Employer-Assisted Housing Program (“EAHP”), administered by the Department of
Housing and Community Development (“DHCD”), serves as an important recruitment and
retention tool for employees across the District government by making first-time
homeownership possible. It also has the potential to increase the number of District residents
who work for the District government, as housing costs all too often lead District government
employees to leave the District or discourage potential talent from joining. Under EAHP, District
government employees, who are first-time homebuyers, can receive matching down payment
funds of up to $5,000 and a deferred loan of $20,000. EAHP also provides additional assistance
to eligible first responders and educators by providing matching down payment funds of up to
$15,000 and a deferred, recoverable grant of $10,000.
District government employees looking to take advantage of EAHP must follow many of the same
steps as those applying for the Home Purchase Assistance Program (“HPAP”), which begins by
reaching out to a DHCD-funded community-based organization to begin housing counseling and
their application. Whereas the Council has taken recent steps to improve the administrability and
transparency of HPAP, the same is desperately needed with EAHP. EAHP is unique in that it does
not see as much demand as HPAP, which results in excess funds and incentivizes the Executive
to use these excess funds to replenish HPAP. While this action may appear innocuous, it has
affected District government employees already under contract for their first home and leaves
an important recruitment and retention tool for District government employees otherwise
unavailable during the fiscal year. EAHP’s poor administration and transparency should be
improved to incentivize more participation and provide a meaningful path to homeownership.
1

To improve EAHP and ensure that funds are available and quickly dispersed, this legislation would
change the $20,000 deferred loan available to program participants to a $20,000 grant beginning
on October 1, 2027. Changing the $20,000 deferred loan to a $20,000 grant would make the
process straightforward, less time-consuming, and more attractive to District government
employees. Additionally, this bill would require DHCD to maintain and publish a handbook and
online portal to facilitate administration, immediately notify applicants of certain events,
coordinate with public safety and education client agencies on providing an annual information
session to interested employees, and submit an annual report to the Mayor and Council with
data to evaluate EAHP’s efficacy.
Please feel free to reach out to me or my Legislative Director, Antonio Nunes, at
anunes@dccouncil.gov, with any questions or for additional information.
Sincerely,
Councilmember Charles Allen, Ward 6
Chair, Committee on Transportation & the Environment

21 To amend the Government Employer-Assisted Housing Amendment Act of 1999 to modernize
22 and improve the administration and accessibility of the District’s Employer-Assisted
23 Housing Program for District government employees by changing the $20,000 deferred
24 loan available to program participants to a $20,000 grant beginning October 1, 2027,
25 requiring the Department of Housing and Community Development to maintain and
26 publish a handbook and online portal to facilitate administration of the program, creating
27 standard program timelines, coordinating with client agencies on providing an annual
28 informational session, and providing the Mayor and Council with an annual report.
29
30 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
31 act may be cited as the “DC Government Employee Home Purchase Support Amendment Act of
32 2026”.
