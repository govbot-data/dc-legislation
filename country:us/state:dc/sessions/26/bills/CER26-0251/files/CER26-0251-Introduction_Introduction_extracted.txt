9 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION

11 _________

14 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
15
16 __________________
17
18
19 To recognize, honor, and celebrate the contributions Matthew F. Shannon has made to the
20 District of Columbia.
21
22 WHEREAS, Matthew F. Shannon received a Bachelor of Arts in History and
23 Communications from Howard University in 1971 and a Juris Doctor from Howard University in
24 1974;
25
26 WHEREAS, Matthew F. Shannon returned to the District of Columbia in 1976, serving
27 as an Assistant Director with the American Savings and Loan League;
28
29 WHEREAS, in 1977, he entered the private practice of law in the District of Columbia
30 handling civil matters;
31
32 WHEREAS, he entered public service with the District of Columbia government on
33 January 2, 1979, serving as Special Assistant to the Mayor for Labor and Religious Affairs;
34
35 WHEREAS, Matthew F. Shannon was appointed Acting Director of the District of
36 Columbia Department of Labor in March 1979, where he primarily implemented the first
37 Mayor’s Summer Youth Employment Program in which 30,000 youth between the ages of 14
38 and 21 were employed;
39
40 WHEREAS, in 1981, he began serving as the Deputy Director for Program Operations of
41 the D.C. Department of Employment Services;
42

43 WHEREAS, in 1983, Matthew F. Shannon was named the Director of the D.C.
44 Department of Employment Services, a position he held for four years, during which time he
45 successfully lowered the unemployment rate in the District by 1.25%;
46
47 WHEREAS, he returned to the private practice of law in 1986, managing complex trusts
48 and estates;
49
50 WHEREAS, he was appointed to the Board of Directors of the District of Columbia
51 Children Youth Investment Corporation in 2008;
52
53 WHEREAS, throughout his career, Matthew F. Shannon has volunteered on countless
54 boards including the boards of Elizabeth Seaton High School, the D.C. Youth Orchestra
55 Program, the D.C. Apprenticeship Council, Luke C. Moore High School, and WE CAN, an East
56 of the Anacostia Youth Development Organization;
57
58 WHEREAS, he served as Chair of the Ward 7 Democrats and as a member of the D.C.
59 State Committee, where he initiated the hats, coats, and gloved program for youth in Ward 7; and
60
61 WHEREAS, he has received multiple awards over the course of his legal career,
62 including the American Jurisprudence Award for the Study of Administrative Law, Insurance
63 Law, Criminal Procedure, Decedents Estates and Trusts, and Small Business Law.
64
65 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, that this
66 resolution is hereby cited as the “Matthew F. Shannon Recognition Resolution of 2026.”
67
68 Sec.2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes, honors, and celebrates
69 Matthew F. Shannon for his leadership and service in support of the District of Columbia.
70
71 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
72 the District of Columbia Register.
