14 To amend, on an emergency basis, the Block Party Act of 2012 to enable residents hosting a
15 musical performance on their property as part of a Porchfest or other distributed musical
16 festival to apply for block party permits.
17
18 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
19 act may be cited as the “Porchfest Permitting Emergency Amendment Act of 2026”.
20 Sec. 2. The Block Party Act of 2012, effective October 23, 2012 (D.C. Law 19-190, D.C.
21 Official Code § 9-631 et seq.), is amended as follows:
22 (a) Section 2 (D.C. Official Code § 9-631) is amended by adding a new paragraph (5) to
23 read as follows:
24 “(5) “Porchfest” means a distributed neighborhood music festival where
25 musicians perform on a resident’s private property, such as their porch or yard, or any abutting
26 public property, and no fee is charged to participants or attendees.”.
27 (b) Section 3 (D.C. Official Code § 9-632) is amended by adding a new subsection (b-1)
28 to read as follows:
29 “(b-1) A District resident may submit a block party application requesting that a street on
30 which they reside be closed for the purpose of holding a porchfest.”.
31 (c) Section 5(a) (D.C. Official Code § 9-634(a)) is amended to read as follows:
32 “(a) It is the policy of the District to approve block parties, including porchfests.”.
