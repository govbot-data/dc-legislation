11 To amend the District of Columbia Revenue Act of 1937 to authorize the Mayor to design and
12 issue one or more motor vehicle identification tags with a design, identifying words, or
13 emblem that demonstrates support for the return of the Washington Commanders football
14 team to the District, and to direct that the proceeds be used to support the State Athletic
15 Activities, Programs, and Office Fund; and to make a conforming amendment.
16
17 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
18 act may be cited as the “Washington Commanders Motor Vehicle Identification Tags
19 Amendment Act of 2026”.
20 Sec. 2. Title IV of the District of Columbia Revenue Act of 1937, approved August 17,
21 1937 (50 Stat. 679; D.C. Official Code § 50-1501.01 et seq.), is amended as follows:
22 (a) A new section 2p is added to read as follows:
23 “Sec. 2p. Issuance of Washington Commanders motor vehicle identification tags.
24 “(a) The Mayor may design and make available for issue one or more Washington
25 Commanders motor vehicle identification tags to demonstrate support for the Washington
26 Commanders football team.

27 “(b)(1) A resident ordering a Washington Commanders tag shall pay a one-time
28 application fee and a display fee each year thereafter. The application fee shall be $25, and the
29 display fee shall be $20, or such other amounts as the Mayor may establish by rule.
30 “(2) The application and annual display fees shall be deposited into the State
31 Athletic Activities, Programs, and Office Fund, established by section 4033 of the State Athletic
32 Activities, Programs, and Office Fund Act of 2013, effective December 24, 2013 (D.C. Law 20-
33 61; D.C. Official Code § 38–2672).”.
34 (b) Section 3 (D.C. Official Code § 50-1501.03) is amended as follows:
35 (1) Subsection (a)(1) is amended by adding a new subparagraph (T) to read as
36 follows:
37 “(T) Any person ordering a Washington Commanders motor vehicle
38 identification tag shall pay the fees set forth in section 2p(b)(1).”.
39 (2) Subsection (d) is amended as follows:
40 (A) Paragraph (16) is amended by striking the phrase “; and” and inserting a
41 semicolon in its place.
42 (B) Paragraph (17) is amended by striking the period and inserting the phrase “;
43 and” in its place.
44 (C) A new paragraph (18) is added to read as follows:
45 “(18) The fees collected for the Washington Commanders motor vehicle identification
46 tag under section 2p shall be deposited into the State Athletic Activities, Programs, and Office
