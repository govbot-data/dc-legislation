11 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA

13 ____________________________
14
15 To recognize Shanel Wilson for her excellent service as Deputy Chief of Staff to Councilmember
16 Robert C. White, Jr., including staff management, communications, constituent services,
17 and community events.
18 WHEREAS, Shanel Wilson served as Deputy Chief of Staff and managed staff and office
19 operations in support of the Councilmember’s work;
20 WHEREAS, she brought communications skills and an established network of media and
21 communications contacts to the office;
22 WHEREAS, she supported constituent services by helping the office respond to
23 residents’ concerns and requests for assistance; and
24 WHEREAS, she helped plan community events that created opportunities for residents to
25 engage with the Councilmember’s office.
26 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
27 resolution may be cited as the “Shanel Wilson Recognition Resolution of 2026.”
28 Sec. 2. The Council recognizes Shanel Wilson for her service and contributions in staff
29 management, communications, constituent services, and community engagement.

30 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
31 the District of Columbia Register.
