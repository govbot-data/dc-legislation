12 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA

15
16 To recognize Jasmine Joyner for her exceptional service as Senior Executive Assistant and
17 Constituent Services Coordinator to Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr., and for her
18 tireless commitment to helping District residents receive the attention and assistance they
19 deserve.
20 WHEREAS, Jasmine Joyner serves as Executive Assistant and Senior Constituent
21 Services Coordinator in the office of Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr., combining the
22 responsibilities of executive support, public engagement, and direct assistance to residents;
23 WHEREAS, Jasmine Joyner has helped make the Councilmember’s office accessible to
24 neighbors seeking guidance, an answer from a District agency, or assistance navigating a
25 difficult and often unfamiliar government process;
26 WHEREAS, Jasmine Joyner approaches constituent concerns with patience, empathy,
27 and persistence, recognizing that behind every request for help is a person or family whose well-
28 being matters;
29 WHEREAS, she has elevated serious housing and building-condition concerns,
30 communicated regularly with District agencies, and worked to secure attention to problems
31 affecting tenants’ safety, health, and quality of life;
32 WHEREAS, while carrying out the complex work of managing executive schedules and
33 coordinating office priorities, Jasmine Joyner has remained attentive to the immediate needs of
34 residents and the many details that make responsive public service possible;

35 WHEREAS, Jasmine Joyner has shown that constituent advocacy often depends on the
36 willingness to ask one more question, make one more call, and continue seeking answers when a
37 resident encounters bureaucratic obstacles;
38 WHEREAS, her work has required discretion and good judgment in matters that are
39 personal, urgent, and consequential to the residents who reach out to their elected
40 representatives;
41 WHEREAS, Jasmine Joyner has also helped coordinate the meetings, events, and daily
42 operations that give the Councilmember opportunities to hear directly from District residents and
43 community leaders;
44 WHEREAS, her willingness to follow up, seek answers, and help residents and team
45 members feel heard reflects the essential role that effective constituent services play in a
46 representative democracy; and
47 WHEREAS, Jasmine Joyner’s compassion, reliability, and dedication have strengthened
48 the connection between the Councilmember’s office and the people it serves, leaving a
49 meaningful impact that reaches far beyond any single case or meeting.
50 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
51 resolution may be cited as the “Jasmine Joyner Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
52 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Jasmine Joyner for her
53 outstanding constituent advocacy, professionalism, and commitment to serving District residents
54 with dignity, empathy, and care.
55 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
56 the District of Columbia Register.
