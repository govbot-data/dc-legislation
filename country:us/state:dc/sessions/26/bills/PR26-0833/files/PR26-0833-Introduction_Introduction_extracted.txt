Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Enclosed for consideration and adoption by the Council of the District of Columbia is a proposed
resolution titled “Volunteers of America Chesapeake & Carolinas Inc. Revenue Bonds Project
Approval Resolution of 2026”.
The resolution authorizes the issuance, sale, and delivery of tax-exempt revenue bonds, notes, or
other obligations in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed $47,075,000. The proceeds of
the bonds will be used to finance, refinance, or reimburse costs incurred by Volunteers of
America Chesapeake & Carolinas Inc in connection with the acquisition, rehabilitation, and
construction of new facilities to support the organization’s work in the region.
In accordance with section 490 of the Home Rule Act, it has been determined that the bonds,
when, as, and if issued, shall be without recourse to the District. The bonds shall not be general
obligations of the District; shall not be a pledge of or involve the full faith and credit or the
taxing power of the District; shall not constitute a debt of the District; and shall not constitute a
lending of public credit for a private undertaking as prohibited in section 602(a)(2) of the Home
Rule Act. The bonds shall not give rise to any pecuniary liability of the District, and the District
shall have no obligation with respect to the purchase of the bonds.
I urge the Council to take prompt and favorable action on the enclosed measure.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowser

FACT SHEET
Volunteers of America Chesapeake and Carolinas Inc. (VOACC) has requested that the District
issue up to $47,075,000 of tax-exempt revenue bonds for three related projects located in
Washington, DC, Baltimore, MD, and Yorktown, VA.
The Applicant
Volunteers of America Chesapeake & Carolinas is an affiliate of Volunteers of America, a
national nonprofit, faith-based organization dedicated to helping people rebuild their lives and
reach their full potential. As one of the largest affiliates, VOACC strategically designs and
locally delivers evidence-based programs tailored to its communities' most pressing needs. VOA
puts faith into action by providing compassionate human services that empower individuals and
strengthen communities. VOA aspires to create a world in which every person lives a life of
emotional, physical, and spiritual well-being, filled with hope.
The name Volunteers of America was chosen by its founders, Ballington and Maud Booth, when
the organization was founded over 130 years ago. The name resembled the organization, which
was comprised of people voluntarily choosing to help others. Back in 1896, a volunteer was
anyone who was committed to a mission or cause. Today, VOA is a nonprofit organization
comprised of over 15,000 paid professional case managers, housing experts, administrators,
clinicians, caregivers, psychologists, and leaders across the country. The staff still carries on the
Booth’s legacy of ministry of service by helping those in need rebuild their lives and reach their
full potential.
VOACC has been promoting a healthy community for all, for over 130 years, by aligning health,
housing, and human services. VOACC actively pursues health equity by filling the gap in
housing and care for underserved populations, including those who are homeless, low-income,
underinsured or uninsured, justice-involved, and veterans. Lines of service include homeless
services, supportive housing, behavioral health, substance use recovery,
intellectual/developmental disability services, veteran services, community re-entry, and
workforce development.
Proposed Project
Volunteers of America Chesapeake and Carolinas Inc. (VOACC) is seeking $47,075,000 in new
money for the purchase, renovation, and new construction of several properties owned by VOA
located in 3 different cities. VOACC is looking for the DC IRB to host approval for 1 project
within the District and 2 projects outside, 1 being in Baltimore, MD and the other in Yorktown,
VA. VOACC is looking to purchase a property in DC located at 2006 MLK Jr. Ave SE,
Washington, DC 20020, renovate a residential reentry center located in Baltimore at 5000 E.
Monument St, Baltimore MD, and construct and develop a family focused recovery center in
Yorktown located at 1701 George Washington Memorial Highway, Yorktown, VA 23693.

and has preliminarily determined that the applicant is a 501(c)(3) organization, and the project

Rule Act.
Based upon the information outlined in the application, the Revenue Bond Program staff has
determined that the proposed project complies with the criteria for approval of a proposed
financing transaction through the District’s Revenue Bond Program and will assist the Borrower
in furthering its organizational mission.
