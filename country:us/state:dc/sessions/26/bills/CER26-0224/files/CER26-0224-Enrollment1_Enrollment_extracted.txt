ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-224
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To honor Vida Senior Services (VIDA) for more than five decades of extraordinary service to
older adults, acting as a home-away-from-home for older adults that offers free meals,
health screenings, nutritional guidance, and mental health support, all in a multilingual
setting.
WHEREAS, Vida Senior Services, founded in 1969, is the oldest Hispanic nonprofit
organization in the District of Columbia and has served the community for more than 55 years;
WHEREAS, VIDA is dedicated to improving the lives of older adults, particularly
through culturally and linguistically appropriate programs and services that recognize the
dignity, traditions, and unique needs of the communities it serves;
WHEREAS, VIDA provides essential services that promote health and wellness, social
connection, independence, dignity, and quality of life for older adults and their families;
WHEREAS, Vida Senior Services has been a trusted community partner, helping older
Washingtonians remain engaged, connected, healthy, and independent while strengthening the
families and neighborhoods in which they live;
WHEREAS, VIDA offers nutritious food, including a daily congregate meal site and a
food distribution program that since the Covid-19 pandemic has delivered 9,500 boxes of fresh
produce and 2,970 Food Bank boxes;
WHEREAS, VIDA provides programming that addresses the mental health needs of
older adults, including individual and group counseling sessions, tai-chi classes, and positive
thinking workshops;
WHEREAS, VIDA recognizes the importance of lifelong learning and engages seniors
with classes on embroidery, knitting, music, computer and technology, painting, and more;

WHEREAS, VIDA promotes the health and wellness of seniors by offering medical
referrals, support with doctors’ appointments, programming on chronic diseases and diabetes,
and exercise classes in dance, Zumba, tai-chi, and yoga;
WHEREAS, VIDA’s mission focuses on the unique needs of Spanish-speaking seniors,
offering Spanish literacy and English language classes to seniors who might otherwise
themselves isolated culturally and linguistically;
WHEREAS, VIDA’s enduring commitment reflects the District of Columbia’s
recognition that every older adult deserves to age with dignity, respect, opportunity, and access
to the resources necessary to live a healthy and fulfilling life; and
WHEREAS, the District recognizes and celebrates the invaluable contributions of VIDA
Senior Services and its staff, volunteers, supporters, and community partners who have devoted
themselves to serving older adults and strengthening the District’s diverse communities.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Vida Senior Services Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors Vida Senior
Services for more than five decades of commitment to older adults, caregivers, and families in
the District of Columbia, extending its sincere appreciation for its continued dedication for
improving the health, independence, and quality of life for the District’s senior population.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
