16 To amend, on an emergency basis due to congressional review, the Department of For-Hire
17 Vehicles Establishment Act of 1985 to clarify the definition of a private vehicle-for-hire
18 operator.
19
20 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
21 act may be cited as the “Private Vehicle-for-Hire Operator Clarification Congressional Review
22 Emergency Amendment Act of 2026”.
23 Sec. 2. Section 4(16C) of the Department of For-Hire Vehicles Establishment Act of
24 1985, effective March 25, 1986 (D.C. Law 6-97; D.C. Official Code § 50-301.03(16C)), is
25 amended to read as follows:
26 “(16C) “Private vehicle-for-hire operator” means an individual who utilizes the
27 digital dispatch of a private vehicle-for-hire company to operate a motor vehicle to provide
28 transportation to passengers.”.
29 Sec. 3. Applicability.
30 This act shall apply as of March 1, 2024.
31 Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.

33 statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved
34 October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
35 Sec. 5. Effective date.

37 Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), and shall remain in effect for no longer than
