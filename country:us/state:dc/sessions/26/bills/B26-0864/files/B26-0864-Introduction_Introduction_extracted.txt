15 To amend, on an temporary basis, the Board of Ethics and Government Accountability
16 Establishment and Comprehensive Ethics Reform Amendment Act of 2011 to clarify that
17 a transition committee may be organized on behalf of the Mayor-elect and to adopt the
18 definition of Mayor-elect provided in the Mayoral Transition Amendment Act of 2026;
19 and to amend the Official Correspondence Regulations to adopt the same definition of
20 “Mayor-elect”.
21
22 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
23 act may be cited as the “Mayoral Transition Temporary Amendment Act of 2026”.
24 Sec. 2. Section 101 of the Board of Ethics and Government Accountability Establishment
25 and Comprehensive Ethics Reform Amendment Act of 2011, effective April 27, 2012 (D.C. Law
26 19-124; D.C. Official Code § 1-1161.01), is amended as follows:
27 (a) The existing paragraph (33C) is redesignated as paragraph (33D).
28 (b) A new paragraph (33C) is added to read as follows:
29 “(33C) “Mayor-elect” means the person who is certified as the successful
30 candidate for the office of Mayor by the Board of Elections following the 2026 general election
31 held to determine the Mayor or, prior to such certification, the person announced and published
32 by the Board of Elections as the unofficial winner of the 2026 general election for Mayor;
33 provided, that such person was announced as the unofficial winner with a margin of victory of at
34 least 2% of the votes cast;
1

35 (c) Paragraph (52) is amended by striking the phrase “on behalf of the Mayor, Attorney
36 General” and inserting the phrase “on behalf of the Mayor, the Mayor-elect, Attorney General”
37 in its place.
38 Sec. 3. Section 6(1) of Official Correspondence Regulations, effective April 7, 1977
39 (D.C. Law 1-118; D.C. Official Code § 2-705(1)) is amended by striking the semicolon and
40 inserting the phrase “, as provided in section 1042 of the Mayoral Transition Act of 2026,
41 effective October 2, 2026 (D.C. Law 26-189; 73 DCR 11735);” in its place.
42 Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
43 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Budget Director as the fiscal impact
44 statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved
45 October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
46 Sec. 5. Effective date.
47 (a) This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by
48 the Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), a 30-day period of Congressional review
49 as provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December
50 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)).
51 (b) This act shall expire after 225 days of its having taken effect.
