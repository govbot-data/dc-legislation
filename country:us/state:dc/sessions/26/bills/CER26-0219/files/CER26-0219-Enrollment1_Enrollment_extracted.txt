ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-219
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To honor and recognize the work of Iona Senior Services for its committed work
and service to various communities.
WHEREAS, September is Healthy Aging Month and this year’s theme celebrates the
value of being curious throughout our lives by staying connected with others, staying active, and
trying new things;
WHEREAS, we in the District of Columbia are fortunate to have in place senior centers
and services that actively involve any resident over the age of 60 who wishes to participate;
WHEREAS, Iona began in 1975 as Iona House in the Tenleytown neighborhood of
Northwest Washington, founded through the collaboration of local faith communities that sought
to respond to the needs of their neighbors;
WHEREAS, the Iona Senior Services House maintains fully operating and outstanding
facilities in both Ward 3 and Ward 8;
WHEREAS, from those modest beginnings, Iona recognized the growing needs of
older adults and their families and devoted itself to helping Washingtonians age with dignity,
independence, connection, and purpose;
WHEREAS, for 5 decades, Iona has provided compassionate and essential services
to older adults and caregivers, including support, counseling, education, wellness, nutrition,
community programs, and assistance during some of life’s most challenging moments;
WHERAS, Iona operates Community Dining Sites in Wards 2, 3, and 4 to provide
seniors with access to crucial social and nutritional needs;
WHERAS, Iona has provided services to seniors afflicted with Alzheimer’s and
Dementia-related diseases via their Dementia Navigators Program;

WHEREAS, Iona has become much more than an organization providing services – it
has become a trusted friend, advocate, safety net, and source of hope for thousands of District
residents and their families;
WHEREAS, Iona’s work recognizes that growing older should not mean growing
isolated, and that every older adult deserves to be treated with respect, compassion, and dignity,
and given the opportunity to remain connected to the community;
WHEREAS, Iona has continually expanded its reach and responded to the changing
needs of seniors and challenges of aging by offering adult day health programs, caregiver
support, community dining, wellness and educational programs, and services for older adults
who may otherwise ace isolation or hardship;
WHEREAS, Iona’s commitment extends not only to older adults themselves but also
the family members, caregivers, and loved ones who so often carry the responsibility of caring
for those they cherish;
WHEREAS, Iona has demonstrated that aging services are not simply about meeting
needs – they are about helping people continue to live meaningful, connected, healthy, and
fulfilling lives;
WHEREAS, the District of Columbia is stronger because of organizations such as Iona
that believe every generation matters and that older Washingtonians should be valued not only
for the lives they have lived, but for the wisdom, expertise, leadership and contributions they
continue to offer our community; and
WHEREAS, as Iona celebrated its 50th anniversary, the District of Columba honors the
dedicated and extraordinary work of the Chief Executive Officer, Joon Bang, and his dedicated
staff, volunteers, supporters, caregivers, partners, and leaders whose compassion and
commitment have sustained this remarkable institution.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Iona Senior Services Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Iona Senior Services for the
extraordinary service it renders to adults age 60 and older; honors their caregivers, families, and
communities; and extends its sincere gratitude to Iona’s Chief Executive Officer, Joon Bang, and
his staff, for their leadership and the compassionate service they provide that helps make
Washington D.C. a more caring and inclusive city.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
