ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-211
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To recognize the contributions that the Kappa Psi chapter of the Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc. has
made to the District of Columbia.
WHEREAS, the Kappa Psi chapter was founded on November 19, 1926, at Howard
University for students enrolled in the School of Law, College of Medicine and the College of
Dentistry;
WHEREAS, the Kappa Psi chapter was originally established for professional students at
Howard University focused on applying the fraternity’s four cardinal principles of manhood,
scholarship, perseverance and uplift through initiatives and projects in support of the District of
Columbia;
WHEREAS, the Kappa Psi chapter was founded by Dr. William Montague Cobb, the
first African American to earn a Ph.D. in physical anthropology, a long-time professor of
anatomy at the Howard University College of Medicine, and president of the NAACP;
WHEREAS, the Kappa Psi chapter has been a prestigious training ground for
accomplished leaders in academia, law, medicine, politics and federal service due to its historical
ties to Howard University’s professional schools;
WHEREAS, the Kappa Psi chapter has served as a home chapter for undergraduate and
professional brothers who attended Georgetown University, George Washington University,
American University and Catholic University;
WHEREAS, the Kappa Psi chapter has acted as an incubator for Omega Psi Phi’s growth
across the DC and Maryland region;
WHEREAS, it has led to the establishment of three undergraduate chapters: the Omicron
Gamma chapter at the University of the District of Columbia; the Epsilon Sigma chapter at

which now covers Georgetown University and George Washington University;
WHEREAS, the Kappa Psi chapter is highly active in the District of Columbia

program to promote STEM education and life skills for male youth.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this resolution
may be cited as the “Kappa Psi Chapter Centennial Ceremonial Recognition Resolution of
2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors the Kapps Psi
chapter of the Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc. for their century of leadership and service in support
of the District of Columbia community.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
