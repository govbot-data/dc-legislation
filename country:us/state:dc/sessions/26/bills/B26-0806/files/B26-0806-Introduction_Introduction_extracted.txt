8 To symbolically designate the 600 Block of Alabama Avenue, S.E., as Mary Cuthbert Way.
9
10 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
11 act may be cited as the “Mary Cuthbert Way Designation Act of 2026”.
12 Sec. 2. Pursuant to sections 401, 403a, and 423 of the Street and Alley Closing and
13 Acquisition Procedures Act of 1982, effective March 10, 1983 (D.C. Law 4-201; D.C. Official
14 Code §§ 9-204.01, 9-204.03a, and 9-204.23), the Council symbolically designates the 600 Block
15 of Alabama Avenue, S.E., as “Mary Cuthbert Way”.
16 Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.
17 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal
18 impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
19 approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
20 Sec. 4. Effective date.

21 This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
22 Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto) and a 30-day period of congressional review
23 as provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December
24 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)).
