57 “(c)(1) The BID taxes for taxable properties in the BID shall be as follows:
58 “(A) The amount of $0.14 per $100 in assessed value for Class 2 Properties,
59 excluding hotels;
60 “(B) The amount of $120 per hotel or motel room annually;
61 “(C) The amount of $110 per unit for Class 1A or 1B Properties;
62 “(D) The amount of $10 per unit restricted to residents based upon income
63 pursuant to a federal or District affordable housing program, capped at $1,500 per building;
64 “(E) The amount of $0.14 per $100 in assessed value for Class 3 Properties;
65 “(F) The amount of $0.14 per $100 in assessed value for surface parking lots;
66 “(G) The amount of $100 per building or portion of a building used primarily for
67 religious worship or religious services, including a church, synagogue, mosque, temple, or
3

68 similar religious institution.
69 “(2) To the extent that a building that is subject to the BID tax is constructed
70 pursuant to a ground lease on land that is exempt from real property taxes, the assessed value of
71 the real property for purposes of the BID tax shall include the value of the building and the
72 leasehold interest, possessory interest, beneficial interest, or beneficial use of the land, and the
73 lessee or user of the land shall be assessed the corresponding BID tax, which shall be collected in
74 the same manner as possessory interest taxes under D.C. Official Code § 47-1005.01, or as
75 otherwise provided in this act.
76 “(3) A 5% annual increase in the BID taxes over the current tax year rates
77 specified in paragraph (1) is authorized subject to the requirements of section 8(b).
78 “(d)(1) Pursuant to section 8(a) and as provided in the BID articles of incorporation or
79 bylaws, the Board of Directors of the BID established by this section shall include one or more
80 members for each of the following:
81 “(A) A representative of Howard University; and
82 “(B) A performance venue or other cultural institution within the
83 geographic boundaries of the BID.
84 “(2) A vacancy for any Board of Directors position set forth in paragraph (1) shall
85 not constitute a violation of this act.
86 “(e) Subject to available funds, the BID shall receive matching funds from the Greater U
87 Street Parking Benefit Fund pursuant to section 2a(c)(4)(B) of the Performance Parking Pilot Zone
88 Act of 2008, effective September 14, 2011 (D.C. Law 19-21; D.C. Official Code § 50-
89 2531.01(c)(4)(B)), in a form and frequency to be determined by the Chief Financial Officer.”.
