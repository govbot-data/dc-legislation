ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-542
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
November 10, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve the issuance
of bonds and notes in support of the New Communities Initiative.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That
this resolution may be cited as the “New Communities Initiative Bond Issuance
Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) The New Communities Initiative (“NCI”) is a District government
program designed to revitalize distressed subsidized housing and redevelop
neighborhoods into vibrant mixed-income communities.
(b) The New Communities Initiative includes four District neighborhoods: Barry
Farm in Ward 8; Lincoln Heights – Richardson Dwellings in Ward 7; Northwest One in
Ward 6; and Park Morton in Ward 1, which includes the Bruce Monroe redevelopment.
(c) The New Communities Initiative is funded through public bonds that allow the
District to leverage funding for development projects. The District’s approved Fiscal
Years 2027-2032 Capital Improvement Plan includes approximately $318 million in bond
financing for Barry Farm, Northwest One, and the Bruce Monroe development. This
represents the estimated amount of additional funding needed to complete development
in all the NCI Communities, while meeting NCI’s core principles.
(d) Before the NCI bonds may be issued, however, separate bond approval
legislation is required to be adopted by the Council.
(e) Emergency action to approve the issuance of the bonds is necessary for the
District to have the authority to advance work on the New Communities Initiative, to
ensure that the District can issue bonds and notes in a timely manner, and to take
advantage of favorable market conditions to provide funding for or to reimburse the
District for funds already expended on projects approved and undertaken as part of the
New Communities Initiative.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
