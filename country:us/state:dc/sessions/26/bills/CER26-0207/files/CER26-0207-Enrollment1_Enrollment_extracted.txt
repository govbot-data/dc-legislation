ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-207
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To honor and recognize Dr. Tiffany L. Lancaster for her many contributions to the
District of Columbia and celebrates her continued commitment to public service, culture,
and the advancement of others.
WHEREAS, Dr. Tiffany L. Lancaster is a proud and involved community resident and an
artist, poet, and advocate whose life reflects a deep commitment to service, creativity, and the
residents of our city;
WHEREAS, Dr. Tiffancy L. Lancaster has demonstrated her commitment to the District
through public service, including her service as an Advisory Neighborhood Commissioner in
Ward Eight and her appointment by Mayor Muriel Bowser to serve as a Commissioner with the
D.C. Mayor's Office on African Affairs, where she has contributed to efforts involving economic
development and the advancement of African and African Diaspora communities;
WHEREAS, Dr. Lancaster has consistently sought to build bridges among people and
communities, using her voice and her talents to encourage greater understanding, opportunity,
cultural appreciation, and unity;
WHEREAS, Dr. Lancaster is a poet and artist whose creative work has provided a
powerful means of expressing hope and love while inspiring others to see the potential in every
person;
WHEREAS, her humanitarian and community efforts reflect a willingness to stand with
others, advocate for those in need, and contribute her time and talents to making the District and
the wider community a better place;
WHEREAS, Dr. Tiffany L. Lancaster has won many awards and has been recognized by
many for her contributions including a Lifetime Achievement Award from President Joseph
Biden.
1

ENROLLED ORIGINAL
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That the
Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Dr. Tiffany L. Lancaster for her contributions to
our community, her humanitarian service, and advocacy for the great diversity in the District.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF
COLUMBIA, That the Council extends its appreciation to Dr. Tiffany L. Lancaster for the many
ways she has given of herself to help others and wishes her continued success in all her
endeavors.
Sec. 2. The resolution may be cited as the “Dr. Tiffany L. Lancaster Recognition
Resolution of 2026.”
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
