Re: Abandonment of the Highway Plan for 601 Mississippi Ave SE (Parcel 236/114)
Mr. Labrador:
This request is submitted on behalf of the D.C. Department of General Services (“DGS”),
as the implementing agency for the District of Columbia Public Schools (“DCPS”), concerning
the removal of a portion of the “Plan for the Extension of a Permanent Systems of Highways” (the
“Highway Plan”) located within the property of 601 Mississippi Avenue SE (Parcel 236/114) (the
“Property”).1 The Highway Plan consists of a 90-foot-wide portion cutting through the center of
the Property, from the northern lot line along Mississippi Ave SE to the southern lot line along
Valley Ave SE. The Highway Plan is unimproved and not paved. Accordingly, please consider
this request pursuant to D.C. Code § 9-101.01 et seq. to remove the Highway Plan from the
Property.
The subject request is made in connection with DCPS’s plans to renovate and modernize
Hart Middle School (“Hart MS”). A portion of the existing Hart MS building is located within the
area of the Highway Plan. As such, the Highway Plan has not historically been used at the Property
and is not necessary for highway purposes. Absent removal, the Highway Plan would hinder the
planned renovation and modernization of Hart MS. Accordingly, abandonment of the Highway
Plan designation for Mississippi Avenue is appropriate.
Pursuant to D.C. Code § 9-101.06, no less than 30 days prior to the submission of this
request to the National Capital Planning Commission (“NCPC”) and the Council of the District of
Columbia (the “Council”), the Mayor shall provide written notice with an opportunity to submit
comments to the following:
(1) Each owner of land within the squares in which or adjacent to which the
proposed modification is located, by registered mail to the address to which taxation
notifications are sent by the District of Columbia Department of Finance and
Revenue;

Subject: S.O. No. 26-00076 – Proposed Elimination of a Portion of 7th Street SE from DC Highway Plan
The District Department of Transportation (DDOT) has reviewed the application materials provided by the Office
of the Surveyor. The purpose of this memorandum is to provide DDOT’s comments on the proposed Highway
Plan Elimination.
OVERVIEW
The District of Columbia Department of General Services (the “Applicant”) seeks approval to eliminate an
approximately 800-foot segment of 7th Street SE from the Plan of a Permanent System of Highways of the
District of Columbia (the “DC Highway Plan”) to facilitate the renovation of Hart Middle School. The portion of
7th Street SE to be eliminated from the DC Highway Plan is a 90-foot-wide portion cutting through the center of
601 Mississippi Avenue SE (Lots 801-803, Square 5963) as shown in Figure 1 below. It is noted that the 800-foot
section called for in the DC Highway Plan for this segment is not currently DDOT right-of-way, and thus, no right-
of-way needs to be closed. Additionally, DDOT has no plans to extend 7th Street SE southward, and it would be
adjacent to and redundant to Wheeler Road SE.
A portion of the existing Hart Middle School building is located within the area of the Highway Plan. As such, the
Highway Plan has not historically been used at the property and is not necessary for highway purposes. Absent
removal, the Highway Plan would hinder the planned renovation and modernization of Hart Middle School.
RECOMMENDATION
DDOT has determined that there is no longer a transportation need for the DC Highway Plan designation on 7th
Street SE and does not object to its elimination, as shown in Figure 1 below.

Local Public Facilities (LPUB) and Parks, Recreation, and Open Space (PROS) development,
and on the Generalized Policy Map (GPM) as Institutional Uses and Parks.

include:

Encourage the siting (or retention and modernization) of public facilities, such as schools, libraries,
and government offices, near transit stations and along transit corridors. Such facilities should be a
focus for community activities and enhance neighborhood identity.
The applicant proposes to modernize the school located on lot 803. Closure of the subject right-of-way
would accommodate the proposed modernization.

Conclusion
Funds are sufficient in the proposed fiscal year 2026 budget and proposed fiscal year 2027 through
fiscal year 2030 budget and financial plan to implement the bill.
Background
The bill amends the District’s highway plan1 to eliminate a stretch of 7th Street, S.E. that, in the plan,
runs from Mississippi Avenue, S.E. to Valley Avenue, S.E. between Charles Hart Middle School and the
Southeast Tennis and Learning Center.2 The approximately 71,000 square feet of land is not built out
and is not functional right-of-way for transportation purposes. The elimination of 7th Street, S.E. will
allow the Department of General Services to move forward with a modernization project of the
adjacent Charles Hart Middle School, which is planned to start construction in 2026.
The bill also establishes a small segment of roadway in the highway plan to ensure continuity of
Valley Avenue, S.E. in the highway plan once 7th Street, S.E. is eliminated.
