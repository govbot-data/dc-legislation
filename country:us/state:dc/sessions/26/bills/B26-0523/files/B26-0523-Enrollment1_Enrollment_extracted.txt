ENROLLED ORIGINAL
AN ACT
__________________
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
__________________
To amend the Department of Health Care Finance Establishment Act of 2007 to require the
Department of Health Care Finance to develop a single, centralized application process
for providers seeking to be credentialed or recredentialed with a managed care
organization, to set timeframes and notice requirements for the Department of Health
Care Finance to respond to applications and notify managed care organizations, to require
the transferal of credentialing status if a provider changes employers, and to ensure a
complete application is consistent with the standards set forth in Title I of the Health
Insurers and Credentialing Intermediaries Uniform Credentialing Form Act of 2002.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited as the “Streamlining Medicaid Credentialing Amendment Act of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Department of Health Care Finance Establishment Act of 2007, effective
February 27, 2008 (D.C. Law 17-109; D.C. Official Code § 7-771.01 et seq.), is amended as
follows:
(a) Section 2 (D.C. Official Code § 7-771.01) is amended by adding a new paragraph (9)
to read as follows:
“(9) “Provider” shall have the same meaning as provided in section 2(7) of the
Prompt Pay Act of 2002, effective July 23, 2002 (D.C. Law 14-176; D.C. Official Code § 31-
3131(7)).”.
(b) A new section 8e is added to read as follows:
“Sec. 8e. Medicaid provider consolidated credentialing.
“(a) By no later than January 1, 2028, the Mayor shall develop and establish a single,
consolidated, and uniform application process for a provider seeking to be credentialed or
recredentialed with a managed care organization (“MCO”) under the District’s Medicaid
program.
“(b) A provider whose credentials are approved by the Department shall be deemed
credentialed with all MCOs, and an MCO may not require a provider to
be separately credentialed by that MCO in order to receive reimbursement.

“(c) Notwithstanding any other provision of this section, the Department may not require
a provider to submit a recredential application solely because the provider’s:
“(1) Federal tax identification number changes;
“(2) Employer’s federal tax identification number changes; or
“(3) Employer changes; provided, that the new employer is a participating
provider on one of the District’s MCO provider panels.
“(d)(1) The Department shall issue a credentialing or recredentialing determination to the
provider within 30 days of receiving a complete application; except, that, if the Department
requires additional time beyond the 30 days to render its decision, the Department shall notify
the provider and provide an estimated date of determination.
“(2) If the Department receives an incomplete application, the Department shall
return the application to the provider at the address listed in the application within 10 days of
receiving the application and notify the provider on the information missing from their
application.
“(e) The Department shall provide the approved application to the MCOs concurrently
with the Department’s issuance of credentials or recredentials to the provider.
“(f) An MCO shall upload provider information on all applicable billing platforms,
including any subcontractors and vendors, within 7 business days of receiving a provider’s
approved credentials or recredentials from the Department.
“(g) The Department shall work with the Department of Insurance, Securities, and
Banking to ensure that the application form established by this section is consistent with the form
established by Title I of the Health Insurers and Credentialing Intermediaries Uniform
Credentialing Form Act of 2002, effective April 13, 2002 (D.C. Law 14-96; D.C. Official Code §
31-3251 et seq.).”.
Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.
The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal
impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
Sec. 4. Applicability
(a) This act shall apply upon the date of inclusion of its fiscal effect in an approved
budget and financial plan.
(b) The Chief Financial Officer shall certify the date of the inclusion of the fiscal effect in
an approved budget and financial plan, and provide notice to the Budget Director of the Council
of the certification.
(c)(1) The Budget Director shall cause the notice of the certification to be published in
the District of Columbia Register.
(2) The date of publication of the notice of the certification shall not affect the
applicability of this act.

Sec. 5. Effective date.
This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto) and a 30-day period of congressional review
as provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December
24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)).
