ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-503
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve Modification Nos.
M0008, M0009, and M0010 to Contract No. CW110323 with Tag-B Parking, LLC, and
to authorize payment for the goods and services received and to be received under the
modifications.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Modification Nos. M0008, M0009 and M0010 to Contract No.
CW110323 with Tag-B Parking, LLC Approval and Payment Authorization Emergency
Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve Modification Nos. M0008, M0009,
and M0010 to Contract No. CW110323 with Tag-B Parking, LLC to provide private student
transportation and attendant services to eligible students, and to authorize payment for the goods
and services received and to be received under the modifications.
(b) By Modification No. M0008, issued on July 27, 2026, the Office of Contracting and
Procurement (“OCP”), on behalf of the Office of the State Superintendent of Education,
exercised a partial option of option year 3 in the not-to-exceed amount of $990,000 for the period
from August 22, 2026, through September 30, 2026.
(c) By Modification No. M0009, issued on August 31, 2026, OCP increased the not-to-
exceed amount by $617,376.96 to a total not-to-exceed amount of $1,607,376.96 for the partial
option period.
(d) Proposed Modification No. M0010 will exercise the remainder of option year 3 and
increase the amount by $17,102,831.04 to a total not-to-exceed amount of $18,710,208 covering
the period of performance from October 1, 2026, through August 21, 2027.
(e) Council approval is necessary because the modifications will increase the value of the
contract by more than $1 million during a 12-month period.
(f) Council approval of the modifications is necessary to allow the continuation of these
vital services. Without Council approval, Tag-B Parking, LLC cannot be paid for goods and
services provided in excess of $1 million for the period from August 22, 2026, through August
21, 2027.

Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Modification Nos. M0008, M0009 and M0010 to Contract No. CW110323 with Tag-B Parking,
LLC Approval and Payment Authorization Emergency Act of 2026 be adopted after a single
reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
