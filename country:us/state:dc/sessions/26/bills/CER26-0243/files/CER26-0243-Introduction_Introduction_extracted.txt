15 To recognize Prita Piekara for her exceptional leadership as Chief of Staff to Councilmember
16 Robert C. White, Jr. and for her steadfast dedication to effective, compassionate, and
17 accountable public service in the District of Columbia.
18 WHEREAS, Prita Piekara serves as Chief of Staff to Councilmember Robert C. White,
19 Jr., providing strategic leadership to an office entrusted with advancing legislation, conducting
20 oversight, responding to constituents, and serving residents in every ward of the District;
21 WHEREAS, Prita Piekara has guided a multifaceted team through consequential policy
22 debates, demanding legislative schedules, and the everyday challenges of public service, helping
23 ensure that the office approaches each responsibility with focus, professionalism, and purpose;
24 WHEREAS, Prita Piekara has worked to bring together the personal office, the
25 Committee on Housing, community partners, and other Council offices so that sound policy,
26 strong constituent service, and thoughtful communication reinforce one another;
27 WHEREAS, her leadership has supported the office’s work to confront the District’s
28 housing challenges, promote responsive government, and give residents a meaningful voice in
29 decisions that shape their lives;

30 WHEREAS, Prita Piekara understands that effective public service requires both careful
31 judgment and genuine care for the people doing the work, and she has helped cultivate an office
32 culture grounded in collaboration and high work ethic;
33 WHEREAS, Prita Piekara has helped establish clear priorities in periods when the office
34 faced competing demands, ensuring that residents, staff, and community partners could continue
35 to depend on the office’s attention and follow-through;
36 WHEREAS, she has recognized the importance of thoughtful preparation and
37 coordination before public hearings, negotiations, and critical decisions, helping the
38 Councilmember and staff act with confidence in moments of public scrutiny;
39 WHEREAS, her service has involved not only leading an office, but also supporting the
40 individuals within it and recognizing the contributions of colleagues whose work makes public
41 policy possible;
42 WHEREAS, Prita Piekara has offered steady guidance in moments that demanded
43 difficult decisions, balancing immediate needs with long-term priorities and helping colleagues
44 navigate change with confidence; and
45 WHEREAS, through her commitment to the Councilmember, her colleagues, and the
46 residents of the District, Prita Piekara has demonstrated that public leadership is measured not
47 only by the decisions one makes, but also by the people one empowers and the communities one
48 serves.
49 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
50 resolution may be cited as the “Sukhprita “Prita” Piekara Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
51 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Prita Piekara for her
52 outstanding leadership, dedicated public service, and invaluable contributions to Councilmember
53 Robert C. White, Jr.’s office and to residents throughout the District.
54 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
55 the District of Columbia Register.
