ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-201
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To recognize, honor, and celebrate the Vermont Avenue Baptist Church for its distinguished
service to the people of the District of Columbia on its 160th celebration of establishment.
WHEREAS, in 1866, 7 former enslaved members gathered in the home of John and Amy
Slaughter for the purpose of forming a church;
WHEREAS, after receiving the necessary certification from the Nineteenth Street Baptist
Church, 7 former enslaved members founded the Fifth Baptist Church at 1630 Vermont Avenue,
N.W., in the Logan Circle Neighborhood;
WHEREAS, Reverend John Henry Brooks, a former Union Army wagon driver, was
selected as the Church’s first Pastor and led the congregation in acquiring the site and constructing
the small wooden structure they named the Fifth Baptist Church;
WHEREAS, the main Sanctuary of the new brick Church was built in 1872, a cornerstone
was laid, and a number of benevolent organizations were established;
WHEREAS, in 1890, under Rev. George Wellington Lee, the growing congregation added
the current façade and steeple and adopted the Vermont Avenue Baptist Church name;
WHEREAS, since its founding, the Church has been blessed with 9 pastors–– Reverend
John Henry Brooks, Reverend George Wellington Lee, Reverend James Edward Willis, Reverend
Dr. Chasteen Theophilus Murray, Reverend Dr. John Rayford Wheeler, Reverend Cornelius
Rayford Wheeler, former interim pastor Reverend Dr. Isaac Alexander Fox, Reverend Dr. John
Julien Cox, and interim pastor Reverend Michael Curtis Miller;
WHEREAS, for more than a century, the Vermont Avenue Baptist Church has been a
meeting place for leaders to provide services to uplift the African American community socially,
economically, and spiritually;

WHEREAS, in the 20th century, the Church was a leader in mission work in the District
and the Vermont Avenue Baptist Church of Zululand dedicated a Church outside of Durban, South
Africa, in 1979; and
WHEREAS the Church family invites the entire community to join in fellowship each
Sunday and during other services and events for praise, worship, study, and service to continue the
mission of the Vermont Avenue Baptist Church in the 21st century.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Vermont Avenue Baptist Church 160th Founders Day Recognition
Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Vermont Avenue Baptist
Church for its exceptional accomplishments and commitment to local and international humanity.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
