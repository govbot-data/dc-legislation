AN ACT
.C. ACT 26-474
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
NOVEMBER 9, 2026
‘To amend, ona temporary basis, the Procurement Practices Reform Act of2010 to require the
Office ofContracting and Procurementto process requests forthe equitable adjustment of
homeless services contractswithin60 days; and to amend section 510ofthe Rental
Housing Act to changethe applicability date
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICTOF COLUMBIA, That this
actmay be cited as the “Homeless Services ContractAdjustment Temporary Amendment Act of
2026”.
Sec. 2. The Procurement Practices Reform Act of2010, effectiveApril 8, 2011 (D:
Law 18-371; D.C. Official Code § 2-351.01 e7seq.) is amended by adding a newsection 1105a
to read as follo
“Sec, 1105a. Equitable adjustments ofhomeless services contracts.
“(a)(1) Within 60 calendardays ofreceivingacomplete request for the equitable
adjustment ofa contract to provide homeless services under section 3 ofthe Homeless Services
Reform Act, effective October22, 2005 (D.C. Law 16-35; D.C. Official Code § 4-754.01), on
the basis oftherevision ofa wage determination issued by the U.S. DepartmentofLabor
pursuantto 41 U.S.C. §6703, OCP shall determine whether therequestingcontractor is entitled
to an equitable adjustment.
“(2) A request for an equitable adjustment under this subsection is complete when
the requesting contractor has submittedall information and documents necessary forOCP to
assess the request.
“(b) Within 7 business days ofthe submission to OCP ofall information and documents
necessaryto complete an equitable adjustmentrequest under subsection (a) ofthis section, OCP
shall inform therequesting contractorin writing that itconsiders therequest complete.”
Sec. 3. Section 510 ofthe Rental Housing Act, effective May 18, 2022 (D.C. Law24-
115; D.C, Official Code § 42-3505.10), is amended as follows:
(a) The lead-in languageofsubsection (b-3) isamended by striking the date “October 1,
2025,”and inserting the date “October 1, 2026,”in its place.

Sec.5. Effective date.
(a) This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor(or inthe eventofveto by
the Mayor, action by theCouncil to override the veto) and a 30-day periodofcongressional
review as provided in section 602(c)(1) ofthe District ofColumbia Home Rule Act, approved
