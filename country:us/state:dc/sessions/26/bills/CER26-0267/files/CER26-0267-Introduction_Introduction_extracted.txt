5 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION

8
9 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
10
11 ____________________
12
13 To recognize and honor Margarine S. Neal, affectionately known as “Go-Go Marge,” for her
14 extraordinary contributions to the development of Washington, D.C.’s signature Go-Go
15 music culture, her decades of service to District youth and families, and to celebrate her
16 90th birthday.
17
18 WHEREAS, Margarine S. Neal, affectionately known throughout Washington, D.C. as
19 “Go-Go Marge,” has devoted her life to supporting young people, strengthening her community,
20 and helping cultivate the musical tradition that has become known worldwide as the District’s
21 indigenous art form; and
22
23 WHEREAS, Ms. Neal was born in Garner, North Carolina, and later made Washington,
24 D.C. her home, where she became a beloved community figure and a source of guidance,
25 encouragement, and opportunity for generations of District residents; and
26
27 WHEREAS, in the early 1970s, Ms. Neal recognized the potential of a group of aspiring
28 young musicians, including her son, Michael “Funky Ned” Neal, and dedicated her time, energy,
29 and resources to helping them pursue their musical ambitions; and
30
31 WHEREAS, in 1974, Ms. Neal purchased her son's first guitar and helped create
32 opportunities for young musicians to rehearse, grow their talents, and develop the skills and
33 confidence necessary to succeed; and
34
35 WHEREAS, Ms. Neal welcomed the early members of Rare Essence into her home for
36 rehearsals and, alongside community supporters including Annie Mac-Thomas and Mattie Mac,
37 played a vital role in helping the group establish itself during the formative years of what would
38 become one of Washington's most celebrated Go-Go bands; and
39
40 WHEREAS, long before Go-Go music earned recognition as the official music of the
41 District of Columbia, Ms. Neal was helping nurture the young artists and musicians who would
1

42 contribute to its emergence and growth, working alongside pioneers of the genre including James
43 “Funk” Thomas and the late Chuck Brown; and
44
45 WHEREAS, Ms. Neal's contributions extended well beyond providing rehearsal space, as
46 she drove musicians to performances, made performance costumes, handled finances, assisted
47 with management responsibilities, and provided countless forms of support that helped sustain
48 and strengthen the band's success; and
49
50 WHEREAS, for more than five decades, Ms. Neal has remained a steadfast presence
51 within the Rare Essence family, offering guidance, encouragement, and support to generations of
52 musicians and performers; and
53
54 WHEREAS, through her work with the District of Columbia Department of Recreation,
55 Ms. Neal helped provide summer employment opportunities to countless young people in
56 Southeast Washington, giving them valuable work experience, fostering responsibility, and
57 helping create pathways to success; and
58
59 WHEREAS, Ms. Neal's legacy is reflected not only in the music she helped bring to life,
60 but also in the many lives she has touched through her generosity, mentorship, service, and
61 unwavering commitment to her community; and
62
63 WHEREAS, as Ms. Neal celebrates her 90th birthday on November 30, 2026, the
64 residents of the District of Columbia recognize her as a treasured community leader, a pioneering
65 supporter of Go-Go music, and the beloved Queen of D.C. Go-Go; and
66
67 WHEREAS, the Council of the District of Columbia is proud to honor Ms. Neal for
68 ninety years of life, service, leadership, and contribution to the cultural heritage of the nation's
69 capital;
70
71 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
72 resolution may be cited as the “Margarine S. Neal Recognition Resolution of 2026.”
73
74 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors Margarine S.
75 Neal, affectionately known as “Go-Go Marge,” for her extraordinary contributions to
76 Washington, D.C.'s Go-Go music community and for her lifelong service to the residents of the
77 District of Columbia.
78
79 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
80 the District of Columbia Register.
