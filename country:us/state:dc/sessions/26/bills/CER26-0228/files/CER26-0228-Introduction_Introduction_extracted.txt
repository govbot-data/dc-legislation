4 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
5
6 _________________________
7
8 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
9 _________________________
10
11
12 To celebrate and recognize the 50th Anniversary of Kramers and the profound impact this
13 establishment has had on the District community.
14
1

15 WHEREAS, Kramerbooks & Afterwords Cafe first opened in 1976 during the Bicentennial
16 celebration of the Declaration of Independence by Bill Kramer, David Tenney, and Henry Posner
17 and was the first bookstore and cafe in the District;
18 WHEREAS, for five decades, Kramers has served generations of Washingtonians visitors,
19 authors, neighbors, and student, becoming an important part of the cultural and social fabric of
20 Dupont Circle;
21 WHEREAS, in 2016, Steve Salis acquired Kramers and has continued to invest in the
22 business while preserving its identity as an independent bookstore, restaurant, bar, and event space;
23 WHEREAS, Kramers continues to reflect the diversity of the neighborhood with book
24 selections of new, old, well-known, and yet-to-be discovered;
25 WHEREAS, Kramers is a space where individuals can learn, laugh, debate, and contribute
26 to the continuing dialogue of literature.
27 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
28 resolution may be cited as the “Kramers 50th Anniversary Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
29 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the 50th Anniversary of
30 Kramers for its commitment to the community of the District and for creating accessible avenues
31 to literature.
32 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
