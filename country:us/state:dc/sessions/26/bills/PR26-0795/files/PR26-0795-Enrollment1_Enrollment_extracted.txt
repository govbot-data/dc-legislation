ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-505
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve Modification Nos.
M0010, M0011, and M0012 to Contract No. CW110212 with USA Guided Tours DC,
LLC, and to authorize payment for the goods and services received and to be received
under the modifications.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Modification Nos. M0010, M0011 and M0012 to Contract No.
CW110212 with USA Guided Tours DC, LLC Approval and Payment Authorization Emergency
Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve Modification Nos. M0010, M0011,
and M0012 to Contract No. CW110212 with USA Guided Tours DC, LLC to provide
transportation services to students with disabilities, and to authorize payment for the goods and
services received and to be received under the modifications.
(b) By Modification No. M0010, issued on July 27, 2026, the Office of Contracting and
Procurement (“OCP”), on behalf of the Office of the State Superintendent of Education,
exercised a partial option of option year 3 in the not-to-exceed amount of $212,625 for the period
from August 23, 2026, through September 30, 2026.
(c) By Modification No. M0011, issued on August 28, 2026, OCP incorporated a revised
price schedule.
(d) Proposed Modification No. M0012 will exercise the remainder of option year 3 and
increase the not-to-exceed amount by $7,509,375 to a total not-to-exceed amount of $7,722,000.
(e) Council approval is necessary because the modifications will increase the value of the
contract by more than $1 million during a 12-month period.
(f) Council approval is necessary to allow the continuation of these vital services.
Without Council approval, USA Guided Tours DC, LLC cannot be paid for goods and services
provided in excess of $1 million for the period from August 23, 2026, through August 22, 2027.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Modification Nos. M0010, M0011 and M0012 to Contract No. CW110212 with USA Guided
