13 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
14 __________
15
16 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
17 __________
18
19
20 To honor and recognize St. Paul African Methodist Episcopal Church, on the 170th
21 anniversary of its founding.
22
23 WHEREAS, St. Paul Methodist Episcopal Church was founded on October 25, 1856, by
24 a courageous group of freed men led by Anthony Bowen, establishing a legacy of faith, freedom,
25 and education that is now celebrating 170 years of service to our community;
26
27 WHEREAS, in its early years, the congregation was known by several names, including
28 the “E” Street Mission, the St. Paul Society, and the St. Paul Chapel, reflecting its growth and
29 evolving role within the African American community of the District of Columbia;
30
31 WHEREAS, in 1859, the founders of the congregation purchased land on 8th Street, SW,
32 establishing a permanent home for the church and creating a lasting institution in the District of
33 Columbia;
34
35 WHEREAS, during its first one hundred years, St. Paul AME Church served not only as
36 a house of worship, but also as an educational institution and a center of community life;
37
38 WHEREAS, St. Paul AME Church was an important gathering place for supporters of
39 the Underground Railroad and African Americans seeking emancipation;
40
41 WHEREAS, the history of St. Paul AME Church is intertwined with the history of
42 African Americans and their continuing struggle for freedom, equality, justice, and human
43 dignity;
44
45 WHEREAS, throughout its history, St. Paul AME Church has remained an institution
46 serving our community with hope, education, faith, fellowship, and ministry;

47
48 WHEREAS, for 170 years, the clergy, leaders, members, and family of St. Paul have
49 contributed to the spiritual, civic, education, and cultural life of DC; and
50
51 WHEREAS, the 170th anniversary of the establishment of St. Paul provides an important
52 opportunity to honor the founders and generation of congregants whose faith, sacrifice, and
53 service have sustained this historic institution.
54
55 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, that the
56 Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the St. Paul American Methodist Episcopal
57 Church.
58
59 Sec. 2. The resolution may be cited as the “St. Paul American Methodist Episcopal
60 Church Recognition Resolution of 2026.”
61
62 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
63 the District of Columbia Register.
