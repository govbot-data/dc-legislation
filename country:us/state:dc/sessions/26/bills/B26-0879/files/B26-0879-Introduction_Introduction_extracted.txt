13 To amend, on an emergency basis, Title II of the District of Columbia Comprehensive Bicycle
14 Transportation and Safety Act of 1984 to require that electric bicycles, electric mobility
15 devices, and traction batteries manufactured, distributed, sold, or offered for lease or rent
16 in the District meet certain safety standards; to amend the District of Columbia Traffic Act,
17 1925 to make conforming amendments; to amend the Department of Transportation
18 Establishment Act of 2002 to authorize the Director of the District Department of
19 Transportation to develop policies for the safe use of electric bicycles, motorized bicycles,
20 and electric mobility devices; and for other purposes.
21
22 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
23 act may be cited as the “Micromobility Fire Safety Standards Emergency Amendment Act of
24 2026”.
25 Sec. 2. Title II of the District of Columbia Comprehensive Bicycle Transportation and
26 Safety Act of 1984, effective May 1, 2008 (D.C. Law 17-149; D.C. Official Code § 50-1611 et
27 seq.), is amended by adding a new section 203 to read as follows:
28 “Sec. 203. Requirements for manufacturer, distribution, sale, lease, rent of micromobility
29 and traction batteries.
30 “(a) For the purposes of this section, the term:
31 “(1) “Certification” means the attestation by a certification body, indicated by a
32 certification mark on the equipment, device, or product, that the equipment, device, or product has
33 been evaluated, tested, and found to conform to the standards specified in section 3.

34 “(2) “Certification body” means an independent third-party organization providing
35 certification for micromobility devices that:
36 “(A) Is recognized by the U.S. Department of Labor’s Occupational Safety
37 and Health Administration (“OSHA”) as a Nationally Recognized Testing Laboratory; or
38 “(B) Has received ISO/IEC 17065 accreditation from an independent
39 accreditation body that is a member of the Global Accreditation Cooperation Incorporated, or any
40 successor organization.
41 “(3) “Certification mark” means a mark of conformity owned by a certification
42 body and registered with the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office that is visible and affixed to a
43 product that has received certification.
44 “(4) “Electric bicycle” shall have the same meaning as provided in section 2(6A)
45 of the District of Columbia Traffic Act, 1925, approved March 3, 1925 (43 Stat. 1119; D.C. Official
46 Code § 50-2201.02(6A)).
47 “(5) “Electric mobility device” shall have the same meaning as provided in section
48 2(6B) of the District of Columbia Traffic Act, 1925, approved March 3, 1925 (43 Stat. 1119; D.C.
49 Official Code § 50-2201.02(6B)).
50 “(6) “Micromobility device” means an electric bicycle or electric mobility device.
51 “(7) “Nationally Recognized Testing Laboratory” means an organization that meets
52 the qualifications provided in 29 C.F.R. § 1910.7(b) and is recognized as a Nationally Recognized
53 Testing Laboratory by the Occupational Safety and Health Administration.

54 “(8) “SFD operating company” shall have the same meaning as provided in section
55 2(14C) of the District of Columbia Traffic Act, 1925, approved March 3, 1925 (43 Stat. 1119; D.C.
56 Official Code § 50-2201.02(14C)).
57 “(9) “Traction battery” means a rechargeable battery used to power the electric
58 motors of a micromobility device.
59 “(b) All micromobility devices, and traction batteries for such devices, that are
60 manufactured, distributed, sold, or offered for lease or rent in the District shall meet the following
61 safety standards:
62 “(1) Electric bicycles shall be evaluated, tested, and certified to UL 2849 or EN
63 15194 and bear the certification mark, except, that for 6 months following the effective date of the
64 Micromobility Fire Safety Standards Amendment Act of 2026, passed on 2nd reading on
65 September 22, 2026 (Enrolled version of Bill 26-244), an SFD operating company may satisfy the
66 requirements of this paragraph by evaluating, testing, and certifying any electric bicycles offered
67 for rental or lease to UL 2272;
68 “(2) Electric mobility devices shall be evaluated, tested, and certified to UL 2272
69 and bear the certification mark; and
70 “(3) Traction batteries for use in micromobility devices shall be evaluated, tested,
71 and certified to UL 2271 or EN 50604 and bear the certification mark.
72 “(c) Notwithstanding subsection (b) of this section, a micromobility device or traction
73 batteries may not display a certification mark if:
74 “(1)(A) The micromobility device or the traction battery is offered for rental or
75 lease by an SFD operating company or by the Capital Bikeshare program; and

76 “(B) The SFD operating company or the Capital Bikeshare program has
77 provided documentation to DDOT establishing that the electric bicycle, electric mobility device,
78 or traction battery meets the applicable safety standard as described in subsection (b) of this
79 section; or
80 “(2) The micromobility device is being offered for a secondhand sale.
81 “(d) Evaluation, testing, and certification of any standard listed in subsection (b) of this
82 section shall be performed by a certification body.”.
83 Sec. 3. Section 2 of the District of Columbia Traffic Act, 1925, approved March 3, 1925
84 (43 Stat. 1119; D.C. Official Code § 50-2201.02), is amended as follows:
85 (a) Paragraph (6A) is redesignated as paragraph (6B).
86 (b) A new paragraph (6A) is added to read as follows:
87 “(6A) Electric bicycle” means a vehicle with:
88 “(A) A post-mounted seat or saddle;
89 “(B) Two or 3 wheels that are in contact with the ground;
90 “(C) Fully operative pedals for human propulsion; and
91 “(D) One or more electric motors to either assist the rider when pedaling or
92 provide motive power to the wheels when the rider is not pedaling.”.
93 (c) Paragraph (6B)(A) is amended to read as follows:
94 “(6B)(A) “Electric mobility device” means a mobility device with a rechargeable
95 electric drive train that propels the rider, and which may be provided with a handle for grasping
96 while riding, regardless of whether the device is self-balancing or if the rider may or may not be
97 seated.”.

98 (d) Paragraph (11) is amended to read as follows:
99 “(11)(A) “Motor vehicle” means any vehicle propelled by internal combustion
100 engines, electricity, or steam.
101 “(B) The term “motor vehicle” shall not include motorized bicycles, traction
102 engines, road rollers, vehicles propelled only upon rails or tracks, electric mobility devices,
103 personal mobility devices, and a battery-operated wheelchair when operated by a person with a
104 disability.”.
105 Sec 4. Section 4(a)(1)(K) of the Department of Transportation Establishment Act of 2002,
106 effective May 21, 2002 (D.C. Law 14-137; D.C. Official Code § 50-921.04(a)(1)(k)), is amended
107 to read as follows:
108 “(K) Develop policies and programs to encourage and provide for the safe
109 use of bicycles, electric bicycles, motorized bicycles, and electric mobility devices, as those terms
110 are defined in section 2 of the District of Columbia Traffic Act of 1925, approved March 3, 1925
111 (43 Stat. 1119; D.C. Official Code § 50-2201.02), for recreation, commercial use, and work-related
112 travel, including planning, developing, operating, and regulating a Bicycle Sharing program, and
113 administering the Bicycle Sharing Fund established by section 9h to fund a Bicycle Sharing
114 program;”.
115 Sec. 5. Subsection 3399.1 of Title 24 of the District of Columbia Municipal Regulations
116 (24 DCMR § 3399.1) is amended by striking the phrase “Electric mobility device – A device
117 weighing less than seventy-five (75) pounds that has an electric motor, is solely powered by the
118 electric motor or human power, is designed to transport only one (1) person in a standing or seated
119 position, where the rider is not enclosed, and is no greater than twenty-four (24) inches wide and

120 fifty-five (55) inches long, but does not include a motorized bicycle, personal mobility device,
121 motorcycle, or moped.” and inserting the phrase “Electric mobility device – a mobility device with
122 a rechargeable electric drive train that propels the rider, and which may be provided with a handle
123 for grasping while riding, regardless of whether the device is self-balancing or if the rider may or
124 may not be seated.” in its place.
125 Sec. 6. Subsection 9901.1 of Title 18 of the District of Columbia Municipal Regulations
126 (18 DCMR § 9901.1), is amended as follows:
127 (a) Strike the phrase “Electric mobility device – a device weighing less than seventy-five
128 (75) pounds that has an electric motor, is solely powered by the electric motor or human power, is
129 designed to transport only one (1) person in a standing or seated position, where the rider is not
130 enclosed, and is no greater than twenty-four (24) inches wide and fifty-five (55) inches long, but
131 does not include a motorized bicycle, personal mobility device, motorcycle, moped, or motor-
132 driven cycle.” and insert the phrase “Electric mobility device – a mobility device with a
133 rechargeable electric drive train that propels the rider, and which may be provided with a handle
134 for grasping while riding, regardless of whether the device is self- balancing or if the rider may or
135 may not be seated” in its place.
136 (b) Strike the phrase “Motor Vehicle - any vehicle propelled by internal-combustion
137 engine, electricity, or steam, including any non-operational vehicle that is being restored or
138 repaired. The term “motor vehicle” shall not include road rollers, farm tractors, vehicles propelled
139 only upon stationary rails or tracks, electric personal assistive mobility devices, and battery-
140 operated wheelchairs when operated by a handicapped person at speeds not exceeding 10 miles
141 per hour.” and insert the phrase “Motor Vehicle - any vehicle propelled by internal-combustion

142 engines, electricity, or steam. The term “motor vehicle” shall not include motorized bicycles,
143 traction engines, road rollers, vehicles propelled only upon rails or tracks, electric mobility devices,
144 personal mobility devices, or a battery- operated wheelchair when operated by a person with a
145 disability” in its place.
