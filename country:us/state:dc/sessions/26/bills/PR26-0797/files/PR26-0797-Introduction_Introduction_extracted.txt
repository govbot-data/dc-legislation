Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Enclosed for consideration and enactment by the Council of the District of Columbia is an
emergency bill, the “Modification Nos. M004, M05 and M06 to Contract No. CW106884 with
See Forever Foundation Approval and Payment Authorization Emergency Act of 2026,” and the
accompanying emergency declaration resolution, not-to-exceed $3,968,957.85.
The legislation will approve Modification Nos. M004, M05 and M06 to Contract No. CW106884
with See Forever Foundation to provide an education program to youth who are in the custody of
the DYRS at the Youth Services Center, and to exercise the remainder of option year three of the
contract. In addition, the legislation will approve payment for the goods and services received and
to be received under the modifications.
My administration is available to discuss any questions you may have regarding the proposed
contract modifications. In order to facilitate a response to any questions you may have, please have
your staff contact Marc Scott, Chief Operating Officer, Office of Contracting and Procurement, at
(202) 724-8759.
I urge the Council to take prompt and favorable action on the enclosed legislation.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowser
