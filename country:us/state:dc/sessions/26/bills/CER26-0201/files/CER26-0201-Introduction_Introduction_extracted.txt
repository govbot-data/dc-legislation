8 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
9
10 _________________________
11
12 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
13 _________________________
14
15
16 To recognize, honor, and celebrate the Vermont Avenue Baptist Church for its distinguished
17 service to the people of the District of Columbia on its 160th celebration of establishment.
18
19 WHEREAS, in 1866, seven former enslaved members gathered in the home of John and
20 Amy Slaughter for the purpose of forming a church;
1

21 WHEREAS, after receiving the necessary certification from the Nineteenth Street Baptist
22 Church, seven former enslaved members founded the Fifth Baptist Church at 1630 Vermont
23 Avenue, N.W., in the Logan Circle Neighborhood;
24 WHEREAS, Reverend John Henry Brooks, a former Union Army wagon driver, was
25 selected as the Church’s first Pastor and led the congregation in acquiring the site and constructing
26 the small wooden structure they named the Fifth Baptist Church;
27 WHEREAS, the main Sanctuary of the new brick Church was built in 1872, a cornerstone
28 was laid, and a number of benevolent organizations were established;
29 WHEREAS, in 1890, under Rev. George Wellington Lee, the growing congregation added
30 the current façade and steeple and adopted the Vermont Avenue Baptist Church name;
31 WHEREAS, since its founding, the Church has been blessed with nine pastors–– Reverend
32 John Henry Brooks, Reverend George Wellington Lee, Reverend James Edward Willis, Reverend
33 Dr. Chasteen Theophilus Murray, Reverend Dr. John Rayford Wheeler, Reverend Cornelius
34 Rayford Wheeler, former interim pastor Reverend Dr. Isaac Alexander Fox, Reverend Dr. John
35 Julian Cox, and interim pastor Reverend Dr. Michael Curtis Miller;
36 WHEREAS, for more than a century, the Vermont Avenue Baptist Church has been a
37 meeting place for leaders to provide services to uplift the African American community socially,
38 economically, and spiritually;
39 WHEREAS, in the 20th century, the Church was a leader in mission work in the District
40 and the Vermont Avenue Baptist Church purchased and gifted a Church to the Baptist
41 Congregation in Kwa Mashu, Zululand, South Africa, in 1979; and
2

42 WHEREAS the Church family invites the entire community to join in fellowship each
43 Sunday and during other services and events for praise, worship, study, and service to continue the
44 mission of the Vermont Avenue Baptist Church in the 21st century.
45 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
46 resolution may be cited as the “Vermont Avenue Baptist Church 160th Founders Day Recognition
47 Resolution of 2026”.
48 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Vermont Avenue Baptist
49 Church for its exceptional accomplishments and commitment to local and international humanity.
50 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
