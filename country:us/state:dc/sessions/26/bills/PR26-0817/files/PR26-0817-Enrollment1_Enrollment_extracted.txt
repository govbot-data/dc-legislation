ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-526
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve a multiyear
Reimbursable Agreement with the Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority to
design and construct station improvements at the Stadium-Armory station.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority Stadium-
Armory Station Improvements Fiscal Year 2026 to Fiscal Year 2030 Reimbursable Agreement
Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve a multiyear Reimbursable
Agreement (“Agreement”) with the Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority
(“WMATA”) for the period from August 5, 2026, to September 30, 2030, for the design and
construction of station improvements at the Stadium-Armory Metrorail station to ensure the
improvements are completed before the opening of the new RFK stadium in 2030.
(b) Council approval is necessary because this is a multiyear agreement in excess of $1
million.
(c) Furthermore, Council approval is necessary to provide sufficient funding for
improvements at the street, mezzanine, and platform levels of the Stadium-Armory Metro
station.
(d) The total amount of the Agreement is $300 million from capital funds, of which
$10,041,155 was appropriated in Fiscal Year 2026, $19,958,845 was appropriated in Fiscal Year
2027, $135 million would be appropriated in Fiscal Year 2028, and $135 million would be
appropriated in Fiscal Year 2029.
(e) The District Department of Transportation Director has the authority to enter into this
Reimbursable Agreement under the general authority specified in sections 3 and 5(a)(1)(A) and
(P) of the Department of Transportation Establishment Act of 2002, effective May 21, 2002
(D.C. Law 14-137; D.C. Official Code §§ 50-921.02 and -921.04(a)(1)(A) and (P)), and section
1(j-1) of An Act To grant additional powers to the Commissioners of the District of Columbia,
and for other purposes, approved December 20, 1944 (58 Stat. 819; D.C. Official Code § 1-
301.01(j-1)).

Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority Stadium-Armory Station Improvements Fiscal
Year 2026 to Fiscal Year 2030 Reimbursable Agreement Emergency Act of 2026 be adopted
after a single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
