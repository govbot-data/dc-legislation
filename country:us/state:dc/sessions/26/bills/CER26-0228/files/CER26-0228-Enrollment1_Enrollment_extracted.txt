ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-228
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 20, 2026
To celebrate and recognize the 50th Anniversary of Kramers and the profound impact this
establishment has had on the District community.
WHEREAS, Kramerbooks & Afterwords Cafe first opened in 1976 during the Bicentennial
celebration of the Declaration of Independence by Bill Kramer, David Tenney, and Henry Posner
and was the first bookstore and cafe in the District;
WHEREAS, for five decades, Kramers has served generations of Washingtonians visitors,
authors, neighbors, and student, becoming an important part of the cultural and social fabric of
Dupont Circle;
WHEREAS, in 2016, Steve Salis acquired Kramers and has continued to invest in the
business while preserving its identity as an independent bookstore, restaurant, bar, and event space;
WHEREAS, Kramers continues to reflect the diversity of the neighborhood with book
selections of new, old, well-known, and yet-to-be discovered;
WHEREAS, Kramers is a space where individuals can learn, laugh, debate, and contribute
to the continuing dialogue of literature.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Kramers 50th Anniversary Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the 50th Anniversary of
Kramers for its commitment to the community of the District and for creating accessible avenues
to literature.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
