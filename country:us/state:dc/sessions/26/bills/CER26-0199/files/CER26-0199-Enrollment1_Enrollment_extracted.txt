ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-199
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To honor and recognize Jacqueline “Jackie” Massey for her lifetime of distinguished public
service and extraordinary leadership in affordable housing, neighborhood revitalization,
resident empowerment, and community development in Ward 8 and throughout the
District of Columbia.
WHEREAS, Jacqueline “Jackie” Massey has devoted decades of service to improving the
quality of life for residents of Southeast Washington, D.C., and is recognized as a respected
community leader, housing advocate, and champion for Ward 8 families;
WHEREAS, Jacqueline “Jackie” Massey emerged as a trusted voice for residents of the
Valley Green community during her 55 years as a Valley Green resident, serving as President of
the Valley Green Resident Council during a pivotal period in the neighborhood’s history and
demonstrating exceptional courage, perseverance, and leadership in advocating for families
living in public housing;
WHEREAS, Jacqueline “Jackie” Massey, through her steadfast leadership and
collaborative spirit, worked alongside residents, public officials, housing agencies, nonprofit
organizations, and community stakeholders to help transform the former Valley Green public
housing community into the vibrant Wheeler Creek neighborhood, the development of which
created new opportunities for affordable housing, homeownership, economic development,
family stability, and a renewed sense of pride among residents;
WHEREAS, Jacqueline “Jackie” Massey consistently championed the belief that
residents should have a meaningful voice in shaping the future of their communities and
empowered families to participate in planning, redevelopment, and neighborhood decision-
making while promoting dignity, self-determination, and collective responsibility;
WHEREAS, Jacqueline “Jackie” Massey served as Executive Director of the Wheeler
Creek Community Development Corporation, where she provided leadership, developed
community partnerships, and advanced programs supporting affordable housing, resident

WHEREAS, Jacqueline “Jackie” Massey’s dedication to affordable housing and resident

WHEREAS, Jacqueline “Jackie” Massey’s advocacy extended beyond housing
development to include support for financial literacy, workforce development, educational
advancement, youth engagement, family services, and pathways to homeownership and
economic mobility;
WHEREAS, Jacqueline “Jackie” Massey has demonstrated throughout her career an
unwavering commitment to ensuring that residents have access to safe and affordable housing,
opportunities for economic advancement, and a meaningful role in the decisions affecting their
neighborhoods;
WHEREAS, Jacqueline “Jackie” Massey has earned the respect and admiration of
residents, elected officials, housing professionals, and community organizations throughout the
District of Columbia through decades of distinguished public service, neighborhood advocacy,
and civic leadership; and
WHEREAS, Jacqueline “Jackie” Massey’s legacy is reflected not only in the physical
transformation of Valley Green into Wheeler Creek, but also in the families she has supported,
the residents she has empowered, the leaders she has inspired, and the stronger community she
helped build.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Jacqueline “Jackie” Massey Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia honors and celebrates Jacqueline
“Jackie” Massey for her exceptional leadership, distinguished public service, and enduring
commitment to affordable housing, community development, resident empowerment, and the
