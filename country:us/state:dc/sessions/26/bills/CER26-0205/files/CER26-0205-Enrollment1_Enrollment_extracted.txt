ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-205
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To recognize, acknowledge, and celebrate Adult Education and Family Literacy Week in the
District of Columbia.
WHEREAS, an estimated 22 percent of District of Columbia residents ages 16 to 74 have
literacy skills at or below the lowest level of proficiency, and approximately 34,500 residents age
25 and older lack a high school diploma;
WHEREAS, 96 percent of jobs in the District require at least a high school diploma or
some postsecondary education, including 63 percent that typically require a postsecondary degree,
underscoring the importance of adult education to meeting the workforce needs of residents and
employers;
WHEREAS, District residents with a high school diploma earn, on average, at least $5,000
more annually than residents without one, demonstrating that adult education is a vital investment
in individual economic mobility, the local talent pipeline, and the District’s long-term prosperity;
WHEREAS, adult education and family literacy programs open doors to opportunity by
helping adults earn high school equivalency diplomas, improve English language skills, prepare
for college and careers, strengthen digital literacy and the ability to use AI and other emerging
technologies, and build the skills needed for lifelong success;
WHEREAS, a strong adult education system strengthens our workforce, helps employers
meet growing talent needs, increases economic mobility, and builds healthier, more resilient
families and communities;
WHEREAS, the District of Columbia is a national leader in adult education, recognizing
that learning, opportunity, and economic mobility should remain accessible throughout every stage
of life;
WHEREAS, the District demonstrates its distinctive commitment to adult learners by
including adult education in its per-pupil funding formula, providing sustained public investment
in residents pursuing foundational skills, secondary credentials, postsecondary education, and
career pathways. This funding approach affirms that the potential of District residents is not limited

by age and that investing in adult learners strengthens families, communities, the workforce, and
the District’s economy;
WHEREAS, by supporting adult education as an integral part of its public education
system, the District of Columbia serves as a model for jurisdictions seeking to expand educational
opportunity and advance economic mobility for residents of all ages;
WHEREAS, the District’s investment in adult education reflects its belief that every
resident deserves the opportunity to build knowledge, develop skills, achieve personal and
professional goals, and contribute fully to the civic and economic life of the nation’s capital; and
WHEREAS, District of Columbia Adult Education and Family Literacy Week recognizes
the achievements of adult learners, honors the educators, volunteers, and partners who support
them, and raises awareness of the essential role adult education plays in strengthening families,
communities, and the nation.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “District of Columbia Adult Education and Family Literacy Week
Ceremonial Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council recognizes and celebrates the important role adult learners play in
building a stronger, better educated, and more connected District of Columbia, and declares
September 13th through September 19th, 2026 as “Adult Education and Family Literacy Week” in
the District of Columbia.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
