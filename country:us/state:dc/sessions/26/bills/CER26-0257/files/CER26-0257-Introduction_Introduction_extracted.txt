12 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
13 __________
14
15 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
16 __________
17
18
19 To honor and recognize the Office of the Inspector General (OIG) for its vigilance in
20 conducting accounting, auditing, and financial oversight of District agencies in order to promote
21 efficient and effective governance.
22
23 WHEREAS, the Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the essential role that the
24 Office of the Inspector General plays in promoting accountability, integrity, transparency, and
25 public confidence in the operation of the DC government;
26
27 WHEREAS, the Office of the Inspector General implements the Annual Comprehensive
28 Financial Report (ACFR), which evaluates the adequacy of internal controls, making specific
29 recommendations and evaluating progress towards improving openness and transparency;
30
31 WHEREAS, Interim Inspector General Matthew Wilcoxson has served at the OIG since
32 2015, leading the Office’s efforts to assess risk and improve processes, strengthen controls, and
33 ensure good governance across District agencies;
34
35 WHEREAS, former Inspector General Daniel Lucas led staff throughout two terms
36 successfully implementing a whole-of-government approach, identifying cross-agency issues,
37 providing informed recommendations, conducting oversight of pandemic programs, and
38 reviewing allegations of sexual assault;
39
40 WHEREAS, the dedicated staff of the Office conducts rigorous investigations,
41 independent audits, and thorough inspections to identify waste, fraud, abuse, mismanagement,
42 and areas for improvement in District government programs and operations;
43
44 WHEREAS, the Medicaid Fraud Control Unit investigates complaints of Medicaid fraud
45 and allegations of criminal abuse, neglect, and financial exploitation within the Medicaid

46 program, collaborating with federal partners to prosecute offenders and using Body-Worn
47 Cameras to document its methodical investigations;
48
49 WHEREAS, the Office has worked to reduce the timeframe for completing investigations
50 and audits and, in response to Council request, sought to provide preliminary results to help
51 agencies respond more quickly to concerns;
52
53 WHEREAS, through its oversight and recommendations, the Office of the Inspector
54 General helps District government agencies strengthen internal controls, improve operational
55 efficiency, safeguard public resources, and better serve the residents of the District of Columbia;
56
57 WHEREAS, the Council acknowledges the dedication and professionalism of the
58 leadership and staff of the Office of the Inspector General in carrying out their responsibilities
59 with diligence, independence, and a commitment to the public interest;
60
61 WHEREAS, the Council further recognizes the importance of effective independent
62 oversight in maintaining public trust and ensuring that District resources are used responsibly in
63 accordance with the laws and regulations governing the duties of this office; and
64
65 WHEREAS, Councilmember Anita Bonds wishes to express her sincere appreciation to
66 the Office of the Inspector General for its service to DC and for its contributions to strengthening
67 the accountability and effectiveness of District government operations.
68
69 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
70 resolution may be cited as the “Office of the Inspector General Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
71
72 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the Office of the Inspector
73 General and expresses its sincere appreciation to leadership and staff for their commitment and
74 independent oversight, responsible stewardship of public funds, and promotion of integrity and
75 accountability throughout the District government.
76
77 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
78 the District of Columbia Register.
