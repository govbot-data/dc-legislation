5 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
6 ______________
7
8 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
9 _________________
10
11
12 To recognize, acknowledge, and celebrate Adult Education and Family Literacy Week in the
13 District of Columbia.
14
15 WHEREAS, an estimated 22 percent of District of Columbia residents ages 16 to 74 have
16 literacy skills at or below the lowest level of proficiency, and approximately 34,500 residents age
17 25 and older lack a high school diploma;
18
19 WHEREAS, 96 percent of jobs in the District require at least a high school diploma or
20 some postsecondary education, including 63 percent that typically require a postsecondary degree,
21 underscoring the importance of adult education to meeting the workforce needs of residents and
22 employers;
23
24 WHEREAS, District residents with a high school diploma earn, on average, at least $5,000
25 more annually than residents without one, demonstrating that adult education is a vital investment
26 in individual economic mobility, the local talent pipeline, and the District’s long-term prosperity;
27
28 WHEREAS, adult education and family literacy programs open doors to opportunity by
29 helping adults earn high school equivalency diplomas, improve English language skills, prepare
30 for college and careers, strengthen digital literacy and the ability to use AI and other emerging
31 technologies, and build the skills needed for lifelong success;
32
33 WHEREAS, a strong adult education system strengthens our workforce, helps employers
34 meet growing talent needs, increases economic mobility, and builds healthier, more resilient
35 families and communities;
36
37 WHEREAS, the District of Columbia is a national leader in adult education, recognizing
38 that learning, opportunity, and economic mobility should remain accessible throughout every stage
39 of life;
40
41 WHEREAS, the District demonstrates its distinctive commitment to adult learners by
42 including adult education in its per-pupil funding formula, providing sustained public investment
43 in residents pursuing foundational skills, secondary credentials, postsecondary education, and
44 career pathways. This funding approach affirms that the potential of District residents is not limited
45 by age and that investing in adult learners strengthens families, communities, the workforce, and
46 the District’s economy;

47
48 WHEREAS, by supporting adult education as an integral part of its public education
49 system, the District of Columbia serves as a model for jurisdictions seeking to expand educational
50 opportunity and advance economic mobility for residents of all ages;
51
52 WHEREAS, the District’s investment in adult education reflects its belief that every
53 resident deserves the opportunity to build knowledge, develop skills, achieve personal and
54 professional goals, and contribute fully to the civic and economic life of the nation’s capital; and
55
56 WHEREAS, District of Columbia Adult Education and Family Literacy Week recognizes
57 the achievements of adult learners, honors the educators, volunteers, and partners who support
58 them, and raises awareness of the essential role adult education plays in strengthening families,
59 communities, and the nation.
60
61 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
62 resolution may be cited as the “District of Columbia Adult Education and Family Literacy Week
63 Ceremonial Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
64 Sec. 2. The Council recognizes and celebrates the important role adult learners play in
65 building a stronger, better educated, and more connected District of Columbia, and declares
66 September 13th through September 19th, 2026 as “Adult Education and Family Literacy Week” in
67 the District of Columbia.
68 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
