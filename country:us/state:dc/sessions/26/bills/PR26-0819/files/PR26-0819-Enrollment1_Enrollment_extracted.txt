ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-520
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 7, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency, due to congressional review, with respect to the need
to amend the Department of For-Hire Vehicles Establishment Act of 1985 to clarify the
definition of a private vehicle-for-hire operator.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Private Vehicle-for-Hire Operator Clarification Congressional
Review Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council has passed emergency and temporary legislation to clarify that the
Department of For-Hire Vehicles (“DFHV”) has had, and continues to have, the authority to
address the range of threats to passenger and driver safety caused by illegal for-hire vehicle
operators and companies, empowering DFHV to continue enforcing registration, insurance, and
trade dress requirements. However, the Council anticipates a potential lapse in the law while the
most recent temporary legislation undergoes congressional review. Further emergency legislation
is therefore necessary.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the Private
Vehicle-for-Hire Operator Clarification Congressional Review Emergency Amendment Act of
2026 be adopted after a single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
