ENROLLED ORIGINAL
AN ACT
__________
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
__________
To amend, on an emergency basis, the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic Development
Limited Grant-Making Authority Act of 2012 to expand permissible uses of certain grant
funding; and to amend the District of Columbia Taxicab Commission Establishment Act
of 1985 to permit rulemaking to implement a carrier-for-hire support surcharge.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited as the “Budget Support Act Implementation Facilitation Emergency
Amendment Act of 2026”.
Sec. 2. Section 2032(pp)(3) of the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic
Development Limited Grant-Making Authority Act of 2012, effective September 20, 2012 (D.C.
Law 19-168; D.C. Official Code § 1-328.04(pp)(3)), is amended by striking the phrase “purchase
of the real property” and inserting the phrase “purchase of or capital improvements to the real
property” in its place.
Sec. 3. Section 208j-8(e) of the District of Columbia Taxicab Commission Establishment
Act of 1985, effective March 21, 2025 (D.C. Law 6-97; D.C. Official Code § 50-301.29h(e)), is
amended by adding a new paragraph (4) to read as follows:
“(4) DFHV, pursuant to Title I of the District of Columbia Administrative
Procedure Act, approved October 21, 1968 (82 Stat. 1204; D.C. Official Code § 2-501 et seq.),
may issue rules to implement the provisions of this subsection.”.
Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Budget Director as the fiscal impact
statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved
October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).

Sec. 5. Effective date.
This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), and shall remain in effect for no longer than
90 days, as provided for emergency acts of the Council of the District of Columbia in section
412(a) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 788;
D.C. Official Code § 1-204.12(a)).
