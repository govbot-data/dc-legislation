Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Enclosed for consideration and adoption by the Council of the District of Columbia is a bill
entitled “Poplar Point Acquisition and Disposition Approval Act of 2026”.
Poplar Point is the District’s last major undeveloped waterfront property, which the District has
been working to acquire for decades. In 2006, Congress enacted the Federal and District of
Columbia Government Real Property Act of 2006 (“Federal Act”), which directs the National
Park Service (“NPS”) to transfer Poplar Point to the District upon satisfaction by the District of
certain requirements, including identifying a location for the relocation of the NPS facilities on
Poplar Point.
The Office of the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic Development has negotiated a land
swap between the District, the Department of the Navy, and developers, RB WNY LLC and CQ
Metro Land LLC, that will satisfy the requirements of the Federal Act, unlock more than 70
acres of riverfront parkland, and ultimately allow for the development of more than 40 acres.
This bill will authorize the Mayor to dispose of two parcels of real property located at Poplar
Point. Additionally, the bill will enable the District to acquire Lot 1041 in Square 5860 in order
to extend Howard Road to create an additional entry point into Poplar Point.
I urge the Council to take prompt and favorable action on the enclosed legislation. To facilitate a
response to any questions the Council may have regarding this bill, please contact Nina Albert,
Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic Development, at 202-727-6365.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowser

JBAB/Poplar Point Land-Swap
This term sheet (“Term Sheet”) is dated September 9, 2026 and is being executed by (i) the District
of Columbia, acting by and through the Office of the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic
Development (“District”), (ii) RB WNY LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (together with
its assigns, “RB WNY”), and (iii) and CQ Metro Land LLC, a Delaware limited liability company
(“Parcel 7 Owner”). Each of District, RB WNY and Parcel 7 Owner are referred to herein as a
“Party” and collectively as the “Parties”.
The purpose of this Term Sheet is to summarize the general terms of the proposed land-swap
transactions among the District, RB WNY, and Parcel 7 Owner and shall not, and is not intended
to, create any legally binding commitment or obligations on any Party.
Background: Pursuant to the Federal and District of Columbia Government Real
Property Act of 2006, Pub. L. 109-396, Dec. 15, 2006, 120 Stat.
2711 (the “Act”), the National Park Service (“NPS”) is required to
transfer Poplar Point (as defined in the Act) to the District upon the
satisfaction of certain conditions, which include the certification by
NPS that the District has adopted a land-use plan for Poplar Point
(the “Land Use Plan”).
The Act provides that the Land Use Plan must identify certain NPS
facilities that are currently located on Poplar Point (the “NPS
Facilities”) and provide for the relocation of those facilities prior to
Poplar Point becoming available for development by the District.
The Department of the Navy, by and through the Naval Facilities
Engineering Systems Command Washington (“Navy”), has
jurisdiction over the north parcel of land within Joint Base
Anacostia Bolling (“JBAB”). RB WNY has an option to purchase
from the Navy approximately 32 acres of such north parcel within
JBAB (the “Redbrick Option Property”).
After many years of analysis and review with the District, NPS has
determined that JBAB is their preferred location for the NPS
Facilities relocation.
The Parties will identify a 12-acre portion of the Redbrick Option
Property (“Parcel A”) as the potential location for the relocation of
the NPS Facilities from Poplar Point.
In order to facilitate the transfer and development of Poplar Point,
the Parties desire to structure a land-swap transaction wherein: (1)
RB WNY will terminate its option with respect to Parcel A, (2) Navy

transfer to Navy a portion of Poplar Point (for re-transfer to RB
WNY pursuant to a separate agreement between Navy and RB
WNY). Such portion of Poplar Point shall consist of approximately
12.5 acres generally proximate to the Bridge District (“Parcel B”).
The Parties also desire to structure a separate land-swap transaction

Conclusion
Funds are sufficient in the revised fiscal year 2026 budget and the fiscal year 2027 through fiscal year
2030 budget and financial plan to implement the bill. The District’s Capital Improvements Plan
includes over $28 million to complete the design of new National Park Service (NPS) facilities, whose
relocation is the first step in finalizing several land transfers in and around Poplar Point.
Background
In 2006 Congress required NPS to transfer Poplar Point to the District subject to an approved land-
use plan.1 Poplar Point is federally owned land located along the Anacostia River between the
Frederick Douglass Memorial Bridge and the 11th Street Bridge. Poplar Point contains parkland and
NPS operational facilities.
The bill authorizes the Mayor to dispose of certain Poplar Point parcels and acquire other property
through various land swaps with federal partners and developers, consistent with the proposed
terms of the transaction and the land-use plan. As part of the transaction, the District must relocate
the NPS facilities on Poplar Point. NPS has determined that the best location for these facilities is west
of South Capitol Street, S.E. along the Anacostia River, on property currently owned by the Navy as

part of Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling (JBAB). However, a private developer, RB WNY LLC, has the right
to acquire 32 acres of land located on the northern part of JBAB, including the desired land for the
relocation of NPS facilities. To facilitate the relocation, the developer will relinquish their right to
acquire twelve of the 32 acres of JBAB land. In exchange for the developer relinquishing its rights, the
District will transfer control of twelve acres of the land it receives at Poplar Point from the federal
government to the Navy, who will then transfer it to the developer through a separate agreement.
In addition to the above transactions, the bill authorizes the District to complete a land swap with a
second developer, CQ Metro Land LLC, who owns a parcel of land along Howard Road, S.E.,2 just north
of Interstate 295. The District will give CQ Metro Land LLC a 0.5 acre parcel on Poplar Point in
exchange for the land. The District is executing this land swap to accommodate the construction of
an access roadway from Howard Road, S.E. into Poplar Point. RB WNY LLC and CQ Metro Land LLC
are affiliated developers known collectively as Redbrick LMD.
The District must first have the land-use plan approved by NPS, and any development at Poplar Point
will be restricted until the District constructs the new NPS facilities on the formerly JBAB land and
they are ready to be occupied.
The bill sunsets the Mayor’s authority to dispose of any properties to the Navy or CQ Metro Land LLC
ten years after the bill’s effective date. Despite the bill’s sunset date, the terms of the transaction
require the land transactions that have RB WNY LLC relinquishing its rights to twelve acres of the
JBAB land and the District transferring twelve acres to the Navy for RB WNY LLC to close by June 30,
2029,3 unless otherwise extended by the Redbrick development team. The terms also require the
District to complete the land swap with CQ Metro Land LLC by June 30, 2030, unless otherwise
extended by the Redbrick development team.
Financial Plan Impact
Funds are sufficient in the revised fiscal year 2026 budget and fiscal year 2027 through fiscal year
2030 budget and financial plan to implement the bill. The bill authorizes the disposal of a 12.5-acre
parcel of the land it receives from NPS as part of the Poplar Point transfer to facilitate the
development of new NPS facilities and a land swap with a private developer to construct an access
road to Poplar Point. The lynchpin in the term sheet to transfer ownership of the various parcels of
land on Poplar Point and JBAB is the District designing and constructing new facilities for NPS
operations. The District’s 6-year Capital Improvements Plan contains over $28 million through fiscal
year 2029 to complete environmental assessments at Poplar Point and design the NPS facilities. The
District’s budget does not include funding for the construction of the NPS facilities, but the extent of
that fiscal need will not be known until the design activities are complete.
