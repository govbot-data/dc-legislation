Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Enclosed for consideration and adoption by the Council of the District of Columbia are proposed
resolutions titled “Madrone – DC Student Housing I, LLC Revenue Bonds Project Emergency
Declaration Resolution of 2026” and “Madrone – DC Student Housing I, LLC, Revenue Bonds
Project Emergency Approval Resolution of 2026”.
The resolutions authorize the issuance, sale, and delivery of tax-exempt revenue bonds, notes, or
other obligations in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed $275,000,000. The project
financed by these obligations will turn an empty 10-story office building at 600 E Street, NW
(Ward 2) into 415 apartments for college students, faculty, and staff. The furnished units will be
offered at a discount to help graduate students afford to live closer to campus. Residents will also
have access to a fitness center, co-working study spaces, and about 140 underground parking
spaces. Additionally, a new Sprouts Farmers Market is expected to open on the ground floor,
providing a full-service grocery store for both the residents and the surrounding neighborhood.
In accordance with section 490 of the Home Rule Act, it has been determined that the bonds, when,
as, and if issued, shall be without recourse to the District. The bonds shall not be general
obligations of the District; shall not be a pledge of or involve the full faith and credit or the taxing
power of the District; shall not constitute a debt of the District; and shall not constitute a lending
of public credit for a private undertaking as prohibited in section 602(a)(2) of the Home Rule Act.
The bonds shall not give rise to any pecuniary liability of the District, and the District shall have
no obligation with respect to the purchase of the bonds.
I urge the Council to take prompt and favorable action on the enclosed measures.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowser
