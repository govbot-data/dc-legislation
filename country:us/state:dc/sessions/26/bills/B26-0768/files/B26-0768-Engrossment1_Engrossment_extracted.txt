11 To amend, on an temporary basis, the Green Building Act of 2006 to exempt certain District owned
12 and funded residential and nonresidential projects from complying with net-zero-energy
13 standards and to modify net-zero-energy standard compliance requirements for
14 nonresidential and residential projects; to amend section 2 of the Clean Energy DC Building
15 Code Amendment Act of 2022 to modify the definition of the net-zero-energy standard and
16 the types of projects to which the net-zero-energy standard applies; and for other purposes.
17
18 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this act
19 may be cited as the “Net Zero Continuity Temporary Amendment Act of 2026”.
