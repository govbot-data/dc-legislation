ENGROSSED ORIGINAL
A BILL
26-651
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
_________________________
1 To amend the Robert F. Kennedy Memorial Stadium and the District of Columbia National
2 Guard Armory Public Safety Act to include the Football Stadium, Downtown Arena, and
3 the Southeast Arena in the prohibition on unauthorized entry onto a playing field or
4 playing surface.
5
6 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
7 act may be cited as the “Arena Safety Conformity Amendment Act of 2026”.
8 Sec. 2. The Robert F. Kennedy Memorial Stadium and the District of Columbia National
9 Guard Armory Public Safety Act, effective November 3, 1977, (D.C. Law 2-37; D.C. Official
10 Code § 3-341 et seq.), is amended as follows:
11 (a) Section 3a (D.C. Official Code § 3-342.01) is amended as follows:
12 (1) New paragraphs (1A) and (1B) are added to read as follows:
13 “(1A) “Downtown Arena” means a sports and entertainment arena located at 601
14 F St. N.W.
1

ENGROSSED ORIGINAL
15 “(1B) “Football Stadium” means a sports and entertainment complex located at or
16 around 2400 East Capitol Street, S.W., the former site of the Robert F. Kennedy Memorial
17 Stadium.”.
18 (2) A new paragraph (3) is added to read as follows:
19 “(3) “Southeast Arena” means a sports and entertainment arena located at 1100
20 Oak Dr. S.E.”.
21 (b) Section 4b (D.C. Official Code § 3-343.02) is amended as follows:
22 (1) Subsection (a) is amended to read as follows:
23 “(a) Unless expressly authorized by the operator of the Football Stadium or its duly
24 authorized agents, no person shall at any time enter onto any portion of the playing field within
25 the Football Stadium.”.
26 (2) New subsections (b-2) and (b-3) are added to read as follows:
27 “(b-2) Unless expressly authorized by the operator of the Downtown Arena or its duly
28 authorized agents, no person shall at any time enter onto any portion of the playing surface
29 within the Downtown Arena.
30 “(b-3) Unless expressly authorized by the Washington Convention and Sports Authority,
31 the lessee or operator of the Southeast Arena, or their duly authorized agents, no person shall at
32 any time enter onto any portion of the playing surface within the Southeast Arena.”.
33 (3) Subsection (c) is amended to read as follows:
2

34 “(c) For the purposes of this section, the term “playing field” or “playing surface”, as
35 applicable, means the area encompassed by the seating facilities within the Football Stadium, the
36 Baseball Stadium, the Soccer Stadium, the Downtown Arena, or the Southeast Arena, as such
37 seating facilities may be arranged from time to time.”.
38 Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.
39 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal
40 impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
41 approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
42 Sec. 4. Effective date.
43 This act shall take effect after approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
44 Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto) and a 30-day period of congressional review
45 as provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December
46 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)).
