15 To declare the existence of an emergency, due to congressional review, with respect to the need
16 to amend section 1507 of the Criminal Justice Coordinating Council for the District of
17 Columbia Establishment Act of 2001 to change the date by which the Prearrest Diversion
18 Task Force shall issue initial recommendations for prearrest diversion of certain
19 misdemeanor offenses and categories of persons to July 2026.

21 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
22 resolution may be cited as the “Prearrest Diversion Task Force Recommendations Congressional
23 Review Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
24 Sec. 2. (a) On July 14, 2026, the Council passed the Prearrest Diversion Task Force
25 Recommendations Emergency Amendment Act of 2026, effective July 30, 2025 (D.C. Act 26-
26 386; 73 DCR 11163) (“emergency act”), which expires on October 28, 2026.
27 (b) On September 22, 2026, the Council passed the Prearrest Diversion Task Force
28 Recommendations Temporary Amendment Act of 2026, enacted on _________, 2026 (D.C. Act 26-___;
29 __ DCR ___) (“temporary act”), which does not yet have a projected law date.
30 (c) This congressional review emergency legislation is necessary to prevent a gap in the
31 law between the expiration of the emergency act and the effective date of the temporary act.

33 enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
34 Prearrest Diversion Task Force Recommendations Congressional Review Emergency
35 Amendment Act of 2026 be adopted after a single reading.
