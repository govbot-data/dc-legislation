25 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26 __________
27
28 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
29 __________
30
31 To honor and recognize Robert Vinson Brannum, a native Washingtonian, United States
32 Air Force veteran, community leader, civil rights advocate, tireless champion for the people of
33 the District of Columbia, and outstandingly active member of the Ward 5 community.
34
35 WHEREAS, Robert Vinson Brannum has devoted his life to the principle that being a
36 resident of the District carries with it a responsibility to serve the District;
37
38 WHEREAS, Mr. Brannum has demonstrated his commitment through decades of
39 military, civic, veterans, and community service;
40
41 WHEREAS, as a veteran of the United States Air Force, Mr. Brannum proudly served his
42 country and has continued his tradition of service by advocating for veterans and ensuring that
43 their voices are heard and their contributions recognized;
44
45 WHEREAS, Mr. Brannum has been a longtime leader within the NAACP Washington,

46 DC Branch, including serving as Chair of its Veterans Committee, where he has worked to
47 advance the interests of veterans while carrying forward NAACP's historic commitment to
48 equality, justice, and civil rights;
49
50 WHEREAS, Robert Vinson Brannum has been a steadfast advocate for full voting rights
51 and statehood for the District of Columbia, upholding with conviction the premise that residents
52 of the Nation's Capital deserve the same democratic voice and representation as residents of
53 every state in the United States;
54
55 WHEREAS, his commitment to our city is not simply political but deeply personal,
56 reflecting his identity as a native Washingtonian and his belief that everyone in the District
57 deserves a government that listens to and serves them;
58
59 WHEREAS, Mr. Brannum has also given his time to neighborhood and civic affairs,
60 including service as an Advisory Neighborhood Commissioner;
61
62 WHEREAS, his dedication to public service has extended well beyond the boundaries of
63 any one organization or cause but has earned him the reputation of consistently being willing to
64 speak up, advocate, and work on behalf of principles in which he believes;
65
66 WHEREAS, in 2017, Robert Vinson Brannum's commitment to Democratic principles
67 led him to seek the chairmanship of the Democratic National Committee; and
68
69 WHEREAS, Robert Vinson Brannum has remained true to the values of service, justice,
70 equality, democracy, and community.
71
72 RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
73 resolution may be cited as the “Robert Vinson Brannum Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
74
75 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors Robert Vinson
76 Brannum as a proud Washingtonian and United States Air Force veteran who has engaged in a
77 lifetime of military and community service, advocacy on behalf of veterans and civil rights, and
78 unwavering commitment to the people and the future of the District of Columbia.
79
80 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
81 the District of Columbia Register.
