3 A BILL
4 _____
5 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
6 _____
7 To amend the Police Officer and Firefighter Cadet Programs Funding Authorization and Human
8 Rights Act of 1977 Amendment Act of 1982 to expand the cadet program to include
9 graduates of District high schools ages 25 to 40 years of age and require those who
10 complete the cadet program to commit to serving at least 5 consecutive years with the
11 Metropolitan Police Department or the District of Columbia Fire and Emergency Medical
12 Service.
13
14 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
15 act may be cited as the “Law Enforcement Cadet Pathways Expansion Amendment Act of 2026”.
16 Sec. 2. Section 2 of the Police Officer and Firefighter Cadet Programs Funding
17 Authorization and Human Rights Act of 1977 Amendment Act of 1982, effective March 9, 1983
18 (D.C. Law 4-172; D.C. Official Code § 5-109.01), is amended as follows:
19 (a) Subsection (a)(2) is amended to read as follows:
20 “(2) The police officer cadet program established in paragraph (1) of this
21 subsection shall be composed of the following persons residing in the District, who shall have
22 substantial ties to the District, such as currently or formerly residing, attending school, or
23 working in the District for a significant period of time:
24 “(A) Senior-year high school students;
1

25 “(B) High school graduates under 25 years of age; and
26 “(C) Graduates of District of Columbia high schools ages 25 years of age
27 to 40 years of age.”.
28 (b) Subsection (b) is amended to read as follows:
29 “(b) A person successfully completing the required training and service in a cadet
30 program established pursuant to this section shall be accorded full preference for appointment as
31 a member of MPD or of the Fire and Emergency Medical Services Department, if the person
32 shall have met all other requirements pertaining to membership in the chosen Department, and
33 must serve at least five years consecutively as a member of MPD or of the Fire and Emergency
34 Medical Services Department.”
35 Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.
36 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal
37 impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
38 approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
39 Sec. 4. Effective date.
40 This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
41 Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto) and a 30-day period of congressional review
42 as provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December
43 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)).
