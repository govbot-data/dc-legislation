14 To amend, on a temporary basis, Chapter 10 of Title 47 of the District of Columbia Official Code
15 to provide a property tax exemption for the property situated on Lot 0005, Square 3766 at
16 219 Riggs Road, NE.
17
18 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
19 act may be cited as the “Food & Friends Property Tax Exemption Temporary Amendment Act
20 of 2026”.
21 Sec. 2. Chapter 10 of Title 47 of the District of Columbia Official Code is amended as
22 follows:
23 (a) The table of contents is amended by adding a new section designation to read as
24 follows:
25 “47-1099.15. Food & Friends, Inc.; Lot 0005, Square 3766.”.
26 (b) A new section 47-1099.15 is added to read as follows:
27 “§ 47-1099.15. Food & Friends, Inc.; Lot 0005, Square 3766.
28 “(a) The real property, and any improvements on the property, located at 219 Riggs
29 Road, NE, known for tax and assessment purposes as Lot 0005, Square 3766 (“Property”), shall
30 be exempt from the tax imposed by Chapter 8 of this title; provided, that the Property is owned
31 by Food & Friends, Inc.; and provided further, that Food & Friends, Inc., or any lessee of the
1

32 Property, continues to use the Property to provide charitable food distribution or related services.
33 “(b) This exemption, based on the square footage of the site, shall apply to 100% of the
34 land.
35 “(c) The tax exemption provided pursuant to this section shall be in addition to and not in
36 lieu of any other tax relief or assistance from any other source applicable to Food & Friends,
37 Inc.”.
38 Sec. 3. Applicability.
39 This act shall apply as of October 1, 2025.
40 Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
41 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Budget Director as the fiscal impact
42 statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved
43 October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
44 Sec. 5. Effective date.
45 (a) This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by
46 the Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), and a 30-day period of congressional
47 review as provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved
48 December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)).
49 (b) This act shall expire after 225 days of having taken effect.
