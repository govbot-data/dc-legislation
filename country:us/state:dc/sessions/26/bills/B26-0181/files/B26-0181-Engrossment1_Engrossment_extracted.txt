10 To amend the Universal Paid Leave Amendment Act of 2016 to prohibit private disability insurance
11 providers from reducing short-term disability benefits based on actual or estimated paid leave
12 benefits to which an eligible individual may be entitled to from the District, regardless of the
13 jurisdiction in which the insurance policy was issued or written; and to amend the Insurance
14 Trade and Economic Development Amendment Act of 2000 to make the prohibition on
15 offsetting or reducing benefits under a private market short-term disability insurance policy
16 based on estimated or actual benefits received under the Universal Paid Leave Amendment Act
17 of 2016 enforceable under that law, regardless of the jurisdiction in which the insurance policy
18 was issued or written.
19
20 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
21 act may be cited as the “Short-Term Disability Insurance Benefit Protection Clarification Amendment
22 Act of 2026”.
23 Sec. 2. Section 107(j)(1) of the Universal Paid Leave Amendment Act of 2016, effective April 7,
24 2017 (D.C. Law 21-264; D.C. Official Code § 32-541.07(j)(1)), is amended to read as follows:
25 “(j)(1) No insurer may offset or reduce benefits or income available to an eligible individual
26 under an individual or group policy for temporary or short-term disability insurance based on estimated
27 or actual benefits the eligible individual may or does receive under this act, regardless of the jurisdiction
28 in which such policy was issued, executed, written, or delivered.”

29 Sec. 3. Section 120a the Insurance Trade and Economic Development Amendment Act of 2000,
30 effective November 13, 2021 (D.C. Law 24-45; D.C. Official Code § 31-2231.20a), is amended as
31 follows:
32 (a) Subsection (a) is amended to read as follows:
33 “(a) No insurer may offset or reduce benefits or income available to an eligible individual
34 under a temporary or short-term disability insurance policy, based on estimated or actual benefits the
35 eligible individual may or does receive under the Universal Paid Leave Amendment Act of 2016,
36 effective April 7, 2017 (D.C. Law 21-264; D.C. Official Code § 32-541.01 et seq.), regardless of in
37 which jurisdiction such policy was executed, written, or delivered.”.
38 (b) Subsection (c) is amended to read as follows:
39 “(c) For the purposes of this section, the term:
40 “(1) “Eligible individual” shall have the same meaning as provided in section 101(6) of
41 the Universal Paid Leave Amendment Act of 2016, effective April 7, 2017 (D.C. Law 21-264; D.C.
42 Official Code § 32-541.01(6)).
43 “(2) “Self-insured employer” shall have the same meaning as provided in section
44 101(19A) of the Universal Paid Leave Amendment Act of 2016, effective April 7, 2017 (D.C. Law 21-
45 264; D.C. Official Code § 32-541.01(19A)).”.
