11 To amend, on a temporary basis, the Procurement Practices Reform Act of 2010 to require the
12 Office of Contracting and Procurement to process requests for the equitable
13 adjustment of homeless services contracts within 45 60 days.
14
15 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
16 Act may be cited as the “Homeless Services Contract Adjustment Temporary Amendment Act of
17 2026”.
18 Sec. 2. The Procurement Practices Reform Act of 2010, effective April 8, 2011 (D.C. Law
19 18-371; D.C. Official Code § 2-351.01 et seq.), is amended by adding a new section 1105a to read
20 as follows:
21 “Sec. 1105a. Equitable adjustments of homeless services contracts.
22 “(a)(1) Within 45 60 calendar days of receiving a complete request for the equitable
23 adjustment of a contract to provide homeless services under section 3 of the Homeless Services
24 Reform Act, effective October 22, 2005 (D.C. Law 16-35; D.C. Official Code § 4-754.01), on the
25 basis of the revision of a wage determination issued by the U.S. Department of Labor under 41
26 U.S.C. § 351, OCP shall determine whether the requesting contractor is entitled to an equitable
27 adjustment.

28 “(2) A request for an equitable adjustment under this subsection is “complete” when
29 the requesting contractor has submitted all information and documents necessary for OCP to assess
30 the request to OCP.
31 “(b) Within 7 business days of the submission to OCP of all information and documents
32 necessary to complete an equitable adjustment request under subsection (a), OCP shall inform the
33 requesting contractor in writing that it considers the request complete.”.
