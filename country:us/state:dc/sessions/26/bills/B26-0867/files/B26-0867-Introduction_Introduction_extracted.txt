4 A BILL
5 _______________
6 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
7 _______________
8 To amend, on an emergency basis, the District of Columbia Traffic Adjudication Act of 1978 to
9 remove the penalty equal to the amount of a civil fine for failure to answer a notice of
10 infraction within 30 days when the infraction has been detected by an automated traffic
11 enforcement system or involves a parking, standing, stopping or pedestrian offense.
12
13 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
14 act may be cited as the “Parking and Traffic Ticket Penalty Reduction Emergency Amendment
15 Act of 2026”.
16 Sec. 2. The District of Columbia Traffic Adjudication Act of 1978, effective September
17 12, 1978 (D.C. Law 2-104; D.C. Official Code § 50-2301.01 et seq.), is amended as follows:
18 (a) Section 105(a)(2)(A) (D.C. Official Code § 50-2301.05(a)(2)(A)) is amended by
19 striking the phrase “and section 305(d)(1)” and inserting the phrase “and section 305(d)” in its
20 place.
21 (b) Section 205(d) (D.C. Official Code § 50-2302.05(d)) is amended to read as follows:
22 “(d) Except for notices of infraction that were detected by an automated traffic
23 enforcement system pursuant to section 901 of the Fiscal Year 1997 Budget Support Act of
1

24 1996, effective April 9, 1997 (D.C. Law 11-198; D.C. Official Code § 50-2209.01), if a person
25 fails to answer a notice of infraction within 30 calendar days of the date the notice was issued, or
26 within a greater period of time as prescribed by the Director by regulation, a penalty equal to the
27 amount of the fine shall be added pursuant to section 105(a).”.
28 (c) Section 304(c) (D.C. Official Code § 50-2303.04(c)) is amended by striking the
29 phrase “The lessor’s failure to answer the notice of infraction within 30 days after mailing shall
30 result in the imposition of monetary penalties established by section 205, in addition to the
31 potential civil fine for the infraction.”.
32 (d) Section 305(d) (D.C. Official Code § 50-2303.05(d)) is amended to read as follows:
33 “(d) A person to whom a notice of infraction has been issued shall answer within 60
34 calendar days of the date the notice was issued. If a person fails to answer within 60 days, or
35 within a greater period of time as prescribed by the Director by regulation, the commission of the
36 infraction shall be deemed admitted and all penalties, fines, and any vehicle conveyance fees
37 shall be assessed. Not more than 50 days after the notice is issued, the Director shall send, by
38 regular mail, to the address in the Department of Motor Vehicles’ records, if such address was
39 supplied to the Department of Motor Vehicles, notice of the outstanding notice of infraction and
40 of the impending deemed admission. This subsection shall not apply to any participant in the
41 fleet adjudication program.”
42 Sec. 3. Applicability.
43 This act shall apply as of October 1, 2030.
44 Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
45 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Budget Director as the fiscal impact
46 statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved
2

47 October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
48 Sec. 5. Effective date.
49 This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
50 Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), and shall remain in effect for no longer than
51 90 days, as provided for emergency acts of the Council of the District of Columbia in section
52 412(a) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 788;
53 D.C. Official Code § 1-204.12(a)).
