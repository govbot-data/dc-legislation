29 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
30
31 __________
32
33 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
34
35 ____________________
36
37 To celebrate the annual 17th Street High Heel Race, a unique and beloved LGBTQ+ tradition in
38 the District of Columbia.
39
40 WHEREAS, on October 31,1986, a group of 25 drag performers and enthusiasts held an
41 informal contest that involved running in high heels from JR’s Bar, up 17th Street, N.W. to
42 Annie’s Paramount Steakhouse, upstairs to the bar for a shot of schnapps, and back;
43
44 WHEREAS, the 17th Street High Heel Race has since become an annual tradition that
45 brings together participants showcasing the diversity, creativity, and vibrancy of the District’s
1

46 lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, queer, and other sexual and gender minority (LGBTQ+)
47 communities, along with thousands of enthusiastic spectators;
48
49 WHEREAS, in 2018, the District of Columbia Office of Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual,
50 Transgender, and Questioning Affairs assumed responsibility for organizing the annual 17th
51 Street High Heel Race;
52
53 WHEREAS, in the face of nationwide attacks on transgender people and drag artists, the
54 District of Columbia government still proudly protects and supports gender and sexual diversity
55 and celebrates the power of drag as a versatile, delightful form of art and play; and
56
57 WHEREAS, on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, the beloved tradition will continue, as drag
58 artists assemble to show off their dazzling drag outfits, and sprint or saunter down a stretch of
59 historic 17th Street, N.W., which remains a hub of LGBTQ+ nightlife.
60
61 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
62 resolution may be cited as the “17th Street High Heel Race Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
63
64 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia celebrates the 17th Street High Heel Race
65 and wishes the participants a fabulous evening.
66
67 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
68 the District of Columbia Register.
