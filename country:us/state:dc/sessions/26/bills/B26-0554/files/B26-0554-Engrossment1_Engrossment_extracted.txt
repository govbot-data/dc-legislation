9 To amend the Uniform Classification and Commercial Driver’s License Act of 1990 to update
10 several references to the U.S. Code regarding the Commercial Motor Vehicle Safety Act,
11 and to modify the standards for disqualification of a person from operation of a
12 commercial motor vehicle.
13 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
14 act may be cited as the “Commercial Driver’s License Amendment Act of 2026”.
15 Sec. 2. The Uniform Classification and Commercial Driver’s License Act of 1990,
16 effective September 20, 1990 (D.C. Law 8-161; D.C. Official Code § 50-401 et seq.), is amended
17 as follows:
18 (a) Section 2(2) (D.C. Official Code § 50-401(2)) is amended by striking the phrase “the
19 informational system established pursuant to the Commercial Motor Vehicle Safety Act of 1986,
20 approved October 27, 1986 (100 Stat. 3207; 49 U.S.C. sec. 2701 et seq.) (“Commercial Motor
21 Vehicle Safety Act”),” and inserting the phrase “the information system maintained by the
22 Secretary of Transportation pursuant to 49 U.S.C. § 31309” in its place.
23 (b) Section 3 (D.C. Official Code § 50-402) is amended as follows:
