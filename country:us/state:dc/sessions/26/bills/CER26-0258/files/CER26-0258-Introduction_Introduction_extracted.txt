12 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
13 __________
14
15 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
16 __________
17
18
19 To recognize the District of Columbia Department of Employment Services (DOES) for
20 the execution of its ongoing mission of providing the necessary tools for the District of Columbia
21 workforce.
22
23 WHEREAS, DOES’ mandate includes helping job seekers be competitive using tailored
24 approaches and offering training programs and support;
25
26 WHEREAS, DOES ensures safe working conditions, advances opportunities for
27 employment, helps employers find qualified workers, and tracks and publicizes labor market
28 information and other national economic measurements impacting the District of Columbia.
29
30 WHEREAS, the Council recognizes the essential role of the Department of Employment
31 Services in strengthening the District’s workforce, expanding employment opportunities, and
32 supporting the economic well-being of District residents;
33
34 WHEREAS, DOES under the stewardship of Dr. Unique Morris-Hughes is the conduit of
35 United States Department of Labor resources devoted to the administration of employment
36 programs, including services for seniors, veterans, youth, and out-of-school young adults, which
37 we look forward to becoming more robust and increasing participation;
38
39 WHEREAS, DOES administers vital employment and workforce development programs
40 that connect job seekers and employers, providing career support and occupational training to
41 help residents develop the skills necessary to compete and grow our economy;
42
43 WHEREAS, DOES administers the Unemployment Compensation Fund supporting
44 residents experiencing unemployment and underemployment, and providing an economic safety
45 net in the wake of federal cuts;
46

47 WHEREAS, DOES administers the First Source program, ensuring District of Columbia
48 residents receive job opportunities and skills training related to large scale development projects
49 across the city, and therefore the agency is challenged to be more effective in holding contractors
50 that receive government dollars accountable to meeting the DC residency requirements;
51
52 WHEREAS, for more than fifteen years, DOES has operated Project Empowerment
53 providing more than 700 residents opportunities to grow through education, training, and
54 subsidized employment placements each year, and expects the agency to strengthen this
55 meaningful work opportunity for returning and justice-involved citizens;
56
57 WHEREAS, DOES works closely with the Workforce Investment Council administering
58 over $10 million in Federal Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act funding annually to
59 support job expansion and job seeker skill alignment;
60
61 WHEREAS, DOES administers the Marion S. Barry Summer Youth Employment
62 Program, providing engagement and work experience for more than 18,000 youth across the
63 District of Columbia and allowing them to accelerate their careers;
64
65 WHEREAS, in 2018, DOES launched the District of Columbia Infrastructure Academy
66 training residents for high-demand careers in construction, energy, telecommunications, road
67 work, and water infrastructure, and, in 2026, opened a $73-million-dollar training facility at the
68 former Spingarn High School;
69
70 WHEREAS, DOES has administered the Paid Family Leave Program since 2020, which,
71 with contributions from private sector employers, provides prenatal, parental, medical, and
72 family leave to those working in the District of Columbia to advance family ties and wellness;
73
74 WHEREAS, through its mission of supporting workforce equity, economic opportunity
75 and professional development, DOES engages in job readiness, training programs, and job
76 placement helping residents overcome employment barriers and achieve financial stability; and
77
78 WHERAS, Councilmember Anita Bonds wishes to express her sincere appreciation to the
79 Department of Employment Services for its service to DC and for its commitment to
80 strengthening the District of Columbia workforce and partnership with the private sector.
81
82 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
83 resolution may be cited as the “District of Columbia Department of Employment Services
84 Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
85
86 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the District of Columbia
87 Department of Employment Services for its continued dedication to empowering and supporting
88 the workforce of the District of Columbia.
89
90 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
91 the District of Columbia Register.
