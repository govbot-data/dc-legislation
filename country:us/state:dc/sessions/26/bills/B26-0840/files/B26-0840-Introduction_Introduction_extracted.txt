Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Enclosed for consideration and enactment by the Council of the District of Columbia is an
emergency bill, the “Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority Stadium-Armory Station
Improvements Fiscal Year 2026 to Fiscal Year 2030 Reimbursable Agreement Emergency Act of
2026,” and the accompanying emergency declaration resolution.
The legislation will approve a multiyear Reimbursable Agreement between the Washington
Metropolitan Area Transit Authority (“WMATA”) and the District Department of Transportation
(“DDOT”), which will provide WMATA with $300,000,000 in local capital funding to design
and construct station improvements at the Stadium-Armory Metrorail Station in time for the
opening of the new RFK Stadium.
My administration is available to discuss any questions you may have regarding the proposed
legislation. In order to facilitate a response to any questions you may have, please contact Sharon
Kershbaum, Director, DDOT, at (202) 671-2317.
I urge the Council to take prompt and favorable action on the enclosed legislation.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowser

Conclusion
Funds are sufficient in the revised fiscal year 2026 budget and the fiscal year 2027 through fiscal year
2030 budget and financial plan to implement the bill. The District’s revised fiscal year 2026 and fiscal
year 2027 through fiscal year 2032 capital improvements plan includes $300 million to support the
multiyear reimbursable agreement between the District and the Washington Metropolitan Area
Transit Authority (WMATA) approved by the bill.
Background
In 2025, the District and the Washington Commanders football team reached an agreement for the
Commanders to return to a stadium in the District for the team’s home games and act as a catalyst
for ancillary development under the Robert F. Kennedy Campus Master Plan. In addition to the
stadium construction and residential, retail, and commercial developments surrounding the stadium,
significant transportation improvements are required to support the stadium and development.
In July 2026, the WMATA Board of Directors authorized the WMATA General Manager to enter into
a multiyear reimbursable agreement with the District to support the design and construction of
improvements to the Stadium-Armory Metro Station. Subsequently, the District and WMATA agreed
WMATA would lead the planning, community engagement, design, construction, testing, and
commissioning of the project with resources provided by the District. The agreement establishes a

Financial Plan Impact
Funds are sufficient in the revised fiscal year 2026 budget and fiscal year 2027 through fiscal year
2030 budget and financial plan to implement the bill. The revised fiscal year 2026 capital plan and
the fiscal year 2027 through fiscal year 2032 capital improvements plan includes $300 million to
support the agreement with WMATA to execute the improvements to the Stadium-Armory Metro
Station that are necessary to support development in the area, including the stadium itself. The $300
million is available over fiscal years 2026 through fiscal year 2029, to align with construction
completion in early 2030. The bill’s approval will allow the Mayor and the Director of the District
Department of Transportation to execute the multiyear reimbursable agreement with WMATA.
