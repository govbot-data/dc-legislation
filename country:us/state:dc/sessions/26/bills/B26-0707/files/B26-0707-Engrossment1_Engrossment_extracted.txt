11 To amend subtitle B of the Rodent Control Act of 2000 to modify prohibited actions under the
12 act, to create a rodent abatement dashboard, and to require the Department of Health to
13 conduct a public education campaign on rodent prevention and control and rodenticide
14 health risks; to amend subtitle A of Title I of the Sustainable Solid Waste Management
15 Amendment Act to provide notice requirements before the removal of a public litter
16 container and to create a pilot program to provide grants to Business Improvement
17 Districts and Main Streets to subsidize or purchase rodent proof trash receptacles; to
18 amend the Pesticide Education and Control Amendment Act of 2012 to prohibit the sale
19 of anticoagulant rodenticide, and to limit the distribution and use of anticoagulant
20 rodenticides by persons not trained or licensed as pesticide applicators; and for other
21 purposes.
22
23 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
24 act may be cited as the “Rodent Education and Management Yields Results Amendment Act of
25 2026”.

152 Sec. 4. The Pesticide Education and Control Amendment Act of 2012, effective Oct. 23,
153 2012 (D.C. Law 19-191; D.C. Official Code § 8-431 et seq.), is amended as follows:
154 (a) Section 2 (D.C. Official Code § 8-431) is amended by adding a new paragraph (1A) to
155 read as follows:
156 “(1A) “Anticoagulant rodenticide” means a pesticide product intended to kill a rat
157 or mouse that has a mode of action that interferes with blood clotting, including products with
158 Brodifacoum, Bromadiolone, Chlorophacinone, Difenacoum, Difethialone, Diphacinonon, or
159 Warfarin, as active ingredients.”.
160 (b) Section 4 (D.C. Official Code § 8-433) is amended by adding a new subsection (d) to
161 read as follows:
162 “(d)(1) It shall be unlawful for a person or entity to sell or otherwise distribute for use
163 anticoagulant rodenticide, and for a person or entity to use anticoagulant rodenticide on public or
164 private property; except, that this subsection shall not apply to the distribution, purchase, or use
165 of anticoagulant rodenticide by:
166 “(A) A District or federal government agency acting within the scope of
167 its official duties;
168 “(B) A contractor acting on behalf of a local or federal government
169 agency; or
