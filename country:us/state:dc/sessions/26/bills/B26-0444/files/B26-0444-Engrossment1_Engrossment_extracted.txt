10 To amend the Food Policy Council and Director Establishment Act of 2014 to change the
11 timeline for the Food Policy Council annual report from annually to triennially and
12 expand the authority of the Food Policy Council to evaluate food procurement practices
13 for District agencies and contractors that regularly purchase food and beverages.
14
15 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
16 act may be cited as the “Food Policy Council Procurement Amendment Act of 2026”.
17 Sec. 2. The Food Policy Council and Director Establishment Act of 2014, effective
18 March 10, 2015 (D.C. Law 20-191; D.C. Official Code § 48-311 et seq.), is amended as follows:
19 (a) Section 2 (D.C. Official Code § 48-311) is amended as follows:
20 (1) Paragraph (1) is redesignated as paragraph (1A).
21 (2) A new paragraph (1) is added to read as follows:
22 “(1) “Covered agency” means a District agency or agency contractor that
23 regularly procures food or beverages. The term “covered agency” shall not include a District
24 agency or agency contractor that procures food or beverages solely for vending machines or one-
25 off catering services for events.”.
26 (b) Section 3(c) (D.C. Official Code § 48-312(c)) is amended to read as follows:
1

27 “(c) Beginning September 30, 2027, and every 3 years thereafter, the Food Policy
28 Council shall provide a report to the Council on the state of the local food system across the
29 District. The report shall assess District food access, including an identification of low food
30 access areas, and the state of the local food economy, and provide policy recommendations to
31 improve the local food economy and food access, including priorities for local investments.”.
32 (c) Section 5 (D.C. Official Code § 48-314) is amended as follows:
33 (1) Subsection (b) is amended as follows:
34 (A) Paragraph (5) is amended by striking the phrase “; and” and inserting
35 a semicolon in its place;
36 (B) Paragraph (6) is amended by striking the period and inserting the
37 phrase “; and” in its place.
38 (C) A new paragraph (7) is added to read as follows:
39 “(7) Oversee efforts to improve the quality of food purchased by covered agencies
40 and to ensure food procured by covered agencies aligns with District requirements, standards,
41 and goals regarding nutrition, the local economy, and environmental sustainability.”.
42 (2) New subsections (c) and (d) are added to read as follows:
43 “(c) To achieve the objectives set forth under subsection (b)(7) of this section, the Office
44 shall:
45 “(1) Advise covered agencies on developing solicitations and contracts that:
46 “(A) Align with District laws, standards, and goals regarding nutrition, the
47 local economy, and environmental sustainability; and
2

48 “(B) Require vendors to regularly report on purchased food products to
49 enable assessment of compliance with purchasing goals;
50 “(2) Collaborate with the Office of Contracting and Procurement to ensure that
51 food service solicitations reach a wide audience of high-quality prospective vendors, including
52 both for-profit and non-profit vendors;
53 “(3) Serve on evaluation panels to contribute industry and food sector subject
54 matter expertise in procurement determinations;
55 “(4) Consolidate food purchasing data from all covered agencies to track District-
56 wide performance on District laws, standards, and goals for food procurement; and
57 “(5) Develop and maintain food procurement standards, practices, and templates
58 to be used in all procurements, in collaboration with the Department of Health, Department of
59 Small and Local Business Development, the Department of Energy and the Environment, and the
60 Office of Contracting and Procurement.
61 “(d) All covered agencies and the Office of Contracting and Procurement shall provide
62 all data regarding food procurement to the Office that are necessary for the Office to fulfill its
63 functions under subsection (c) of this section.”.
