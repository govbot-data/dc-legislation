11 To amend Tenant Receivership Amendment Act of 2021 to remove the cap on money that may be
12 deposited into the fund; and to amend the Tenant Receivership Amendment Act of 2001
13 to remove the cap on the Tenant Receivership Abatement Fund; to amend Abatement and
14 Condemnation of Nuisance Properties Omnibus Amendment Act of 2000 to clarify
15 grounds, notice, and procedures for receivership, clarify the powers and duties of a
16 receiver, establish cost recovery and lien mechanisms, and authorize tenant protections;
17 to amend the Construction Codes to strengthen injunctive relief; and to amend the Title
18 29 to permit dissolution of limited liability companies and limited partnerships, to
19 authorize related remedies in cases of fraud or abuse, and for other purposes.
20
21 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
22 act may be cited as the “Strengthening Tenant Receivership and Oversight for Neighborhood
23 Growth (STRONG) Homes Amendment Act of 2026”.
