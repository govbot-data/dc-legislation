11 To amend the District of Columbia Comprehensive Bicycle Transportation and Safety Act of 1984
12 to require that electric bicycles, electric mobility devices, and traction batteries
13 manufactured, distributed, sold, or offered for lease or rent in the District meet certain
14 safety standards; to amend the District of Columbia Traffic Act of 1925 to make
15 conforming amendments; to amend the Department of Transportation Establishment Act
16 of 2002 to empower the Director of the District Department of Transportation to develop
17 policies for the safe use of electric bicycles, motorized bicycles, and electric mobility
18 devices; to amend Section 3399 of Title 24 of the District of Columbia Municipal
19 Regulations to make conforming amendments; and to amend Section 9901 of Title 18 of
20 the District of Columbia Municipal Regulations to make conforming amendments.
21
22 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
23 act may be cited as the “Micromobility Fire Safety Standards Amendment Act of 2026”.

79 Sec. 3. Section 2 of the District of Columbia Traffic Act, 1925, approved March 3, 1925
80 (43 Stat. 1119; D.C. Official Code § 50-2201.02), is amended as follows:
81 (a) Paragraph (6A) is redesignated as paragraph (6B).
82 (b) A new paragraph (6A) is added to read as follows:
83 “(6A) Electric bicycle” or “e-bike” means a vehicle with:
84 “(A) A post-mounted seat or saddle;
85 “(B) Two or 3 wheels that are in contact with the ground;
86 “(C) Fully operative pedals for human propulsion; and
4

87 “(D) One or more electric motors to either assist the rider when pedaling or
88 provide motive power to the wheels when the rider is not pedaling.”.
89 (b) Paragraph (6B)(A) is amended to read as follows:
90 “(A) “Electric mobility device” means a mobility device with a
91 rechargeable electric drive train that propels the rider, and which may be provided with a handle
92 for grasping while riding, regardless of whether the device is self-balancing or if the rider may or
93 may not be seated.”.
