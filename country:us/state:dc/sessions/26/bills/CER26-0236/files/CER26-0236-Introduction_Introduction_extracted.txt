27 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
28
29 ____________________________
30
31 To honor and celebrate the life and legacy of Talib Shakir, a dedicated public servant and
32 advocate for returning citizens, whose service expanded opportunity and strengthened
33 communities across the District of Columbia.
34 WHEREAS, Talib Shakir was born and raised in the District of Columbia and devoted
35 himself to serving the city he called home;
36 WHEREAS, after more than two decades of incarceration, Mr. Shakir returned to his
37 community and dedicated his life to helping others rebuild their lives and pursue new
38 opportunities;

39 WHEREAS, Mr. Shakir understood that learning could bring people together, and, after
40 challenging a student from Honduras to learn English, he committed himself to learning Spanish
41 and became certified to teach it;
42 WHEREAS, after returning home, Mr. Shakir continued his education through
43 Georgetown University’s Pivot Program and built a career in public service that drew on his own
44 experience of rebuilding his life after incarceration;
45 WHEREAS, Mr. Shakir served as Deputy Director of Community Relations in the
46 Mayor’s Office of Community Relations and Services, coordinating constituent engagement and
47 government services across all eight wards;
48 WHEREAS, Mr. Shakir also served as Commercial Safe Corridor Manager, coordinating
49 the District’s work at the Chinatown and Anacostia Hubs to support safer neighborhoods;
50 WHEREAS, as Director of the Mayor’s Office on Returning Citizen Affairs, Mr. Shakir
51 worked to ensure that people coming home from incarceration had the support they needed to
52 find their footing, recognizing how difficult it can be to secure a job or a stable place to live;
53 WHEREAS, Mr. Shakir called attention to the needs of women returning from
54 incarceration and the importance of helping families reconnect after years of separation;
55 WHEREAS, Mr. Shakir began mentoring younger people while he was still incarcerated
56 and continued that work after his release, speaking from experience about the choices they faced
57 and the futures still open to them; and
58 WHEREAS, Mr. Shakir passed away on October 9, 2026, at the age of 50, leaving a
59 community that benefited from his service and returning citizens who saw in his life a reason to
60 believe in their own possibilities.
61 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
62 resolution may be cited as the “Talib Shakir Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
63 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia honors the life of Talib Shakir and his
64 service to returning citizens, expresses its deepest condolences to his family and all who loved
65 him, and recognizes his legacy of helping others build a life after incarceration.

66 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
67 the District of Columbia Register.
