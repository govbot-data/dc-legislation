To amend, on a temporary basis, the Healthy SchoolsActof2010 to modify , the requirements
forDistrictschool breakfast service.
BEIT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, Thatthis
actmay be cited as the“AlternativeBreakfast Service Model Temporary Amendment Act of
2026”.
Sec. 2. The Healthy Schools Actof2010, effectiveJuly27, 2010 (D.C. Law 18-209;
D.C. Official Code § 38-821.01 efseq.), is amended as follows:
(a) Section 101(1B) (D.C. Official Code § 38-821.01(1B)) is amended to read as follows:
“(1B) “Breakfast inthe classroom”means an alternative breakfastserving model
where studentsare served breakfast in the classroom.”
(b) Section203(a) (D.C. Official Code § 38-822.03(a)) is amendedas follows:
(1) Paragraph (2) is amended as follows:
(A) Subparagrph (A) is amended by strikingthe phrase“breakfast in the
classroom”and inserting the phrase “all students grab- a nd-go breakfast, breakfastinthe
classroom, or a combinationofboth breakfastmodels” in its place.
(B) Subparagraph (C) is amended by striking the phrase “rate, without
breakfast-in-the-classroom”and inserting the word “rate” in its place.
(2) Paragraph(3) isamended as follows:
(A) Subparagraph (A) is amended by strikingthe phrase“breakfast inthe
classroom” and insertingthephrase “grab-and-go breakfast, breakfast inthe classroom, or a
combination ofboth breakfastmodels” in its place.
(B) Subparagraph(B) is amended by striking the phrase “breakfast inthe
classroom”and insertingthe phrase“grab-and-go breakfast, breakfastin the classroom,ot a
combination ofboth breakfast models” in its place.
