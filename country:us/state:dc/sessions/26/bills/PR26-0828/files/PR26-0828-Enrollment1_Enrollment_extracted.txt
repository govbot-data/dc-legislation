ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-529
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 20, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency, due to congressional review, with respect to the need
to amend section 1507 of the Criminal Justice Coordinating Council for the District of
Columbia Establishment Act of 2001 to change the date by which the Prearrest Diversion
Task Force shall issue initial recommendations for prearrest diversion of certain
misdemeanor offenses and categories of persons to July 2026.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Prearrest Diversion Task Force Recommendations Congressional
Review Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) On July 14, 2026, the Council passed the Prearrest Diversion Task Force
Recommendations Emergency Amendment Act of 2026, effective July 30, 2026 (D.C. Act 26-
386; 73 DCR 11163) (“emergency act”), which expires on October 28, 2026.
(b) On September 22, 2026, the Council passed the Prearrest Diversion Task Force
Recommendations Temporary Amendment Act of 2026, enacted on October 21, 2026 (D.C. Act 26-446;
__ DCR ___) (“temporary act”), which does not yet have a projected law date.
(c) Congressional review emergency legislation is necessary to prevent a gap in the
law between the expiration of the emergency act and the effective date of the temporary act.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Prearrest Diversion Task Force Recommendations Congressional Review Emergency
Amendment Act of 2026 be adopted after a single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
