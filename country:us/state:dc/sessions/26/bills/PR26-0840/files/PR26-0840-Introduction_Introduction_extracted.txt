Manuel Geraldo
Mr. Manuel “Manny” Geraldo currently serves as Director of
Government Affairs at Washington Gas, where he leads the company’s
engagement on legislative and regulatory matters across Maryland, the
District of Columbia, Virginia, Michigan, and federal.
From 2024 to 2026, he was a Principal at Cornerstone Government
Affairs, representing a diverse portfolio of clients on policy and
regulatory issues in Maryland and the District. He previously served as
Washington Gas’ Policy Manager for Maryland and the District of
Columbia from 2021 to 2024.
Earlier in his career, Mr. Geraldo was a senior legislative staffer for the
Council of the District of Columbia and an attorney at the District Department of Energy &
Environment, where he focused on energy and environmental policy.
Mr. Geraldo currently serves on the Board of Directors for Rainbow Place Shelter, the Prince
George’s Community College Foundation Board, and the San Miguel Associate Board. He has
previously served on the Board of Directors for the Portuguese-American Leadership Council of
the United States, the Sustainable Energy Utility Advisory Board, the Maryland Clean Energy
Center, the Prince George’s County Chamber of Commerce, and the Comptroller of Maryland
Climate Advisory Council. He is a member of Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity, Inc.
A Ward 4 resident, Mr. Geraldo holds a Bachelor of Arts from Temple University and a Juris
Doctor from Howard University School of Law.
