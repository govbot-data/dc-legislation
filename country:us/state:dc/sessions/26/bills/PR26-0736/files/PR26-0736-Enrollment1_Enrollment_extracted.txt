ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-484
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
July 14, 2026
To approve an amendment to the term sheet for the disposition for the Hill East Phase II Bundle
2 property located at 1900 Massachusetts Avenue, SE.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Hill East Phase II Bundle 2 Disposition Term Sheet Amendment
Approval Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) The Council approved the Hill East Phase II Bundle 2 Surplus Declaration and
Disposition Approval Act of 2022, effective March 10, 2023 (D.C. Law 24-322; 70 DCR 865)
(“Hill East Phase II Bundle 2 Act”), which was accompanied by a term sheet, dated September
20, 2022, that was executed by the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic Development and
the proposed developer. The term sheet outlined certain terms and conditions of the disposition
of real property located at 1900 Massachusetts Avenue, SE, and known for taxation and
assessment purposes as a portion of Lot 7 in Square E-1112 (“Property”).
(b) Section 3(k) of the Hill East Phase II Bundle 2 Act states that the term sheet for the
disposition of the Property was submitted by the Mayor to the Council in accordance with
section 1(b-1)(2) of An Act Authorizing the sale of certain real estate in the District of Columbia
no longer required for public purposes, approved August 5, 1939 (53 Stat. 1211; D.C. Official
Code § 10-801(b-1)(2)) (the “Property Disposition Act”).
(c) Term sheets submitted to Council in accordance with section 1(b-1)(2) of the Property
Disposition Act (D.C. Official Code § 10-801(b-1)(2)), may be substantively modified pursuant
to section 1(b-4) of the Property Disposition Act (D.C. Official Code § 10-801(b-4)).
(d) Pursuant to section 1(b-4) of the Property Disposition Act (D.C. Official Code § 10-
801(b-4)), the Mayor submitted to the Council, along with this resolution, an amended term
sheet, in redline form, that reflects certain substantive changes related to the business terms of
the transaction of the Property (“amended term sheet”).
(e) The Council approves the amended term sheet submitted with this resolution.
Sec. 3. Transmittal.
The Council shall transmit a copy of this resolution, upon its adoption, to the Mayor.
