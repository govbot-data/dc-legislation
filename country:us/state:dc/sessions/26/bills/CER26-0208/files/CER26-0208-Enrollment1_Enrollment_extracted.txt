ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-208
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To remember, honor, and celebrate the life of Philip A. Lepanto.
WHEREAS, Mr. Lepanto was a beloved husband, son, brother, uncle, mentor, neighbor
and friend who is remembered for his kind, funny, and generous spirit and endless curiosity,
enthusiasm, and love of the District of Columbia;
WHEREAS, Mr. Lepanto was born in Philadelphia, grew up in Kentucky, West Virginia,
and Virginia, and eventually settled in Washington, D.C.;
WHEREAS, Mr. Lepanto earned a degree in political science and history from
Vanderbilt University, participating in student government and the community radio station;
WHEREAS, Mr. Lepanto’s career spanned politics, public affairs, cybersecurity, and
angel investing, combining his passions for community, technology, and entrepreneurship, and;
WHEREAS, Mr. Lepanto served as an Advisory Neighborhood Commissioner for ANC
1D in Mount Pleasant;
WHEREAS, for 15 years Mr. Lepanto was a partner and Chief Technology Officer with
Connections Media, LLC an award-winning DC-based digital public affairs firm;
WHEREAS, Mr. Lepanto owned and operated Old School Hardware in Mount Pleasant,
a beloved neighborhood small business; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Lepanto championed housing security for vulnerable women and
families, and actively supported efforts to bring live music back to the Mount Peasant
neighborhood.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Philip Lepanto Memorial Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
1

ENROLLED ORIGINAL
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia remembers, honors, and celebrates the life
of Mr. Philip Lepanto and his many contributions to the community.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
