15 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
16 ____________
17 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
18 _____________________________
19
20 To celebrate Dr. Lonnie Bunch, an extraordinary historian, scholar, builder, leader, and curator
21 who has dedicated his career to uncovering and sharing a more complete understanding
22 of our nation’s history, and who today has served for eight years as the 14th Secretary of
23 the Smithsonian Institution; and to declare November 18th as “Dr. Lonnie Bunch III Day.”
24 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch is the first African American and the first historian to serve as
25 Secretary of the Smithsonian Institution;
26 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch manages the world’s largest research complex which includes 21
27 museums, 21 libraries, the National Zoo and archeological, oceanographic, marine biology,
28 ornithology, air and space, and planetary research and conservation centers;
29 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch was the Founding Director of the Smithsonian’s National
30 Museum of African American History and Culture;
31 WHEREAS, as a young man in Belleville, New Jersey, Dr. Bunch developed a passion
32 for history, inspired by his family;
33 WHEREAS, following his studies, Dr. Bunch served in leadership roles at museums
34 across the country, including the California African American Museum, the Smithsonian
35 Institution, and the Chicago Historical Society, where he led a successful capital campaign and
36 institutional reorganization, helping to lay the foundation for its transformation into the Chicago
37 History Museum;
38 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch curated groundbreaking exhibitions, including “The Black
39 Olympians, 1904-1984” and “Black Angelenos: The Afro-American in Los Angeles, 1850-1950”
40 at the California African American Museum; the outreach program and exhibition “Teen

41 Chicago” at the Chicago Historical Society; and the permanent exhibition “The American
42 Presidency: A Glorious Burden,” at the Smithsonian National Museum of American History;
43 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch curated the exhibition “American Aspirations” at the Smithsonian
44 Institution in honor of the nation’s 250th Anniversary;
45 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch raised over $500 million dollars from private donors, and secured
46 $250 million dollars from Congress to build the African American Museum;
47 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch was appointed to the Committee for the Preservation of the
48 White House, first by President George W. Bush in 2002, and subsequently by President Barack
49 Obama in 2010;
50 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch is a member of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences, the
51 American Philosophical Society, and Phi Beta Kappa;
52 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch has been awarded the French Legion of Honor, one of the
53 nation’s highest honors;
54 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch is the President elect of the American Historical Association;
55 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch is the recipient of the NAACP’s President’s Award, the NAACP
56 Legal Defense Fund’s National Equal Justice Award, and the Ebony Power 100 Humanitarian of
57 the Year Award;
58 WHEREAS, he has received the Roosevelt Institute’s Freedom Medal, Harvard Hutchins
59 Center’s W.E.B. Du Bois Medal, Black Women’s Agenda, Inc., President’s Award, Congressional
60 Black Caucus Chairman’s Award, and Lifetime Achievement Award from both the American
61 Alliance of Museums and the African American Museum Association;
62 WHEREAS, he has been honored by the Choral Arts Society of Washington and the
63 Historical Society of Washington;
64 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch has always maintained that his studies at Howard University and
65 American University, in his early Washington years, shaped his 38 years at the Smithsonian as a
66 curator, as founding director of the National Museum of African American History and Culture,
67 and as Secretary;
68

69 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch has received over 25 Honorary Doctor of Human Letters awards,
70 including Prairie View A & M University, Yale University, Brown University, the University of
71 Pennsylvania, Princeton University, and the honorary Doctor of Laws from Harvard University;
72 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch has been awarded the degree of Doctor of Humane Letters,
73 honoris causa from Georgetown University and the American University in Washington, D.C.;
74 WHEREAS, in recognition of Dr. Bunch’s singular commitment to advancing and
75 illuminating the full range, depth, and complexity of our shared American history, the Council of
76 the District of Columbia recognizes and honors Dr. Lonnie G. Bunch III for his extraordinary
77 contributions to our nation and its capital city, and his lifelong commitment to humanity,
78 scholarship, truth seeking and equality; and
79 WHEREAS, Dr. Bunch and Mrs. Bunch are long-term residents of Washington D.C.
80 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
81 resolution may be cited as the “Dr. Lonnie G. Bunch III Recognition Resolution of 2026".
82 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia celebrates Dr. Lonnie Bunch and his
83 extraordinary contributions to the Smithsonian Institution and our nation.
84 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
85 the District of Columbia Register.
