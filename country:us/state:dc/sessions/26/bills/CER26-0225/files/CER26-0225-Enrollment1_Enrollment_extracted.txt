ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-225
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To recognize and celebrate October 10, 2026, and the second Saturday of October thereafter, as
“DC Table Tennis Day” in the District of Columbia; and to promote health, wellness,
youth development, inclusion, and community engagement through the sport of table
tennis.
WHEREAS, it is important to promote physical activity, healthy lifestyles, and
recreational opportunities that improve the physical, mental, behavioral, and social well-being of
residents of all ages and abilities;
WHEREAS, table tennis is an affordable, accessible, and inclusive sport that can be
enjoyed by people of every age, ability, and background, providing lifelong opportunities for
physical exercise, cognitive development, social interaction, and friendly competition;
WHEREAS, participation in table tennis has been shown to improve cardiovascular
health, balance, coordination, reflexes, concentration, strategic thinking, emotional well-being,
and resilience while encouraging healthy aging and lifelong fitness;
WHEREAS, table tennis is one of the few sports in which children, adults, older adults,
and individuals with physical, mental, or behavioral disabilities can participate together in
meaningful recreational and competitive play, making it a uniquely inclusive activity that
strengthens intergenerational relationships, advances equity, and fosters mutual respect
throughout the community;
WHEREAS, during a time when many communities face challenges related to youth
engagement, social isolation, behavioral health, and sedentary lifestyles, organized table tennis
programs provide safe, structured, and positive after-school, evening, weekend, and summer
activities that encourage discipline, teamwork, mentorship, constructive use of free time, and
healthy personal development;
WHEREAS, expanding opportunities for young people to participate in organized sports
and educational enrichment programs strengthens families, supports academic achievement,
promotes positive decision-making, reduces exposure to crime and violence, and contributes to
safer, healthier, and more vibrant neighborhoods throughout the District of Columbia;

WHEREAS, table tennis is a nationally and internationally competitive sport, and an
Olympic sport since 1988, and District youth who are introduced to the game early may go on to
pursue advanced competitive play, collegiate opportunities, professional or Olympic
competition, and income generating careers coaching, playing, and officiating;
WHEREAS, table tennis serves as a powerful bridge across generations and cultures,
fostering friendships, mutual respect, teamwork, and community engagement among residents of
diverse backgrounds throughout the District of Columbia;
WHEREAS, table tennis can improve public health, support youth development,
strengthen families, encourage educational achievement, promote inclusion, and inspire lifelong
participation in physical activity; and
WHEREAS, the observance of DC Table Tennis Day will encourage residents, families,
schools, recreation centers, community organizations, businesses, and public agencies to
participate in exhibitions, instructional clinics, friendly competitions, demonstrations, and other
activities celebrating the many benefits of table tennis.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “DC Table Tennis Day Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes October 10, 2026, and the
second Saturday of October thereafter, as “DC Table Tennis Day” in the District of Columbia;
commends the Washington, DC Table Tennis Academy, its officers, directors, coaches, athletes,
volunteers, community partners, and supporters for their outstanding leadership and dedication to
advancing health, wellness, education, youth development, inclusion, recreation, and community
service in the District of Columbia through the sport of table tennis; and encourages all residents,
schools, community organizations, businesses, and government agencies in the District of
Columbia to observe DC Table Tennis Day by participating in table tennis and by hosting safe,
inclusive events and programs that engage youth and families in the sport.
Sec. 3. The Council shall transmit a copy of this resolution, upon its adoption, to the
Mayor, the Department of Parks and Recreation, the Department of Employment Services Office
of Youth Programs, and the Office of the State Superintendent of Education.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
