15 To recognize Sean Cuddihy for his exemplary service to the Council of the District of Columbia,
16 including his leadership as Deputy Committee Director for the Committee on Housing,
17 his dedication to public accountability, and his continued service to District residents.
18 WHEREAS, Sean Cuddihy served as Deputy Committee Director for the Committee on
19 Housing and the prior Committee on Government Operations and Facilities under
20 Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr., helping guide the Committees’ legislative, budget, and
21 oversight responsibilities;
22 WHEREAS, Sean Cuddihy brought sound legal judgment, keen policy analysis, and deep
23 institutional knowledge to the demanding work of the Committee on Housing;
24 WHEREAS, he helped prepare the Committee’s budget recommendations and examine
25 housing programs, agency performance, and the stewardship of public resources during
26 consequential budget cycles;
27 WHEREAS, Sean Cuddihy worked to strengthen oversight of the District of Columbia
28 Housing Authority and other agencies responsible for advancing the District’s housing goals;
29 WHEREAS, he understood that a successful hearing begins long before the first witness
30 speaks, and he helped ensure that the Committee entered agency oversight and budget
31 deliberations prepared to ask difficult and informed questions;

32 WHEREAS, Sean Cuddihy supported efforts to examine tenant protections and access to
33 legal assistance, recognizing the importance of meaningful rights and fair processes for District
34 residents at risk of losing their homes;
35 WHEREAS, Sean Cuddihy’s contributions to the work of the Committee on Government
36 Operations and Facilities included shepherding the creation of the Department of General
37 Services work order dashboard and several bills expanding the District’s civil rights protections;
38 WHEREAS, he cultivated constructive working relationships with agency officials,
39 colleagues, and housing stakeholders while maintaining the independence and rigor required for
40 effective oversight;
41 WHEREAS, colleagues valued Sean Cuddihy as a trusted sounding board whose
42 thoughtful legal advice, willingness to share institutional knowledge, and steady perspective
43 helped others navigate complicated policy questions;
44 WHEREAS, following his service in Councilmember White’s office, Sean Cuddihy
45 continued serving District residents as Committee Director for the Committee on Public Works
46 and Operations, carrying forward his experience in legislative leadership and public
47 accountability; and
48 WHEREAS, through his professionalism, generosity as a colleague, and commitment to
49 good government, Sean Cuddihy has left an enduring mark on the Committee on Housing and on
50 the people who had the privilege of working with him.
51 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
52 resolution may be cited as the “Sean Cuddihy Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
53 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Sean Cuddihy for his
54 outstanding legal and legislative judgment, leadership in housing oversight and budgeting,
55 generosity to his colleagues, and continued commitment to serving the residents of the District.
56 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
57 the District of Columbia Register.
