11 To amend the District of Columbia Funeral Services Regulatory Act of 1984 to authorize
12 reduction by alkaline hydrolysis at funeral services establishments and to define
13 cremation.
14
15 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
16 act may be cited as the “Posthumous Care Modernization Amendment Act of 2026”.
17 Sec. 2. The District of Columbia Funeral Services Regulatory Act of 1984, effective May
18 22, 1984 (D.C. Law 5-84; D.C. Official Code § 3-401 et seq.), is amended as follows:
19 (a) Section 3 (D.C. Official Code § 3-402) is amended as follows:
20 (1) A new paragraph (1A) is added to read as follows:
21 “(1A) “Alkaline Hydrolysis” means the process of reducing human remains using
22 water, alkaline chemicals, and heat inside a watertight vessel to accelerate decomposition at a
23 funeral services establishment.”.
24 (2) A new paragraph (5A) is added to read as follows:
25 “(5A) “Cremation” means the disposition of a dead human body by means of
26 incineration.”.
