16 To recognize Francesca Bryce, Esq. for her outstanding service as Senior Legislative Counsel to
17 Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr., and for her legal and policy leadership in housing,
18 justice and public safety, returning citizens, and youth affairs.
19 WHEREAS, Francesca Bryce is a proud native Washingtonian whose dedication to
20 public service is rooted in the city, communities, and people who raised her;
21 WHEREAS, her grandfather, Arthur, first introduced her to community service and
22 inspired her belief that she could help shape the future of her hometown;
23 WHEREAS, her great grandmother Gabrielle, grandmother Jocelyne, her mother
24 Daniella, her great-aunt Daniella, and generations of family instilled in her a strong work ethic,
25 determination, and the confidence to make a difference in her city;
26 WHEREAS, her family, friends, and neighbors, both longtime and new, have shaped her
27 outlook and reinforced her conviction that public service begins with showing up for one
28 another;
29 WHEREAS, Francesca Bryce serves as Senior Legislative Counsel to Councilmember
30 Robert C. White, Jr., providing legal analysis, legislative drafting, policy advice, and strategic
31 support on matters affecting District residents;

32 WHEREAS, during her tenure with the Councilmember, she has worked on matters
33 touching nearly every committee of the Council, bringing versatility, curiosity, and a willingness
34 to take on complex and unfamiliar issues;
35 WHEREAS, her previous responsibilities have included oversight and policy matters
36 involving the Office of the Tenant Advocate, the Real Estate Commission, and the Board of Real
37 Estate Appraisers;
38 WHEREAS, she has co-led the office’s work involving the Department of Housing and
39 Community Development, including affordable housing policy, program oversight, and the use
40 of public housing resources;
41 WHEREAS, she has also co-led the office’s work involving the District of Columbia
42 Housing Finance Agency, helping scrutinize housing finance programs and investments intended
43 to support affordable housing;
44 WHEREAS, Francesca Bryce has led work involving the Office of Returning Citizen
45 Affairs and policies supporting returning citizens as they rebuild their lives and rejoin their
46 communities;
47 WHEREAS, she has led the office’s judiciary and public safety work, advancing policies
48 concerning public accountability, residents’ rights, and the fair administration of justice;
49 WHEREAS, her legislative work has included initiatives to resolve rental disputes
50 earlier, prevent avoidable evictions, and strengthen dignity, family connections, and
51 accountability in District correctional facilities;
52 WHEREAS, she currently leads the office’s youth affairs work, recognizing that the
53 District’s decisions today help determine the safety, opportunities, and futures of its young
54 people;
55 WHEREAS, the stories and experiences of countless District residents have inspired and
56 informed her work, continually reminding her whom the Council serves and why its decisions
57 matter;

58 WHEREAS, Francesca Bryce has carried the values instilled by her loved ones into her
59 public service, combining careful legal judgment with an enduring commitment to the people
60 and neighborhoods of Washington, D.C.; and
61 WHEREAS, she makes everyone feel included and appreciated through her warmth,
62 kindness, laugh, and curiosity;
63 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
64 resolution may be cited as the “Francesca Bryce Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
65 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Francesca Bryce for her legal
66 and legislative work across housing, justice and public safety, returning citizens, and youth
67 affairs; for her wide-ranging service across Council committees; and for her dedication to her
68 hometown, grounded in the values passed down by her family and strengthened by her friends,
69 neighbors, and the countless residents she serves.
70 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
71 the District of Columbia Register.
