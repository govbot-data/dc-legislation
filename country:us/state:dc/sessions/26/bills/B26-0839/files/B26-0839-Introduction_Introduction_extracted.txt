Dear Secretary Howard,
Today, I am introducing the “Johnny Gant Way Designation Act of 2026.” Please find enclosed
a signed copy of the legislation.
This legislation would designate the 20 block of 50th Street NE, between East Capitol Street NE
and Ames Street NE in Ward 7, as “Johnny Gant Way.” This designation is in honor of Johnny
Gant, a longtime Ward 7 resident, professional boxer, businessman, coach, and mentor.
Born and raised in Ward 7's Lincoln Heights, John Lestant “Johnny” Gant Sr. was a proud,
lifelong native Washingtonian whose journey embodied the spirit of the District. He rose to
become a world-ranked welterweight boxing contender in the 1970s, compiling 45 professional
victories and headlining historic local bouts against legends like Sugar Ray Leonard. After
studying at the University of the District of Columbia, he successfully transitioned into the
technology sector and worked for General Electric’s Computer Systems Division. He dedicated
his later life to mentorship, establishing the Johnny Gant Foundation to provide sports, tutoring,
and discipline to area youth.
The designation of 50th Street NE as “Johnny Gant Way” would honor the memory of Johnny
Gant, and his dedication and legacy in the Ward 7 community.
Please feel free to reach out to me or my Legislative Director, Ben Raab, with any questions. He
can be reached at braab@dccouncil.gov or by phone at 202-741-0912.
Sincerely,
Wendell Felder
