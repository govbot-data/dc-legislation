15 To modify, on an emergency basis, the requirements for District school breakfast service.
16
17 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
18 act may be cited as the “Alternative Breakfast Service Model Emergency Amendment Act of
19 2026”.
20 Sec. 2. The Healthy Schools Act of 2010, effective July 27, 2010 (D.C. Law 18-209;
21 D.C. Official Code §§ 38-821.01 et seq.) is amended as follows:
22 (a) Section 101(1B) (D.C. Official Code § 38–821.01) is amended to read as follows:
23 “(1B) “Breakfast in the classroom” means an alternative breakfast serving model where
24 students are served breakfast in the classroom.”
25 (b) Section 203(a) (D.C. Official Code § 38–822.03(a)) is amended as follows:
26 (1) Subparagraph (2)(A) is amended by striking the phrase “breakfast in the
27 classroom” and inserting the phrase “all students grab-and-go breakfast, breakfast in the
28 classroom, or a combination of both breakfast models” in its place.
29 (2) Subparagraph (2)(C) is amended by striking the phrase “, without breakfast-in-
30 the-classroom”.
1

31 (3) Subparagraph (3)(A) is amended by striking the phrase “breakfast in the
32 classroom” and inserting the phrase “grab-and-go breakfast, breakfast in the classroom, or a
33 combination of both breakfast models” in its place.
34 (4) Subparagraph (3)(B) is amended by striking the phrase “breakfast in the
35 classroom” and inserting the phrase “grab-and-go breakfast, breakfast in the classroom, or a
36 combination of both breakfast models” in its place.
37 Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.
38 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Budget Director as the fiscal impact
39 statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved
40 October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
41 Sec. 4. Effective date.
42 This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
43 Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), and shall remain in effect for no longer than
44 90 days, as provided for emergency acts of the Council of the District of Columbia in section
45 412(a) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 788;
46 D.C. Official Code § 1-204.12(a)).
