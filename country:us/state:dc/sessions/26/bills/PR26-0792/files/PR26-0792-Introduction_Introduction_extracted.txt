14 To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need for the Department of Small
15 and Local Business Development to award a grant to the Duke Business Improvement
16 District Corporation to improve conditions for property owners, businesses, residents and
17 visitors within the Greater Shaw/U Street area.
18
19 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
20 resolution may be cited as the “Duke District BID Grantmaking Emergency Declaration
21 Resolution of 2026”.
22 Sec. 2. (a) The Duke Business Improvement District Corporation, d/b/a Duke District
23 BID has been organized under the District of Columbia Nonprofit Corporation Act of 2010 to
24 operate exclusively to improve neighborhood vitality and act as a Business Improvement District
25 for property owners, businesses, residents, and visitors within the Greater Shaw/U Street
26 neighborhood (“Greater Shaw/U Street”) – referred to collectively as the “Duke District”, in
27 honor of Duke Ellington, who grew up and launched his music career in this area, one that
28 continues to be a vibrant cultural hub to this day.
29 (b) Pursuant to the Business Improvements District Act of 1996 (D.C. Law 11-134; D.C.
30 Official Code § 2-1215.01), the Duke District BID has put together the business plan,
31 boundaries, and property owner petitions necessary to submit a BID application to the
1

32 Department of Small and Local Business Development. The Duke District BID petition has
33 surpassed the signatures necessary to advance with its application, which will be accompanied by
34 Council legislation.
35 (c) In 2023, Council established the Greater U Street Performance Parking Zone to
36 manage curbside availability, address congestion during peak nightlife hours, and generate a
37 local source of revenue deposited into the Greater U Street Parking Benefit Fund. In the Fiscal
38 Year 2027 Budget Support Act, $1 million of revenue deposited into the fund was authorized to
39 be issued as a grant in support of a place management organization in the area covered by the
40 Greater U Street Performance Parking Zone. This funding was authorized with a BID
41 organization in mind, but the Duke District BID had not yet attained the signatures necessary to
42 advance. Per application materials, the proposed boundaries of the Duke District BID are
43 coincident with the boundary of the Greater U Street Performance Parking Zone.
44 (d) Emergency and temporary legislation is necessary to appropriately direct place
45 management grantmaking funds authorized in the Fiscal Year 2027 BSA, in order to provide
46 seed funding advancing the establishment of the Duke District BID and allow for services to be
47 stood up alongside existing neighborhood-serving partners, including Main Streets and Clean
48 Teams. These funds will support the economic vitality, cultural vibrancy, cleanliness, safety, and
49 overall quality of life of the community and District as a whole.
50 Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances in
51 section 2 constitute emergency circumstances, making it necessary that the Duke District BID
52 Grantmaking Emergency Amendment Act of 2026 be adopted after a single reading.
53 Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
