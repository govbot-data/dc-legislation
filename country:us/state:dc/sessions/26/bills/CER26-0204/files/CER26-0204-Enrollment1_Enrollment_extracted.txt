ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-204
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To recognize, honor, and celebrate District of Columbia public charter schools on the occasion of
their 30th anniversary.
WHEREAS, the District of Columbia School Reform Act of 1995 opened the door for
public charter schools in the District of Columbia;
WHEREAS, since the opening of the District’s first public charter schools in 1996,
thousands of educators, school leaders, board of trustee members, families, and community
partners have built and sustained one of the nation’s most exemplary public charter school sectors;
WHEREAS, for 3 decades, DC public charter schools have expanded educational
opportunity, promoted student achievement, and prepared students for college, careers, and
lifelong success;
WHEREAS, DC public charter schools have strengthened education in the District by
giving families access to a wide range of public-school options designed to meet a wide range of
diverse needs and interests;
WHEREAS, DC public charter schools have adopted and developed distinctive missions
and educational approaches that reflect the aspirations and preferences of the students and
communities they serve;
WHEREAS, DC public charter schools have served as community anchors, by opening
their facilities for Advisory Neighborhood Commission meetings, community meetings, and other
neighborhood events and by connecting residents with food, healthcare services, and other
community resources;
WHEREAS, DC’s adult-serving public charter schools have made the District a national
model for adult education by strengthening adult learners’ literacy and language skills and by
expanding their ability to earn diplomas and credentials and their pathways to employment and
economic mobility;

WHEREAS, DC public charter schools provide quality and accessible education to
multilingual learners, students with disabilities, and students with other specialized educational
needs;
WHEREAS, DC public charter schools continue to strengthen their commitment to
academic excellence, transparency, and accountability—including through the ASPIRE academic
accountability System;
WHEREAS, during school year 2025-2026, 66 public charter local education agencies
operated 133 schools and educated 48,065 students, representing nearly half of all public school
students in the District of Columbia;
WHEREAS, DC public charter schools enrich communities in all 8 wards and serve
learners from early childhood through adulthood; and
WHEREAS, 2026 marks the 30th anniversary of public charter schools in the District of
Columbia, and 3 decades of service to DC students, families, educators, and communities.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “District of Columbia Public Charter Schools 30th Anniversary
Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes, honors, and celebrates District
of Columbia public charter schools on the occasion of their 30th anniversary and commends their
contributions to public education and the students and families of the District of Columbia.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
