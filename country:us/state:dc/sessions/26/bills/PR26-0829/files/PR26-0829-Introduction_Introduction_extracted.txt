1 ______________________________
2 Councilmember Trayon White, Sr.
3 A PROPOSED RESOLUTION
4 _____________
5 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
6 _____________
7 To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to amend, on a temporary
8 basis, the District of Columbia Traffic Adjudication Act of 1978 to remove the penalty
9 equal to the amount of a civil fine for failure to answer a notice of infraction within 30
10 days when the infraction has been detected by an automated traffic enforcement system
11 or involves a parking, standing, stopping or pedestrian offense.
12
13 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
14 resolution may be cited as the “Parking and Traffic Ticket Penalty Reduction Emergency
15 Declaration Resolution of 2026.”
16 Sec. 2. (a) There is an immediate need to amend the District of Columbia Traffic
17 Adjudication Act of 1978 to remove the penalty equal to the amount of a civil fine for failure to
18 answer a notice of infraction within 30 days when the infraction has been detected by an
19 automated traffic enforcement system or involves a parking, standing, stopping or pedestrian
20 offense.
21 (b) Tickets act as a regressive tax, and late-payment penalties make the fines even more
22 regressive.
1

23 (c) Tickets and the penalty that doubles the ticket amount creates a substantial burden for
24 low-income residents and households already struggling to make ends meet.
25 (d) From 2000 to 2021, drivers collectively owe nearly $1.3 billion in unpaid parking and
26 traffic tickets in the District.
27 (e) From 2016 to 2020, 62% of all traffic fines were issued in majority-Black
28 neighborhoods, totaling nearly $467 million, while affluent White neighborhoods saw less than a
29 quarter of that.
30 (f) Eliminating fine-doubling also would reduce racial inequity.
31 Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
32 enumerated in Section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
33 “Parking and Traffic Ticket Penalty Reduction Emergency Amendment Act of 2026” be adopted
34 after a single reading.
35 Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
