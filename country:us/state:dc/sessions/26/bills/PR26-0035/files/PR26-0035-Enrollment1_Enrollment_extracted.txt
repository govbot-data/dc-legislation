ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-61
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
April 1, 2025
To reappoint Ms. Heidi Tseu to the District of Columbia Uniform Law Commission.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “District of Columbia Uniform Law Commission Heidi Tseu
Reappointment Resolution of 2025”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia reappoints:
Ms. Heidi Tseu
Paper Mill Court, NW
Washington, DC 20007
(Ward 2)
as a member of the District of Columbia Uniform Law Commission, established by section 2 of
the District of Columbia Uniform Law Commission Act of 2010, effective March 12, 2011 (D.C.
Law 18-313; D.C. Official Code § 3-1431), for a 3-year term to end on July 1, 2027.
Sec. 3. The Council shall transmit a copy of this resolution, upon its adoption, to the
appointee.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
