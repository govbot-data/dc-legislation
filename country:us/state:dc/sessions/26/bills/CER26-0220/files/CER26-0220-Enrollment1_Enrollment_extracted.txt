ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-220
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To honor and recognize the Bernice Fonteneau Senior Wellness Center of Ward 1 for being a
vital community resource since 2011, dedicated to enhancing the health, well-being,
independence, and quality of life of adults aged 60 and above throughout the District of
Columbia.
WHEREAS, September is Healthy Aging Month and this year’s theme celebrates the
value of being curious throughout our lives by staying connected with others, staying active, and
trying new things;
WHEREAS, we in the District of Columbia are fortunate to have in place Senior
Wellness Centers that actively involve any resident over the age of 60 who wishes to participate
or enjoy the services offered by the Centers;
WHEREAS, the Senior Wellness Centers of the District of Columbia have served as a
vital community resource since 1985 and are dedicated to enhancing the health, well-being,
independence, and quality of life of seniors throughout the District of Columbia;
WHEREAS, these centers provide opportunities for lifelong learning, physical fitness,
nutrition education, health promotion, disease prevention, social engagement, and cultural
enrichment;
WHEREAS, the Senior Wellness Centers foster meaningful connections among
residents, helping to reduce isolation, strengthen community bonds, and promote active and
healthy aging;
WHEREAS, the dedicated staff, volunteers, community partners, and supporters of the
Senior Wellness Centers work tirelessly to provide programs and services that empower older
adults to live with dignity, purpose and independence;
WHEREAS, the Senior Wellness Centers reflect the District of Columbia’s commitment
to honoring and supporting its senior residents, recognizing their contributions, wisdom, and
continued engagement in civic and community life;

WHEREAS, the success of the Senior Wellness Centers is a testament to the vision,
leadership, and dedication of those who have championed services for older adults in DC;
WHEREAS, it is with pride that we extend our congratulations to the Bernice Fonteneau
Senior Wellness Center for fifteen remarkable years of providing a place where seniors in Ward
1 can gather;
WHEREAS, the Bernice Fonteneau Center is full of warmth, friendship, love, and
activity; and
WHEREAS, the center continuously embodies the vision of their namesake, the
trailblazing Bernice Fonteneau whose advocacy for older adults helped shape senior services in
the District.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Bernice Fonteneau Senior Wellness Center Recognition
Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the Bernice Fonteneau Senior
Wellness Center for fifteen years of excellence as a community hub, a source of fellowship, and
a place where experience is honored and lives are enriched, and extends its best wishes that the
center’s mission of empowerment, inclusion, and opportunity for seniors continues to flourish for
many years to come.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
