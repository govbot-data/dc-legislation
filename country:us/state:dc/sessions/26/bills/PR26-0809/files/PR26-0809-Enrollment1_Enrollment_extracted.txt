ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-535
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 20, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve Contract No.
CW130810 with Capitol Paving of D.C., Inc. and Modification Nos. M01 and M02
thereto, and to authorize payment for the goods and services received and to be received
under the contract.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Contract No. CW130810 with Capitol Paving of D.C., Inc. and
Modification Nos. M01 and M02 Approval and Payment Authorization Emergency Declaration
Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2.(a) There exists an immediate need to approve Contract No. CW130810 with
Capitol Paving of D.C., Inc. and Modification Nos. M01 and M02 thereto, and to authorize
payment for the goods and services received and to be received under the contract.
(b) On November 7, 2025, the Office of Contracting and Procurement (“OCP”), on behalf
of the Department of Public Works, awarded Letter Contract CW130810 to Capitol Paving of
D.C., Inc., to perform snow and ice removal services in the not-to-exceed amount of $1 million.
(c) On January 22, 2026, OCP definitized emergency Contract No. CW130810,
increasing the estimated amount by $3,717,000, to a total estimated amount of $4,717,000.
(d) On June 1, 2026, by Modification M01, OCP increased the estimated amount of the
contract by $283,000, to a total estimated amount of $5 million.
(e) On June 1, 2026, OCP executed Modification M02, an administrative modification to
change the price schedule, but did not increase the estimated amount of the contract.
(f) Council approval is necessary because the value of the contract is more than $1
million during a 12-month period.
(g) Council approval is necessary to allow the continuation of these vital services.
Without Council approval, Capitol Paving of D.C., Inc. cannot be paid for goods and services
provided in excess of $1 million for the period from November 7, 2025, through November 6,
2026.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
