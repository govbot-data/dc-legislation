ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-62
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
April 1, 2025
To reappoint Mr. Jinwoo Park as the Executive Director of the Criminal Code Reform Commission.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Executive Director of the Criminal Code Reform Commission
Jinwoo Park Reappointment Resolution of 2025”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia approves the reappointment of:
Mr. Jinwoo Park
Ingomar Street, NW
Washington, DC 20015
(Ward 3)
as Executive Director of the Criminal Code Reform Commission, established by section 3122(a) of
the Criminal Code Reform Commission Establishment Act of 2016, effective October 8, 2016
(D.C. Law 21-160; D.C. Official Code § 3-151(a)), for a 3-year term to end March 26, 2028.
Sec. 3. The Council shall transmit a copy of this resolution, upon its adoption, to the
appointee.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
