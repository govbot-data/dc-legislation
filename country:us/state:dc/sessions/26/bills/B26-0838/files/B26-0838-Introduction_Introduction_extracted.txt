Dear Secretary Howard,
Today, I am introducing the “Kentrell Devaughn Way Designation Act of 2026.” Please find
enclosed a signed copy of the legislation.
This legislation would designate the 1500 block of 45th Street NE, between Douglass Street NE
and Quarles Street NE in Ward 7, as “Kentrell Devaughn Way.” This designation is in honor of
Kentrell Devaughn, a Ward 7 resident, who tragically lost his life to gun violence.
Kentrell was a son, brother, coach, and mentor whose work touched young people throughout the
Kenilworth community and across the District. He was killed by gun violence on April 20, 2021,
on the same block where he was born and raised. Honoring his memory there recognizes both the
loss our community carries and the investment he made in its youth.
The designation of 45th Street NE as “Kentrell Devaughn Way” would honor the memory of
Kentrell Devaughn, and his dedication and legacy in the Ward 7 community.
Please feel free to reach out to me or my Legislative Director, Ben Raab, with any questions. He
can be reached at braab@dccouncil.gov or by phone at 202-741-0912.
Sincerely,
Wendell Felder
Ward 7 Councilmember
