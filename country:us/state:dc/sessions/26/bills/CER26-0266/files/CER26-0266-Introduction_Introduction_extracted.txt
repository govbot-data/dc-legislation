14
15 To recognize and celebrate Mary Rosolia Holland Thomas on the occasion of her 100th birthday.
16
17 WHEREAS, Mrs. Mary Rosolia Holland Thomas was born in Ward 6 at her home at 318
18 Kentucky Avenue, SE, on Saturday, January 1, 1927, to Earnest and Lillian Augusta (Gussie
19 Greenfield) Holland;
20
21 WHEREAS, Mary was the 10th child born in her family - 3 sisters and 8 brothers - and her
22 brother James lovingly nicknamed her “Sugar”;
23
24 WHEREAS, Mary was educated in DC Public Schools, attending Payne and Lovejoy
25 Elementary Schools, Brown Junior High School, and Armstrong Vocational School, from which
26 she graduated in the specialty area of home economics with a focus on sewing;
27
28 WHEREAS, the Hollands were devoted Catholics, and Mary was baptized into the
29 Catholic faith;
30
31 WHEREAS, her family attended the 2nd oldest African American Catholic Church in the
32 Archdiocese of Washington - St. Cyprian’s - located close to her home on 13th and C Streets, SE;
33
34 WHEREAS, Mary began her career as an elevator operator at the Philipsborn Department
35 Store, located at 606 11th Street, NW;
36
37 WHEREAS, Mary then moved to work at the Washington Hospital Center, where she was
38 a supervisor of the Personal File Section in Human Resources;
39
40 WHEREAS, Mary further worked in the Defense Printing Service Division at the Pentagon,
41 from which she later retired as a purchasing agent in 1987;
42
43 WHEREAS, on May 18, 1949, Mary married Herbert Elwood Thomas II at St. Cyprian’s;
44
45 WHEREAS, Mary and Herbert had two children, Herbert III and Marian, a grandson,
46 Herbert, IV, and two great-granddaughters, Kiara and Janay;
1

47
48 WHEREAS, Mary is actively engaged in her faith, and she loves spiritual music of all
49 kinds;
50
51 WHEREAS, she loves to cook and bake, and her specialties are candied sweet potatoes
52 with sliced apples or pineapple chunks and green beans; and
53
54 WHEREAS, for the past 45 years, she has lived in her family home at 333 Kentucky
55 Avenue, SE.
56
57 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
58 resolution may be cited as the “Mary Rosolia Holland Thomas Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
59
60 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and celebrates Mary Rosolia
61 Holland Thomas on the occasion of her 100th birthday.
62
63 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
