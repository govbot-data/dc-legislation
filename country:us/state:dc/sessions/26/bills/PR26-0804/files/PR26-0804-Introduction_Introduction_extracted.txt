Chairman Mendelson:
In accordance with the provisions ofD.C. Official Code§ 1-204.51, and D.C. Official Code §2-
352.02, enclosed for the Council of the District of Columbia's consideration and approval is a
proposed multi-year contract for software as a service (SAAS) between the University of the District
of Columbia (and Ellucian Company LLC. Included is the Council Contract Summary and the
"Proposed Multiyear Contract No. GF-2026-C-0019 with Ellucian Company LLC Approval
Resolution of 2026".
Ellucian Company LLC will provide strategic transition for the University's Enterprise Resource
Planning (ERP) infrastructure to a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) environment as part of the Ellucian
Student Lighthouse Program under a five (5)-year modernization
Contract. The contract is performance-based, with clear standards that allow the University to ensure
the provision of quality services.
I respectfully request the Council's favorable consideration of the enclosed contract. Should you have
any questions, please contact Av is Marie Russell, General Counsel at 202-274-5604.
