16 To recognize Kate Conquest for her outstanding service as Senior Legislative Assistant to
17 Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr., and for her leadership in housing, tenant advocacy,
18 health, transportation, and environmental policy in the District of Columbia.
19 WHEREAS, Kate Conquest serves as Senior Legislative Assistant in the office of
20 Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr., bringing diligent research, thoughtful analysis, and a
21 commitment to residents to the Councilmember’s legislative and oversight work;
22 WHEREAS, Kate Conquest has led the office’s work involving the District of Columbia
23 Housing Authority, examining the conditions, performance, and accountability of an agency
24 central to the lives of public housing residents and voucher participants;
25 WHEREAS, Kate Conquest has also led matters involving the Office of the Tenant
26 Advocate, helping elevate the needs and rights of renters navigating the District’s housing
27 system;
28 WHEREAS, Kate Conquest’s policy insights and legislative drafting abilities were
29 essential to the refinement and passage of major legislation affecting tenants’ rights;
30 WHEREAS, her housing work has required close attention to testimony, agency
31 operations, legislative proposals, and the lived experiences of residents seeking safe, stable, and
32 affordable homes;

33 WHEREAS, beyond housing, Kate Conquest has managed the office’s health policy
34 portfolio, recognizing that access to care and healthy communities are essential to residents’
35 well-being;
36 WHEREAS, she has led work on transportation, helping the office examine the systems
37 and decisions that connect residents with jobs, education, services, and opportunity;
38 WHEREAS, she has carried responsibility for environmental policy, drafting numerous
39 bills to address rising utility costs that burden Distric residents, and bringing a careful eye to the
40 health of the District’s neighborhoods and the long-term stewardship of its natural resources;
41 WHEREAS, Kate Conquest is exceptionally intelligent, effortlessly charming, and
42 remarkably organized, demonstrating an extraordinary ability to stay on top of seemingly
43 everything while bringing thoughtfulness, precision, and creativity to each assignment;
44 WHEREAS, her infectious sense of humor, quick wit, and warm personality have
45 brought countless moments of laughter and joy to the office, even during the longest hearings
46 and most demanding legislative sessions;
47 WHEREAS, Kate Conquest has earned a reputation among her colleagues for being a
48 reflection of the admiration, affection, and confidence inspired by her brilliance, kindness,
49 humor, and unwavering commitment to excellence;
50 WHEREAS, through her legislative work and her example as a colleague, Kate Conquest
51 has shown that exceptional public service combines a sharp mind, tireless preparation, genuine
52 care for residents, and a spirit that lifts everyone around her.
53 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
54 resolution may be cited as the “Kate Conquest Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
55 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Kate Conquest for her
56 leadership in housing authority and tenant advocacy oversight, her service across health,
57 transportation, and environmental policy, and her dedicated contributions to District residents.
58 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
59 the District of Columbia Register.
