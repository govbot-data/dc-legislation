ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-341
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
March 3, 2026
To confirm the reappointment of Trent Williams to the Real Property Tax Appeals Commission.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Real Property Tax Appeals Commission Trent Williams
Confirmation Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia confirms the reappointment of:
Trent Williams
16th Street, NW
Washington, DC 20009
(Ward 2)
as the full-time Chairperson of the Real Property Tax Appeals Commission, established by D.C.
Official Code § 47-825.01a, for a term to end April 30, 2030.
Sec. 3. The Council shall transmit a copy of this resolution, upon its adoption, to the
nominee and to the Mayor.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
