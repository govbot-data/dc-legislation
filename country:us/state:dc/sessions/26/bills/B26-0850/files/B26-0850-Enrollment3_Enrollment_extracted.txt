ENROLLED ORIGINAL
AN ACT
_________
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
__________________
To amend, on an emergency basis, the Procurement Practices Reform Act of 2010 to require the
Office of Contracting and Procurement to process requests for the equitable adjustment of
homeless services contracts within 60 days; and to amend section 510 of the Rental
Housing Act to change the applicability date.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited as the “Homeless Services Contract Adjustment Emergency Amendment Act of
2026”.
Sec. 2. The Procurement Practices Reform Act of 2010, effective April 8, 2011 (D.C.
Law 18-371; D.C. Official Code § 2-351.01 et seq.), is amended by adding a new section 1105a
to read as follows:
“Sec. 1105a. Equitable adjustments of homeless services contracts.
“(a)(1) Within 60 calendar days of receiving a complete request for the equitable
adjustment of a contract to provide homeless services under section 3 of the Homeless Services
Reform Act, effective October 22, 2005 (D.C. Law 16-35; D.C. Official Code § 4-754.01), on
the basis of the revision of a wage determination issued by the U.S. Department of Labor
pursuant to 41 U.S.C. § 6703, OCP shall determine whether the requesting contractor is entitled
to an equitable adjustment.
“(2) A request for an equitable adjustment under this subsection is complete when
the requesting contractor has submitted all information and documents necessary for OCP to
assess the request.
“(b) Within 7 business days of the submission to OCP of all information and documents
necessary to complete an equitable adjustment request under subsection (a) of this section, OCP
shall inform the requesting contractor in writing that it considers the request complete.”.
Sec. 3. Section 510 of the Rental Housing Act, effective May 18, 2022 (D.C. Law 24-
115; D.C. Official Code § 42-3505.10) is amended as follows:
(a) The lead-in language of subsection (b-3) is amended by striking the date “October 1,
2025,” and inserting the date “October 1, 2026,” in its place.

(b) Subsection (b-4)(1) is amended by striking the date “October 1, 2025,” and inserting

Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Budget Director as the fiscal impact
statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved
October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
Sec. 5. Effective date.
This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), and shall remain in effect for no longer than
90 days, as provided for emergency acts of the Council of the District of Columbia in section
412(a) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 788;
D.C. Official Code § 1-204.12(a)).
