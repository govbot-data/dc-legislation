ENROLLED ORIGINAL
AN ACT
_________________________
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
_________________________
To require the Department of Energy and Environment, in consultation with the Public Service
Commission, the Office of the People’s Counsel, utilities, and agency designees, to create
a process through which it automatically identifies eligible District residents,
supplemented by data provided by relevant health agencies, for enrollment into utility
affordability programs or other DOEE-administered income-qualified programs, and to
require the relevant entities to enroll eligible residents.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited as the “Automatic Enrollment for Utility Affordability Programs Act of 2026”.
Sec. 2. Household identification and automatic enrollment process.
(a)(1) Within one year after the applicability date of this act, the Department of Energy
and Environment (“DOEE”), in consultation with the Public Service Commission, the Office of
People’s Counsel, utilities, and agency designees, shall implement a process for automatic
identification of eligible households for participation in District utility affordability programs or
other DOEE-administered, income-qualified programs. DOEE shall use the information obtained
in response to its request pursuant to subsection (b) of this section to identify eligible households
to be enrolled using this process, subject to paragraphs (2) and (3) of this subsection.
(2) DOEE may prioritize automatic identification of eligible households based on
household income levels or other criteria demonstrating a need for assistance if a District utility
affordability program or other DOEE-administered, income-qualified program is not able to
support automatic enrollment of all eligible households due to funding constraints or other
limitations.
(3) DOEE may choose the District utility affordability programs or other DOEE-
administered, income-qualified programs for which the agency identifies eligible households to
be enrolled. DOEE shall not be required to include the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance
Program or the Weatherization Assistance Program in the automatic identification process. Once
DOEE or its designee identifies a household for automatic enrollment, DOEE or its designee
may enroll eligible households or facilitate enrollment in additional programs as relevant.
(4) The process developed pursuant to paragraph (1) of this subsection shall
include a data-sharing protocol that ensures that DOEE or its designee will not share individuals’

personal identifying information, including the individual’s name, account number, service
address, and phone number, for purposes other than administering this section or for the purposes
of verification, research, evaluation, and analysis.
(b)(1) Consistent with 7 C.F.R. § 272.1(c)(1), and notwithstanding any other District law,
upon request by DOEE for the purposes described in subsections (a) and (d) of this section, the
Mayor shall provide, or cause to be provided, the enrollment data listed below to DOEE:
(A) For the Department of Human Services (“DHS”), enrollment data
from households participating in any program administered by DHS for which the eligibility is
based on household income, including the District’s Temporary Assistance for Needy Families
Program and the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program;
(B) For the Department of Health (“DOH”), enrollment data from
households participating in any program administered by DOH, for which the eligibility is based
on household income, including the Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women,
Infants, and Children; and
(C) For the Department of Health Care Finance (“DHCF”), enrollment
data for households participating in any program administered by DHCF, for which the eligibility
is based on household income, including Medicaid.
(2) Notwithstanding any other District law, the electric company, the natural gas
company, and the District of Columbia Water and Sewer Authority shall, at DOEE’s request,
provide the agency with customer-specific data requested by DOEE for the purposes of, and as
provided for by, this section.
(3) The electric company, natural gas company, and District of Columbia Water
and Sewer Authority shall work with DOEE to facilitate and implement the technological
capabilities or upgrades needed to carry out the automatic identification process provided for by
this section, including allowing for the secure transmission of data requested by DOEE through
an interface with DOEE’s information technology infrastructure.
(c) Before providing the information listed in subsection (b)(1) of this section in response
to DOEE’s request, the applicable agency shall, in compliance with applicable confidentiality
requirements, obtain affirmative consent from a household member for the information to be
shared with DOEE for the purposes described in subsections (a) and (d) of this section.
(d)(1) Within 60 days after receiving the list of account numbers of potentially eligible
households from DOEE as described in this section, the electric company, natural gas company,
and District of Columbia Water and Sewer Authority shall confirm eligibility and enroll eligible
households into, respectively, the Residential Aid Discount, Residential Essential Services, and
Customer Assistance Program.
(2) No later than 60 days after enrolling an eligible household pursuant to this
subsection, the relevant utility shall inform the household:
(A) That the household has been enrolled in a program, including the
name of the specific program, based on the eligibility of a member of the household due to their
enrollment in a qualifying public benefit program;

(B) The anticipated date by which the household will begin receiving

(C) Of the procedure to withdraw from automatic enrollment at any time

Sec. 3. Applicability.
(a) This act shall apply upon the date of inclusion of its fiscal effect in an approved
budget and financial plan.
(b) The Chief Financial Officer shall certify the date of inclusion of the fiscal effect in an
approved budget and financial plan and provide notice to the Budget Director of the Council of
the certifications.
(c)(1) The Budget Director shall cause the notices of the certification to be published in
the District of Columbia Register.
(2) The dates of publication of the notices of certification shall not affect the
applicability of this act.
Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal
impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
Sec. 5. Effective date.
This act shall take effect after approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto) and a 30-day period of congressional review
