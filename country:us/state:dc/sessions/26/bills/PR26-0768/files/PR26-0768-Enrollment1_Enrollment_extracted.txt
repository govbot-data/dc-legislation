ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-471
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
July 14, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve Modification No.
M001 to Contract No. CW132368 with Eastern Salt Company, Inc., and to authorize
payment for the goods and services received and to be received under the contract.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Modification No. M001 to CW132368 with Eastern Salt
Company, Inc. Approval and Payment Authorization Emergency Declaration Resolution of
2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve Modification No. M001 to
Contract No. CW132368 with Eastern Salt Company, Inc. to provide de-icing salt for snow
events, and to authorize payment for the goods and services received and to be received under
the contract.
(b) The Office of Contracting and Procurement, on behalf of the Department of Public
Works, executed Contract No. CW132368 in the not-to-exceed amount of $1 million for the
period from January 22, 2026, through December 14, 2026.
(c) Emergency Modification No. M001 increased the not-to-exceed amount by $4
million, to a total not-to-exceed amount of $5 million.
(d) Council approval is necessary because the modification increased the value of the
contract by more than $1 million during a 12-month period.
(e) Council approval is necessary to pay for these vital goods and services. Without
Council approval, Eastern Salt Company, Inc. cannot be paid for goods and services provided in
excess of $1 million for the period from January 22, 2026, through December 14, 2026.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Modification No. M001 to Contract No. CW132368 with Eastern Salt Company, Inc. Approval
and Payment Authorization Emergency Act of 2026 be adopted after a single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
