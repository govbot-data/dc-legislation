11 To amend, on an emergency basis, the Department of Behavioral Health Establishment Act of
12 2013 to require funding extension to all Fiscal Year 2026 grantees awarded to implement
13 the Substance Use and Behavioral Health Services Targeted Outreach Grant Act of 2025
14 pilot program in order to continue services without disruption; and to amend the Opioid
15 Abatement Fund Establishment Act of 2022 to authorize DBH to expend funds from the
16 Opioid Abatement Fund for the existing Targeted Outreach pilot program.
17
18 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
19 act may be cited as the “Extension of Substance Use and Targeted Outreach Grants Emergency
20 Amendment Act of 2026”.
21 Sec. 2. Section 5120e of the Department of Behavioral Health Establishment Act of 2013,
22 effective July 18, 2026 (D.C. Law 26-142; D.C. Official Code § 7-1143.02), is amended by
23 adding a new subsection (g) to read as follows:
1

24 “(g)(1) Notwithstanding the requirements of this section, and subject to available funds,
25 the Department shall issue grants in Fiscal Year 2027 to the organizations that received grants
26 (“grantees”) in Fiscal Year 2026 pursuant to the Substance Use and Behavioral Health Services
27 Targeted Outreach Grant Act of 2025, effective December 6, 2025 (D.C. Law 26-55; Note at
28 D.C. Official Code § 7-1141.01), for the purposes of continuing services at the locations where
29 the grantees operated in Fiscal Year 2026.
30 “(2) The Department may issue grant awards for services at additional locations.”.
31 Sec. 3. Section 5012 of the Opioid Abatement Fund Establishment Act of 2022, effective
32 September 21, 2022 (D.C. Law 24-167; D.C. Official Code § 7–3221) is amended by inserting a
33 new sub-section (b-8) to read as follows:
34 “(b-8) Before Fiscal Year 2026 grant agreements expire, the Department of Behavioral
35 Health (DBH) shall fully extend all Fiscal Year 2026 grantee organizations’ awards to continue
36 implementation of the Substance Use and Behavioral Health Services Targeted Outreach Grant
37 Act of 2025 pilot program, in order to avoid disruption of services. This requirement applies
38 regardless of whether new Fiscal Year 2027 grant awards for additional locations are made by
39 the agency under D.C. Law 26-0142, the Place-Based Substance Use Disorder Outreach
40 Amendment Act of 2026, A26-0320, effective from Jul 18, 2026).
41 “(1) The funding shall be extended for all current grantees currently operating at
42 the following locations:
43 “(A) The vicinity and related geography of the 600 block of T Street,
44 NW in Wards 1 and 2;
45 “(B) The vicinity and related geography of the 1300-1700 blocks of North
46 Capitol Street, 2566 NW, and 1600-1700 blocks of Lincoln, Road, NE in Ward 5;
2

47 “(C) The vicinity and related geography of the King Greenleaf Recreation
48 Center, located at 2571 201 N Street, SW in Ward 6;
49 “(D) Ward 1 Community Navigator sites at the following locations:
50 “(i) Columbia Heights Civil Plaza;
51 “(ii) The intersection of Mount Pleasant Street, NW, and Kenyon
52 Street, NW;
53 “(iii) Georgia Avenue, NW, between New Hampshire Avenue,
54 NW, and Harvard Street, NW; and
55 “(iv) U Street, NW, between 14th Street, NW, and Georgia
56 Avenue, NW.”.
57 Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
58 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Budget Director as the fiscal impact
59 statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved
60 October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
61 Sec. 5. Effective date.
62 This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
63 Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), and shall remain in effect for no longer than
64 90 days, as provided for emergency acts of the Council of the District of Columbia in section
65 412(a) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 788;
66 D.C. Official Code § 1-204.12(a)).
