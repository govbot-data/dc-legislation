ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-222
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To honor and recognize Iona’s Washington Home Center, located in Ward 8, for its commitment
to older adults, caregivers, and families in the District of Columbia and acknowledge its
vital role as a place of support and comfort to District residents.
WHEREAS, the Washington Home has for generations has been an important part of
caring for older adults and supporting individuals caring for or living with serious illnesses in the
District of Columbia;
WHEREAS, the Washington Home, a charitable foundation, expanded its commitment to
District residents east of the Anacostia River through the establishment of the Washington Home
Center, located at Alabama Avenue and Stanton Road Southeast;
WHEREAS, the Washington Home Center opened in 2020, recognizing that with rising
rates of Alzheimer’s, Parkinson’s, and Type 2 diabetes, and high prevalence among African
Americans, there was an urgent need for specialized, supervised care in the area;
WHEREAS, the Washington Home donated one million dollars to Iona Senior Services
to fund the construction of a new adult day center to support older adults and their families in
Wards 7 and 8;
WHEREAS, the Washington Home’s legacy reflects the fundamental idea that every
older adult deserves to live with dignity, compassion, and the opportunity to remain connected to
their community; and
WHEREAS, the work carried forward in Southeast Washington, D.C. ensures that the
growing population of seniors throughout the District has access to services that provide them
with support and the opportunity for meaningful engagement;
WHEREAS, the center represents an important investment in bringing high-quality
senior services closer to the homes and communities of District residents east of the Anacostia
River;

WHEREAS, the Washington Home Center helps older adults remain connected to their
communities while providing families and caregivers with valuable support and resources;
WHEREAS, we recognize and appreciate the many caregivers, professionals, volunteers,
families, and community partners whose dedication has sustained this important work over many
years of care; and
WHEREAS, the Washington Home’s long-standing legacy of service demonstrates the
importance of community-based service that responds directly to the needs of District seniors
and their families.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Washington Home Center Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors the Washington
Home Center for its commitment to older adults, caregivers, and families in the District of
Columbia, extending its sincere appreciation for its continued service to our community.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
