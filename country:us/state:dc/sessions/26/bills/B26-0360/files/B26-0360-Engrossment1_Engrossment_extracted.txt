11 To amend section 47-863 of the District of Columbia Official Code to allow for the aggregation
12 of the ownership interests of 2 or more individuals over the age of 65 or who are disabled
13 to meet the 50% ownership requirement to qualify for the reduced property tax liability
14 program.
15
16 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
17 act may be cited as the “Senior Property Tax Aggregation Amendment Act of 2026”.
