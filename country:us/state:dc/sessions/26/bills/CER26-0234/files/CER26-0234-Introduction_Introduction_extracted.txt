22 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
23
24 __________
25
26 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
27
28 ______________
29
30 To recognize and honor Saint Martin of Tours Catholic Church on the occasion of its 125th
31 anniversary and celebrate its enduring legacy of faith, education, fellowship, and service
32 to generations of District residents.
33
34 WHEREAS, Saint Martin of Tours Catholic Church (“St. Martin’s”), now located at 1908
35 North Capitol Street NW, was established on September 8, 1901, under the leadership of its
36 founding pastor, Reverend Eugene A. Hannon;
37
38 WHEREAS, on October 13, 1901, the parish celebrated its first Mass at the Moore
39 Mansion near North Capitol and T Streets, marking the beginning of 125 years of worship,
40 fellowship, and service;
41
42 WHEREAS, in 1902, St. Martin’s began construction of its parish hall, which served as
43 both a place of worship and a school, establishing a longstanding commitment to education;
44

45 WHEREAS, as enrollment grew, Reverend Michael J. Riordan led a fundraising
46 campaign in 1923 to construct a larger school, which opened in September 1925 to
47 approximately 400 students, expanding educational opportunities for District children;
48
49 WHEREAS, in 1938, parishioners organized a fundraising campaign to construct the
50 church’s present sanctuary, laying its cornerstone on May 14, 1939, and celebrating its first Mass
51 on Christmas Eve of that year;
52
53 WHEREAS, in 1948, under the leadership of Archbishop Patrick A. O’Boyle, St.
54 Martin’s participated in the integration of Catholic schools in Washington, D.C., welcoming
55 African American students and advancing educational access during an era of widespread racial
56 segregation;
57
58 WHEREAS, in 1963, Reverend John J. McBride, led a group of St. Martin’s parishioners
59 in the historic March on Washington for Jobs and Freedom, affirming the church’s commitment
60 to justice and equality beyond the walls of the church;
61
62 WHEREAS, throughout the 1960s, 1970s, and 1980s, St. Martin’s expanded its
63 community outreach through the Forever Young Club, Teen Club, Senior Center, and other
64 ministries that fostered fellowship and provided support to residents of all ages;
65
66 WHEREAS, since 1989, St. Martin’s has joined with its sister parish in Rockville,
67 Maryland, to host an annual Thanksgiving dinner, bringing together congregations from different
68 communities and backgrounds in a shared tradition of service and fellowship;
69
70 WHEREAS, in 1990, St. Martin’s leased its former convent to Catholic Charities,
71 establishing St. Martin’s House as a transitional shelter for young mothers recovering from
72 substance abuse and working toward stability and independence;
73
74 WHEREAS, following the closure of its Catholic school, St. Martin’s continued to
75 dedicate its school building to education, including making the property available to City Lights
76 Public Charter School in 1991 to serve at-risk and special needs youth;
77
78 WHEREAS, under the leadership of Reverend Michael J. Kelley, St. Martin’s has
79 strengthened its commitment to the surrounding community through partnerships supporting
80 affordable housing, including the development of The Summit at St. Martin’s Apartments;
81
82 WHEREAS, through its Community Center, food and clothing ministries, youth
83 programs, Christmas Giving Tree, and other charitable initiatives, St. Martin’s has continued to
84 meet the needs of its neighbors, extending compassion and assistance to people regardless of
85 their faith or circumstances;
86
87 WHEREAS, for 10 years, St. Martin’s Black and Women’s History Ministry has honored
88 the contributions of African Americans and women through education, historical preservation,
89 and community engagement, celebrating their achievements and advancing the values of
90 equality, justice, and inclusion;

91
92 WHEREAS, during the COVID-19 pandemic, Reverend Michael J. Kelley guided the
93 parish's transition to virtual worship, extending its ministry beyond the District and connecting a
94 community of worshippers across the country;
95
96 WHEREAS, guided by its tradition of taking faith to the streets, St. Martin’s continues to
97 demonstrate that the spirit of the church lives not only within its sanctuary, but through the
98 commitment and celebration of generosity and justice its members extend to others; and
99
100 WHEREAS, in 2026, St. Martin’s celebrates 125 years of faith and service under the
101 banner of “Spirit Alive,” honoring the generations of clergy, religious sisters, parishioners,
102 educators, and volunteers whose devotion has sustained the church and its community.
103
104 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, that this
105 resolution be cited as the “Saint Martin of Tours Catholic Church 125th Anniversary Recognition
106 Resolution of 2026”.
107
108 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and congratulates Saint
109 Martin of Tours Catholic Church on its 125th anniversary and honors its enduring legacy of faith,
110 education, and service with doors open to all.
111
112 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
113 the District of Columbia Register
