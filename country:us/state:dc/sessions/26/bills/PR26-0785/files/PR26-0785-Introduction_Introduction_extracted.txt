10 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA

14 To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to amend the Ballpark
15 Omnibus Financing and Revenue Act of 2004 and section 47-2762 of the District of
16 Columbia Official Code to authorize the deposit of sports facilities fees in Fiscal Year
17 2027 into the Ballpark Preservation and Improvement Fund for the purpose of repaying
18 early redemption borrowing made to maintain the timeline for full repayment of the
19 bonds used to build the Nationals Ballpark, and to make conforming amendments.
20
21 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
22 resolution may be cited as “Ballpark Bonds Loan Repayment Emergency Declaration Resolution
23 of 2026”.
24 Sec. 2. (a) The District plans to assume short-term debt to take advantage of current
25 market conditions and accelerate repayment of the remaining debt on the ballpark bonds.
26 (b) This legislation will ensure that the Mayor and the Chief Financial Officer have the
27 appropriate authority to repay any such early redemption borrowing, pay debt service on the
28 bonds for the RFK infrastructure financing, fund any reserves required under the indenture for
29 the RFK infrastructure financing, and maintain the necessary timeline for full repayment of the
30 ballpark bonds.
31 (c) This authority must be in place at the start of the fiscal year to allow the Office of the
32 Chief Financial Officer to complete the early redemption borrowing in early October.

33 Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
34 enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
35 Ballpark Bonds Loan Repayment Emergency Amendment Act of 2026 be adopted after a single
36 reading.
37 Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
