16 To amend, on an emergency basis due to congressional review, the Language Access Act of 2004
17 to authorize the Mayor to issue rules to implement the provisions of that act.
18
19 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
20 act may be cited as the “Language Access Rulemaking Authority Congressional Review
21 Emergency Amendment Act of 2026”.
22 Sec. 2. Section 6 of the Language Access Act of 2004, effective June 19, 2004 (D.C. Law
23 15-167; D.C. Official Code § 2-1935), is amended by adding a new subsection (c) to read as
24 follows:
25 “(c) The Mayor, pursuant to Title I of the District of Columbia Administrative Procedure
26 Act, approved October 21, 1968 (82 Stat. 1204; D.C. Official Code § 2-501 et seq.), shall issue
27 rules to implement the provisions of this act.”.
28 Sec. 3. Applicability.
29 This act shall apply as of June 19, 2004.
30 Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
1

31 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Chief Financial Officer as the fiscal
32 impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
33 approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
34 Sec. 5. Effective date.
35 This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
36 Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), and shall remain in effect for no longer than
37 90 days, as provided for emergency acts of the Council of the District of Columbia in section
38 412(a) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 788;
39 D.C. Official Code § 1-204.12(a)).
