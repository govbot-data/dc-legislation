8 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
9
10 _________________________
11
12 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
13
14 _________________________
15
16
17 To recognize, honor, and celebrate Parisa Norouzi for 25 years of service to the Ivy City
18 community in Ward 5.
19
20 WHEREAS, Parisa Norouzi began working with Ivy City residents in 2001, helping to
21 document the neighborhood's history and supporting residents' efforts to improve their
22 community without displacement.
23
24 WHEREAS, over the past 25 years, Parisa’s work has grown to include the long-term
25 campaign to reclaim the historic Alexander Crummell School for community use, environmental
26 justice advocacy, resident-led land use planning, anti-displacement efforts, and continued work
27 to preserve and celebrate Ivy City's history.
28
29 WHEREAS, Parisa co-founded Empower DC with Linda Leaks in 2003 and has
30 continued to work alongside Ivy City residents throughout her tenure as Executive Director.
31
32 WHEREAS, Parisa’s 25-year commitment to Ivy City demonstrates a longstanding
33 commitment to sustained community organizing, a belief that residents should have meaningful
34 power over the decisions that shape their community, and to the leadership of the Ivy City
35 residents who have driven these efforts over the years.
36
37 WHEREAS, after decades of organizing by Ivy City residents, continuously supported by
38 Parisa and Empower DC, the Ivy City community recently celebrated a groundbreaking for the
39 new Crummell Community Center, a $35.6 million project that will transform the historic
40 Crummell School into a community center and recreation space.
41
42 WHEREAS, in 2025 under Parisa’s leadership, Empower DC, in partnership with the
43 Douglass Community Land Trust, secured a grant of over $1 million for the Ivy City Anti-
44 Displacement Demonstration Project, Keep Ivy City Home, to equip Ivy City residents with
1

45 knowledge, tools and support to shape short, medium, and long-term land use decisions and
46 prevent displacement.
47
48 WHEREAS, Parisa and Empower DC have advocated for environmental justice for
49 residents of Brentwood and Ivy City who continue to experience disproportionate exposure to
50 environmental harms and nuisances.
51
52 WHEREAS, on December 10, 2026, Empower DC will celebrate and thank Parisa for 25
53 years of service to the Ivy City Community.
54
55 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, that this
56 resolution may be cited as the “Parisa Norouzi Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
57
58 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and celebrates Parisa Norouzi
59 for 25 years of organizing and service alongside Ward 5 Ivy City residents nd for her long-
a
60 standing commitment to resident leadership, environmental and housing justice, and equitable
61 community development in the District of Columbia.
62
63 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
64 the District of Columbia Register.
