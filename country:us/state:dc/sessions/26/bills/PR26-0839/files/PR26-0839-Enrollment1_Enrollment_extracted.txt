ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-540
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
November 10, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to amend the Deputy Mayor
for Planning and Economic Development Limited Grant-Making Authority Act of 2012
to expand permissible uses of certain grant funding; and to amend the District of
Columbia Taxicab Commission Establishment Act of 1985 to permit rulemaking to
implement a carrier-for-hire support surcharge.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Budget Support Act Implementation Facilitation Emergency
Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) The Fiscal Year 2027 Budget Support Act of 2026, effective October 2, 2026
(D.C. Law 26-189; 73 DCR 11735) (“BSA”), contains numerous amendatory provisions to
conform the practices of the District government to the Council-approved budget for Fiscal Year
2027.
(b) BSA Title II, Subtitle F, the Rosemount Center Grant Amendment Act of 2026,
instructed the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic Development to issue a grant to the
Rosemount Center to support acquisition of the Center’s then-leased facility. Center
representatives have informed the Council that the purposes of the grant would be better served
if the grant funding could also be used for capital improvements.
(c) BSA Title VI, Subtitle T, the Carrier-for-Hire and Food Access Support Amendment
Act of 2026, established a surcharge on certain carrier-for-hire trips to raise revenue for food
access supports and other District government programs. The Department of For-Hire Vehicles,
which administers carrier-for-hire industry regulation, has noted that neither the BSA nor the
underlying law amended by this subtitle expressly authorizes the Mayor to issue regulations to
carry out the implementation of the surcharge.
(d) Immediate legislative action is necessary to address the emerging BSA
implementation challenges enumerated in subsections (b) and (c) of this section.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the Budget
