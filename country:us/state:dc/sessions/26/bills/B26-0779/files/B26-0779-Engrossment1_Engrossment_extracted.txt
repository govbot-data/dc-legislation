10 To amend, on a temporary basis, the Ballpark Omnibus Financing and Revenue Act of 2004 and
11 section 47-2762 of the District of Columbia Official Code to authorize the deposit of
12 sports facilities fees in Fiscal Year 2027 into the Ballpark Preservation and Improvement
13 Fund for the purpose of repaying early redemption borrowing made to maintain the
14 timeline for full repayment of the bonds used to build the Nationals Ballpark, and to
15 make conforming amendments.
16
17 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
18 act may be cited as the “Ballpark Bonds Loan Repayment Temporary Amendment Act of 2026”.
19 Sec. 2. (a) Section 102a of the Ballpark Omnibus Financing and Revenue Act of 2004,
20 effective March 7, 2025 (D.C. Law 15-320; D.C. Official Code § 10-1601.02a), is amended by
21 adding a new subsection (c-1) to read as follows:
22 “(c-1) Notwithstanding subsections (a), (b), and (c) of this section, in Fiscal Year 2027,
23 pursuant to D.C. Official Code § 47-2762(d), the revenues received by the District from the
24 sports facilities fee imposed by D.C. Official Code § 47-2762 shall be deposited in the Fund and
25 shall be used as follows:
26 “(1) All amounts up to $49,505,000, shall be used to pay the debt service on any
27 ballpark early redemption borrowing and to pay debt service on the bonds for the RFK

43 as follows:
44 “(d) The revenues received by the District from the sports facilities fee imposed by this

46 interest on the bonds issued pursuant to § 10-1601.03 has been paid:

58 (a) This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by
59 the Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), and a 30-day period of congressional
60 review as provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved
61 December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 788; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)).
