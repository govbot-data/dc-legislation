4 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
5
6 _________________________
7
8 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
9 _________________________
10
11
12 To celebrate the 50th anniversary of the Greater Washington Hispanic Chamber of Commerce and
13 recognize the impact the organization has had on advancing economic opportunity through
14 the District and Metropolitan region.
15
1

16 WHEREAS, the Greater Washington Hispanic Chamber of Commerce (GWHCC) was
17 founded in 1976 as the Ibero American Chamber of Commerce and has grown from its roots
18 serving the region’s Hispanic business community into a leading regional organization connecting
19 entrepreneurs and small businesses with corporations, financial institutions, government agencies,
20 and economic-development resources;
21 WHEREAS, the GWHCC has provided entrepreneurs with technical assistance,
22 educational programming, access-to-capital resources, business development support, workshops,
23 and one-on-one guidance designed to help businesses start, strengthen, and expand;
24 WHEREAS, GWHCC has increasingly served as a bridge between the Washington
25 business community and the international marketplace welcoming more than 190 attendees, 54+
26 ambassadors, and representation from more than 64 countries to annual luncheons;
27 WHEREAS, throughout its history, the Chamber has built partnerships across the private,
28 public, nonprofit, and financial sectors, helping connect small and minority-owned businesses to
29 resources and opportunities that might otherwise be difficult to access;
30 WHEREAS, GWHCC has received national recognition for their work, being named
31 Hispanic Chamber of Commerce of the Year by the United States Hispanic Chamber of
32 Commerce; and
33 WHEREAS, the milestone recognizes not only the Chamber’s longevity, but also the
34 thousands of entrepreneurs, businesses, community leaders, corporate partners, government
35 agencies, and advocates who have contributed to strengthening the Hispanic and minority business
36 community in Washington over the past five decades.
2

37 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
38 resolution may be cited as the “Greater Washington Hispanic Chamber of Commerce 50th
39 Anniversary Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
40 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the 50th anniversary of the
41 Greater Washington Hispanic Chamber of Commerce and their focus to expand economic
42 opportunity, strengthen small businesses, and build a more connected and competitive regional
43 business community.
44 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
