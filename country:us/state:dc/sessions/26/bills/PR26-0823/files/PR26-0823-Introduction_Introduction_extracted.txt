Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Enclosed for consideration and approval by the Council of the District of Columbia is the “Compensation
and Working Conditions Agreement between the District of Columbia Government and the International
Association of Fire Fighters, Local 36, AFL-CIO Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026” and the
“Compensation and Working Conditions Agreement between the District of Columbia Government and the
International Association of Fire Fighters, Local 36, AFL-CIO Emergency Approval Resolution of 2026.”
The collective bargaining agreement that will be approved by these resolutions provides the following wage
increases to bargaining unit members of the International Association of Fire Fighters, Local 36, AFL-CIO:
• 2.0% for Fiscal Year 2025 (effective the first full pay period on or after October 1, 2024);
• 3.5% for Fiscal Year 2026 (effective the first full pay period on or after October 1, 2025); and
• 3.5% for Fiscal Year 2026 (effective the first full pay period on or after October 1, 2026).
The agreement also increases the premium pay provided to dual role Firefighter/Paramedics. Currently,
Firefighter/Paramedics are paid a premium of 15 percent of the Class 1, Step 1 of the Class and Step to
which they are entitled. The new Agreement increases the premium rate to 18 percent of their current Class
and Step beginning October 1, 2026.
Additionally, the agreement also increases employer contributions to Optical and Dental insurance
premiums. The employer contribution for optical insurance increases in steps from $10.80 per month to
$24.33 by the end of fiscal year 2027. The employer contribution for dental insurance changes from $60.81
per month for any plan, to $56.82 for a single plan and $108.48 for a family plan.
I appreciate your attention to this important agreement that will benefit our first responders. Please contact
me or E. Lindsey Maxwell II, Director, Office of Labor Relations and Collective Bargaining, at (202) 724-
4953, should you have questions concerning this transmittal.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowser
Mayor

17 To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve the negotiated

23 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
24 resolution may be cited as the "Compensation and Working Conditions Agreement between the
25 District of Columbia Government and the International Association of Fire Fighters, Local 36,
26 AFL-CIO Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026".
27 Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve the negotiated compensation and
28 working conditions agreement submitted by the Mayor for employees of the Fire and Emergency
29 Medical Services Department ("FEMS") who are represented by the International Association of
30 Fire Fighters, Local 36, AFL-CIO ("IAFF").
31 (b) The District of Columbia negotiated a compensation and working conditions
32 agreement ("negotiated agreement") for certain FEMS employees represented by the IAFF that
33 includes compensation increases over a period of 3 years.

34 (c) The negotiation of this agreement significantly increases compensation for FMS
35 employees as well as addresses working conditions concerns of both FEMS and IAFF. FEMS
36 values a positive work environment and the best resources and opportunities for professional
37 growth for all of its employees. This negotiated agreement will enhance FEMS’s strong system
38 of support by providing employees represented by IAFF with additional professional
39 compensation and premier working conditions.
40 (d) In order to implement the compensation increases, the Department of Human Resources
41 and the Office of the Chief Financial Officer must carry out a number of lengthy administrative
42 and technological actions.
43 (e) It is important this negotiated agreement be approved as soon as possible so that the
44 Department of Human Resources and the Office of the Chief Financial Officer can promptly
45 begin to carry out and complete these actions so that employees represented by the IAFF can
46 benefit in a timely manner.
47 Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
48 enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
49 Compensation and Working Conditions Agreement between the District of Columbia
50 Government and the International Association of Fire Fighters, Local 36, AFL-CIO Emergency
51 Approval Resolution of 2026 be adopted on an emergency basis.
52 Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
