ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-506
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve multiyear Contract
No. DCAM-21-NC-RFQuals-0001C PPA-26-01 with Flywheel Development, LLC.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Contract No. DCAM-21-NC-RFQuals-0001C PPA-26-01 with
Flywheel Development, LLC Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve Contract No. DCAM-21-NC-
RFQuals-0001C PPA-26-01 with Flywheel Development, LLC.
(b) On June 26, 2026, the Department of General Services (“DGS”) executed a letter
contract with Flywheel Development, LLC to authorize the immediate commencement of early-
stage work to develop solar photovoltaic systems at 27 District-owned sites.
(c) DGS now wishes to enter into a definitive multiyear contract with Flywheel
Development, LLC for the same purpose.
(d) Council approval is necessary to allow the District to receive the benefit of reducing
the District’s energy consumption from existing traditional energy sources and lower overall
energy costs.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Contract No. DCAM-21-NC-RFQuals-0001C PPA-26-01 with Flywheel Development, LLC
Emergency Approval Resolution of 2026 be adopted on an emergency basis.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
