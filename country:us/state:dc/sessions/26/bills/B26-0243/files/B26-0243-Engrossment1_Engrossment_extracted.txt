10 To require that the Department of Energy and Environment, in consultation with the Public
11 Service Commission, the Office of the People’s Counsel, utilities, and agency designees,
12 create a process through which it automatically identifies eligible District residents,
13 supplemented by data provided by relevant health agencies, for enrollment into utility
14 affordability programs or other DOEE-administered income-qualified programs, and to
15 require the relevant entities to enroll eligible residents.
16
17 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
18 act may be cited as the “Automatic Enrollment for Utility Affordability Programs Act of 2026”.
19 Sec. 2. Household identification and automatic enrollment process.
20 (a)(1) Within one year after the applicability date of this act, the Department of Energy
21 and Environment, in consultation with the Public Service Commission, the Office of People’s
22 Counsel, utilities, and agency designees, shall implement a process for automatic identification
23 of eligible households for participation in District utility affordability programs or other DOEE-
24 administered, income-qualified programs. DOEE shall use the information obtained in response
25 to its request pursuant to subsection (b) of this section to identify eligible households to be
26 enrolled using this process, subject to paragraph (2) and (3) of this subsection.
27 (2) DOEE may prioritize automatic identification of eligible households based on
28 household income levels or other criteria demonstrating a need for assistance if a District utility

29 affordability program or other DOEE-administered, income-qualified program is not able to
30 support automatic enrollment of all eligible households due to funding constraints or other
31 limitations.
32 (3) DOEE may choose the District utility affordability programs or other DOEE-
33 administered, income-qualified programs for which the agency identifies eligible households to
34 be enrolled. DOEE shall not be required to include the Low-Income Home Energy Assistance
35 Program or the Weatherization Assistance Program in the automatic identification process. Once
36 DOEE or its designee identifies a household for automatic enrollment, DOEE or its designee
37 may enroll eligible households or facilitate enrollment in additional programs as relevant.
38 (4) The process developed pursuant to paragraph (1) of this subsection shall
39 include a data-sharing protocol that ensures that DOEE or its designee will not share individuals’
40 personal identifying information, including the individual’s name, account number, service
41 address, and phone number, for purposes other than administering this section or for the purposes
42 of verification, research, evaluation, and analysis.
43 (b)(1) Consistent with 7. C.F.R. § 272.1(c)(1) and notwithstanding any other District law,
44 upon request by DOEE for the purposes described in subsections (a) and (d) of this section, the
45 Mayor shall provide, or cause to be provided, the enrollment data listed below to DOEE:
46 (A) For the Department of Human Services (“DHS”), enrollment data
47 from households participating in any program administered by DHS for which the eligibility is
