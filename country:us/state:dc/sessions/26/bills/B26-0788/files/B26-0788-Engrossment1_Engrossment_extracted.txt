10 To amend, on a temporary basis, the Community Schools Incentive Act of 2012 to revise the
11 requirement that appointees to the Community Schools Task Force must be District
12 residents.
13
14 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
15 act may be cited as the “Community Schools Grant Program Temporary Amendment Act of
16 2026”.
17 Sec. 2. Section 403(i)(2) of the Community Schools Incentive Act of 2012, effective June
18 19, 2012 (D.C. Law 19-142; D.C. Official Code § 38-754.03(i)(2), is amended by striking the
19 phrase “District residents” and inserting the word “individuals” in its place.
20 Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.
21 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Budget Director as the fiscal impact
22 statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved
23 October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
24 Sec. 4. Effective date.
25 This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
26 Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto) and a 30-day period of congressional review
