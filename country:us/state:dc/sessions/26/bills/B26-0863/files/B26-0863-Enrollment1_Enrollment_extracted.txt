ENROLLED ORIGINAL
AN ACT
_______________
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
_______________
To amend, on an emergency basis, the Board of Ethics and Government Accountability
Establishment and Comprehensive Ethics Reform Amendment Act of 2011 to clarify that
a transition committee may be organized on behalf of the Mayor-elect and to adopt the
definition of “Mayor-elect” provided in the Mayoral Transition Act of 2026; and to
amend the Official Correspondence Regulations to adopt the same definition of “Mayor-
elect”.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited as the “Mayoral Transition Emergency Amendment Act of 2026”.
Sec. 2. Section 101 of the Board of Ethics and Government Accountability Establishment
and Comprehensive Ethics Reform Amendment Act of 2011, effective April 27, 2012 (D.C. Law
19-124; D.C. Official Code § 1-1161.01), is amended as follows:
(a) Paragraph (33C) is redesignated as paragraph (33D).
(b) A new paragraph (33C) is added to read as follows:
“(33C) “Mayor-elect” means the person who is certified as the successful
candidate for the office of Mayor by the Board of Elections following the 2026 general election
held to determine the Mayor or, prior to such certification, the person announced and published
by the Board of Elections as the unofficial winner of the 2026 general election for Mayor;
provided, that such person was announced as the unofficial winner with a margin of victory of at
least 2% of the votes cast.”.
(c) Paragraph (52) is amended by striking the phrase “on behalf of the Mayor, Attorney
General” and inserting the phrase “on behalf of the Mayor, the Mayor-elect, Attorney General”
in its place.
Sec. 3. Section 6(1) of the Official Correspondence Regulations, effective April 7, 1977
(D.C. Law 1-118; D.C. Official Code § 2-705(1)), is amended by striking the semicolon and
inserting the phrase “, as defined in section 1042 of the Mayoral Transition Act of 2026,
effective October 2, 2026 (D.C. Law 26-189; 73 DCR 11735);” in its place.

Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement.
The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Budget Director as the fiscal impact
statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved
October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
Sec. 5. Effective date.
This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), and shall remain in effect for no longer than
90 days, as provided for emergency acts of the Council of the District of Columbia in section
412(a) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 788;
D.C. Official Code § 1-204.12(a)).
