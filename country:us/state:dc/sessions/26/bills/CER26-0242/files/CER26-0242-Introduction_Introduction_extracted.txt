14 To recognize Jakaila Davis for her dedicated service as Legislative Clerk to Councilmember
15 Robert C. White, Jr., and for her contributions to legislative operations, education policy,
16 and protecting residents with pets in housing.
17 WHEREAS, Jakaila Davis serves as Legislative Clerk in the office of Councilmember
18 Robert C. White, Jr., contributing to the organization, coordination, and continuity of the office’s
19 legislative work;
20 WHEREAS, Jakaila Davis helps manage the documents, schedules, and procedures that
21 enable a busy Council office to respond to residents and meet the demands of the legislative
22 calendar;
23 WHEREAS, her work reflects the importance of accuracy, reliability, and preparation in
24 helping the Councilmember and policy staff fulfill their responsibilities;
25 WHEREAS, Jakaila Davis has taken on the office’s policy work concerning pets in
26 housing, supporting consideration of the protections and practical challenges affecting residents
27 who share their homes with companion animals;
28 WHEREAS, these housing matters call for thoughtful consideration of tenant
29 circumstances, housing rules, and the relationship between stable housing and family life;

30 WHEREAS, Jakaila Davis has also assumed responsibility for education matters, helping
31 the office follow issues that affect District students, families, and schools;
32 WHEREAS, by taking on substantive policy assignments in addition to her work as
33 Legislative Clerk, she has helped expand the office’s capacity to monitor legislation and engage
34 with community concerns;
35 WHEREAS, Jakaila Davis’s willingness to learn new portfolios and take on additional
36 responsibilities reflects her initiative and commitment to the work of the Council;
37 WHEREAS, colleagues rely on the behind-the-scenes coordination she provides and on
38 the care with which she approaches assignments both administrative and substantive;
39 WHEREAS, Jakaila Davis possesses a remarkable ability to solve a mystery,
40 approaching unanswered questions and unexpected challenges with curiosity, determination, and
41 an impressive instinct for uncovering details others might overlook;
42 WHEREAS, Jakaila Davis commands policy debates with confidence, intelligence, and
43 sharp insight, while her hilarious and spirited personality brings laughter, energy, and an
44 unmistakable sense of joy to the office; and
45 WHEREAS, Jakaila Davis has shown that meaningful public service often grows through
46 a readiness to contribute wherever the work requires, keeping the needs of District residents at
47 the center.
48 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
49 resolution may be cited as the “Jakaila Davis Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
50 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Jakaila Davis for her
51 dedicated legislative service, leadership on education and pets-in-housing matters, keen
52 investigative instincts, confident contributions to policy debates, and joyful sense of humor that
53 strengthens the office.
