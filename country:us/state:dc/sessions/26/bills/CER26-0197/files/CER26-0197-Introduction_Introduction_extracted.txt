23 To commemorate L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc.’s 35th Anniversary.
24
25 WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. (LSC) has specialized in the development,
26 implementation, and administration of contracting, employment, and community awareness
27 compliance programs since its founding and under the leadership of President and CEO Loretta
28 S. Caldwell and Vice President C. Eugene Harvey;
29
30 WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. has spent the last three and a half decades
31 operating as an economic development engine, community advocate, and trusted business
32 partner across the District, contributing to the growth and equity of the District’s economy;
33
34 WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. is headquartered in Ward 4 at 5427 14th
35 Street, N.W.;
36
37 WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. has impacted the Ward 8 community
38 through projects such as the St. Elizabeths West Campus Modernization, where the company
39 managed Disadvantaged Business Enterprise (DBE) and Certified Business Enterprise (CBE)
40 compliance, local resident job placements, certified payroll auditing, and local business
41 participation monitoring for the major federal and municipal redevelopment; The Commons at
42 Stanton Square, a Community of Hope facility, where LSC administered community outreach
43 programs, local hiring requirements, and CBE subcontracting compliance for essential
44 community healthcare infrastructure; and DC Water critical utility and clean water
45 modernization initiatives, where LSC managed workforce development, CBE tracking, and
46 compliance services across Ward 8;
1

47
48 WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. has directly expanded job training,
49 contracting, and apprenticeship opportunities for Ward 8 residents and small businesses;
50
51 WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. managed contracting, Certified Business
52 Enterprise compliance, local hiring, and community outreach for the $62 million Ward 4 Short-
53 Term Family Housing project;
54
55 WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. provided workforce development,
56 Certified Business Enterprise tracking, and compliance services for 27 DC Water and Clean
57 Rivers capital projects in Ward 4, including water main and utility modernization work;
58
59 WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. has also administered programs for
60 transformative public and private projects across the District of Columbia beyond Wards 4 and 8
61 such as CityCenterDC, Fort Lincoln and The Shops at Dakota Crossing, the Purple Line Transit
62 Project, and the Woodrow Wilson Bridge Megaproject;
63
64 WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. has crafted several vital economic
65 development policies, legislation, and procurement frameworks, including the origin and updates
66 of the District CBE program to protect and support local firms; and
67
68 WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. hosted an anniversary reception on Friday,
69 September 18, 2026, to highlight the economic programs successfully implemented and achieved
70 resulting in billions in contract dollars for District businesses and thousands of employment
71 opportunities for District residents.
72
73 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
74 resolution may be cited as the “L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. 35th Anniversary Recognition
75 Resolution of 2026”.
76
77 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia thanks and commends L. S. Caldwell &
78 Associates, Inc. for its 35 years of commitment to local equity, employment, economic inclusion
79 and business compliance, and recognizes Friday, September 18, 2026, as a day of celebration for
80 L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc.
81
82 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
