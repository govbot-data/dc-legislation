Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Pursuant to section 451 of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act (D.C. Official Code § 1-
204.51) and section 202 of the Procurement Practices Reform Act of 2010 (D.C. Official Code §
2-352.02), enclosed for consideration and approval by the Council of the District of Columbia is a
proposed resolution, the “Multiyear Contract No. CW134469 with Reworld Fairfax, LLC
Emergency Approval Resolution of 2026,” and the accompanying emergency declaration
resolution along with proposed Multiyear Contract No. CW134469 with Reworld Fairfax, LLC.
Under the proposed contract, Reworld Fairfax, LLC will provide municipal solid waste disposal
services in the amount of $46,818,000.
My administration is available to discuss any questions you may have regarding the proposed
contract. In order to facilitate a response to any questions you may have, please have your staff
contact Marc Scott, Chief Operating Officer, Office of Contracting and Procurement, at (202) 724-
8759.
I urge the Council to take prompt and favorable action on the enclosed legislation.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowser
