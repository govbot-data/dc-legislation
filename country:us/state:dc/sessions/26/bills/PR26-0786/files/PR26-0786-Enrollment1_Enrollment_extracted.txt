ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-511
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to amend Chapter 10 of Title
47 of the District of Columbia Official Code to provide a property tax exemption for the
property situated on Lot 0005, Square 3766 at 219 Riggs Road, NE.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Food & Friends Property Tax Exemption Emergency Declaration
Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) On November 4, 2025, the Council approved the Food & Friends Property Tax
Exemption Emergency Amendment Act of 2025, effective November 4, 2025 (D.C. Act 26-211;
72 DCR 13678)(“Emergency Act”) to ensure that Food & Friends retains an administrative
exemption from real property tax on 97% the land located at 219 Riggs Road, NE. The
Emergency Act expired on February 22, 2026.
(b) On December 2, 2025, the Council approved the Food & Friends Property Tax
Exemption Temporary Amendment Act of 2025, effective February 12, 2026 (D.C. Law 26-91;
73 DCR 7)(“Temporary Act”), which is identical to the Emergency Act. The Temporary Act
expires on September 25, 2026.
(c) The expiration of the Temporary Act, without a new round of emergency legislation,
would have an adverse impact on the operation of Food & Friends. As such, a second round of
emergency legislation is necessary. The Committee of the Whole intends to mark up a permanent
version of the legislation on September 22.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the Food &
Friends Property Tax Exemption Emergency Amendment Act of 2026 be adopted after a single
reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
