15 To recognize and honor Amber Gove for her years of exemplary leadership and service to the
16 residents of Advisory Neighborhood Commission 6A, Ward 6, and the District of
17 Columbia.
18
19 WHEREAS, Amber Gove earned a PhD in International Comparative Education and an MA
20 in Economics from Stanford University;
21
22 WHEREAS, Amber Gove was awarded both a Rotary International Ambassadorial
23 Scholarship and a Fulbright Scholarship;
24
25 WHEREAS, Amber Gove worked as education researcher, economist, and development
26 practitioner with projects in more than 20 countries for more than 20 years;
27
28 WHEREAS, Amber Gove is the author of at least 35 professional publications;
29
30 WHEREAS, Amber Gove has lived in the District of Columbia since 1999 and in the Capitol
31 Hill community since 2007;
32
33 WHEREAS, despite having two young children, an international career, and a frequently
34 traveling husband, in 2017, Amber Gove decided to run for the position of Advisory Neighborhood
35 Commissioner in ANC 6A;
36
37 WHEREAS, Amber Gove was elected Chair of ANC 6A, a position in which she served from
38 February 2019 until January 2025 and again from February 2026 until her departure in January
39 2027, also serving as Vice-Chair and Parliamentarian during the interim period;
40
41 WHEREAS, in addition to her roles as ANC Commissioner and Chair, Amber Gove is the Co-
42 Founder and Chief Fun Officer of Hill Family Biking, an organization which advocates for safer
43 streets and bike routes and that sponsors monthly safe and joyful family-oriented rides attracting
44 more than 700 riders for some events;
45
1

46 WHEREAS, during Commissioner Gove’s tenure with ANC 6A, the ANC has earned a
47 reputation for professionalism, community engagement, and effectiveness, and has enhanced the
48 internal infrastructure and external relationships necessary to effectively represent ANC 6A’s
49 approximately 14,000 constituents;
50
51 WHEREAS, under Commissioner Gove’s leadership, the ANC adopted written Standard
52 Operating Procedures and a Code of Conduct to further advance the integrity of the ANC’s
53 operations;
54
55 WHEREAS, she has attended hundreds of ANC, ANC Committee and related DC government
56 meetings, and written, collaborated on, and signed hundreds of official letters and other documents
57 on behalf of ANC 6A;
58
59 WHEREAS, Commissioner Gove’s tenure has been marked by a passion for expanded
60 transportation options and safety, including championing Vision Zero initiatives and traffic safety
61 improvements across the neighborhood, and spearheading community input and oversight for the
62 major redesign of North Carolina Avenue and C Street, NE;
63
64 WHEREAS, Commissioner Gove is also a deeply committed DC Public Schools parent, and
65 she has worked hard to guide community input during major school modernizations and
66 renovations, including at Maury Elementary School and Eliot-Hine Middle School;
67
68 WHEREAS, Commissioner Gove has organized countless community safety walks and
69 neighborhood cleanups;
70
71 WHEREAS, Commissioner Gove is a superuser of the District’s 311 system and a constant
72 voice of encouragement for others to do the same;
73
74 WHEREAS, Commissioner Gove wants every community member to receive the city services
75 they deserve, and she has relentlessly advocated for the resolution of everyday constituent issues
76 ranging from uncollected trash, streets and alleys not cleared of snow, rodent infestations, and
77 remediation of vacant, blighted and nuisance property;
78
79 WHEREAS, Commissioner Gove found time in her busy professional and family schedule to
80 draft, submit, and deliver testimony on numerous occasions to the Council and various agencies
81 on behalf of ANC 6A;
82
83 WHEREAS, during her tenure on ANC 6A, Commissioner Gove oversaw the approval and
84 distribution of numerous ANC 6A grants to Parent Teacher Organizations affiliated with schools
85 located in or serving children from the ANC 6A community and to other not-for-profit
86 organizations supporting community development, beautification, and engagement;
87
88 WHEREAS, Commissioner Gove devoted a significant amount of time and effort to providing
89 meaningful, thoughtful, and balanced agendas for monthly ANC meetings, including recruiting
90 knowledgeable and relevant experts and government and community representatives for opening
91 presentations;

92
93 WHEREAS, when 3 of 7 ANC 6A positions became open mid-term in 2026, Commissioner
94 Gove ensured that special elections were held in a timely and legal manner;
95
96 WHEREAS, Commissioner Gove devotes significant time and effort to creating a respectful
97 and collaborative community among a diverse group of ANC commissioners, committee chairs
98 and committee members, and she displays a unique talent for allowing various opinions and voices
99 to be heard and for mediating potential conflicts;
100
101 WHEREAS, Commissioner Gove’s ANC colleagues and constituents have benefited from her
102 willingness to share her expertise, encouragement, and time, as evidenced by the following small
103 sample of laudatory comments:

112 • “Amber was our advocate [as a small business]. She was always checking-in and working

114 neighborhood concerns …when necessary. Amber will be missed.”;

116 super deep into the Byzantine world that is city government. …because she realized that
117 [it] can be an incredibly powerful lever for change at the neighborhood level.”; and
118 • “I would say that her biggest accomplishment is tireless community building and being a
119 steady, positive and uniting force to accomplish goals.”; and
120
121 WHEREAS, Commissioner Gove has made a significant and lasting impact on ANC 6A and
122 the greater Capitol Hill Community.
123
124 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this resolution
125 may be cited as the “Amber Gove Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
126
127 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors Amber Gove for her
128 years of exemplary leadership and service to the residents of Advisory Neighborhood Commission
129 6A, Ward 6, and the District.

131 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
