Statement of Introduction
Health Benefit Exchange Authority Technical Amendment Act of 2026
September 21, 2026
Today, I am pleased to introduce the Health Benefit Exchange Authority Technical
Amendments Act of 2026. This legislation would make several technical and clarifying
amendments to the Health Benefit Exchange Authority’s (HBX) authorizing legislation.
The Health Benefit Exchange Authority was first established in 2012 via the Health Benefit
Exchange Authority Establishment Act of 2011 (the Act). The Act established HBX as an
independent authority of the District government and provides for the agency’s Executive Director
and staff to be overseen by an Executive Board that includes seven voting members appointed by
the Mayor and approved by the Council.
Since HBX’s inception in 2012, the Council has, on several occasions, amended the Act to
clarify the statutory language and adopt other changes. For example, in 2015, the Council approved
legislation to clarify the statutory language for HBX’s independent procurement authority.1 The
legislation I am introducing today would similarly make clarifying and technical amendments.
First, the legislation would clarify that HBX’s purposes and authorities include effectuating
anything in the Affordable Care Act and applicable District law. Second, the legislation amends
Executive Board membership to include one board member with specific expertise in information
technology. Third, the legislation clarifies HBX’s independent personnel status by de-linking the
agency’s compensation scale from the District’s to enhance their competitiveness in hiring,
especially in areas of IT and technology. Fourth, the legislation refines the Act’s code of conduct
to remove a conflicting provision with the District’s Comprehensive Code of Conduct. The
legislation also makes other technical amendments.
I look forward to working with my Council colleagues to move forward this legislation,
which would enhance the operations of the Health Benefit Exchange Authority and its Executive
Board, which provides critical access to health insurance for District residents.
