10 To prohibit the sale and distribution of mercury-containing, compact fluorescent lamps and
11 linear fluorescent lamps, to establish periodic inspections of distributors or retailers to
12 determine compliance, to require the Department of Energy and the Environment to
13 issue warnings or civil penalties for violations of this act.
14
15 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That
16 this act may be cited as “Shine Safely Act of 2026”.
17 Sec. 2. Definitions.
18 For the purposes of this act, the term:
19 (1) “Compact fluorescent lamp” means a compact low-pressure, mercury-
20 containing, electric discharge light source in which a fluorescent coating transforms some of the
21 ultraviolet energy generated by the mercury discharge into visible light, and includes all of the
22 following characteristics:
23 (A) One base (end cap) of any type, including screw, bayonet, 2 pins, and
24 4 pins;
25 (B) Integrally ballasted or non-integrally ballasted;

26 (C) Light emission between a correlated color temperature of 1700K and
27 24000K and a Duv of +0.024 and -0.024 in the International Commission on Illumination
28 (“CIE”) Uniform Color Space (“CAM02-UCS”);
29 (D) All tube diameters and all tube lengths; and
30 (E) All lamp sizes and shapes for directional and nondirectional
31 installations, including: PL, spiral, twin tube, triple twin, 2D, U-bend, and circular.
32 (2) “Linear fluorescent lamp” means a low-pressure, mercury-containing, electric-
33 discharge light source in which fluorescent coating transforms some of the ultraviolet energy
34 generated by the mercury discharge into visible light, and includes all of the following
35 characteristics:
36 (A) Two bases (end caps) of any type, including single-pin, 2-pin, and
37 recessed double contact;
38 (B) Light emission between a correlated color temperature of 1700K and
39 24000K and a Duv of +0.024 and -0.024 in the CIE CAM02-UCS;
40 (C) All tube diameters, including, but not limited to, T5, T8, T10, and
41 T12;
42 (D) All tube lengths from 0.5 to 8.0 feet, inclusive; and
43 (E) All lamp shapes, including linear, U-bend, and circular.

87 Sec. 4. Implementation.
88 (a)(1) The Department of Energy and the Environment (“DOEE”) may conduct
89 inspections of distributors or retailers in order to determine compliance with the provisions of
90 this act. As part of the inspection, DOEE may request and review records of sales in the
91 District of products prohibited by this act, and distributors and retailers shall produce such
92 requested records.
93 (2) DOEE shall track all complaints received concerning violations of this act,
94 the agency’s response to each complaint, and the resolution of each complaint.
95 (b)(1) If DOEE finds that a person has committed a violation of any provision of this
96 act, DOEE shall issue a warning to such person if it is their first violation.
97 (2) A person who commits a second violation shall be subject to a civil penalty
98 of up to $500 for each offense.
99 (3) A person who commits any further violations shall be subject to a civil
100 penalty of not more than $1,000 for each offense.
101 (c) In addition to other enforcement authority provided in this section, DOEE may:
