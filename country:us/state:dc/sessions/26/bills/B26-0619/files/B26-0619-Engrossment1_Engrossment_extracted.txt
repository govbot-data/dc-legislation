9 To amend section 7 of the District of Columbia Traffic Act, 1925, to allow applicants for a driver’s
10 license, permit, or identification card to indicate on the credential the applicant’s
11 nonapparent disability, to designate a butterfly symbol as the nonapparent disability
12 indicator, to require the Department of Motor Vehicles to conduct public outreach on the
13 availability of a nonapparent disability indicator, to prohibit requiring proof of a
14 nonapparent disability, to prohibit the Department from using an applicant’s nonapparent
15 disability indicator as a means to require a driver license medical review process unless
16 otherwise required by District law or regulation, and to require the Metropolitan Police
17 Department to implement training concerning interactions with individuals with
18 nonapparent disabilities.
19
20 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
21 act may be cited as the “Eric’s ID Amendment Act of 2026”.
22 Sec. 2. Section 7 of the District of Columbia Traffic Act, 1925, approved March 3, 1925
23 (43 Stat. 1121; D.C. Official Code § 50-1401.01), is amended by adding a new subsection (i-1) to
24 read as follows:
25 “(i-1)(1) On any new or renewal application for a driver’s license, permit, or
26 identification card issued pursuant to this section, section 8a, or section 8c, the Department shall
27 offer an applicant the option to have their driver’s license, permit, or identification card indicate
28 that the applicant has a nonapparent disability through the use of an indicator or notation.
29 “(2)(A) For an applicant who chooses to indicate a nonapparent disability, an

41 “(4) The Department shall conduct a public outreach campaign on the availability
42 of a nonapparent disability indicator in consultation with the Office of Disability Rights.
43 “(5) The Department shall remove a nonapparent disability indicator or notation
44 from a driver’s license, permit, or identification card upon request.
45 “(6) The Department shall not:
46 “(A) Require proof of a nonapparent disability before approving the
47 nonapparent disability indicator or notation on a driver’s license, permit, or identification card;
48 “(B) Use information disclosed by an individual under this section as a
49 basis to require the individual to undergo a driver's license medical review process, unless the

51 “(7)(A) Within 180 days after the effective date of the Eric’s ID Amendment Act
52 of 2026, as approved by the Committee on Transportation and the Environment on September 16,
53 2026 (Committee Print of Bill 26-619), the Metropolitan Police Department (“MPD”) shall

59 ADA coordinator.
60 “(8) For the purposes of this subsection, the term:
61 “(A) “Disability” shall have the same meaning as provided in section 2(4)
62 of the Disability Rights Protection Act of 2006, effective March 8, 2007 (D.C. Law 16-239; D.C.
63 Official Code § 2-1431.01(4)).
64 “(B) “Nonapparent disability” means a disability that is not immediately
65 obvious or visible, including developmental disabilities and intellectual disabilities.”.
