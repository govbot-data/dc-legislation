IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICTOFCOLUMBIA

To amend, on an emergency basis, the ProcurementPracticesReformAct of2010 to require the
Office ofContracting andProcurement to process requests forthe equitableadjustmentof
homeless services contracts within 60 days; and to amend section 510ofthe Rental
Housing Act to changethe applicability date.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited as the“Homeless Services Contract AdjustmentEmergency Amendment Act of
2026”.
Sec. 2. The Procurement Practices Reform Act of2010, effective April 8, 2011 (D.C.
Law 18-371; D.C. Official Code § 2-351.01 efseq.), is amended by adding a newsection 1105a
to read as follows:
“Sec. 1105a. Equitable adjustments ofhomeless services contracts.
“(a)(1) Within 60 calendardaysofreceiving acompleterequest forthe equitable
adjustment ofa contractto providehomeless services undersection 3ofthe Homeless Services
Reform Act, effective October22, 2005 (D.C. Law 16-35; D.C. Official Code § 4-754.01), on
thebasisof the revisionof a wage determination issued by the U.S. Department ofLabor
pursuant to 41 U.S.C. § 6703, OCP shall determine whethertherequesting contractorisentitled
to anequitable adjustment.
“(2)A request foran equitable adjustment underthis subsection iscomplete when
therequesting contractorhas submitted all informationand documents necessary forOCPto
assesstherequest.
“(b) Within 7 business daysofthe submission to OCPofall information and documents
necessary to completeanequitable adjustmentrequest under subsection (a)ofthis section, OCP
shall informthe requestingcontractor in writingthat it considerstherequestcomplete.”.
Sec. 3. Section 510ofthe Rental HousingAct, effective May 18, 2022 (D.C. Law24-
115; D.C. Official Code § 42-3505.10) is amended as follows:
(a)Thelead-in languageofsubsection (b-3) is amended by striking thedate“October 1,
2025,” and inserting the date “October 1, 2026,” in its place.
