8 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
9
10 _________________________
11
12 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
13
14 _________________________
15
16
17 To honor and celebrate the life of Mrs. Bessie M. Johnson on the occasion of her 102nd birthday,
18 and to recognize her vital role in the Ward 5 community.
19
20 WHEREAS, Mrs. Bessie M. Johnson has lived in Washington, D.C., for 87 years, having
21 moved to the District from Cambridge, Maryland, at the age of 14;
22
23 WHEREAS, Mrs. Johnson has proudly called Ward 5 home for more than 65 years,
24 becoming an integral part of the community;
25
26 WHEREAS, Mrs. Johnson, together with her late husband, James Johnson, raised their
27 nine children in the District, building a strong and loving family foundation that continues to
28 flourish;
29
30 WHEREAS, Mrs. Johnson and the legacy of love she built with her late husband, has
31 grown into a beautiful multigenerational family that includes 19 grandchildren, 23 great-
32 grandchildren, and 14 great-great-grandchildren;
33
34 WHEREAS, Mrs. Johnson’s love extends far beyond her family and into the
35 neighborhood she has called home for decades - she is deeply cherished by her neighbors, and
36 even former neighbors who return to visit, share memories and reminisce with her, especially
37 during the holiday season;
38
39 WHEREAS, Mrs. Johnson’s home and presence continue to represent a special
40 connection to the community and the lasting bonds she has built over the years;
41
42 WHEREAS, Mrs. Johnson is known for her famous sweet potato pies, warm heart, and
43 generous spirit, and she has always made others feel welcome and loved;
44
1

45 WHEREAS, throughout the years, Mrs. Johnson embraced countless children in Ward 5
46 as if they were her own, offering the care, encouragement, and affection that have made her a
47 treasured figure in the community;
48
49 WHEREAS, Mrs. Johnson continues to live life with joy, purpose, and enthusiasm;
50
51 WHEREAS, Mrs. Johnson enjoys getting out of the house to exercise her right to vote,
52 attend church, go shopping, and take vacations with her family;
53
54 WHEREAS, Mrs. Johnson’s active lifestyle and joyful spirit reflect her appreciation for
55 life’s blessings and her determination to embrace each new day;
56
57 WHEREAS, Mrs. Johnson is a woman of unwavering faith and her life reflects the
58 message of her favorite scripture, Psalm 118:24, which embodies the gratitude, faith, and joyful
59 spirit with which she continues to live each day;
60
61 WHEREAS, Mrs. Johnson is a cornerstone of her community, a beloved family
62 matriarch, and a shining example of what it means to live a life filled with love, faith, and
63 compassion; and
64
65 WHEREAS, on November 16, 2026, Mrs. Johnson will celebrate her 102nd birthday
66 commemorating an extraordinary life journey that continues to serve as an inspiration to young
67 and old alike.
68
69 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, that this
70 resolution may be cited as the “Bessie M. Johnson 102nd Birthday Recognition Resolution of
71 2026”.
72
73 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia joins the family and friends of Mrs.
74 Bessie M. Johnson in wishing her a happy 102nd birthday, and celebrates her milestone and her
75 long-standing dedication to the residents of Ward 5 and the D.C. community.
76
77 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
