ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-502
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve Modification Nos.
M0009, M0010, and M0011 to Contract No. CW110208 with Ramjay Inc., and to
authorize payment for the goods and services received and to be received under the
modifications.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Modification Nos. M0009, M0010 and M0011 to Contract No.
CW110208 with Ramjay Inc., Approval and Payment Authorization Emergency Declaration
Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve Modification Nos. M0009, M0010,
and M0011 to Contract No. CW110208 with Ramjay Inc. to provide transportation services to
students with disabilities, and to authorize payment for the goods and services received and to be
received under the modifications.
(b) By Modification No. M0009, issued on July 27, 2026, the Office of Contracting and
Procurement (“OCP”), on behalf of the Office of the State Superintendent of Education,
exercised a partial option of option year 3 in the not-to-exceed amount of $311,850 for the period
from August 22, 2026, through September 30, 2026.
(c) By Modification No. M0010, issued on August 22, 2026, OCP increased the not-to-
exceed amount of partial option year 3 by $155,925 to a total not-to-exceed amount of $467,775.
(d) Proposed Modification No. M0011 will exercise the remainder of option year 3and
increase the not-to-exceed amount by $4,334,715 to a total not-to-exceed amount of $4,802,490
and a total performance period from August 22, 2026, through August 21, 2027.
(e) Council approval is necessary because the modifications will increase the value of the
contract by more than $1 million during a 12-month period.
(f) Council approval is necessary to allow the continuation of these vital services.
Without Council approval, Ramjay Inc. cannot be paid for goods and services provided in excess
of $1 million for the period from August 22, 2026, through August 21, 2027.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
