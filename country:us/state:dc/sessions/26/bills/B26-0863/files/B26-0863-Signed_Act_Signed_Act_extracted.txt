To amend,on an emergency basis, the Board ofEthics and Government Accountability
Establishmentand Comprehensive Ethics Reform AmendmentActof 2011 to clarifythat
a transitioncommittee may be organized onbehalfof theMayor-electand toadoptthe

elect”.
BEITENACTED BYTHE COUNCIL OF THEDISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited as the“Mayoral Transition Emergency Amendment Act of2026”.
Sec. 2. Section 101 of the Board ofEthics and GovernmentAccountability Establishment
and Comprehensive Ethics Reform Amendment Act of2011, effective April 27, 2012 (D.C. Law
19-124; D.C. Official Code § 1-1161.01), is amended as follows:
(a) Paragraph (33C) is redesignated as paragraph (33D).
(b) A newparagraph (33C) is added to read as follows:
“(33C) “Mayor-elect” meansthe person who iscertified as the successful
candidateforthe office ofMayor by the Board of Electionsfollowing the 2026 general election
held to determinethe Mayoror, priorto such certification, theperson announced and published
by the Board ofElections as the unofficial winner ofthe 2026 general election forMayor;
provided, thatsuch person was announced asthe unofficial winner with amarginofvictoryofat
least 2%ofthe votes cast.”.
(©)Paragraph (52) is amended by striking the phrase “on behalf of the Mayor, Attomey
General”and inserting the phrase “onbehalf oftheMayor, the Mayor-elect, Attorney General”
inits place.
Sec. 3. Section6(1)ofthe Official Correspondence Regulations, effective April 7, 1977
(D.C. Law 1-118; D.C. Official Code § 2-705(1)), isamended by strikingthe semicolon and
inserting thephrase “, asdefined in section 1042of the Mayoral Transition Act of2026,
effective October 2, 2026 (D.C. Law26-189; 73 DCR 11735);” in its place.
