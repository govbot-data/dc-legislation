16 To authorize the Mayor to extend the lease of District-owned real property located beneath
17 Dupont Circle, commonly known as Dupont Underground, and to extend the lease of
18 District-owned real property located at 1900 M Street, S.E., commonly known as the
19 Anacostia Boathouse, and known for real property taxation and assessment purposes as
20 Square 1080E, Lot 0803.
21
22 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, This act
23 may be cited as the “Dupont Underground and Anacostia Boathouse Lease Extension Authority
24 Act of 2026”.
25 Sec. 2. Disposition authority.
26 (a) Notwithstanding An Act Authorizing the sale of certain real estate in the District
27 of Columbia no longer required for public purposes, approved August 5, 1939 (53 Stat. 1211;
28 D.C. Official Code § 10-801 et seq.), or other provision of law, the Council authorizes the Mayor
29 to dispose of the following real properties:
30 (1) The real property located beneath Dupont Circle containing
31 approximately 75,000 square feet of the subterranean space beneath the circular roadway
32 encompassing Federal Reservation 60 (Square 114-E, Lot 800), the associated subterranean track
33 tunnels and ramps north and south, the stairwells and access or egress associated with said
34 subterranean space, commonly known as Dupont Underground, by amending the existing lease

35 with DCenter Inc., d/b/a Arts Coalition for Dupont Underground to extend the term, which
36 currently expires on November 18, 2029, to expire on November 18, 2042.
37 (2) The real property located at 1900 M Street, S.E. (Square 1080E, Lot
38 0803), commonly known as the Anacostia Boathouse site, by amending the existing lease with
39 The Anacostia Community Boathouse Association, Inc., to extend the term, which currently
40 expires on June 7, 2030, to expire on June 7, 2047.
41 Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.
42 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal
43 impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
44 approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
45 Sec. 4. Effective date.
46 This act shall take effect following approval of the Mayor (or, in the event of veto by the
47 Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), a 30-day period of Congressional review as
48 provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December
49 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813, D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02 (c)(1)), and publication in the District of
50 Columbia Register.
