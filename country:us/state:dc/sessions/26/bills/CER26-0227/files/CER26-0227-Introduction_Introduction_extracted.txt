16 To recognize and celebrate John and Kim Snedden, owners of Rocklands Barbeque and Grilling
17 Company, for their contributions to Palisades, Glover Park, and the District of Columbia.
18
19 WHEREAS, Mr. and Mrs. Snedden have made an indelible mark on the Palisades
20 community, regularly volunteering at local events and sharing their generosity with the
21 neighborhood, including hosting the annual Palisades Pig Picking, bringing neighbors together
22 and creating local traditions;
23 WHEREAS, they opened the first Rocklands Barbeque location on December 1, 1990, in
24 the Glover Park neighborhood and later expanded the restaurant to locations in Alexandria,
25 Arlington, and Section 117 at Nationals Park;
26 WHEREAS, Rocklands Barbeque won the 2024 District Sustainability Award from the
27 District Department of Energy and Environment, and was chosen as WTOP’s Best BBQ in the
28 DMV in 2020 and 2023 and Washington City Paper’s Top 3 Best Barbeque in 2023;
29 WHEREAS, Mr. and Mrs. Snedden contribute more than 1% of Rocklands Barbeque
30 sales annually to support local nonprofits, sponsor Little League teams, provide meals to
31 individuals experiencing homelessness, and consistently support neighborhood events;
1

32 WHEREAS, they have crafted Rocklands Barbeque to be impact-focused, partnering
33 with local nonprofits to host events highlighting social causes that include ending homelessness,
34 stopping domestic violence, advocating for burn survivors, empowering young immigrants, and
35 supporting local artists;
36 WHEREAS, Mr. Snedden co-founded the Right Proper Brewing Company, which has
37 locations in the Shaw and Brookland neighborhoods;
38 WHEREAS, Mr. Snedden has served on the board of Glover Park Main Street since 2019
39 and currently serves as Vice Chairman, supporting and promoting small businesses, showcasing
40 the unique characteristics of the Glover Park community, and cultivating partnerships on behalf
41 of the neighborhood; and
42 WHEREAS, Mr. Snedden served two terms as chair of the Restaurant Association of
43 Metropolitan Washington, from April 2016 to March 2018, and currently serves on the Board of
44 Directors, a role in which he advocates for the needs of the restaurant and foodservice industry in
45 the Washington Metropolitan area.
46 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
47 resolution may be cited as the “John and Kim Snedden Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
48 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and celebrates John and Kim
49 Snedden for their contributions to the Glover Park and Palisades neighborhoods, their work in
50 feeding the District through Rocklands Barbeque, their advocacy on behalf of the restaurant
51 industry and small businesses, and their numerous charitable community-based endeavors.
52 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
53 the District of Columbia Register.
