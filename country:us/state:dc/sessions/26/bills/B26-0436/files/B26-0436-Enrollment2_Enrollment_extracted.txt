ENROLLED ORIGINAL
AN ACT
_______________
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
_______________
To amend the District of Columbia Election Code of 1955 to require the Board of Elections to
reject initiatives which address more than one subject matter, extend the amount of time
for the Attorney General for the District of Columbia and the General Counsel of the
Council of the District of Columbia to provide an advisory opinion on whether a
proposed measure is a proper subject for an initiative from 15 days to 30 days after
receipt from the Board of Elections, require the Office of the Chief Financial Officer to
provide a fiscal impact statement for a proposed initiative measure within 15 days after
receipt from the Board of Elections and require the Board of Elections to post the fiscal
impact statement on its website for a period of 3 years, require the Board of Elections to
post on its website the full legislative text and the proposer’s verified statement of
contributions filed with the Director of Campaign Finance, require the Board of Elections
to accept and post on its website public comments on the measure, require the Board of
Elections to hold a public meeting to issue a determination accepting or refusing a
proposed initiative measure, require the Board of Elections to prepare a fiscal impact
summary of an accepted proposed initiative measure, require the petition sheet to include
the text of the official summary, short title, and fiscal impact summary in at least 11-point
font, require the petition sheet to include the name of the proposer and indicate where the
verified statement of contributions can be found, require the fiscal impact summary be
included on the ballot, and for other purposes; and to amend section 4a of the General
Legislative Procedures Act of 1975 to make conforming amendments.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited as the “Fairness and Accountability in Initiative Reform Amendment Act of
2026”.

the Board publishes its determination in the District of Columbia Register, to the Superior Court
of the District of Columbia for a writ in the nature of mandamus to compel the Board to accept
such measure.
“(iii) The Superior Court of the District of Columbia shall

under the terms of title IV of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24,
