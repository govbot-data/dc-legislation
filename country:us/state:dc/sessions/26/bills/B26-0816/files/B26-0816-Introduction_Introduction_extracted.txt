Dear Secretary Howard:
Today, I am introducing the Board of Library Trustees Technical Amendment Act of 2026. This legislation
makes several necessary technical revisions to the statute that established the District of Columbia Library
(DCPL) and its governing Board of Library Trustees (BOLT). DCPL requested these changes to align the statute
with BOLT’s bylaws and DCPL’s administrative processes. The requested revisions ensure DCPL can continue
to operate efficiently by replacing outdated language, ensuring leadership continuity, clarifying the agency’s
reporting responsibilities, and making explicit several previously implicit characteristics of BOLT’s authority
to regulate revenue-generating activity.
This legislation makes several technical, administrative, and clarifying amendments to the governing statute.
First, it changes outdated terms to reflect current practice. This legislation replaces the outdated term for
the head of the Board (“president”) with “president or chair.” It also replaces the outdated term for the
Executive Director of DCPL (“librarian of the public library”) with updated language used elsewhere in the
statute (“Chief Librarian or Executive Director”).
Second, the bill ensures leadership continuity by allowing the Board to elect its chair every two years instead
of every year. DCPL has been following this schedule since January 2021, and the Library has benefited from
the steady leadership this practice allows.
Third, this legislation clarifies that the agency’s annual performance oversight responses can constitute the
oversight report required by the statute. For many years, the Council has allowed the Library to treat its
responses to performance oversight questions as the “annual report . . . on the operation of the public library”
required by D.C. Code § 39–105(a)(9). This practice allows the Library to focus on its substantive
responsibilities rather than generating duplicative reports.
Finally, this bill aligns the provision of the statute governing revenue-generating activity with the Board’s
bylaws and current practice. Statutory changes are necessary to ensure the Board can continue to oversee
the Library’s collection of revenue from third parties, including those that hold events on Library property.
Specifically, this legislation clarifies that: (1) the Board can act through a designee to issue permits for third-

26 Sec. 2. An Act To establish and provide for the maintenance of a free public library and
27 reading room in the District of Columbia, approved April 1, 1926 (44 Stat. 229; D.C. Official Code
28 § 39-104 et seq.), is amended as follows:
29 (a) Section 4 (D.C. Official Code § 39-104) is amended as follows:
30 (1) Subsection (g) is amended to read as follows:
31 “(g) Every 2 years, the Board shall elect 1 of its members to serve as its chair or president
32 and may elect any other officer it requires.”.
33 (2) Subsection (i) is amended by striking the phrase “librarian of the public library”
34 and inserting the phrase “Chief Librarian or Executive Director” in its place.
35 (b) Section 5(a) (D.C. Official Code § 39-105(a)) is amended as follows:
1

36 (1) Paragraph (9) is amended to read as follows:
37 “(9) Make an annual oversight report to the Mayor and the Council on the operation
38 of the public library for the preceding fiscal year; provided, that responses to written questions
39 prepared by the Council committee with jurisdiction over the public library as part of an annual
40 Council performance oversight process shall satisfy the requirements of this paragraph;”.
41 (2) Paragraph (10) is amended by striking the phrase “librarian of the public library”
42 wherever it appears and inserting the phrase “Chief Librarian or Executive Director of the public
43 library” in its place.
44 (3) Paragraph (14)(B) is amended to read as follows:
45 “(B)(i) Revenue-generating activity may be conducted by private users only
46 with a fee-based use permit granted by the Board or its designee, subject to such conditions as the
47 Board or its designee may impose, and only upon payment of a fee pursuant to a fee schedule
48 approved by the Board.
49 (ii) The library may waive the fee required by this subparagraph if,
50 in the Board or its designee’s sole discretion, doing so is in the public interest and payment of the
51 fee would impose undue hardship on the private user.
52 (iii) This subparagraph shall not apply to activities conducted
53 pursuant to Paragraph (17) of this subsection.”.
54 (c) Section 107a(a) (D.C. Official Code § 39–107a) is amended by striking the phrase
55 “librarian of the public library” and inserting the phrase “Chief Librarian or Executive Director or
56 his or her designees” in its place.
