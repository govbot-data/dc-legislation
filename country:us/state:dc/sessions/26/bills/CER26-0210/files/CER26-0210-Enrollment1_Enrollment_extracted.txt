ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-210
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To recognize, honor, and remember the life of Robert A. Kennedy Jr. and his tireless advocacy
for and impact on people with disabilities in the District of Columbia.
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy was born on March 23, 1952 in Washington, DC, grew up in
the Brookland neighborhood, and remained a lifelong resident of the District of Columbia, living
in Ward 5 when he passed on May 23, 2026;
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy devoted his life to uplifting the voices of people with
disabilities and and ensuring their voices were respected in decisions that shaped their lives;
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy worked at the National Labor Relations Board and retired
after 38 years of service;
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy worked at RCM of Washington for 17 years from 2009 until is
retirement in April 2026, starting in Facilities Maintenance before moving to Human Resources;
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy served the National Children’s Center (NCC) Board of
Directors for 18 years;
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy was honored with the NCC’s Advocacy Award at its 65th
Anniversary Gala and drew on his lived experience and the voices of fellow advocates to
advance inclusion and strengthen NCC’s mission of empowering individuals with disabilities;
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy was involved with Lifeline Partnership for over 30 years as a
self-advocate, program advisor, distinguished volunteer, event attendee, and at-large board
member;
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy participated in the Special Olympics for 58 years in
basketball, track and field, and numerous other sports; beginning in 1969 when he joined the
newly created District of Columbia chapter of Special Olympics;

WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy was inducted into the Special Olympics District of Columbia
Hall of Fame in 2015;
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy was a member of Health Services for Children with Special
Needs’s Male Caregivers Advisory Coalition (MCAC) and the Parent Advocacy Leadership
Support (PALS) for over 35 years, advocating with parents of children with disabilities.
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy was an advocate and ardent supporter of Quality Trust;
attending events, volunteering, and training others;
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy served on the Department on Disability Services’ (DDS)
Human Rights Committee, and was a trainer with the Person-Centered Planning initiative;
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy was a leader of Project Action! and testified at oversight
hearings at the Council of the District of Columbia ( the Council) for himself and other people
with disabilities who received supports and services from DDS;
WHEREAS, during Mr. Kennedy’s most recent testimony to the Council for DDS’
Budget Oversight Hearing, he pressed the Council to “fully fund the DDS budget so District
residents with disabilities can get Medicaid benefits, a career, and what they need to live a good
life in the District of Columbia”;
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy was a strong trainer, as he travelled across the country to train
self-advocates and speak at conferences to professionals, self-advocates, and family members
and, in the District, provided advocacy training to youth in District of Columbia Public Schools,
and disability professionals, parents, and others in Washington, DC;
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy’s advocacy impacted the lives of thousands of people in the
District of Columbia and beyond; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Kennedy leaves behind a powerful example of courage and
determination that will continue to guide advocacy for people with disabilities in the District for
years to come.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Robert A. Kennedy, Jr. Memorial Recognition Resolution of
2026”.

ENROLLED ORIGINAL
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes, honors, and remembers the life
of Robert A. Kennedy, Jr. and his his tireless advocacy for and impact on people with disabilities
in the District of Columbia.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
