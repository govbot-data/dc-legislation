ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-214
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To recognize and celebrate the inaugural Capital Queens Women’s Sports Week, held
September 20th through September 26th, and jointly established by the Washington Spirit
and Washington Mystics, celebrating the value of girls’ and women’s participation in
sports.
WHEREAS, the Washington Mystics and Washington Spirit came together to launch a
weeklong crossover called Capital Queens, pioneering the District of Columbia’s first women’s
sports week;
WHEREAS, Washington D.C. is the epicenter of women’s sports, where women’s teams
have brought national championships in professional sports, collegiate programs, and high
school programs;
WHEREAS, the collaboration between two of DC’s longest women’s professional sports
teams will bring together players, fans, local businesses, and the community at large to celebrate
women’s sports and increase awareness of the importance of girls’ participation in youth sports;
WHEREAS, girls’ participation in sports leads to more healthier communities, stronger
leaders, robust professional careers, and more equitable futures, empowering girls to build
confidence, and leadership, especially during critical developmental years;
WHEREAS, girls’ participation in sports supports stronger self-worth and emotional
resilience as they grow and face increased social pressures, body image concerns, fear of
judgment and feelings of not belonging;
WHEREAS, according to the Women’s Sports Foundation, girls still have fewer
opportunities to play sports than boys, considering barriers such as limited resources, access to
school sports, transportation, and community programs that make it more difficult for girls to
participate and stay engaged;

WHEREAS, more than 38% of U.S. girls do not participate in youth sports, and by the
age of 14 are twice as likely to drop out of sports completely compared to boys;
WHEREAS, 69% of women who earned more than $100,000 per year and are in
leadership roles played competitive sports and 85% of women who played sports report the skills
they developed playing sports were important to success in their professional careers; and
WHEREAS, the correlation between professional success and adolescent athletics has
demonstrated that 94% of women in C-suite positions played sports, more than half played at the
collegiate level, and approximately 80% of Fortune 500 women executives played sports in their
earlier years.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Capital Queens Women’s Sports Week Recognition Resolution
of 2026.”
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Capital Queens Women’s
Sports Week, encourages the participation of girls and women in sports, and celebrates the
athletes, fans, and local community in making the District a leader in women’s sports.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
