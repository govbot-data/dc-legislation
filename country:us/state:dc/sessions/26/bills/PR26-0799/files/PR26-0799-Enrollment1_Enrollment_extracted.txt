ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-500
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve Proposed
Modification No. M003 to Contract No. DCRL-2026-C-0024 with Crawford
Consulting and Mental Health Service, Inc. to provide community-based residential
and social services, and to authorize payment for the services received under the
modification.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Modification No. M003 to Contract No. DCRL-2026-C-
0024 with Crawford Consulting and Mental Health Service, Inc. Approval and Payment
Authorization Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists a need to approve Proposed Modification No. M003 to
Contract No. DCRL-2026-C-0024 with Crawford Consulting and Mental Health Service,
Inc. to provide community-based residential and social services, and to authorize payment
for the services received under the modifications.
(b) On May 11, 2026, The Child and Family Services Agency (“CFSA”) executed
Contract No. DCRL-2026-C-0024 with Crawford Consulting and Mental Health Service,
Inc. to provide community-based residential and social services in the District of Columbia
in the not-to-exceed amount of $691,648.03 for the period from May 12, 2026, through
September 30, 2026.
(c) By Modification No. M001, issued on May 19, 2026, CFSA decreased the not-
to-exceed amount to $690,762.37.
(d) By Modification No. M002, issued on July 27, 2026, CFSA incorporated a
revised price schedule.
(e) Proposed Modification No. M003 will exercise option year one for the period of
October 1, 2026, through September 30, 2027, in the not-to-exceed amount of
$1,774,850.84.
(f) Council approval is necessary because the modification will increase the value
of the contract to more than $1 million during a 12-month period.
(g) Council approval is necessary to allow the continuation of these vital services.
Without Council approval, Crawford Consulting and Mental Health Service, Inc. cannot be
paid for services provided in excess of $1 million for the period from October 1, 2026,
through September 30, 2027.

Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Modification No. M003 to Contract No. DCRL-2026-C-0024 with Crawford Consulting
and Mental Health Service, Inc. Approval and Payment Authorization Emergency Act of
2026 be adopted after a single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
