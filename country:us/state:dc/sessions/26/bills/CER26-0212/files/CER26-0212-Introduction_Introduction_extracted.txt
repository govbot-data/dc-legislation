11 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
12 __________
13
14 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
15 __________
16
17 To honor and recognize Battle’s Transportation, Inc., for fifty years of excellence in
18 service.
19
20 WHEREAS, in 1976, Battle’s Transportation, Inc. was founded in the District of
21 Columbia and has proudly served the District and the surrounding community for fifty years;
22
23 WHEREAS, as a passenger transportation company dedicated to safety, reliability and
24 exceptional service, Battle’s Transportation, Inc. has built a distinguished reputation through five
25 decades of commitment to its customers and our community;
26
27 WHEREAS, from its early years, Battle’s Transportation, Inc. established a legacy of
28 service by securing a major contract to provide non-emergency medical transportation to the
29 D.C. Veterans Medical Center, reflecting the company’s longstanding commitment to serving
30 our nation’s veterans;
31
32 WHEREAS, over the past fifty years, Battle’s Transportation, Inc. has grown into a
33 trusted provider of executive transportation and passenger services continuing to uphold the high
34 standards of professionalism, dependability, and customer service upon which the company was
35 founded;
36
37 WHEREAS, fifty years of successful service is a remarkable milestone, representing
38 generations of dedication, hard work, perseverance, and a steadfast commitment to excellence;
39 and
40
41 WHEREAS, the Council of the D.C. is proud to recognize businesses that have deep
42 roots in our community and have contributed to the District’s economic vitality and quality of
43 life through decades of responsible and dedicated service;
44
45 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
46 resolution may be cited as the “Battle’s Transportation, Inc. Recognition Resolution of 2026”.

47
48 BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF
49 COLUMBIA, that the Council hereby recognizes Battle’s Transportation, Inc. for fifty years of
50 service to the District and neighboring communities.
51
52 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors Battle’s
53 Transportation, Inc. on the occasion of its 50th anniversary, celebrating five decades of
54 excellence, safety, reliability, and exceptional service, and extending its congratulations and best
55 wishes for continued success in the years ahead.
56
57 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
58 the District of Columbia Register.
