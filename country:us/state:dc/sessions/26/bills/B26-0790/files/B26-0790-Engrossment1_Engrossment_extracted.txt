10 To require, on a temporary basis, that the Department of Small and Local Business Development
11 to award a $1 million dollar grant in Fiscal year 2027 to the Duke Business Improvement
12 District Corporation.
13
14 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
15 act may be cited as the “Duke District BID Grantmaking Temporary Amendment Act of 2026”.
16 Sec. 2. Amendatory paragraph (4)(A) of section 6302(c)(3) of the Fiscal Year 2027
17 Budget Support Act of 2026, enacted on August 21, 2026 (D.C. Act 26-418; 73 DCR 11735) is
18 amended to read as follows:
19 “(4)(A) Notwithstanding paragraph (3) of this subsection, within the first quarter
20 of Fiscal Year 2027, the Department of Small and Local Business Development shall award a
21 grant in the amount of $1 million from the Fund to support the Duke Business Improvement
22 District Corporation, d/b/a Duke District BID, to improve conditions for property owners,
23 businesses, residents, and visitors within the Greater Shaw/U Street area.”.
