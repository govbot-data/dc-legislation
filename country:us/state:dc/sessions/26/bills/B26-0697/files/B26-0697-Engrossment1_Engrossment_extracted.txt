9 To amend the District of Columbia Applications Insurance Implementation Act, the Green
10 Building Act of 2006, the District Department of the Environment Establishment Act of
11 2005, the District of Columbia Office of Energy Act of 1980, the Commission on Climate
12 Change and Resiliency Establishment Act of 2016, the Sustainable Solid Waste
13 Management Amendment Act of 2014, the District of Columbia Hazardous Waste
14 Management Act of 1977, the District of Columbia Soil and Water Conservation Act of
15 1982, the CleanEnergy DC Omnibus Amendment Act of 2018, the Clean and Affordable
16 Energy Amendment Act of 2008, the Smart Lighting Study Act of 2009, the Wildlife
17 Protection Act of 2010, the Renewable Energy Portfolio Standard Act of 2004, the Lead
18 Service Line Priority Replacement Assistance Act of 2004, the Retail Service Station Act
19 of 1976, and the Fees for Clinical Services and Asbestos Abatement Act of 1984 to make
20 various changes to the reporting requirements established by those acts, including replacing
21 requirements to transmit reports to the Council with requirements to publish and maintain
22 the reports on the agency website, adjusting reporting deadlines, reducing the frequency of
23 reports, adjusting the timeframe a report covers, consolidating certain reports, repealing
24 outdated or duplicative reporting requirements, and making conforming amendments.
25
26 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
27 act may be cited as the “Department of Energy and Environment Reporting Requirements
28 Amendment Act of 2026”.
