10 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA

14
15 To recognize and acknowledge the professional contributions of Ann Chisholm on the occasion of
16 her retirement from the Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority.
17
18 WHEREAS, Ann Chisholm joined the Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority
19 (“WMATA”) in May 2006 to lead its Community Relations Office;
20
21 WHEREAS, prior to joining WMATA, Ms. Chisholm served as the spokesperson for both
22 Howard University and the DC General Hospital;
23
24 WHEREAS, after joining WMATA, Ms. Chisholm soon transitioned her skills to the
25 Government Relations Office, serving as Assistant Government Relations Officer for DC and
26 directly handling WMATA’s outreach to the District’s nearly 350 Advisory Neighborhood
27 Commissioners;
28
29 WHEREAS, Ms. Chisholm was promoted to DC Government Relations Officer, serving
30 as WMATA’s liaison to all parts of the District government;
31
32 WHEREAS, Ms. Chisholm is deeply committed to safe, reliable, and accessible public
33 transit for District residents, employees, and visitors;
34
35 WHEREAS, Ms. Chisholm has been central to ensuring WMATA’s success through strong
36 relationships between the agency and the District, including in navigating major challenges like
37 COVID-19 impacts and innovations like the transition to the Better Bus Network;
38
39 WHEREAS, Ms. Chisholm has ably represented WMATA on the DowntownDC BID
40 Board of Directors since 2012;
41
42 WHEREAS, in addition to her professional successes, Ms. Chisholm is known for her
43 exceptional style, grace, and poise;
44
45 WHEREAS, in late 2026, Ms. Chisholm will retire from WMATA after more than 20 years
46 of exemplary service;
1

47
48 WHEREAS, Ms. Chisholm has one adult daughter, Kendra, for whom she provides care
49 and support;
50
51 WHEREAS, in retirement, in addition to traveling and supporting Kendra, Ms. Chisholm
52 looks forward to honing her talents in metalworking and jewelry making; and
53
54 WHEREAS, Ms. Chisholm’s home Metro station is Takoma.
55
56 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
57 resolution may be cited as the “Ann Chisholm Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
58
59 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and acknowledges the
60 professional contributions of Ann Chisholm on the occasion of her retirement from the Washington
61 Metropolitan Area Transit Authority.
62
63 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
