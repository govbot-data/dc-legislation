ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-519
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency, due to congressional review, with respect to the need
to amend the Green Building Act of 2006 to exempt certain District-owned and -funded
residential and nonresidential projects from complying with net-zero-energy standards
and to modify net-zero-energy standard compliance requirements for nonresidential and
residential projects; to amend section 2 of the Clean Energy DC Building Code
Amendment Act of 2022 to modify the definition of the net-zero-energy standard and the
types of projects to which the net-zero-energy standard applies; and for other purposes.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Net Zero Continuity Congressional Review Emergency
Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) On July 14, 2026, the Council adopted the Net Zero Continuity Emergency
Amendment Act of 2026, effective July 30, 2026 (D.C. Act 26-0394; 73 DCR 011187)
(“emergency act”), which will expire on October 28, 2026.
(b) On September 22, 2026, the Council adopted the Net Zero Continuity Temporary
Amendment Act of 2026, passed on 2nd reading on September 22, 2026 (Enrolled version of Bill
26-768) (“temporary act”), which has not completed congressional review.
(c) Congressional emergency legislation is necessary to prevent a gap in the law between
the expiration of the emergency act and the effective date of the temporary act.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the Net
Zero Continuity Congressional Review Emergency Amendment Act of 2026 be adopted after a
single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
