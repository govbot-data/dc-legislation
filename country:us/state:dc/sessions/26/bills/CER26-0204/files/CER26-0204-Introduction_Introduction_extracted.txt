5 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
6 ______________
7
8 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
9 _________________
10
11
12 To recognize, honor, and celebrate District of Columbia public charter schools on the occasion of
13 their 30th anniversary.
14
15 WHEREAS, the District of Columbia School Reform Act of 1995 opened the door for
16 public charter schools in the District of Columbia;
17
18 WHEREAS, since the opening of the District’s first public charter schools in 1996,
19 thousands of educators, school leaders, board of trustee members, families, and community
20 partners have built and sustained one of the nation’s most exemplary public charter school sectors;
21
22 WHEREAS, for three decades, DC public charter schools have expanded educational
23 opportunity, promoted student achievement, and prepared students for college, careers, and
24 lifelong success;
25
26 WHEREAS, DC public charter schools have strengthened education in the District by
27 giving families access to a wide range of public school options designed to meet a wide range of
28 diverse needs and interests;
29
30 WHEREAS, DC public charter schools have adopted and developed distinctive missions
31 and educational approaches that reflect the aspirations and preferences of the students and
32 communities they serve;
33
34 WHEREAS, DC public charter schools have served as community anchors, by opening
35 their facilities for Advisory Neighborhood Commission meetings, community meetings, and other
36 neighborhood events and by connecting residents with food, healthcare services, and other
37 community resources;
38
39 WHEREAS, DC’s adult-serving public charter schools have made the District a national
40 model for adult education by strengthening adult learners’ literacy and language skills and by
41 expanding their ability to earn diplomas and credentials and their pathways to employment and
42 economic mobility;
43
44 WHEREAS, DC public charter schools provide quality and accessible education to
45 multilingual learners, students with disabilities, and students with other specialized educational
46 needs;

47
48 WHEREAS, DC public charter schools continue to strengthen their commitment to
49 academic excellence, transparency, and accountability—including through the ASPIRE academic
50 accountability System;
51
52 WHEREAS, during school year 2025-2026, 66 public charter local education agencies
53 operated 133 schools and educated 48,065 students, representing nearly half of all public school
54 students in the District of Columbia;
55
56 WHEREAS, DC public charter schools enrich communities in all eight wards and serve
57 learners from early childhood through adulthood; and
58
59 WHEREAS, 2026 marks the 30th anniversary of public charter schools in the District of
60 Columbia, and three decades of service to DC students, families, educators, and communities.
61
62 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
63 resolution may be cited as the “District of Columbia Public Charter Schools 30th Anniversary
64 Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
65 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes, honors, and celebrates District
66 of Columbia public charter schools on the occasion of their 30th anniversary and commends their
67 contributions to public education and the students and families of the District of Columbia.
68 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
