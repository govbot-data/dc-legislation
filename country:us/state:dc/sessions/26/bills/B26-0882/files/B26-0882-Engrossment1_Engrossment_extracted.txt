9 To amend, on a temporary basis, the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic Development
10 Limited Grant-Making Authority Act of 2012 to expand permissible uses of certain grant
11 funding; and to amend the District of Columbia Taxicab Commission Establishment Act
12 of 1985 to permit rulemaking to implement a carrier-for-hire support surcharge.
13
14 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
15 act may be cited as the “Budget Support Act Implementation Facilitation Temporary
16 Amendment Act of 2026”.
17 Sec. 2. Section 2032(pp)(3) of the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic
18 Development Limited Grant-Making Authority Act of 2012, effective September 20, 2012 (D.C.
19 Law 19-168; D.C. Official Code § 1-328.04(pp)(3)), is amended by striking the phrase “purchase
20 of the real property” and inserting the phrase “purchase of or capital improvements to the real
21 property” in its place.
22 Sec. 3. Section 208j-8(e) of the District of Columbia Taxicab Commission Establishment
23 Act of 1985, effective March 25, 1986 (D.C. Law 6-97; D.C. Official Code § 50-301.29h(e)) is
24 amended by adding a new paragraph (4) to read as follows:
25 “(4) DFHV, pursuant to Title I of the District of Columbia Administrative
26 Procedure Act, approved October 21, 1968 (82 Stat. 1204; D.C. Official Code § 2-501 et seq.),
27 may issue rules to implement the provisions of this subsection.”.
