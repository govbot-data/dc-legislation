ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-188
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
July 14, 2026
To recognize and honor Maureen L. Dwyer and the Sitar Arts Center for their contributions to
the District of Columbia.
WHEREAS, Maureen L. Dwyer served the community at Sitar Arts Center for 27 years,
including 15 years as Executive Director, working to ensure Sitar Arts Center serves as a strong
community hub for Ward 1 and the District;
WHEREAS, Ms. Dwyer helped grow enrollment, expand artistic and workforce
partnerships, build coalitions and strengthen community partnerships, and deepen and diversify
programming;
WHEREAS, under Ms. Dwyer’s leadership Sitar Arts Center has been recognized with
several honors and awards, including the 2015 Washington Post Award for Excellence in
Nonprofit Management, the 2017 Mayor’s Arts Award, and the National Arts and Humanities
Youth Program Award, among others;
WHEREAS, Sitar Arts Center advances equitable access to high-quality arts education
and workforce development in the visual, performing, and digital arts;
WHEREAS, Sitar Arts Center serves up to 900 families each year from all eight wards,
removing financial and cultural barriers to arts education and career training; and
WHEREAS, Sitar Arts Center provides quality, accessible arts education and supports
youth creativity through classes in visual arts, music, drama, dance, digital arts, and creative
writing.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, that this
resolution may be cited as the “Maureen L. Dwyer Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
1

ENROLLED ORIGINAL
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia honors and celebrates the Sitar Arts Center
and its longtime Executive Director Maureen L. Dwyer for their contributions to the community
and arts education in the District of Columbia.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
