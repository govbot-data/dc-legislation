28 Sec. 2. (a) In 2007, the District passed Title II of the Fiscal Year 2007 Budget Support
29 Act of 2006 ("BSA") to provide funding for affordable housing for extremely low-income
30 households in the District. The passage of the BSA created the Local Rent Supplement Program
31 ("LRSP"), a program designed to provide affordable housing and supportive services to
32 extremely low-income District residents, including those who are homeless or in need of
33 supportive services, such as elderly individuals or those with disabilities, through project-based,
34 tenant-based, and sponsored-based LRSP affordable housing units. The BSA provided for the
35 District of Columbia Housing Authority ("DCHA") to administer the LRSP on behalf of the
36 District.

1 (b) In August of 2023, the District of Columbia Department of Housing and Community
2 Development (“DHCD”) issued a Consolidated Request for Proposals for Affordable Housing
3 Projects. Of the proposals received, five (5) developers were chosen to work with DCHA and
4 other District agencies to develop affordable housing and permanent supportive housing units for
5 extremely low-income families making zero to thirty percent (30%) of the area’s median income,
6 as well as the chronically homeless and individuals with mental or physical disabilities. Upon
7 approval of the contract by the Council, DCHA will enter into an Agreement to Enter into a
8 Long-Term Subsidy Contract (“ALTSC”) with the selected housing provider under the LRSP for
9 housing services.
10 (c) There exists an immediate need to approve the long term subsidy contract with
11 Congress Park Community Partners, LLC under the LRSP in order to provide long-term
12 affordable housing units for extremely low-income households for units located at The
13 Wilhelmina Rolark at Congress Heights Metro at 1353 Alabama Avenue SE.
14 (d) The legislation to approve the contract will authorize an ALTSC between the District
15 of Columbia Housing Authority and Congress Park Community Partners, LLC with respect to
16 the payment of a rental subsidy and allows the owner to lease the newly constructed units at The
17 Wilhelmina Rolark at Congress Heights Metro and house extremely low-income households
18 with incomes at thirty percent (30%) or less of the area median income.
