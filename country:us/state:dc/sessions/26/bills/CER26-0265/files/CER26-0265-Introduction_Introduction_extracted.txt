8 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
9 ____________
10 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
11 _____________________________
12
13 To recognize and celebrate the 2026 National Women's Soccer League Championship at Audi
14 Field in Washington, DC, on November 21, 2026, and to acknowledge the significance of
15 hosting world-class women's soccer in the nation's capital, attracting visitors, stimulating
16 economic activity, and strengthening the District's position as the Sport’s Capital of the United
17 States, the premier destination for major sporting events.
18 WHEREAS, the National Women's Soccer League (NWSL) is a premier professional
19 women's soccer league, showcasing extraordinary athletic talent and advancing the growth,
20 visibility, and impact of women's sports;
21 WHEREAS, on November 21, 2026, Washington, DC, will host the NWSL
22 Championship at Audi Field, bringing one of the nation's most prominent women's sporting
23 events to the District, including our hometown team, Washington Spirit;
24 WHEREAS, major sporting events such as the NWSL Championship serve as economic
25 drivers for the District, creating opportunities for local businesses, supporting jobs, and
26 reinforcing Washington, DC's reputation as a leading destination for sports, entertainment, and
27 tourism;
28 WHEREAS, the championship and its surrounding events will bring together athletes,
29 fans, families, and visitors for a celebration of women's soccer, extending the economic and
30 cultural benefits of the event beyond the field and into the broader community;
31 WHEREAS, Audi Field, home to the Washington Spirit, has become a premier
32 destination for professional soccer in the nation’s capital, showcasing the District’s ability to host
33 nationally significant sporting events while inspiring the next generation of athletes and fans and
34 highlighting the importance of investing in women’s sports and expanding opportunities for
35 women and girls;
36 WHEREAS, the championship presents an opportunity to showcase Washington, DC, to
37 a national television audience, highlighting the District's world-class venues, passionate sports
38 community, and capacity to welcome major events;

39 WHEREAS, the Council recognizes the National Women's Soccer League, the
40 Washington Spirit, Events DC, and the many partners whose efforts have contributed to bringing
41 this championship to the District.
42 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
43 resolution may be cited as the "2026 National Women's Soccer League Championship
44 Recognition Resolution of 2026".
45 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and celebrates the 2026
46 NWSL Championship at Audi Field and welcomes the athletes, teams, fans, and visitors
47 participating in this historic occasion.
48 Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia acknowledges the economic, cultural, and
49 community benefits of hosting major sporting events and reaffirms the importance of attracting
50 world-class sports competitions that support local businesses, strengthen tourism, and elevate
51 Washington, DC's national and international profile.
52 Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
53 the District of Columbia Register.
