9 To amend, on a temporary basis, the Restoration of Covenanted Roads and Alleys by the District
10 Government Act of 2024 to further clarify the District’s authority to repair or restore
11 certain private rights-of-way to a condition appropriate for the passage of motor vehicles
12 owned or operated by the District government.
13
14 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
15 act may be cited as the “Restoration of Covenanted Roads and Alleys by the District
16 Government Clarification Temporary Amendment Act of 2026”.
17 Sec. 2. Section 2 of the Restoration of Covenanted Roads and Alleys by the District
18 Government Act of 2024, effective March 28, 2025 (D.C. Law 25-320; D.C. Official Code § 9-
19 401.19), is amended as follows:
20 (a) Subsection (c) is amended as follows:
21 (1) The lead-in language is amended by striking the phrase “this section” and
22 inserting the phrase “subsection (a) of this section” in its place.
23 (2) Paragraph (2) is amended by striking the phrase “, and impose such other
24 conditions as the Mayor deems appropriate.” and inserting the phrase “as the Mayor deems
25 appropriate.” in its place.
26 (b) New subsections (e) and (f) are added to read as follows:

27 “(e)(1) The Mayor may also repair or restore a private right-of-way to such condition as
28 the Mayor deems appropriate for the passage of motor vehicles owned or operated by the District
29 government, and the Mayor may enter such private right-of-way for the purpose of such repair or
30 restoration, if:
31 “(A) The private right-of-way is used by the District government to
32 provide regular trash removal service;
33 “(B) The private right-of-way is not in a state of good repair, as
34 determined by the Mayor, for the passing of motor vehicles by the District government for
35 regular trash removal service;
36 “(C) The owner of the private right-of-way has not covenanted for the
37 benefit of the District government, and is not otherwise legally obligated for the benefit of the
38 District government, to maintain the private right-of-way in a state of good repair for regular
39 trash removal service; and
40 “(D) The owner of the private right-of-way has consented to the Mayor’s
41 repair or restoration of the private right-of-way and has provided a release of liability and
42 indemnification related to the repair or restoration.
43 “(2) Before repairing or restoring a private right-of-way under this subsection, the
44 Mayor shall attempt to:
45 “(A) Obtain from each owner of property likely to be impacted by the
46 repair or restoration of the private right-of-way a release of liability and indemnification related

52 Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.
53 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal
54 impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
55 approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
56 Sec. 4. Effective date.
57 (a) This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by
58 the Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), a 30-day period of congressional review
59 as provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December
60 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)), and publication in the District of
61 Columbia Register.
62 (b) This act shall expire after 225 of having taken effect.
