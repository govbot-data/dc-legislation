ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-197
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To commemorate L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc.’s 35th Anniversary.
WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. (LSC) has specialized in the development,
implementation, and administration of contracting, employment, and community awareness
compliance programs since its founding and under the leadership of President and CEO Loretta
S. Caldwell and Vice President C. Eugene Harvey;
WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. has spent the last three and a half decades
operating as an economic development engine, community advocate, and trusted business
partner across the District, contributing to the growth and equity of the District’s economy;
WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. is headquartered in Ward 4 at 5427 14th
Street, N.W.;
WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. has impacted the Ward 8 community
through projects such as the St. Elizabeths West Campus Modernization, where the company
managed Disadvantaged Business Enterprise (DBE) and Certified Business Enterprise (CBE)
compliance, local resident job placements, certified payroll auditing, and local business
participation monitoring for the major federal and municipal redevelopment; The Commons at
Stanton Square, a Community of Hope facility, where LSC administered community outreach
programs, local hiring requirements, and CBE subcontracting compliance for essential
community healthcare infrastructure; and DC Water critical utility and clean water
modernization initiatives, where LSC managed workforce development, CBE tracking, and
compliance services across Ward 8;
WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. has directly expanded job training,
contracting, and apprenticeship opportunities for Ward 8 residents and small businesses;
WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. managed contracting, Certified Business
Enterprise compliance, local hiring, and community outreach for the $62 million Ward 4 Short-
Term Family Housing project;

WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. provided workforce development,
Certified Business Enterprise tracking, and compliance services for 27 DC Water and Clean
Rivers capital projects in Ward 4, including water main and utility modernization work;
WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. has also administered programs for
transformative public and private projects across the District of Columbia beyond Wards 4 and 8
such as CityCenterDC, Fort Lincoln and The Shops at Dakota Crossing, the Purple Line Transit
Project, and the Woodrow Wilson Bridge Megaproject;
WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. has crafted several vital economic
development policies, legislation, and procurement frameworks, including the origin and updates
of the District CBE program to protect and support local firms; and
WHEREAS, L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. hosted an anniversary reception on Friday,
September 18, 2026, to highlight the economic programs successfully implemented and achieved
resulting in billions in contract dollars for District businesses and thousands of employment
opportunities for District residents.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc. 35th Anniversary Recognition
Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia thanks and commends L. S. Caldwell &
Associates, Inc. for its 35 years of commitment to local equity, employment, economic inclusion
and business compliance, and recognizes Friday, September 18, 2026, as a day of celebration for
L. S. Caldwell & Associates, Inc.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
