11 To designate the school in Lots 800 and 936 in Square 2882, bounded by Euclid Street, N.W.,
12 9th Street, N.W., Barry Place, N.W., and Route 29, as Alma W. Thomas Middle School.
13 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
14 act may be cited as the “Alma W. Thomas Middle School Designation Act of 2026”.
15 Sec. 2. Pursuant to sections 401 and 422(b) through (e) of the Street and Alley Closing
16 and Acquisition Procedures Act of 1982, effective March 10, 1983 (D.C. Law 4-201; D.C.
17 Official Code §§ 9-204.01 and 9-204.22(b) through (e)) (“Act”), and notwithstanding section
18 422(a) of the Act (D.C. Official Code § 9-204.22(a)), the Council designates the school on Lots
19 800 and 936 in Square 2882, bounded by Euclid Street, N.W., 9th Street, N.W., Barry Place,
20 N.W., and Route 29 as “Alma W. Thomas Middle School”.
21 Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.
22 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal
23 impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
24 approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
