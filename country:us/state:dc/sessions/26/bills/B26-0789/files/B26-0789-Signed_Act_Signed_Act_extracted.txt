To amend, onan emergency basis, section 2aofthe Performance ParkingPilot Zone Actof 2008
to requiretheDepartment ofSmall and Local BusinessDevelopment to award a $1

Corporation; andto makeaconformingamendment.
BE ITENACTED BY THECOUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OFCOLUMBIA, Thatthis
act may becited asthe “Duke District BID Grantmaking Emergency AmendmentAct of2026".
Sec. 2, Section 2a(c)(4)(A) ofthe PerformanceParking Pilot Zone Actof2008,effective
September 14, 2011 (D.C. Law 19-21; D.C. Official Code § 50-2531.01(c)(4)(A)). is amended to
read as follows:
“(4)(A)Notwithstanding paragraph (3)ofthis subsection, within the first quarter
ofFiscal Year 2027, the DepartmentofSmall and Local BusinessDevelopment shall award a
grantin the amountof$1 million from the Fund to supportthe Duke Business Improvement
DistrictCorporation, d/b/aDuke District BID, to improveconditions forproperty owners,
businesses, residents, and visitors within the Greater Shaw/U Street area.”.
Sec. 3, Amendatory section 2a(c)(4)(A) insection 6302(c)(3) ofthe PerformanceParking
Zone Fund Modification AmendmentActof2026, enacted on August 14, 2026 (D.C. Act 26-
418; 73 DCR 11735), is amended to readas follows:
#(4)(A)Notwithstanding paragraph (3)ofthis subsection, within thefirst quarter
