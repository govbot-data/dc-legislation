ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-196
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To proclaim the week of September 21 – 27, 2026 as "DC Calls It Quits Week" and to promote
smoking cessation as critical in efforts to protect the health of District of Columbia
residents.
WHEREAS, tobacco use is the single most preventable cause of disease, disability, and
death in the United States, killing 800 District residents every year and leading to a significant
decline in the quality of life for smokers;
WHEREAS, smoking prevalence among Black adults in DC is nearly 14%, and
prevalence is highest among residents in Wards 1, 7, and 8;
WHEREAS, in 2023, 3.1% of high school students in the District of Columbia smoked
and 9.6% used e-cigarettes at least one day in the past 30 days;
WHEREAS, smoking accounts for $454 million in annual health care costs in the
District, $101.5 million of which are paid by Medicaid;
WHEREAS, the state and federal tax burden for District residents from smoking-caused
government expenditures is equal to $812 per household;
WHEREAS, the total economic cost burden of smoking in the United States exceeds
$600 billion annually, including $241 billion spent on direct medical care for adults;
WHEREAS, stopping smoking is associated with reduced heart disease risk within 1 to 2
years of quitting, along with lowered risk for lung cancer and many other types of cancer;
WHEREAS, nearly 7 in 10 adults who smoke would like to quit smoking, and over 63%
of District residents who smoke attempted to quit in 2022;
WHEREAS, the District of Columbia has recognized that flavored tobacco products,
including menthol cigarettes and other menthol and flavored tobacco products, pose a significant
public health threat because flavors can mask the harshness of tobacco smoke, make tobacco

products more appealing to youth and new users, and make it easier to initiate and sustain
nicotine use;
WHEREAS, menthol is particularly concerning because it creates a cooling sensation that
can make smoking feel less harsh, while contributing to nicotine dependence and making
quitting more difficult;
WHEREAS, the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services recognizes tobacco
dependence as a chronic disease that often requires repeated intervention and multiple attempts
to quit;
WHEREAS, of the over 28 million people in the United States who smoke cigarettes,
only 5% are able to quit without assistance from healthcare providers;
WHEREAS, counseling and medication are both effective for treating tobacco
dependence and using them together is more effective than using either one alone;
WHEREAS, people who smoke desiring to quit should have access to approved
therapies, such as counseling, nicotine replacement therapy and pharmaceutical interventions, as
well as multiple channels for outreach and support;
WHEREAS, tobacco is an environmental issue, as the cultivation, manufacturing,
distribution, consumption, and disposal of tobacco products contribute to environmental
degradation, pollution, toxic waste, greenhouse gas emissions, and litter; and
WHEREAS, DC Calls It Quits is a smoking cessation campaign that raises awareness of
the burden of tobacco on the District and provides resource and support for smoking cessation.
IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF
COLUMBIA, That this resolution may be cited as the “DC Calls It Quits Week Recognition
Resolution of 2026”.
Sec 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes September 21 through
September 27, 2026 as “DC Calls It Quits Week”.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
