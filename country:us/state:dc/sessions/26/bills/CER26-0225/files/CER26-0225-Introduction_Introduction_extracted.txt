1 _____________________________
2 Councilmember Trayon White, Sr.
3 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
4 ______________
5 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
6 _____________________
7 To recognize and celebrate October 10, 2026, and the second Saturday of October thereafter, as
8 “DC Table Tennis Day” in the District of Columbia; and to promote health, wellness,
9 youth development, inclusion, and community engagement through the sport of table
10 tennis.
11
12 WHEREAS, it is important to promote physical activity, healthy lifestyles, and
13 recreational opportunities that improve the physical, mental, behavioral, and social well-being of
14 residents of all ages and abilities;
15
16 WHEREAS, table tennis is an affordable, accessible, and inclusive sport that can be
17 enjoyed by people of every age, ability, and background, providing lifelong opportunities for
18 physical exercise, cognitive development, social interaction, and friendly competition;
19
20 WHEREAS, participation in table tennis has been shown to improve cardiovascular
21 health, balance, coordination, reflexes, concentration, strategic thinking, emotional well-being,
22 and resilience while encouraging healthy aging and lifelong fitness;
23
24 WHEREAS, table tennis is one of the few sports in which children, adults, older adults,
25 and individuals with physical, mental, or behavioral disabilities can participate together in
26 meaningful recreational and competitive play, making it a uniquely inclusive activity that
27 strengthens intergenerational relationships, advances equity, and fosters mutual respect
28 throughout the community;
29
30 WHEREAS, during a time when many communities face challenges related to youth
31 engagement, social isolation, behavioral health, and sedentary lifestyles, organized table tennis
32 programs provide safe, structured, and positive after-school, evening, weekend, and summer
33 activities that encourage discipline, teamwork, mentorship, constructive use of free time, and
34 healthy personal development;
35
1

36 WHEREAS, expanding opportunities for young people to participate in organized sports
37 and educational enrichment programs strengthens families, supports academic achievement,
38 promotes positive decision-making, reduces exposure to crime and violence, and contributes to
39 safer, healthier, and more vibrant neighborhoods throughout the District of Columbia;
40
41 WHEREAS, table tennis is a nationally and internationally competitive sport, and an
42 Olympic sport since 1988, and District youth who are introduced to the game early may go on to
43 pursue advanced competitive play, collegiate opportunities, professional or Olympic
44 competition, and income generating careers coaching, playing, and officiating;
45
46 WHEREAS, table tennis serves as a powerful bridge across generations and cultures,
47 fostering friendships, mutual respect, teamwork, and community engagement among residents of
48 diverse backgrounds throughout the District of Columbia;
49
50 WHEREAS, table tennis can improve public health, support youth development,
51 strengthen families, encourage educational achievement, promote inclusion, and inspire lifelong
52 participation in physical activity; and
53
54 WHEREAS, the observance of DC Table Tennis Day will encourage residents, families,
55 schools, recreation centers, community organizations, businesses, and public agencies to
56 participate in exhibitions, instructional clinics, friendly competitions, demonstrations, and other
57 activities celebrating the many benefits of table tennis.
58
59 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
60 resolution may be cited as the “DC Table Tennis Day Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
61
62 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes October 10, 2026, and the
63 second Saturday of October thereafter, as “DC Table Tennis Day” in the District of Columbia;
64 commends the Washington, DC Table Tennis Academy, its officers, directors, coaches, athletes,
65 volunteers, community partners, and supporters for their outstanding leadership and dedication to
66 advancing health, wellness, education, youth development, inclusion, recreation, and community
67 service in the District of Columbia through the sport of table tennis; and encourages all residents,
68 schools, community organizations, businesses, and government agencies in the District of
69 Columbia to observe DC Table Tennis Day by participating in table tennis and by hosting safe,
70 inclusive events and programs that engage youth and families in the sport.
71
72 Sec. 3. The Council shall transmit a copy of this resolution, upon its adoption, to the
73 Mayor, the Department of Parks and Recreation, the Department of Employment Services Office
74 of Youth Programs, and the Office of the State Superintendent of Education.
75
76 Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
