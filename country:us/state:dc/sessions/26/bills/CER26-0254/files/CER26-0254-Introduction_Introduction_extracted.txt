12 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
13 __________
14
15 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
16 __________
17
18
19 To honor and recognize the Department of Aging and Community Living (DACL) for its
20 support of the District’s senior population through programs including community activities and
21 events, nutrition and transportation services, healthcare and insurance counseling, caregiver
22 support resources, and adult protective services
23
24 WHEREAS, the Department of Aging and Community Living fulfills a longstanding
25 commitment as a point of contact for improving the quality of life of older adults throughout the
26 District of Columbia;
27
28 WHEREAS, the Department of Aging and Community Living, previously titled the
29 Office of Aging, has advanced into a separate department with additional services and
30 responsibilities;
31
32 WHEREAS, the Council appreciates the dedication of Director Charon P.W. Hines and
33 her staff who work daily to assure their programs meet the needs of their constituents, advocating
34 for programs, services, resources and policies that enable District seniors to remain active and
35 engaged members of their community;
36
37 WHEREAS, the Council recognizes and appreciates the efforts of our LEAD Agencies:
38 East River Family Strengthening Collaborate; IONA Senior Services; and Seabury Resources for
39 Aging in operating our Senior Wellness Centers and providing essential services to our seniors;
40
41 WHEREAS, the District recognizes and appreciates the critical role of the District’s
42 Senior Villages, a major grant recipient, in the lives of elderly residents by encouraging
43 socialization and community-building amongst our senior population, operating in
44 neighborhoods throughout the city;
45

46 WHEREAS, DACL supports our LGBTQ+ seniors via programming such as the DC
47 Center and Out & About;
48
49 WHEREAS, DACL supports the efforts of Sibley Memorial Hospital and IONA Senior
50 Services in operating dementia care programs to assist seniors afflicted with memory related
51 illnesses;
52
53 WHEREAS, the VIDA Senior Center, led by Dr. María Teresa McPhail, supports our
54 Hispanic and Latinx senior population;
55
56 WHEREAS, DACL serves as a critical agency in the District's efforts to address the
57 needs of its growing older adult population, providing and coordinating services that include
58 health and wellness programs, social engagement opportunities, caregiver support,
59 transportation, and other services designed to promote independence while preventing isolation;
60
61 WHERAS, DACL facilitates the Commission on Aging to assess the needs and be a
62 voice of advocacy for District seniors;
63
64 WHEREAS, DACL meets the critical need of assuring the presence of accessible
65 neighborhood-based services and has supported efforts to strengthen community resources where
66 older adults can receive services to maintain their physical, emotional, and social well-being;
67
68 WHEREAS, DACL has worked on initiatives addressing the diverse needs of older
69 Washingtonians, including efforts to expand access to programs that are designed to help DC’s
70 growing senior population age in place and avoid the social isolation that is so harmful to their
71 quality of life;
72
73 WHEREAS, DACL will seek to develop strategies to proactively engage seniors to
74 address social isolation as our population ages and technology separates us;
75
76 WHEREAS, DACL must be vigilant about the need to expand congregate meal locations,
77 senior activities and programming;
78
79 WHEREAS, Councilmember Anita Bonds, joined by colleagues, expresses her sincere
80 appreciation to DACL for trying to mediate the circumstances brought on in life to those persons
81 considered in their senior time of life and does so with care, thoughtfulness, and dedication
82 through the services of DACL.
83
84 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, that this
85 resolution may be cited as the “Department of Aging and Community Living Recognition
86 Resolution of 2026”.
87
88 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Department of Aging and
89 Community Living for its role in supporting the senior population of the District of Columbia.
90

91 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
92 the District of Columbia Register.
