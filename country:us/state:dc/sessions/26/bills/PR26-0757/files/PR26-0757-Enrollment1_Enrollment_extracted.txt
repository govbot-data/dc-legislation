ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-495
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
July 14, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to amend the Language Access
Act of 2004 to authorize the Mayor to issue rules to implement the provisions of that act.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Language Access Rulemaking Authority Emergency
Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. Emergency legislation is necessary to ensure that the Mayor, through the Office
of Human Rights, can promulgate and amend rules to allow for the fair and effective
enforcement of the Language Access Act of 2004, effective June 19, 2004 (D.C. Law 15-167;
D.C. Official Code § 2-1931 et seq.).
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances, making it necessary that the
Language Access Rulemaking Authority Emergency Amendment Act of 2026 be adopted after a
single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
