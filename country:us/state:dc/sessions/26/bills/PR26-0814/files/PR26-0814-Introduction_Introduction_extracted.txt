Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Enclosed for consideration and approval by the Council of the District of Columbia is a proposed
resolution entitled “Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority Fiscal Year 2027 to 2032
Dedicated Funding Grant Agreement Emergency Approval Resolution of 2026” along with an
accompanying emergency declaration resolution entitled “Washington Metropolitan Area Transit
Authority Fiscal Year 2027 to 2032 Dedicated Funding Grant Agreement Emergency
Declaration Resolution of 2026.”
The resolutions will approve a grant agreement between the District and the Washington
Metropolitan Area Transit Authority (“WMATA”) under which the District will provide
dedicated funding to WMATA to support capital improvements to maintain WMATA’s assets in
a state of good repair.
The grant agreement is for a term of six fiscal years, in a total not-to-exceed amount of
$1,071,000,000 and an annual not-to-exceed amount of $178,500,000.
I urge the Council to take prompt and favorable action on the enclosed resolutions.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowswer

23 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
24 resolution may be cited as the "Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority Fiscal Year
25 2027 to 2032 Dedicated Funding Grant Agreement Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026".
26 Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve a Dedicated Funding Grant
27 Agreement between the District and the Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority
28 ("WMATA") by which the District will provide funding to WMATA for state-of-good-repair
29 capital improvements to WMATA's Metrorail and Metrobus systems from October I, 2026, to
30 September 30, 2032.
31 (b) In 2018, the District of Columbia, Virginia, and Maryland agreed to provide WMATA
32 dedicated funding of $500 million annually, starting in Fiscal Year 2020, to help maintain the
33 Metrorail and Metrobus systems in a state of good repair.

34 (c) Thereafter, on February 14, 2020, the Director of the District Department of
35 Transportation executed a one-year Dedicated Funding Grant Agreement with WMATA,
36 effective for Fiscal Year 2020, in the not-to-exceed amount of $178,500,000, to help maintain
37 WMATA’s assets in a state of good repair.
38 (c) On October 22, 2020, the Mayor executed a six-year Dedicated Funding Grant
39 Agreement with WMATA, effective from October 1, 2020, through September 30, 2026, in an
40 annual amount of $178,500,000 and a total not-to-exceed amount of $1,071,000,000, to help
41 maintain WMATA’s assets in a state of good repair.
42 (d) Because the existing Dedicated Funding Grant Agreement is scheduled to expire on
43 September 30, 2026, it is now necessary for the District and WMATA to enter into a new
44 Dedicated Funding Grant Agreement in order to continue the District’s support for WMATA’s
45 investments to maintain the Metrorail and Metrobus systems in a state of good repair.
46 (e) The Mayor therefore has transmitted to the Council a proposed six-year Dedicated
47 Funding Grant Agreement with WMATA, to be effective from October 1, 2026, through
48 September 30, 2032, in an annual amount of $178,500,000 and a total not-to-exceed amount of
49 $1,071,000,00.
50 (e) The Council’s approval of the proposed agreement is necessary under section 451 of
51 the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 803; D.C.
52 Official Code § 1-204.51), as the agreement is a contract involving expenditures of more than
53 $1,000,000 during a 12-month period and is a multiyear contract.
54 (f) The Council’s approval of the agreement is necessary to support the continuation of
55 WMATA’s vital Metrorail and Metrobus services in a state of good repair.

56 Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
57 enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
58 Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority Fiscal Year 2027 to 2032 Dedicated Funding
59 Grant Agreement Emergency Approval Resolution of 2026 be adopted on an emergency basis.
60 Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
