Dear Secretary Howard:
Today, I am introducing the “Elevator Reliability and Maintenance Amendment Act of 2026”, along
with Councilmembers Robert C. White, Jr., Zachary Parker, and Anita Bonds. Please find enclosed a
signed copy of the legislation.
Elevators are essential for multi-story buildings. The Department of Buildings (“DOB”) is the agency
responsible for enforcing the District’s Property Maintenance Code, which sets standards and
requirements for the maintenance and operations of elevators. Currently, the District’s Property
Maintenance Code only requires a single elevator to be operational in a building where there are
multiple elevators, and this is true regardless of building height, resident population, or residents’
accessibility needs.
My office has been helping residents in a multifamily building in Ward 6 that experiences persistent
elevator outages. This building, which was designed and built to have five elevators, at one time for
approximately six weeks had only one semi-operational elevator, and it did not even service the first
two floors of the building. This meant that residents, the majority of whom are seniors and have
difficulties using stairs, needed to have groceries and other essentials brought to them. These
residents were effectively trapped in their units because there was no elevator that could reach their
floor. For those residents that had access to the elevator, many of them had to wait an inordinate
amount of time for the elevator to respond to a call for service. This is unacceptable. Further adding
insult to injury is the fact that the District’s Property Maintenance Code currently does not incentivize
property owners to maintain all elevators in a building in good, working order.
I am introducing legislation to require that all elevators installed in a building be maintained in good,
working order at all times unless the Department of Buildings issues a permit to take the elevator
out of service or place an elevator in temporarily dormant status. The bill would specifically define a
good, working elevator as one that services every floor that it is designed or required to serve and
responds to a call for service and arrives at the requested floor within a reasonable period of time.
1

This bill would also provide DOB with explicit authorization to utilize its Nuisance Abatement Fund to
correct elevator outages in a building by specifically defining “imminent danger” to include situations
where there is no working elevator in a building and the outage of one or more elevators lasts longer
than 14 consecutive calendar days. The establishment of straightforward, enforceable standards for
elevator operation and explicitly authorizing DOB to use its Nuisance Abatement Fund to address
elevator outages will better ensure that residents have access to reliable and operational elevators.
Please feel free to reach out to me or my Legislative Director, Antonio Nunes, at
anunes@dccouncil.gov, with any questions or for additional information.
Sincerely,
Councilmember Charles Allen, Ward 6
Chairperson, Committee on Transportation & the Environment

21 To amend An act to regulate the construction and operation of elevators within the District of
22 Columbia, and for other purposes to require that all elevators installed in a building be
23 maintained in good, working order at all times unless the Department of Buildings issues
24 a permit to take the elevator out of service or place an elevator in temporarily dormant
25 status, and to define when an elevator is deemed to be in good, working order; and to
26 amend An Act to provide for the abatement of nuisances in the District of Columbia by
27 the Commissioners of said District, and for other purposes to allow the Department of
28 Buildings to utilize the Nuisance Abatement Fund to abate elevator issues by defining
29 imminent danger to include situations where there is no working elevator in a building
30 and the outage of one or more elevators lasts longer than 14 consecutive calendar days.
31
32 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
33 act may be cited as the “Elevator Reliability and Maintenance Amendment Act of 2026”.
