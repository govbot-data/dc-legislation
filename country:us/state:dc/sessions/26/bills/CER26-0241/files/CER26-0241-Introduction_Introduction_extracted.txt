17 To recognize Jaya Pillai for her exceptional leadership as Legislative and Committee Director in
18 the office of Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr., and for her significant contributions to
19 the legislative and oversight work of the Council of the District of Columbia.
20 WHEREAS, Jaya Pillai serves as Legislative and Committee Director to Councilmember
21 Robert C. White, Jr., helping guide both the Councilmember’s legislative agenda and the work of
22 the Committee on Housing;
23 WHEREAS, Jaya Pillai has brought rigor, strategic judgment, and a deep sense of
24 responsibility to complex legislative matters, helping move ideas from public concern to
25 substantive proposals and careful Council consideration;
26 WHEREAS, her work has supported hearings, oversight, budget review, stakeholder
27 engagement, and the detailed coordination necessary to examine the District’s housing programs
28 and policies;
29 WHEREAS, Jaya Pillai has helped navigate challenging questions involving housing
30 affordability, tenant protections, housing production, homeownership, and the public institutions
31 responsible for carrying out the District’s housing commitments;
32 WHEREAS, she understands that strong legislation depends on attentive listening, sound
33 research, honest debate, and the willingness to refine policy so that it can serve residents
34 effectively;

35 WHEREAS, Jaya Pillai has contributed to the creation of public records through
36 committee hearings and legislative deliberations, helping ensure that elected officials can
37 evaluate proposals in light of testimony, research, and agency accountability;
38 WHEREAS, she has helped the team confront policy tradeoffs without losing sight of the
39 families, homeowners, renters, and communities affected by the outcomes;
40 WHEREAS, Jaya Pillai has supported the preparation of colleagues and the coordination
41 of complex workstreams, helping the office navigate the demands of both broad legislative
42 strategy and the details of individual measures;
43 WHEREAS, Jaya Pillai’s leadership has strengthened cooperation among attorneys,
44 policy staff, committee professionals, advocates, and agency representatives, while helping
45 colleagues meet the highest standards of legislative preparation;
46 WHEREAS, Jaya Pillai transformed the office through her spirit of execution, strategic
47 vision, and remarkable patience, bringing order to complexity, turning ambitious ideas into
48 action, and inspiring colleagues to meet challenges with purpose and confidence;
49 WHEREAS, her genuine kindness has made colleagues feel valued and supported, while
50 her quick wit and perfectly timed jokes have lifted office morale through long hearings, late
51 nights, and demanding deadlines;
52 WHEREAS, Jaya Pillai’s thoughtful reading recommendations have encouraged
53 curiosity, reflection, and lively conversations among colleagues, enriching the office beyond its
54 daily policy work; and
55 WHEREAS, Jaya Pillai has distinguished herself through her work ethic, thoughtful
56 counsel, and commitment to public institutions that are accountable to the people of the District
57 of Columbia.
58 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
59 resolution may be cited as the “Jaya Pillai Recognition Resolution of 2026”.

60 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes Jaya Pillai for her exceptional
61 legislative and committee leadership, her transformative approach to strategy and execution, and
62 the patience, kindness, humor, and generosity through which she strengthened the office and
63 served the residents of the District.
64 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
65 the District of Columbia Register.
