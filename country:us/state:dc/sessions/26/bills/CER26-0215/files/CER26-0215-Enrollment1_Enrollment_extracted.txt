ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-215
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
October 6, 2026
To honor and recognize Lindsey Parker for her demonstration of and unwavering
commitment to excellence in government, helping to shape policies and initiatives that
promote opportunity and prosperity across all eight wards.
WHEREAS, it is both a privilege and a pleasure to recognize an individual whose
dedication, professionalism, and commitment to public service strengthen the fabric of the
District of Columbia;
WHEREAS, Lindsey Parker has served with distinction in many important roles in the
District government, including as the Director of the Office of the Chief Technology Officer, the
Chief of Staff and the Senior Advisor handling major initiatives for Mayor Bowser, providing
trusted counsel, excellent leadership, and steadfast guidance in advancing the priorities of the
administration and the well-being of District residents;
WHEREAS, her leadership reflects a deep understanding of the needs of the
community, coupled with a collaborative spirit that brings together stakeholders, government
agencies, community organizations, and residents to achieve meaningful results;
WHEREAS, Lindsey Parker’s integrity, dedication, and professionalism exemplify the
highest ideals of public service and serve as an example to colleagues and constituents alike;
WHEREAS, her contributions have helped move the District forward, strengthening its
position as a world-class city and ensuring its government remains responsive, innovative, and
accountable to the people and businesses it represents;
WHEREAS, Lindsey Parker has been one of Mayor Bowser’s most trusted senior
managers, particularly in government operations, technology modernization, and complex cross-
agency projects;

WHEREAS, Ms. Parker deserves recognition for her outstanding service to the residents
of Washington, D.C. and for her leadership, dedication, and lasting contributions to the District
government;
WHEREAS, Lindsey Parker has now been named the Executive Director of the
Maryland Stadium Authority, where she will lead the authority on major projects such as the re-
development of Oriole Park at Camden Yards and other large public infrastructure efforts.
RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Lindsey Parker Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors Lindsey Parker for
her service to the District in many trusted high-level positions where her accomplishments have
greatly benefited the community and the residents of this great city, applauds and thanks Ms.
Parker for her dedication, and wishes her the best in all her future endeavors.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
