ENROLLED ORIGINAL
AN ACT
_________________________
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
_________________________
To amend the Parking Enforcement Modernization Act of 2024 to establish a reimbursable detail
program for parking enforcement by Department of Public Works officers and to clarify
the authority of campus police to conduct parking enforcement.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
act may be cited as the “Strategic Parking Enforcement Enhancement Amendment Act of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Parking Enforcement Modernization Act of 2024, effective May 2, 2025
(D.C. Law 25-325; D.C. Official Code § 50-2431 et seq.) is amended as follows:
(a) Section 102 (D.C. Official Code § 50-2431) is amended as follows:
(1) New paragraphs (1A) and (1B) are added to read as follows:
“(1A) “Agreement” means a written contract, including provisions for the staffing
requirement of the reimbursable details, in accordance with section 115, and compensation of the
Department by the eligible entity when reimbursable details are requested by the eligible entity.
“(1B) “Campus police officer” shall have the same meaning as provided in D.C.
Official Code § 47-2853.207(1).”.
(2) A new paragraph (4A) is added to read as follows:
“(4A) “Eligible entity” means a business, nonprofit organization, promoter or
organizer of a District-licensed special event, Business Improvement District, DC Main Streets
Program, a university, or other entity designated by the Mayor, or a group of any such entities.”.
(3) A new paragraph (15A) is added to read as follows:
“(15A) “Reimbursable detail” means an assignment of Parking Enforcement
Management Administration parking enforcement officers to patrol a designated area for the
purpose of enforcing parking violations and managing curbside access.”.
(b) Subtitle B is amended by adding new sections 115 and 116 to read as follows:
“Sec. 115. Reimbursable detail for parking enforcement.
“(a) An eligible entity may enter into an agreement with the Department to provide for
reimbursable details.
“(b) Subject to adequate staffing and an assessment of its staffing requirements, the
Department may staff reimbursable details as requested; provided, that:
“(1) The Department shall only use parking enforcement officers who are
overtime and would not otherwise be on duty at the time of the reimbursable detail; and

“(2) Parking enforcement officers participating in reimbursable details shall
enforce parking restrictions impartially according to their own discretion and shall remain
subject solely to the direction of their commanding officers.
“(c) Pursuant to funding, the Mayor shall establish a program to subsidize eligible entities
for a portion of reimbursement costs borne by eligible entities for reimbursable detail for parking
enforcement pursuant to this section.
“(d) The Department shall establish data standards to track citations issued by an officer
while working a reimbursable detail shift pursuant to this section, including an indication of the
specific detail agreement, as feasible.
“Sec. 116. Parking enforcement by campus police officers.
“(a) Subject to the limitations in subsections (b) and (c) of this section, campus police
officers may issue parking citations, regarding vehicles found to be in violation of Chapter 24 of
Title 18 of the District of Columbia Municipal Regulations (18 DCMR § 2400 et seq.):
“(1) On property subject to their jurisdiction;
“(2) In areas of the public right of way bordered on both sides by property subject
to their jurisdiction; and
“(3) In parking spaces in the public right of way that are reserved for persons
affiliated with or visiting properties subject to their jurisdiction.
“(b)(1) The educational institution at whose request a campus police officer was
appointed shall submit a notice to the District, in a form to be specified by the Mayor, before the
officer may conduct parking enforcement pursuant to this section.
“(2) Nothing in this section shall be interpreted to supersede any other law that
expressly precludes a specific category or organization of campus police officers from enforcing
parking violations.
“(c) Before a campus police officer may issue any parking citations under this section,
the educational institution shall certify that the campus police officer has satisfactorily completed
a training program comparable to the Department’s, including any topics and skills identified by
the Department by rule pursuant to section 141(a)(5).”.
(c) Section 141(a) (D.C. Official Code § 50-2471(a)) is amended as follows:
(1) Paragraph (3) is amended by striking the phrase “; and” and inserting a
semicolon in its place.
(2) Paragraph (4) is amended by striking the period and inserting the phrase “;
and” in its place.
(3) A new paragraph (5) is added to read as follows:
“(5) Provisions to facilitate campus police’s parking enforcement pursuant to
section 116, including provisions to address officer training, the issuance and adjudication of
citations, and revenue-sharing arrangements.”.
Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.
The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal
impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).

Sec. 4. Effective date.
This act shall take effect after approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto) and a 30-day period of congressional review
as provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December
24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)).
