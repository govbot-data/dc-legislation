ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-468
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
July 14, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve Modification Nos.
M01, M02, and M03 to Contract No. CW131646 with Shaw Solutions, LLC, and to
authorize payment for the goods and services received and to be received under the
contract.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Modification Nos. M01, M02 and M03 to CW131646 with Shaw
Solutions LLC Approval and Payment Authorization Emergency Declaration Resolution of
2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve Modification Nos. M01, M02, and
M03 to Contract No. CW131646 with Shaw Solutions, LLC to provide equipment and qualified
operators for snow plowing and removal, and to authorize payment for the goods and services
received and to be received under the contract.
(b) On January 20, 2026, the Office of Contracting and Procurement (“OCP”), on behalf
of the Department of Public Works, executed Contract No. CW131646 in the not-to-exceed
amount of $1 million for the period from January 20, 2026, through January 19, 2027.
(c) On January 22, 2026, by Emergency Modification No. M01, OCP increased the not-
to-exceed amount by $4 million, to a total not-to-exceed amount of $5 million.
(d) On April 27, 2026, by Emergency Modification No. M02, OCP decreased the not-to-
exceed amount by $2 million, to a total not-to-exceed amount of $3 million.
(e) On June 2, 2026, OCP executed Emergency Modification No. M03, an administrative
modification to clarify the price schedule, but did not change the total not-to-exceed amount of
$3 million.
(f) Council approval is necessary because the modifications increased the value of the
contract by more than $1 million during a 12-month period.
(g) Council approval is necessary to pay for these vital goods and services. Without
Council approval, Shaw Solutions, LLC cannot be paid for goods and services provided in
excess of $1 million for the period from January 20, 2026, through January 19, 2027.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
