7 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
8
9 _________________________
10
11 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
12
13 _________________________
14
15
16 To recognize, honor, and celebrate the 100th Annual Holy Convocation of The United House of
17 Prayer for All People.
18
19 WHEREAS, The United House of Prayer for All People was founded in 1919 by Bishop
20 C.M. Grace, having built the first edifice with his own hands, Bishop Grace declared the First
21 Annual Holy Convocation of The United House of Prayer to improve the spiritual condition of
22 “all people”;
23 WHEREAS, The United House of Prayer first purchased property in 1926 by acquiring a
24 mansion—and its slave quarters, which now serve as the organization’s national headquarters;
25 WHEREAS, the public outreach of Christian fellowship, including the
26 Convocation, began under Bishop Grace and was exemplified by the Church’s willingness to
27 feed the needy during the Great Depression;
28 WHEREAS, Bishop W. McCollough, grew the Church’s outreach by launching the
29 McCollough Voters League, establishing the McCollough Scholarship College Fund, and
30 creating an extensive housing program which has made it possible for many residents from
31 lower- and middle-income backgrounds to have an opportunity to live, work, and flourish in the
32 District of Columbia;
1

33 WHEREAS, Bishop S.C. Madison, a longtime District resident and pastor, led his
34 congregations into 17 Convocations, increased the Church’s real estate holdings, and advanced
35 the scholarship initiative by beginning the First Lady’s Scholastic Achievement Program to
36 complement and feed into the organization’s scholarship program;
37 WHEREAS, Bishop C.M. Bailey worked with DC residents to encourage them to turn
38 their lives, hearts, and souls over to a higher power, leading members into 15 Convocations, even
39 spending his last moments on earth administering the 97th Annual Holy Convocation; and
40 WHEREAS, Bishop A.D. Cunningham, the 5th and current leader, ensured to conclude
41 the 97th Convocation, protect the Church’s intellectual properties, build homes for those of all
42 walks of life, and work to improve the spiritual walk of “all people” as the organization’s name
43 implies.
44 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
45 resolution may be cited as the “The United House of Prayer for All People 100th Annual Holy
46 Convocation Recognition Resolution of 2026.”
47 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and celebrates the 100th
48 Annual Holy Convocation of The United House of Prayer for All People, its past leadership, and
49 its current leader Bishop A.D. Cunningham, who are commended for the efforts to improve the
50 District of Columbia, its residents, and the lives of “all people.”
51 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
52 the District of Columbia Register.
