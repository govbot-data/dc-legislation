11 To order the closing of a portion of the public alley system in Square 785.
12
13 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
14 act may be cited as the “Closing of a Portion of a Public Alley in Square 785, S.O. 21-03369, Act
15 of 2026”.
16 Sec. 2. (a) Pursuant to section 404 of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved
17 December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 787; D.C. Official Code § 1-204.04), and consistent with the Street
18 and Alley Closing Acquisition Procedures Act of 1982, effective March 10, 1983 (D.C. Law
19 4-201; D.C. Official Code § 9-201.01 et seq.), the Council finds that the portion of the public
20 alley system in Square 785, as shown on the Surveyor’s plat filed under S.O. 21-03369, is
21 unnecessary for alley purposes and orders it closed with title to the land to vest as shown on the
22 Surveyor’s plat.
23 Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.
24 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal
25 impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975,
26 approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
