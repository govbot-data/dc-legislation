12 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
13 __________
14
15 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
16 __________
17
18
19 To honor and recognize the District of Columbia Department of Human Resources
20 (DCHR) for its commitment to strengthening the District government’s workforce, advancing
21 employee development, promoting effective human resource management, and supporting the
22 recruitment, retention, and professional growth of public servants through the District of
23 Columbia.
24
25 WHEREAS, the Department of Human Resources serves as the central human resources
26 agency for the District government, providing human resources management services designed
27 to strengthen individual and organizational performance and enable the District to attract,
28 develop, and retain a highly qualified and diverse workforce;
29
30 WHEREAS, DCHR supports the full employee lifecycle, from recruitment and
31 onboarding to professional development, benefits administration, retirement planning, and
32 separation from government service, helping ensure that employees receive the guidance and
33 resources necessary throughout their public service careers;
34
35 WHEREAS, DCHR plays an essential role in supporting the operational needs of District
36 government agencies by providing executive management, recruitment and staffing assistance,
37 personnel policy guidance, workforce planning, classification and compensation services, and
38 strategic human capital support;
39
40 WHEREAS, DCHR's recruitment and talent development efforts include initiatives such
41 as the Capital City Fellows Program, the District Leadership Program, the Inspiring Service
42 Youth Leadership Program, and Career Pathways, which help develop future public servants and
43 create opportunities for District residents to pursue careers in government;
44
45 WHEREAS, DCHR's Benefits and Retirement Administration provides critical assistance
46 to eligible District government employees and retirees by administering health insurance,

47 voluntary benefits, and retirement programs, helping individuals and families navigate important
48 decisions concerning their financial security and well-being;
49
50 WHEREAS, the Department's responsibilities extend to managing citywide benefits,
51 retirement, and personnel programs supporting over 32,000 active District government
52 employees, retirees, and beneficiaries across both subordinate and independent personnel
53 authorities, representing a vast network whose collective efforts are essential to the
54 administration, operation, and delivery of government services throughout the nation's capital;
55
56 WHEREAS, Director Steven Walker was appointed Interim Director of DCHR in June
57 2026 by Mayor Muriel Bowser, bringing more than 35 years of administrative, human resources,
58 operational, and community organizing experience to the agency's leadership;
59
60 WHEREAS, the Department of Human Resources operates under the legislative and
61 oversight jurisdiction of the Council of the District of Columbia’s Committee on Executive
62 Administration and Labor whose operations directly affect the administration, management, and
63 delivery of services within District government;
64
65 WHEREAS, the Committee on Executive Administration and Labor is responsible for
66 examining the operations, policies, programs, and performance of DCHR, ensuring that the
67 Department remains accountable for its responsibilities and effectively carries out its mission of
68 supporting the District government's workforce;
69
70 WHEREAS, through the Council's annual performance oversight process, the Committee
71 on Executive Administration and Labor performs oversight for evaluating DCHR's
72 accomplishments, identifying operational challenges, reviewing workforce management
73 practices, and examining opportunities to improve the delivery of human resources services to
74 District government agencies and employees;
75
76 WHEREAS, the Committee on Executive Administration and Labor recognizes that
77 effective human resources management is essential to the overall functioning of District
78 government, and that the recruitment, retention, professional development, and fair treatment of
79 public employees are fundamental to maintaining an effective and responsive government;
80
81 WHEREAS, Councilmember Anita Bonds, through her leadership of the Committee on
82 Executive Administration and Labor, remains committed to ensuring that the District's
83 government institutions are held to high standards of accountability, transparency, and
84 operational effectiveness, recognizing that a well-supported public workforce is essential to
85 serving the residents of the District of Columbia; and
86
87 WHERAS, Councilmember Anita Bonds wishes to express her sincere appreciation to the
88 Department of Human Resources for its service to DC and for its commitment to advancing its
89 support of District agencies, strengthening workforce management, improving employee
90 services, and cultivating a capable and effective public sector workforce.
91
92

93 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
94 resolution may be cited as the “District of Columbia Department of Human Resources
95 Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
96
97 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the District of Columbia
98 Department of Human Resources for its continued contributions to strengthening the District
99 government's workforce, advancing employee development, and supporting the effective
100 delivery of public services throughout the District of Columbia.
101
102 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
103 the District of Columbia Register.
