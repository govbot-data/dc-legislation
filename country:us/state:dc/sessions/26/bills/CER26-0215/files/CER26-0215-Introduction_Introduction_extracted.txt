24 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
25 __________
26
27 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
28 __________
29
30 To honor and recognize Lindsey Parker for her demonstration of and unwavering
31 commitment to excellence in government, helping to shape policies and initiatives that promote
32 opportunity and prosperity across all eight wards.
33
34 WHEREAS, it is both a privilege and a pleasure to recognize an individual whose
35 dedication, professionalism, and commitment to public service strengthen the fabric of the
36 District of Columbia;
37
38 WHEREAS, Lindsey Parker has served with distinction in many important roles in the
39 District government, including as the Director of the Office of the Chief Technology Officer, the
40 Chief of Staff and the Senior Advisor handling major initiatives for Mayor Bowser, providing
41 trusted counsel, excellent leadership, and steadfast guidance in advancing the priorities of the
42 administration and the well-being of District residents;
43
44 WHEREAS, her leadership reflects a deep understanding of the needs of the
45 community, coupled with a collaborative spirit that brings together stakeholders, government
46 agencies, community organizations, and residents to achieve meaningful results;

47
48 WHEREAS, Lindsey Parker’s integrity, dedication, and professionalism exemplify the
49 highest ideals of public service and serve as an example to colleagues and constituents alike;
50
51 WHEREAS, her contributions have helped move the District forward, strengthening its
52 position as a world-class city and ensuring its government remains responsive, innovative, and
53 accountable to the people and businesses it represents;
54
55 WHEREAS, Lindsey Parker has been one of Mayor Bowser’s most trusted senior
56 managers, particularly in government operations, technology modernization, and complex cross-
57 agency projects;
58
59 WHEREAS, Ms. Parker deserves recognition for her outstanding service to the residents
60 of Washington, D.C. and for her leadership, dedication, and lasting contributions to the District
61 government;
62
63 WHEREAS, Lindsey Parker has now been named the Executive Director of the
64 Maryland Stadium Authority, where she will lead the authority on major projects such as the re-
65 development of Oriole Park at Camden Yards and other large public infrastructure efforts.
66
67 RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
68 resolution may be cited as the “Lindsey Parker Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
69
70 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes and honors Lindsey Parker for
71 her service to the District in many trusted high-level positions where her accomplishments have
72 greatly benefited the community and the residents of this great city, applauds and thanks Ms.
73 Parker for her dedication, and wishes her the best in all her future endeavors.
74
75 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
76 the District of Columbia Register.
