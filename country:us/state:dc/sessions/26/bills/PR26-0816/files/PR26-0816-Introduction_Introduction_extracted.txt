Dear Chairman Mendelson:
Enclosed for consideration and enactment by the Council of the District of Columbia is an
emergency bill, the “Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority Gold Line Bus Rapid
Transit Phase 1 Fiscal Year 2027 and 2028 Reimbursable Agreement Emergency Act of 2026”
and the accompanying emergency declaration resolution.
The legislation will approve a multiyear Reimbursable Agreement between the Washington
Metropolitan Area Transit Authority (“WMATA”) and the District Department of Transportation
(“DDOT”), which will provide WMATA with $5,000,000 in local capital funding for Phase 1 of
the development of a bus rapid transit system on H Street and Benning Road between Union
Station and RFK Stadium named the “Gold Line”.
My administration is available to discuss any questions you may have regarding the proposed
legislation. In order to facilitate a response to any questions you may have, please contact Sharon
Kershbaum, Director, DDOT, at (202) 671-2317.
I urge the Council to take prompt and favorable action on the enclosed legislation.
Sincerely,
Muriel Bowser
Mayor

17 To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve a
18 Reimbursable Agreement for more than $1 million with the Washington
19 Metropolitan Area Transit Authority to develop a bus rapid transit system on H
20 Street and Benning Road between Union Station and RFK Stadium named the
21 "Gold Line".
22
23 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That
24 this resolution may be cited as the "Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority
25 Gold Line Bus Rapid Transit Phase 1 Fiscal Year 2027 and 2028 Reimbursable
26 Agreement Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026".
27 Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve a Reimbursable Agreement
28 (Agreement) with the Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority to develop a bus
29 rapid transit system named the "Gold Line" of which Phase 1 will support the development
30 of the Hand Benning Street corridors, Union Station and the area surrounding the existing
31 RFK Stadium.
32 (b) Council approval is necessary since this is a multiyear agreement in excess of
33 $1,000,000.

34 (c) Furthermore, Council approval is necessary to ensure the frequent bus service
35 operating in dedicated, enforced center-running bus lanes along H Street and Benning
36 Road, NE, connecting the RFK stadium and redevelopment to Union Station is completed
37 in time for the opening of the new RFK stadium.
38 (d) The total amount to the District for this Agreement is $5,000,000 from capital
39 funds appropriated in FY 2027.
40 (e) The DDOT Director has the authority to enter into this Reimbursable
41 Agreement under the general authority specified in sections 3 and 5(a)(1)(A) and (1)(P)
42 of the Department of Transportation Establishment Act of 2002, effective May 21, 2002
43 (D.C. Law 14–137; D.C. Official Code §§ 50–921.02 and 50-921.04(a)(1)(A) and (1)(P))
44 and Section 1(j-1) of An Act To grant additional powers to the Commissioners of the
45 District of Columbia, and for other purposes, approved December 20, 1944 (58 Stat. 819;
46 D.C. Official Code § 1–301.01(j-1)).
47 Sec. 3. The Secretary of the Council shall submit a copy of this resolution, upon
48 its adoption to the Mayor.
49 Sec. 4. The Council of the District of Columbia adopts the fiscal impact
50 statement of the Chief Financial Officer as required by section 602(c)(3) of the District of
51 Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24, 1973 (D.C. Code § 1-206.02(c)(3);
52 87 Stat. 813).
53 Sec. 5. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
