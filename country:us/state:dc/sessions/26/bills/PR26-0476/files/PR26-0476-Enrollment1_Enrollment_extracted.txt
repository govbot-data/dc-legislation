ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-344
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
March 3, 2026
To confirm the reappointment of Leif Dormsjo to the Washington Convention and Sports
Authority Board of Directors.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Washington Convention and Sports Authority Board of Directors
Leif Dormsjo Confirmation Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia confirms the reappointment of:
Leif Dormsjo
39th Street, NW
Washington, DC 20007
(Ward 3)
as a public member with proven expertise in municipal finance and chairperson of the
Washington Convention and Sports Authority Board of Directors, established by section 205 of
the Washington Convention Center Authority Act of 1994, effective September 28, 1994 (D.C.
Law 10-188; D.C. Official Code § 10-1202.05), for a term to end May 16, 2030.
Sec. 3. The Council shall transmit a copy of this resolution, upon its adoption, to the
nominee and to the Mayor.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
