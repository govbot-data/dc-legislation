ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-516
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need for the Department of Small
and Local Business Development to award a grant to the Duke Business Improvement
District Corporation to improve conditions for property owners, businesses, residents,
and visitors within the Greater Shaw/U Street area.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Duke District BID Grantmaking Emergency Declaration
Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) The Duke Business Improvement District Corporation, d/b/a Duke District
BID has been organized under the District of Columbia Nonprofit Corporation Act of 2010 to
operate exclusively to improve neighborhood vitality and act as a Business Improvement District
(“BID”) for property owners, businesses, residents, and visitors within the Greater Shaw/U Street
neighborhood. The neighborhood is referred to collectively as the “Duke District”, in honor of
Duke Ellington, who grew up and launched his music career in this area, one that continues to be
a vibrant cultural hub to this day.
(b) Pursuant to the Business Improvements District Act of 1996, effective May 29, 1996
(D.C. Law 11-134; D.C. Official Code § 2-1215.01 et seq.), the Duke District BID has put
together the business plan, boundaries, and property owner petitions necessary to submit a BID
application to the Department of Small and Local Business Development. The Duke District BID
petition has surpassed the signatures necessary to advance with its application, which will be
accompanied by Council legislation.
(c) In 2023, the Council established the Greater U Street Performance Parking Zone to
manage curbside availability, address congestion during peak nightlife hours, and generate a
local source of revenue deposited into the Greater U Street Parking Benefit Fund. In the Fiscal
Year 2027 Budget Support Act of 2026, enacted on August 14, 2026 (D.C. Act 26-418; 73 DCR
11735) (“BSA”), $1 million of revenue deposited into the fund was authorized to be issued as a
grant in support of a place management organization in the area covered by the Greater U Street
Performance Parking Zone. This funding was authorized with a BID organization in mind, but
the Duke District BID had not yet attained the signatures necessary to advance. Per application
materials, the proposed boundaries of the Duke District BID are coincident with the boundary of
the Greater U Street Performance Parking Zone.

(d) Emergency legislation is necessary to appropriately direct place management
grantmaking funds authorized in the BSA, in order to provide seed funding advancing the
establishment of the Duke District BID and allow for services to be stood up alongside existing
neighborhood-serving partners, including Main Streets and Clean Teams. These funds will
support the economic vitality, cultural vibrancy, cleanliness, safety, and overall quality of life of
the community and District as a whole.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the Duke
District BID Grantmaking Emergency Amendment Act of 2026 be adopted after a single
reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
