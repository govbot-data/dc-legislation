18 To amend Chapter 46 of Title 47 of the District of Columbia Official Code to provide a limited
19 real property tax abatement for the real property located at 4020 Minnesota Avenue, N.E.,
20 Washington, D.C. 20019, known for tax and assessment purposes as Lot 811 in Square
21 5052, conditioned upon the owner making qualifying capital improvements to the
22 property in an amount at least equal to the value of the tax abatement received,
23 maintaining the affordability of the residential units, complying with District law, and
24 satisfying reporting and certification requirements; and to authorize the Mayor to
25 suspend, terminate, or recapture the tax abatement for failure to satisfy the requirements
26 of this act.
27
28 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
29 act may be cited as the “Park 7 Affordable Housing Preservation Tax Abatement Amendment Act
30 of 2026”.
31 Sec. 2. Chapter 46 of Title 47 of the District of Columbia Official Code is amended as
32 follows:
33 (a) The table of contents is amended by adding a new section designation to read as
34 follows:
35 “47-4685. Park 7 affordable housing preservation tax abatement.”.

37 “Sec. 47-4685. Park 7 affordable housing preservation tax abatement.
38 “(a) For purposes of this section, the term:
39 “(1) “Capital Improvement” means a physical improvement to the
40 Property that:
41 “(A) Materially extends the useful life of the Property or a major
42 building component or system;
43 “(B) Improves the health, safety, security, accessibility, energy
44 efficiency, or habitability of the Property;
45 “(C) Replaces or substantially rehabilitates a major building
46 component or system; or
47 “(D) Otherwise constitutes an eligible capital improvement
48 approved by the Mayor.
49 “(2) “District financial assistance” means a grant, loan, tax credit, tax
50 abatement, or other financial assistance provided by the District government for the
51 rehabilitation or operation of the Property; provided, that the term shall not include the tax
52 abatement authorized by this section.
53 “(3) “Owner” means the Park 7 Residential Limited Partnership, its
54 successor in interest, or another entity that acquires the Property and assumes the obligations
55 imposed by this section.
56 “(4) “Property” means the real property, including any improvements
57 thereon, located at 4020 Minnesota Avenue, N.E., Washington, D.C. 20019, known for tax and
58 assessment purposes as Lot 811 in Square 5052 (SSL 5052-0811), or as the land may
2

59 subsequently be subdivided into record lots, assessment and taxation lots, condominium lots, air-
60 rights lots, or any combination thereof.
61 “(5) “Qualifying Capital Expenditure” means an expenditure actually paid
62 by the Owner from non-District funds for a Capital Improvement included in a capital
63 improvement plan approved pursuant to subsection (c) of this section.
64 “(B) The term “Qualifying Capital Expenditure” shall not include:
65 “(i) Routine maintenance or ordinary operating expenses;
66 “(ii) Management fees;
67 “(iii) Debt service;
68 “(iv) Distributions to owners or investors;
69 “(v) Penalties or interest;
70 “(vi) Legal, accounting, or other professional expenses not
71 directly attributable to a Capital Improvement; or
72 “(vii) The portion of an expenditure paid or reimbursed
73 with District financial assistance.
74 “(6) “Tax year” means a real property tax year of the District.
75 “(b)(1) Subject to subsections (c) through (j) of this section, for the 7 consecutive tax
76 years beginning on October 1, 2027, the Property shall be allowed an annual real property tax
77 abatement equal to the lesser of:
78 “(A) Two hundred thirty thousand dollars;
79 “(B) The amount of real property taxes assessed and imposed on the
80 Property pursuant to Chapter 8 of this title for the applicable tax year, after application of any
81 other real property tax exemption, abatement, credit, or reduction applicable to the Property; or
3

82 “(C) The amount of Qualifying Capital Expenditures certified by the
83 Mayor as eligible to support the abatement for the applicable tax year.
84 “(2) The aggregate amount of the real property tax abatement provided pursuant
85 to this section shall not exceed $1,610,000.
86 “(3) An unused portion of the annual limitation provided in paragraph (1)(A) of
87 this subsection shall not be carried forward to increase the maximum abatement available in a
88 subsequent tax year.
89 “(4) An abatement shall not be allowed for a tax year unless the Mayor certifies
90 the Property as eligible for the abatement pursuant to subsection (e) of this section.
91 “(5) A suspension or disallowance of an abatement for any tax year shall not
92 extend the 7-tax-year period established by paragraph (1) of this subsection.
93 “(c)(1) As a condition of receiving an abatement pursuant to this section, the Owner shall
94 make Qualifying Capital Expenditures in an amount not less than $1 for each $1 of real property
95 tax abated pursuant to this section.
96 “(2) Before the Property may receive the first abatement pursuant to this section,
97 the Owner shall submit to the Mayor a capital improvement plan that includes:
98 “(A) A comprehensive assessment of the Property's capital needs;
99 “(B) A detailed scope of work;
100 “(C) An itemized rehabilitation budget;
101 “(D) A schedule for completing the proposed Capital Improvements;
102 “(E) The projected useful life of each major Capital Improvement;
103 “(F) All anticipated sources and uses of funds for the rehabilitation;
4

104 “(G) The amount of funding anticipated from owner equity, reserves,
105 loans, grants, tax credits, District financial assistance, federal assistance, or other sources;
106 “(H) A description of how the proposed Capital Improvements will benefit
107 existing residents; and
108 “(I) Any other information the Mayor determines necessary to evaluate the
109 proposed Capital Improvements.
110 “(3) The Mayor shall approve the capital improvement plan before certifying the
111 Property as eligible for an abatement.
112 “(4) The Owner shall obtain the Mayor's approval before making a material
113 modification to the approved capital improvement plan if the Owner intends to count
114 expenditures associated with the modification as Qualifying Capital Expenditures.
115 “(5) Capital Improvements may include:
116 “(A) Heating, ventilation, and air-conditioning systems;
117 “(B) Plumbing and water systems;
118 “(C) Electrical systems;
119 “(D) Elevators;
120 “(E) Roofs and building-envelope systems;
121 “(F) Water-intrusion, moisture, and mold remediation;
122 “(G) Residential-unit rehabilitation;
123 “(H) Common-area improvements;
124 “(I) Security and building-access systems;
125 “(J) Fire and life-safety systems;
126 “(K) Accessibility improvements;
5

127 “(L) Energy-efficiency and water-conservation improvements;
128 “(M) Pest-prevention improvements;
129 “(N) Structural repairs; and
130 “(O) Other Capital Improvements approved by the Mayor.
131 “(d)(1) As a condition of receiving an abatement pursuant to this section, the Owner shall,
132 throughout the abatement period:
133 “(A) Maintain all affordability restrictions applicable to the Property under
134 any affordability covenant, regulatory agreement, financing agreement, federal Low-Income
135 Housing Tax Credit requirement, Housing Production Trust Fund requirement, or other binding
136 District or federal requirement;
137 “(B) Not reduce the number of affordable residential units at the Property,
138 except as temporarily necessary to perform rehabilitation work;
139 “(C) Remain in substantial compliance with applicable housing, building,
140 fire, property-maintenance, and life-safety laws and regulations; and
141 “(D) Comply with the reporting and certification requirements of this
142 section.
143 “(2) No resident shall be permanently displaced solely as a result of a Capital
144 Improvement for which an expenditure is counted as a Qualifying Capital Expenditure.
145 “(3) If temporary relocation of a resident is necessary to complete a Capital
146 Improvement, the Owner shall, consistent with applicable District and federal law:
147 “(A) Provide reasonable advance notice;
148 “(B) Pay required relocation expenses;
6

149 “(C) Provide suitable temporary accommodations when required by law;
150 and
151 “(D) Provide the resident a right to return to the resident's unit or a
152 comparable unit at the Property upon completion of the work.
153 “(4) The Owner shall not use a Qualifying Capital Expenditure as the basis for a
154 rent surcharge, capital-improvement charge, or other separate charge to a tenant to recover the
155 portion of the Capital Improvement credited toward the matching requirement established by
156 subsection (c)(1) of this section.
157 “(e)(1) For each tax year for which an abatement is sought, the Owner shall provide the
158 Mayor with information sufficient for the Mayor to determine whether the Property satisfies the
159 requirements of this section.
160 “(2) The information required by paragraph (1) of this subsection shall include:
161 “(A) The amount of Qualifying Capital Expenditures made during the
162 applicable reporting period;
163 “(B) Documentation demonstrating that the expenditures were made in
164 accordance with the approved capital improvement plan;
165 “(C) Documentation demonstrating the source of funds used to pay each
166 expenditure claimed as a Qualifying Capital Expenditure;
167 “(D) The cumulative amount of Qualifying Capital Expenditures made
168 pursuant to this section;
169 “(E) The cumulative amount of real property tax abated pursuant to this
170 section;

171 “(F) Documentation demonstrating compliance with the affordability
172 requirements of subsection (d) of this section;
173 “(G) Information regarding any material housing, building, fire, property-
174 maintenance, or life-safety violations at the Property;
175 “(H) The amount of the real property tax abatement requested for the
176 applicable tax year; and
177 “(I) Any other information the Mayor determines necessary.
178 “(3) Documentation supporting an expenditure claimed as a Qualifying Capital
179 Expenditure shall include, as applicable:
180 “(A) Paid invoices;
181 “(B) Proof of payment;
182 “(C) Executed construction contracts or purchase orders;
183 “(D) Permits and inspection records;
184 “(E) Documentation of the source of funds;
185 “(F) Photographs or other evidence of completed work; and
186 “(G) An independent certification by a certified public accountant or other
187 qualified third party, if required by the Mayor.
188 “(4) By December 31 of each tax year during the abatement period, the Mayor

192 “(A) The Owner's taxpayer identification number;

196 this section;
197 “(D) The amount of the abatement for which the Property is eligible for
198 the applicable tax year; and
199 “(E) Any other information the Office of Tax and Revenue considers
200 necessary or appropriate.
201 “(6) If the Mayor determines at any time that the Property has become ineligible
202 for an abatement, the Mayor shall notify the Office of Tax and Revenue of the ineligibility and
203 the date on which the Property became ineligible.
204 “(f)(1) Following certification by the Mayor pursuant to subsection (e) of this section,
205 the Office of Tax and Revenue shall process the abatement for the applicable tax year.
206 “(2) The Chief Financial Officer may audit information or documentation relating
207 to the amount of an abatement or the application of an abatement to the Property.

209 the Owner:
210 “(A) Has failed to satisfy the dollar-for-dollar expenditure requirement

213 without approval;
214 “(C) Has materially violated an applicable affordability requirement;

9

217 “(F) Is in material violation of an applicable housing, building, fire,
218 property-maintenance, or life-safety law or regulation and has failed to timely correct the
219 violation; or
220 “(G) Is otherwise in material violation of this section.
221 “(2) Except in a case involving fraud, intentional misrepresentation, an imminent
222 threat to health or safety, or a violation that cannot reasonably be cured, the Mayor shall provide
223 the Owner written notice of the violation and at least 60 days to cure the violation before

225 “(3) No abatement shall accrue during a period in which the Property is ineligible.
226 “(h)(1) If the Owner receives an abatement that exceeds the amount for which the
