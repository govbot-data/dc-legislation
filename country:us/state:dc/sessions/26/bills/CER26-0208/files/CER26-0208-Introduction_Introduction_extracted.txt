15
16 To remember, honor, and celebrate the life of Philip A. Lepanto.
17 WHEREAS, Mr. Lepanto was a beloved husband, son, brother, uncle, mentor, neighbor
18 and friend who is remembered by those who knew him for his kind, funny, and generous spirit
19 and endless curiosity, enthusiasm, and love for the District of Columbia, and;
20 WHEREAS, Mr. Lepanto was born in Philadelphia, grew up in Kentucky, West Virginia,
21 and Virginia, eventually settling in Washington, D.C., and;
22 WHEREAS, Mr. Lepanto earned a degree in political science and history from
23 Vanderbilt University, participating in student government and the community radio station, and;
24 WHEREAS, Mr. Lepanto’s career spanned politics, public affairs, cybersecurity, and
25 angel investing, combining his passions for community, technology, and entrepreneurship, and;
26 WHEREAS, Mr. Lepanto served as an Advisory Neighborhood Commissioner for ANC
27 1D in Mount Pleasant, and;
28 WHEREAS, for 15 years Mr. Lepanto was a partner and Chief Technology Officer with
29 Connections Media LLC, an award-winning DC-based digital public affairs firm, and;
1

30 WHEREAS, Mr. Lepanto owned and operated Old School Hardware in Mount Pleasant,
31 a beloved neighborhood small business, and;
32 WHEREAS, Mr. Lepanto championed housing security for vulnerable women and
33 families, and actively supported efforts to bring live music back to the Mount Pleasant
34 neighborhood, and;
35 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, that this
36 resolution may be cited as the “Philip Lepanto Ceremonial Recognition Resolution of 2026”.
37 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia remembers, honors, and celebrates the life
38 of Mr. Philip Lepanto and his many contributions to the community.
39 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
40 the District of Columbia Register.
