16 To amend, on an emergency basis, due to congressional review, section 1507 of the Criminal
17 Justice Coordinating Council for the District of Columbia Establishment Act of 2001 to
18 change the date by which the Prearrest Diversion Task Force shall issue initial
19 recommendations for prearrest diversion of certain misdemeanor offenses and categories
20 of persons to July 2026.
21 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
22 act may be cited as the “Prearrest Diversion Task Force Recommendations Congressional
23 Review Emergency Amendment Act of 2026”.
24 Sec. 2. Section 1507 of the Criminal Justice Coordinating Council for the District of
25 Columbia Establishment Act of 2001, effective June 8, 2024 (D.C. Law 25-175; D.C. Official
26 Code § 22-4237), is amended as follows:
27 (a) Subsection (g) is amended by striking the phrase “Within 3 months after
28 the applicability date of section 29 of the Secure DC Omnibus Amendment Act of 2024, passed
29 on 2nd reading on March 5, 2024 (Enrolled version of Bill 25-345),” and inserting the phrase
30 “No later than June 2025,” in its place.
1

31 (b) Subsection (h) is amended by striking the phrase “Within one year after
32 the applicability date of section 29 of the Secure DC Omnibus Amendment Act of 2024, passed
33 on 2nd reading on March 5, 2024 (Enrolled version of Bill 25-345),” and inserting the phrase
34 “No later than July 31, 2026,” in its place.
35 Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.
36 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement of the Budget Director as the fiscal impact
37 statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, approved
38 October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a).
39 Sec. 4. Applicability Date.
40 This act shall apply as of October 28, 2026.
41 Sec. 5. Effective date.
42 This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the
43 Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), and shall remain in effect for no longer than
44 90 days, as provided for emergency acts of the Council of the District of Columbia in section
45 412(a) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 24, 1973 (87 Stat. 788;
46 D.C. Official Code § 1-204.12(a)).
