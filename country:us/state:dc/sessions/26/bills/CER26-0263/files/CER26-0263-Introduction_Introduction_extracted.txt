12 A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
13 __________
14
15 IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
16 __________
17
18
19 To honor and recognize the Court Services and Offender Supervision Agency for its
20 partnership with the District of Columbia in advancing public safety and supporting the
21 reintegration of justice-involved people into the community.
22
23 WHEREAS, on August 5, 1997, the National Capital Revitalization and Self-
24 Government Improvement Act of 1997 became law, establishing the Court Services and
25 Offender Supervision Agency for the District of Columbia (CSOSA);
26
27 WHEREAS, CSOSA assumed responsibility for adult probation supervision from the
28 Superior Court of the District of Columbia and for parole supervision from the District of
29 Columbia Board of Parole, bringing these functions together under a single agency dedicated to
30 the District’s public safety;
31
32 WHEREAS, on August 4, 2000, following a three-year trusteeship, CSOSA was certified
33 as an independent executive branch agency, and in August 2025 the agency marked 25 years of
34 independent service to the District;
35
36 WHEREAS, CSOSA’s mission is to enhance public safety by effectively managing
37 people under community supervision, and to collaborate with community and justice partners to
38 support the reintegration of those supervised;
39
40 WHEREAS, CSOSA is guided by three principles – community, accountability, and
41 justice – that inform how the agency supports people under supervision, holds them to the terms
42 of their release, and works alongside residents and partners to keep neighborhoods safe;
43
44 WHEREAS, CSOSA’s more than 600 dedicated employees support District residents on
45 probation, parole, and supervised release, supporting each person’s return to the community and
46 contributing to the safety of District neighborhoods;

47
48 WHEREAS, the Pretrial Services Agency for the District of Columbia (PSA), an
49 independent entity within CSOSA, supports the fair administration of justice by supervising
50 defendants in the community awaiting trial;
51
52 WHEREAS, the PSA has been shaped by the leadership of its directors including Jasper
53 Ormond, the agency’s first interim director; the late Paul A. Quander, Jr.; its first permanent
54 director, Nancy M. Ware; Richar S. Teschner; and its current interim director Marcus M.
55 Hodges, and by the many public servants who have carried out its mission each day;
56
57 WHEREAS, CSOSA works closely with District government agencies, including the
58 Metropolitan Police Department, the Department of Employment Services, the Department of
59 Behavioral Health, and the Mayor’s Office on Returning Citizen Affairs, to hold the people it
60 supervises accountable and connect them with employment, treatment, housing, and other
61 supports that make their future success possible;
62
63 WHEREAS, Councilmember Anita Bonds has an early history of support for the
64 Organization during the tenure of Mr. Ormond;
65
66 WHEREAS, CSOSA is well regarded throughout the communities of the District through
67 the work and communication of the Intergovernmental and Community Affairs Specialist; and
68
69 WHEREAS, when people under supervision work and have stable housing and other
70 supports, families are strong, neighborhoods are safer, and the District is better for all residents.
71
72 RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
73 resolution may be cited as the “Court Services and Offender Supervision Agency Recognition
74 Resolution of 2026”.
75
76 Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the Court Services and
77 Offender Supervision Agency for its service to the District and honors its leaders, employees,
78 and neighborhoods, and successful returns to the community.
79
80 Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon the first date of publication in
81 the District of Columbia Register.
