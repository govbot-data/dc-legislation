ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-479
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
July 14, 2026
To declare the existence of an emergency with respect to the need to approve Modification
Nos. M009, M011, M012, and M012A to Contract No. DCRL-2024-D-0073 with
All Pro All Services, and to authorize payment for the services received and to be
received under these modifications.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “Modification Nos. M009, M011, M012, and M012A to
Contract No. DCRL-2024-D-0073 with All Pro All Services Approval and Payment
Authorization Emergency Declaration Resolution of 2026”.
Sec. 2. (a) There exists an immediate need to approve Modification Nos. M009,
M011 M012, and M012A to Contract No. DCRL-2024-D-0073 with All Pro All Services
to provide school transportation services, and to authorize payment for the services
received and to be received under these modifications.
(b) By Modification No. M005, the Child and Family Services Agency (“CFSA”)
exercised option year one in the not-to-exceed amount of $1,619,000 for the period from
August 7, 2025, through August 6, 2026. This modification was deemed approved by the
Council on May 19, 2025 (CA26-0166).
(c) By Modification No. M009, issued on January 27, 2026, CFSA increased the
not-to-exceed amount by $629,640.00, to a not-to-exceed total of $2,248,640.
(d) By Modification No. M011, issued on March 3, 2026, CFSA increased the not-
to-exceed amount by $370,200.00, to a not-to-exceed total of $2,617,640.00.
(e) By Modification No. M012, issued on May 6, 2026, the CFSA increased the
not-to-exceed amount by $1,295,185 for the period from May 6, 2026, through August 7,
2026.
(f) By Modification No. M012A, CFSA incorporated the price schedule for the
remainder of the option year one for the period from August 7, 2025, through August 6,
2026, in the not-to-exceed amount of $1,295,185, making the total not-to-exceed amount
for the option year period $3,914,025.
(g) Council approval is necessary because the modifications increase the total
contract amount by more than $1 million during a 12-month period.

(h) Council approval is necessary to allow the continuation of these vital services.
Without Council approval, All Pro All Services cannot be paid for services provided in
excess of $1 million for the period from August 7, 2025, through August 6, 2026.
Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia determines that the circumstances
enumerated in section 2 constitute emergency circumstances making it necessary that the
Modification Nos. M009, M011 M012, and M012A to Contract No. DCRL-2024-D-0073
with All Pro All Services Approval and Payment Authorization Emergency Act of 2026 be
adopted after a single reading.
Sec. 4. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
