ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A CEREMONIAL RESOLUTION
26-202
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
September 22, 2026
To honor and recognize St. Paul African Methodist Episcopal Church, on the 170th anniversary
of its founding.
WHEREAS, St. Paul Methodist Episcopal Church was founded on October 25, 1856, by
a courageous group of freed men led by Anthony Bowen, establishing a legacy of faith, freedom,
and education that is now celebrating 170 years of service to our community;
WHEREAS, in its early years, the congregation was known by several names, including
the “E” Street Mission, the St. Paul Society, and the St. Paul Chapel, reflecting its growth and
evolving role within the African American community of the District of Columbia;
WHEREAS, in 1859, the founders of the congregation purchased land on 8th Street, SW,
establishing a permanent home for the church and creating a lasting institution in the District of
Columbia;
WHEREAS, during its first 100 years, St. Paul AME Church served not only as a house
of worship, but also as an educational institution and a center of community life;
WHEREAS, St. Paul AME Church was an important gathering place for supporters of
the Underground Railroad and African Americans seeking emancipation;
WHEREAS, the history of St. Paul AME Church is intertwined with the history of
African Americans and their continuing struggle for freedom, equality, justice, and human
dignity;
WHEREAS, throughout its 170-year history, St. Paul AME Church has remained an
institution serving our community with hope, education, faith, fellowship, and ministry;

WHEREAS, the history of St. Paul AME Church is intertwined with the history of
African Americans and their continuing struggle for freedom, equality, justice, and human
dignity;
WHEREAS, for 170 years, the clergy, leaders, members, and family of St. Paul have
contributed to the spiritual, civic, education, and cultural life of DC; and
WHEREAS, the 170th anniversary of the establishment of St. Paul provides an important
opportunity to honor the founders and generation of congregants whose faith, sacrifice, and
service have sustained this historic institution.
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That the
Council of the District of Columbia recognizes the St. Paul American Methodist Episcopal
Church.
Sec. 2. The resolution may be cited as the “St. Paul American Methodist Episcopal
Church Recognition Resolution of 2026.”
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
